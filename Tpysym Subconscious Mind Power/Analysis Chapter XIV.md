# Chapter XIV: Your Subconscious Mind and Marital Problems

## Chapter Title and Number
**Chapter XIV - Your Subconscious Mind and Marital Problems**

## Actionable Lessons with Step-by-Step Instructions

### 1. How to Attract the Ideal Husband

**Instructions:**
1. Sit down at night in your armchair
2. Close your eyes, let go, and relax your body
3. Become very quiet, passive, and receptive
4. Talk to your subconscious mind with the following affirmation:
   > "I am now attracting a man into my experience who is honest, sincere, loyal, faithful, peaceful, happy and prosperous. These qualities which I admire are sinking down into my subconscious mind now. As I dwell upon these characteristics, they become a part of me and are embodied subconsciously."
5. Continue with:
   > "I know there is an irresistible law of attraction and that I attract to me a man according to my subconscious belief. I attract that which I feel to be true in my subconscious mind."
6. Add:
   > "I know I can contribute to his peace and happiness. He loves my ideals, and I love his ideals. He does not want to make me over, neither do I want to make him over. There is mutual love, freedom and respect."
7. Practice this process of impregnating your subconscious regularly
8. Maintain a keen desire to give the best of love, devotion, and cooperation
9. Be receptive to this gift of love

**Expected Result:** Your subconscious intelligence will open up a pathway whereby both of you will meet according to the irresistible and changeless flow of your own subconscious mind.

### 2. How to Attract the Ideal Wife

**Instructions:**
1. Affirm as follows:
   > "I now attract the right woman who is in complete accord with me. This is a spiritual union because it is divine love functioning through the personality of someone with whom I blend perfectly. I know I can give to this woman love, light, peace and joy. I feel and believe I can make this woman's life full, complete and wonderful."
2. Continue with:
   > "I now decree that she possesses the following qualities and attributes: She is spiritual, loyal, faithful and true. She is harmonious, peaceful and happy. We are irresistibly attracted to each other. Only that which belongs to love, truth and beauty can enter my experience. I accept my ideal companion now."
3. Think quietly and with interest on the qualities and attributes you admire in the companion you seek
4. Build the mental equivalent into your mentality

**Expected Result:** The deeper currents of your subconscious mind will bring both of you together in divine order.

---

### 3. How to Break a Negative Pattern (Case Study: Attracting Dominant Men)

**Instructions:**
1. Recognize the pattern: Understand that subconscious beliefs attract corresponding reality
2. Use the following specific prayer to break the old subconscious pattern and attract the ideal mate:
   > "I am building into my mentality the type of man I deeply desire. The man I attract for a husband is strong, powerful, loving, very masculine, successful, honest, loyal and faithful. He finds love and happiness with me. I love to follow where he leads."
3. Continue with:
   > "I know he wants me, and I want him. I am honest, sincere, loving and kind. I have wonderful gifts to offer him. They are good will, a joyous heart and a healthy body. He offers me the same. It is mutual, I give and I receive. Divine Intelligence knows where this man is, and the deeper wisdom of my subconscious mind is now bringing both of us together in its own way and we recognize each other immediately. I release this request to my subconscious mind which knows how to bring my request to pass. I give thanks for the perfect answer."
4. Pray in this manner night and morning
5. Affirm these truths repeatedly
6. Continue frequent occupation of the mind until you reach the mental equivalent of that which you seek

**Key Insight:** When you really believe you can have the type of man or woman you idealize, it is done unto you as you believe.

### 4. Steps in Prayer: Pray Together and Stay Together

#### The First Step
1. Never carry over from one day to another accumulated irritations arising from little disappointments
2. Be sure to forgive each other for any sharpness before you retire at night
3. The moment you awaken in the morning:
   - Claim Infinite Intelligence is guiding you in all your ways
   - Send out loving thoughts of peace, harmony, and love to your marriage partner
   - Send loving thoughts to all members of the family and to the whole world

#### The Second Step
1. Say grace at breakfast
2. Give thanks for the wonderful food, for your abundance, and for all your blessings
3. Make sure that no problems, worries, or arguments enter into the table conversation (same applies at dinner time)
4. Say to your wife or husband: "I appreciate all you are doing and I radiate love and good will to you all day long."

#### The Third Step
1. The husband and wife should alternate in praying each night
2. Do not take your marriage partner for granted
3. Show your appreciation and love
4. Think appreciation and good will rather than condemnation, criticism, and nagging
5. Read the following before going to sleep:
   - 23rd Psalm
   - 27th Psalm
   - 91st Psalm
   - 11th chapter of Hebrews
   - 13th chapter of 1 Corinthians
   - Other great texts of the Bible
6. Build your marriage on a foundation of love, beauty, harmony, mutual respect, faith in God, and all things good

### 5. How to Reverse a Marriage Drifting Toward Divorce

**Instructions:**
1. Both partners must agree to return and experiment with prayer therapy together
2. Each one practices radiating harmony, health, peace, and love to the other
3. Alternate in reading the Psalms every night
4. When tempted to question, waver, doubt, or vacillate, remind yourself that Infinite Intelligence is bringing it to pass in its own way
5. Refrain from concern; trust the process

**Expected Result:** The marriage will grow more beautiful every day.

### 6. Mental Picturing for Marriage Improvement

**Instructions:**

2. See your husband as he ought to be: strong, powerful, loving, harmonious, and kind
3. Maintain this mental picture consistently

**Expected Result:** You will experience the marriage made in heaven, which is harmony and peace.

**Instructions:**

## Key Principles and Concepts

1. **Spiritual Basis of Marriage:** Marriage to be real must first be on a spiritual basis. It must be of the heart, for the heart is the chalice of love.

2. **Ignorance Causes Marital Trouble:** Ignorance of the functions and powers of the mind is the cause of all marital trouble. Problems of divorce, separation, annulment, and endless litigation are directly traceable to lack of knowledge of the working and interrelationship of the conscious and subconscious mind.

3. **Law of Mental Attraction:** Whatever you impress upon your subconscious mind will be experienced in your world. You attract to you a man or woman according to your subconscious belief. You attract that which you feel to be true in your subconscious mind.

4. **Divorce Begins in the Mind:** Divorce takes place first in the mind; the legal proceedings follow after. Thoughts of resentment, fear, suspicion, and anger create the conditions that lead to separation.

5. **Mental Equivalent Principle:** You must build into your mentality the mental equivalent of what you want in a marriage partner. If you want to attract an honest, sincere, and loving partner, you must be honest, sincere, and loving yourself.

6. **Law of Action and Reaction:** The thought is the action and the response of the subconscious mind is the reaction. Fear patterns projected to a marriage partner create the very conditions feared.

7. **Mental Saturation:** To manifest the desired partner, you must claim it mentally until you reach the point of saturation, mentally and emotionally uniting with the idea until it becomes a part of you.

8. **Divine Intelligence Knows How:** Do not wonder how, why, or where you will meet your ideal mate. Trust implicitly the wisdom of your subconscious mind. It has the "know-how," and you do not have to assist it.

9. **Marriage is Union of Hearts:** Marriage is the union of a man and woman bound together by love. Their hearts beat as one, and they move onward, upward, and Godward.

10. **Marriage Does Not Bequeath Happiness:** Happiness comes from dwelling on the eternal truths of God and the spiritual values of life. Then, the man and woman can contribute to each other's happiness and joy.

11. **Hate Divides, Love Unites:** Hate divides and love unites. Positive mental attitudes build marriage; negative ones destroy it.

12. **Brooding is Psychological Adultery:** When a man is silently resenting his wife and full of hostility toward her, he is unfaithful to his marriage vows. One meaning of adultery is idolatry—giving attention to or uniting mentally with that which is negative and destructive.

13. **Inner Security:** A woman (or anyone) is not dependent on a spouse for health, peace, joy, inspiration, guidance, love, wealth, security, or happiness. Security comes from knowledge of inner powers and constant use of the laws of one's own mind in a constructive fashion.

## Best Practices Mentioned

1. **Prevent divorce before marriage:** The best time to prevent divorce is before marriage. Learn to pray correctly and attract the right mate.

2. **Pray together:** By praying together, couples stay together. Scientific prayer solves all marital problems.

3. **Forgive daily:** Never carry over accumulated irritations. Forgive each other for any sharpness before retiring at night.

4. **Morning practice:** Upon awakening, claim Infinite Intelligence is guiding you and send loving thoughts to your partner, family, and the world.

5. **Gratitude at meals:** Say grace at breakfast and dinner. Give thanks for food, abundance, and blessings. Keep table conversation free of problems, worries, and arguments.

6. **Alternate nightly prayer:** Husband and wife should alternate in praying each night. Do not take your partner for granted.

7. **Read spiritual texts before sleep:** Read the 23rd, 27th, and 91st Psalms, the 11th chapter of Hebrews, the 13th chapter of 1 Corinthians, and other great spiritual texts before going to sleep.

8. **Focus on virtues:** Give attention and praise for the constructive and wonderful qualities in your partner. Praise and exalt all their good points.

9. **Seek expert counsel:** Always seek advice from a trained counselor for marital problems, not from neighbors or relatives.

10. **Keep quarrels private:** Never display the unhappy side of your marriage to friends. Keep quarrels to yourself. Refrain from criticism and condemnation of your partner.

11. **Radiate positive vibrations:** Radiate love, peace, and good will to each other. These vibrations are picked up by the subconscious mind, resulting in mutual trust, affection, and respect.

12. **Assume the harmonious state:** Through praise and mental effort, get out of the habit of antagonism. Assume the harmonious state, and eventually you will find peace and harmony.

13. **Mental picturing:** Mentally picture your spouse as they ought to be, maintaining that image consistently.

14. **Express appreciation:** Regularly say to your partner, "I appreciate all you are doing and I radiate love and good will to you all day long."

## Common Pitfalls Mentioned

1. **Marrying for wrong reasons:** Marrying for money, social position, or to lift one's ego indicates a lack of security, honesty, and true love. Such a marriage is a farce, a sham, and a masquerade.

2. **False premise of security:** Believing that marriage will provide security is a false premise. Security depends on knowledge of the interaction of the conscious and subconscious mind and its application.

3. **Repeating mistakes:** Not learning from previous marital mistakes and unconsciously repeating patterns that attract undesirable partners.

4. **Discussing problems with outsiders:** Discussing marital problems with neighbors and relatives degrades and belittles the partner, creates negative opinions, and reinforces negative states within yourself. Relatives usually give biased, prejudiced advice.

5. **Trying to change your partner:** A husband or wife must not try to make the other into a second edition of themselves. These attempts are foolish, destroy pride and self-esteem, and arouse a spirit of contrariness and resentment that proves fatal to the marriage bond.

6. **Nagging:** Nagging is usually a craving for love and affection or an attempt to make the partner conform to a particular pattern. It is one of the quickest ways to get rid of a partner.

7. **Brooding and resentment:** A husband who broods, grows morbid, and silently resents his wife is psychologically committing adultery. Bitterness and resentment weaken and exhaust the whole being.

8. **Projecting fear patterns:** Constantly fearing that a partner will leave, be unfaithful, or reject you creates an atmosphere of loss and separation that brings about the very conditions feared.

9. **Indulging in negative emotions:** You are mentally divorced when you indulge in peeves, grudges, ill will, and hostility toward your marriage partner. You are dwelling with error in the bed of your mind.

10. **Displaying unhappy marriage:** Never display the unhappy side of your marriage to friends. This creates negative mental patterns and reinforces problems.

11. **Focusing on faults:** Husband and wife must cease being scavengers always looking at the petty faults or errors in each other.

12. **Taking partner for granted:** Failing to show appreciation and love, assuming the partner will always be there without expressing gratitude.
