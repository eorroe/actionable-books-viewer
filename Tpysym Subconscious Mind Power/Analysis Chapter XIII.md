# Chapter XIII: Your Subconscious — The Wonders Of Sleep

## List of All Actionable Lessons with Step-by-Step Instructions

### Lesson 1: Use Sleep to Receive Answers to Problems
- Before sleeping, identify a problem you need guidance on.
- Speak or think a prayer to your subconscious mind prior to sleep, requesting guidance, strength, and greater intelligence in all phases of your life.
- Withdraw periodically from sense evidence and the objective world to commune silently with the inner wisdom of your subconscious mind.
- By claiming guidance, strength, and greater intelligence, you will be enabled to overcome all difficulties and solve your daily problems.

### Lesson 2: Use Sleep as a Form of Prayer / Withdrawal from the Senses
- Become "asleep to the world of the senses" and "alive to the wisdom and power of your subconscious mind."
- This regular withdrawal from sense evidence and the noise and confusion of everyday living is itself a form of sleep that connects you to your subconscious.

### Lesson 3: Ensure Adequate Sleep for Health
- Sleep a minimum of eight hours per night.
- Understand that adequate sleep is essential to produce joy and vitality in life.
- Recognize that lack of sleep can cause you to become irritable, moody, and depressed.
- Be aware that severe insomnia can precede psychotic breakdown in some instances.

### Lesson 4: Use the "Sleep Lullaby" Prayer (Insomnia Cure)
- Prior to sleep, repeat the following slowly, quietly, and lovingly:
  - "My toes are relaxed, my ankles are relaxed, my abdominal muscles are relaxed, my heart and lungs are relaxed, my hands and arms are relaxed, my neck is relaxed, my brain is relaxed, my face is relaxed, my eyes are relaxed, my whole mind and body are relaxed."
  - "I fully and freely forgive everyone, and I sincerely wish for them harmony, health, peace and all the blessings of life."
  - "I am at peace; I am poised, serene and calm. I rest in security and in peace. A great stillness steals over me, and a great calm quiets my whole being as I realize the divine presence within me."
  - "I know that the realization of life and love heals me. I wrap myself in the mantel of love and fall asleep filled with good will for all. Throughout the night peace remains with me and in the morning I shall be filled with life and love."
  - "A circle of love is drawn around me. I will fear no evil, for Thou art with me, I sleep in peace, I wake in joy, and in Him I live, move and have my being."

### Lesson 5: Set Your Subconscious to Wake You at a Specific Time
- Before going to sleep, suggest to your subconscious mind the exact time you wish to arise.
- Your subconscious will awaken you without a clock.
- Apply this same principle to all problems — nothing is too hard for your subconscious.

### Lesson 6: Forgive Before Sleeping for Faster Healing
- Before going to sleep, forgive yourself and everyone else.
- The healing process takes place more rapidly while you are asleep because there is no interference from your conscious mind.

### Lesson 7: Use Sleep to Solve Problems (Creative Problem-Solving)
- Think about the answer to a problem prior to sleep.
- When you fall asleep, your subconscious will work on the problem and may present the answer in a dream or as a persistent feeling upon awakening.
- Examples:
  - Ray Hammerstrom lay down for a nap, thought about a faulty switch problem before sleep, dreamed the perfect design, and received $15,000 for the solution.
  - Dr. H.V. Helprecht went to bed weary, asked for the answer to deciphering ancient fragments, dreamed the solution, and found the dream verified upon waking.
  - Robert Louis Stevenson gave his subconscious specific instructions every night prior to sleep to evolve stories for him while he slept, and his subconscious responded with marketable novels.

### Lesson 8: Expect Guidance and Watch for the Lead Upon Awakening
- Prior to sleep, claim that the Infinite Intelligence of your subconscious mind is guiding and directing you.
- Watch for the lead or answer which comes, perhaps upon awakening.

### Lesson 9: Trust Your Subconscious Completely
- Know that the tendency of your subconscious is always lifeward.
- Trust that it will warn you if needed, sometimes through a vivid dream or vision in the night.
- Be open to warnings coming through dreams, sometimes in the voice of a mother or loved one, to protect you from danger.

### Lesson 10: Claim That Infinite Intelligence Leads and Guides You
- Claim that Infinite Intelligence leads and guides you; that all good is yours, and your future will be wonderful.
- Believe it and accept it. Expect the best and invariably the best will come to you.

### Lesson 11: Speak to Your Subconscious for Creative Work
- If you are writing a novel, play, or book, or are working on an invention, speak to your subconscious mind at night.
- Claim boldly that its wisdom, intelligence, and power are guiding, directing, and revealing to you the ideal work or perfect solution, whatever it may be.
- Wonders will happen as you pray this way.

### Lesson 12: Understand That Your Future Is in Your Subconscious Mind Now
- Remember that your future, the result of your habitual thinking, is already in your mind except when you change it through prayer.
- Through scientific prayer, you can mold, fashion, and create your own future.
- Whatsoever a man soweth, that shall he also reap.
- Your mental attitude — the way you think, feel, and believe — determines your destiny.

## Key Principles and Concepts

1. **Sleep is a divine law** — You spend about one third of your life in sleep, and it applies to the animal and vegetable kingdoms.

2. **Nothing truly rests in sleep** — Your heart, lungs, and all vital organs function while you sleep. Your skin secretes perspiration, and your nails and hair continue to grow.

3. **Your subconscious mind never rests or sleeps** — It is always active, controlling all your vital forces. The healing process takes place more rapidly while you are asleep because there is no interference from your conscious mind.

4. **Answers come during sleep** — Remarkable answers to your problems come to you when you are sound asleep.

5. **Prayer is a form of sleep** — Withdrawing from sense evidence and the objective world to commune with your subconscious mind is akin to sleep; you become asleep to the world of the senses and alive to the wisdom and power of your subconscious.

6. **Your future is in your subconscious mind** — Tomorrow's events are already in your subconscious mind based on your habitual thinking, and they may be seen by a highly psychic or clairvoyant person. Your future is in your mind now, based on your habitual thinking and beliefs.

7. **Scientific prayer changes the future** — Nothing is predetermined or foreordained. Through scientific prayer, you can mold, fashion, and create your own future.

8. **Sleep deprivation has severe effects** — The tired brain craves sleep so hungrily that it will sacrifice anything to get it. Sleep-deprived people experience micro-sleeps (lapses), poor memory, and lack proper coordination.

9. **The subconscious knows all things** — It is all-wise, knows all things, and will sometimes speak to you in a voice your conscious mind will immediately accept as true (e.g., the voice of a mother or loved one).

## Best Practices Mentioned

1. **Sleep eight hours per night** — This is the minimum recommended for healthy functioning; most people need more.

2. **Forgive before sleeping** — Forgive yourself and everyone else before going to sleep to enable faster healing.

3. **Use a pre-sleep prayer or affirmation** — Claim guidance, strength, and greater intelligence before sleep to commune with your subconscious.

4. **Use a relaxation prayer for insomnia** — Systematically relax each body part and affirm peace, forgiveness, and divine presence before sleep.

5. **Set your subconscious to wake you** — Suggest the exact time you wish to arise before sleep rather than relying on an alarm.

6. **Give your subconscious specific instructions for creative work** — Whether writing a novel, working on an invention, or solving a problem, speak to your subconscious with bold, specific claims before sleep.

7. **Watch for the answer upon awakening** — After praying or setting a problem before your subconscious at night, watch for the lead or answer that comes, perhaps on awakening.

8. **Trust the inward sense of knowing** — When your subconscious prompts you with a persistent feeling or dream, trust it and act accordingly.

---

## Common Pitfalls Mentioned

1. **Thinking you can get by on less than six hours of sleep** — Dr. George Stevenson states that those who think they can get along on less are fooling themselves.

2. **Ignoring sleep deprivation effects** — Severe insomnia has preceded psychotic breakdown in some instances.

3. **Allowing the conscious mind to interfere with healing** — Healing takes place more rapidly while you are asleep because there is no interference from your conscious mind.

4. **Being befuddled by sleep deprivation** — Sleep-deprived people have poor memories, lack proper coordination, and become befuddled, confused, and disorientated.

5. **Falling asleep at dangerous times** — Micro-sleeps can occur even when operating machinery or driving, leading to accidents.

6. **Not trusting subconscious warnings** — Failing to heed warnings from your subconscious (through dreams or inner knowing) can lead to disaster.

## Techniques and Methods Described

### 1. Pre-Sleep Prayer / Affirmation for Guidance
- **Method**: Before sleeping, repeat a prayer affirming that the creative intelligence of your subconscious mind knows what is best for you, that its tendency is always lifeward, and that it will reveal the right decision which blesses you and all concerned.
- **Example**: "The creative intelligence of my subconscious mind knows what is best for me. Its tendency is always lifeward, and it reveals to me the right decision which blesses me and all concerned. I give thanks for the answer which I know will come to me."

### 2. Pre-Sleep Prayer for Major Decisions
- **Method**: Before sleeping, pray: "Infinite Intelligence within me knows all things, and the right decision is revealed to me in divine order. I will recognize the answer when it comes."
- Repeat as a lullaby prior to sleep and watch for the answer in a dream, vision, or persistent feeling upon awakening.

### 3. The Relaxation and Forgiveness Prayer (For Insomnia)
- **Method**: Systematically relax each body part (toes, ankles, abdominal muscles, heart and lungs, hands and arms, neck, brain, face, eyes) while affirming relaxation.
- Follow with forgiveness of all people and affirmations of peace, divine presence, and love.
- Repeat slowly, quietly, and lovingly prior to sleep.

### 4. Setting a Wake-Up Time via Subconscious Suggestion
- **Method**: Before sleeping, suggest to your subconscious mind the exact time you wish to arise.
- Your subconscious will awaken you without a clock.

### 5. Creative Problem-Solving During Sleep
- **Method**: Think about the problem you need to solve before sleep. Your subconscious will continue working on it during sleep and may provide the answer in a dream or as a realization upon waking.

### 6. Directing the Subconscious for Creative Projects
- **Method**: Before sleep, give your subconscious specific instructions for what you want to create (e.g., "Give me a good thrilling novel which will be marketable and profitable").
- Your subconscious will work on the project while you sleep and deliver ideas piece by piece.

### 7. Prayer as Withdrawal from the Senses
- **Method**: Periodically withdraw from sense evidence and the noise and confusion of everyday living. Commune silently with the inner wisdom of your subconscious mind. This is a form of sleep that connects you to your subconscious.
