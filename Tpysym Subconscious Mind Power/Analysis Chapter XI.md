# Chapter XI: Your Subconscious Mind As A Partner In Success

## Actionable Lessons with Step-by-Step Instructions

### The Three Steps To Success

**Step 1: Find and Pursue Your True Calling**
- Identify the thing you love to do, then commit to doing it
- Success is found in loving your work
- If you don't know what you should do, pray for guidance using this prayer: "The Infinite Intelligence of my subconscious mind reveals to me my true place in life."
- Repeat this prayer quietly, positively, and lovingly to your deeper mind
- Persist with faith and confidence until the answer comes as a feeling, hunch, tendency, or inner silent awareness
- Continue advancing in your chosen field (example: a psychiatrist must keep up with the latest scientific articles, attend conventions, and continue studying)

**Step 2: Specialize and Become the Expert**
- Choose a particular branch of work and concentrate exclusively on it
- Give all of your time and attention to your chosen specialty
- Become ardently interested in your work and desire to serve the world
- Strive to know more about your field than anyone else
- Adopt a servant mindset rather than merely wanting to "make a living" or "get by"

**Step 3: Ensure Your Work Benefits Humanity**
- Make sure your desire does not redound to your success only
- Your desire must not be selfish; it must benefit humanity
- Form a complete circuit: your idea must go forth with the purpose of blessing or serving the world
- When you serve others, your success returns "pressed down, shaken together and running over"
- If your desire benefits only yourself, you may experience a "short circuit" in life manifesting as limitation or sickness

### Prayer for Guidance (When You Don't Know Your Path)
- Use this prayer: "The Infinite Intelligence of my subconscious mind reveals to me my true place in life."
- Repeat quietly, positively, and lovingly
- Persist with faith and confidence
- Wait for the answer as a feeling, hunch, tendency in a certain direction, or inner silent awareness that comes clearly and in peace

### Technique for Buying and Selling

**Preparation Phase:**
- Relax completely
- Immobilize the attention
- Get still and be quiet
- Maintain a quiet, relaxed, and peaceful attitude of mind to prevent extraneous matter and false ideas from interfering

**For Buying a Home or Property:**
- Begin to imagine the reality of that which you desire
- Affirm: "The Infinite Intelligence of my subconscious mind is all-wise. It reveals to me now the ideal home which is central, ideal, is in a lovely environment, meets all my requirements and is commensurate with my income. I am now turning this request over to my subconscious mind, and I know it responds according to the nature of my request. I release this request with absolute faith and confidence in the same way that a farmer deposits a seed in the ground, trusting implicitly in the laws of growth."
- Trust that the answer will come through various channels (advertisement, friend, direct guidance)
- The principle to rely on: the answer always comes provided you trust the working of your deeper mind

**For Selling a Home or Property:**
- Use imagination to act as if the sale is already complete
- Example technique: Ask yourself "Supposing you sold your house, what would you do?" Then answer by mentally performing the actions (e.g., taking down the sign, throwing it into the garage)
- Feel the inner satisfaction of it being finished
- The outer action will conform to the inner

**Alternative Selling Affirmation:**
- Affirm slowly, quietly, and feelingly: "Infinite Intelligence attracts to me the buyer for this home who wants and who prospers in it. This buyer may look at many other homes, but mine is the only one he wants and will buy, because he is guided by the Infinite Intelligence within him. I know the buyer is right, the time is right and the price is right. Everything about it is right. The deeper currents of my subconscious mind are now in operation bringing both of us together in divine order. I know that it is so."
- Remember: what you are seeking is also seeking you; there is always someone who wants what you have to offer
- This technique frees your mind of all sense of competition and anxiety in buying and selling

### Study and Memory Improvement Technique

**For Students with Poor Grades or Memory Issues:**
- Identify and change negative attitudes (indifference, resentment toward teachers and fellow students)
- Affirm several times a day, particularly at night before sleep and in the morning after awakening (these are the best times to impregnate the subconscious mind)
- Use these specific affirmations:
  - "I realize that my subconscious mind is a storehouse of memory. It retains everything I read and hear from my teachers."
  - "I have a perfect memory and the Infinite Intelligence in my subconscious mind constantly reveals to me everything I need to know at all my examinations, whether written or oral."
  - "I radiate love and good will to all my teachers and fellow students. I sincerely wish for them success and all good things."
- Imagine teachers and loved ones congratulating you on your success

### Sustained Imagination Technique (Visualization for Years)

- Clearly visualize your desired outcome (example: a movie actor imagining his name in big lights at a large theater)
- Keep this up consistently over a long period of time
- The power of sustained imagination brings success through the subconscious mind

### Business Success Visualization Technique (Goethe Method)

- Have imaginary conversations with friends or mentors
- Imagine them in a chair before you, answering you in the right way
- Include usual gestures and tonal qualities of the voice
- Make the entire imaginary scene as real and vivid as possible
- Use this for business decisions and problem-solving

### Executive Success Technique (Word Repetition)

- Quietly repeat the abstract term "success" over and over many times a day
- Continue until you reach a conviction that success is yours
- Your subconscious mind will accept it as true of you
- You will be under a subconscious compulsion to succeed

### Goal Achievement Through Imagination (Pharmacy Example)

- First step: Awaken to the powers of your subconscious mind
- Second step: Realize that conveying an idea to your subconscious mind will bring it to pass
- Mentally act as though you already have what you want
- Example for owning a store:
  - Imagine being in your own store
  - Mentally arrange bottles, dispense prescriptions
  - Imagine clerks waiting on customers
  - Visualize a big bank balance
  - Live the role completely: "Act as though I am, and I will be"
- Put yourself wholeheartedly into the act, living, moving, and acting on the assumption that you already possess it

### Bedtime Success Technique

- Go to sleep feeling successful every night
- Be perfectly satisfied as you drift off
- This implants the idea of success in your subconscious mind
- Believe you were born to succeed

## Key Principles and Concepts

- **Definition of Success:** Success means successful living - a long period of peace, joy, and happiness on this plane. The real things of life (peace, harmony, integrity, security, happiness) are intangible and come from the Deep Self of man.
- **Subconscious Mind as Storehouse:** Your subconscious mind is a storehouse of memory that retains everything you read and hear.
- **Creative Thought:** Thought fused with feeling becomes a subjective faith or belief, and according to your belief it is done unto you (Matthew 9:29).
- **As Within, So Without:** According to the image impressed on your subconscious mind, so it is on the objective screen of life. The outside mirrors the inside. External action follows internal action.
- **Complete Circuit Principle:** Your desire must benefit humanity and form a complete circuit. What goes forth to serve returns multiplied.
- **No Success Without Peace of Mind:** There is no true success without peace of mind. Wealth obtained through fraud or guilt brings no real success.
- **Subconscious Compulsion:** You are compelled to express your subjective beliefs, impressions, and convictions.
- **Infinite Intelligence:** The Infinite Intelligence within your subconscious mind is all-wise and responds according to the nature of your request.
- **Sustained Imagination:** The power of sustained imagination draws forth the miracle-working powers of your subconscious mind.
- **Idea Contains All Elements:** The idea of success contains all the essential elements of success.

## Best Practices Mentioned

1. Meditate on peace, harmony, integrity, security, and happiness to build treasures in your subconscious mind
2. Keep up with the times, attend conventions, and continue studying your field
3. Read the latest scientific articles in your profession
4. Have the interest of others at heart (patients, clients, students)
5. Serve others and cast your bread upon the waters
6. Use the best times to impregnate the subconscious mind: at night prior to sleep and in the morning after awakening
7. Relax completely before conveying desires to the subconscious mind
8. Release requests with absolute faith and confidence, like a farmer planting a seed
9. Make your imaginary scenes as real and vivid as possible, including sounds, gestures, and sensations
10. Repeat success affirmations frequently with faith and conviction
11. Radiate love and good will to all people
12. Wish success and good things for others
13. Believe you were born to succeed
14. Go to sleep feeling successful every night

## Common Pitfalls Mentioned

1. **"Getting By" Mentality:** "Getting by" is not true success. Your motive must be great, nobler, and more altruistic.
2. **Selfish Desires:** If your desire benefits only yourself, you may experience a "short circuit" in life manifesting as limitation or sickness.
3. **Fraudulent Success:** Wealth obtained through fraud, dishonesty, or harming others:
   - Takes wings and flies away
   - Creates a mood of lack and limitation
   - Manifests as sickness, guilt complex, inner disorders
   - Brings constant dread and fear
   - Robbing others is robbing yourself
4. **Accumulating Wealth Without Peace:** What good is accumulated wealth if you cannot sleep nights, are sick, or have a guilt complex?
5. **Indifference and Resentment:** Negative attitudes toward teachers, fellow students, or others hinder success and memory.
6. **Lack of Specialization:** Failing to concentrate on a particular branch of work prevents becoming an expert.

## Techniques and Methods Described

### 1. The Three-Step Success Method
- Find what you love to do
- Specialize and become the expert
- Ensure your work benefits humanity

### 2. Guided Prayer Technique
- Formulate a clear prayer for guidance
- Repeat quietly, positively, and lovingly
- Persist with faith and confidence
- Receive answer as inner awareness

### 3. Relaxation and Mental Absorption Method
- Relax completely
- Immobilize attention
- Get still and be quiet
- Maintain peaceful, receptive attitude
- Reduces effort to a minimum for mental absorption

### 4. Visualization/Imagination Method
- Imagine the reality of your desired outcome
- Make scenes vivid and real
- Include sensory details (sights, sounds, feelings, gestures)
- Live in the imagination as though it were already true

### 5. Affirmation Method
- Repeat specific statements with feeling and conviction
- Best times: morning after awakening and night before sleep
- Use first-person present tense statements of truth

### 6. Imaginary Conversation Technique (Goethe Method)
- Imagine a friend, mentor, or authority figure before you
- Have them answer your questions in the right way
- Include gestures and tonal qualities of voice
- Dramatize until psychologically fixed as belief

### 7. Sustained Imagination Technique
- Hold a clear mental picture of your desired outcome for an extended period
- The movie actor imagined his name in lights for years until it manifested

### 8. "Acting As If" Method
- Mentally act out the role of the person you want to become
- Perform actions in imagination that you would do if you already had it
- Feel the satisfaction of possession

### 9. Word Repetition/Conviction Building
- Repeat the word "success" (or other desired state) repeatedly throughout the day
- Continue until you reach genuine conviction
- The subconscious accepts repeated ideas as truth

### 10. The Seed Planting Metaphor
- Release your request with faith and confidence like a farmer planting a seed
- Trust implicitly in the laws of growth and manifestation
- The subconscious mind works according to natural laws

### 11. The Complete Circuit Principle
- Ensure your desires form a complete circuit by serving humanity
- What goes out in service returns multiplied
- Selfish desires create short circuits

## Summary of Core Message

This chapter teaches that the subconscious mind is a partner in success when properly directed. True success is defined as successful living characterized by peace, joy, and happiness, not mere accumulation of wealth. The path to success involves: (1) discovering and pursuing what you love, (2) specializing and becoming the authority in your field, and (3) ensuring your work serves humanity. The subconscious mind operates through imagination, sustained visualization, affirmation, and feeling. When you impress your subconscious with clear, feeling-filled images of success, it works behind the scenes to bring them into reality. The key is to serve others, maintain peace of mind, and trust the inner workings of your deeper mind.
