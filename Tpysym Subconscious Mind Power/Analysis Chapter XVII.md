# Chapter XVII: How To Use Your Subconscious For Forgiveness

---

## Actionable Lessons with Step-by-Step Instructions

### 1. The Technique of Forgiveness
This is the primary method taught in the chapter for practicing forgiveness:

1. Quiet your mind and relax; let go.
2. Think of God and His love for you.
3. Affirm: "I fully and freely forgive [name of offender]; I release him mentally and spiritually."
4. State: "I completely forgive everything connected with the matter in question. I am free, and he/she is free."
5. Declare it your "day of general amnesty" and release anyone and everyone who has ever hurt you.
6. Wish for each person health, happiness, peace, and all the blessings of life.
7. Do this freely, joyously, and lovingly.
8. Whenever the person or persons who hurt you come to mind, say: "I have released you, and all the blessings of life are yours. I am free and you are free."
9. **Important:** Once you have forgiven the person, do NOT repeat the full prayer. Instead, whenever the thought enters your mind, simply wish the person well and say, "Peace be to you."

### 2. How to Respond to Criticism
1. If the criticism is true, rejoice, give thanks, and appreciate the comments immediately.
2. Take corrective action to supplement your deficiencies (e.g., enroll in a course to improve speech).
3. Write to the critic to express appreciation for their findings, which enabled you to correct the matter.
4. If the criticism is not true, recognize that the critic is a psychologically ill person pouring forth resentment because a "psychological boil" has been hurt.
5. Understand this fact to become compassionate.
6. The next logical step is to pray for the other person's peace, harmony, and understanding.

### 3. How to Be Compassionate
1. Realize that when someone criticizes you unjustly, their class material or words have upset their own prejudices, superstitions, or narrow sectarian beliefs.
2. Recognize that a psychologically ill person is simply pouring forth resentment.
3. To understand this fact is to be compassionate.
4. Pray for the other person's peace, harmony, and understanding.

### 4. How to Forgive Yourself
1. Recognize that God is not punishing you; you are punishing yourself.
2. Understand that all suffering is due to the reaction of your subconscious mind to your own negative and destructive thinking.
3. Realize that if you misuse the laws of life, you will suffer accordingly — but the forces of nature are not evil; it is your use of them that determines their effect.
4. Get your thoughts in line with the divine law of harmony.
5. Forgiveness is getting your thoughts in harmony with divine law and order.
6. Self-condemnation is called hell (bondage and restriction); forgiveness is called heaven (harmony and peace).

### 5. How to Respond When Praying for Divine Guidance
1. Pray for divine guidance and right action.
2. Take what comes without bitterness.
3. Realize it is good and very good.
4. Trust the answer in the same way that you trusted your mother when she held you in her arms.
5. This is how you acquire poise and mental and emotional health.

### 6. How to Overcome False Conditioning and Guilt
1. Recognize when you have been brainwashed, mesmerized, or conditioned by ignorant, superstitious, or bigoted influences.
2. Break away completely from the source of the false conditioning.
3. Learn the workings of the conscious and subconscious mind to understand the source of your guilt.
4. Give up all false beliefs and adopt the truths of life and a new estimate of yourself.
5. Claim that Infinite Spirit will attract to you what harmonizes with you thoroughly.

### 7. How to Meet the Acid Test for Forgiveness
1. Ask yourself: If someone tells you something wonderful about the person who wronged you, do you "sizzle" at hearing the good news?
2. If you do, the roots of hatred are still in your subconscious mind.
3. Ask yourself about a past physical pain (e.g., an abscess): "Do I have pain now?" You will say, "Of course not, I have memory of it but no pain."
4. Apply the same standard to emotional wounds: You may have memory of the incident but no sting or hurt anymore.
5. Continue until you can meet the person in your mind and sincerely react with benediction of peace and goodwill without any sting.

## Key Principles and Concepts

### The Nature of Life/God
- Life plays no favorites. God is life, and this life-principle flows through you at every moment.
- God loves to express Himself as harmony, peace, beauty, joy, and abundance through you.
- God has nothing to do with unhappy or chaotic conditions; all such conditions are brought about by man's negative and destructive thinking.
- Your concept of God is the most important thing in your life.

### Life Always Forgives You
- Life forgives you when you cut your finger; the subconscious intelligence within you immediately sets about to repair it.
- If you take tainted food by error, life forgives you and causes you to regurgitate it.
- If you burn your hand, the life-principle reduces the edema and congestion and gives you new skin, tissue, and cells.
- Life holds no grudges against you and is always forgiving you.
- Life brings you back to health, vitality, harmony, and peace if you cooperate by thinking in harmony with nature.
- Negative, hurtful memories, bitterness, and ill will clutter up and impede the free flow of the life-principle in you.

### Self-Punishment vs. Divine Punishment
- God does not condemn or punish anyone; all suffering is due to the reaction of the subconscious mind to one's own negative and destructive thinking.
- The only sin is ignorance of the law, and the only punishment is the automatic reaction of man's misuse of the law.
- If you misuse the principle of chemistry, you may blow up the office; if you strike your hand on a board, your hand may bleed. The board is not for that purpose.
- Electricity is not evil; it depends on how you use it. You can use it to burn down a structure or light up a home.
- Man punishes himself by his false concepts of God, life, and the universe. His thoughts are creative and he creates his own misery.

### The Changing Nature of the Self
- Physically and psychologically, you are not the same person you were years ago; every cell of your body changes approximately every eleven months.
- Mentally and spiritually, you can become a new person. The "old" person who committed past wrongs can be mentally and spiritually dead.
- Therefore, condemning yourself for past actions is condemning an innocent person.

### Criticism and Emotional Sovereignty
- Criticism cannot hurt you without your consent.
- You are master of your thoughts, reactions, and emotions.
- Emotions follow thoughts, and you have the power to reject all thoughts which may disturb or upset you.

### Forgiveness Is Love In Action
- The essential ingredient in forgiveness is the willingness to forgive; if you sincerely desire to forgive, you are 51% over the hurdle.
- To forgive does not necessarily mean you like the person or want to associate with them.
- You cannot be compelled to like someone; neither can a government legislate goodwill, love, peace, or tolerance.
- However, you can love people without liking them.
- Love means wishing for the other health, happiness, peace, joy, and all the blessings of life.
- Forgiveness is not magnanimity; it is self-interest, because what you wish for the other, you are actually wishing for yourself. You are thinking it and feeling it. As you think and feel, so are you.

### To Understand All Is To Forgive All
- When man understands the creative law of his own mind, he ceases to blame other people and conditions for making or marring his life.
- He knows that his own thoughts and feelings create his destiny.
- Externals are not the causes and conditioners of his life and experiences.
- To think that others can mar your happiness, that you are the football of a cruel fate, or that you must oppose and fight others for a living are all untenable when you understand that thoughts are things.

### Forgiveness Is Necessary for Healing
- Forgiveness of others is essential to mental peace and radiant health.
- You must forgive everyone who has ever hurt you if you want perfect health and happiness.
- Forgive yourself by getting your thoughts in harmony with divine law and order.
- You cannot really forgive yourself completely until you have forgiven others first.
- To refuse to forgive yourself is nothing more or less than spiritual pride or ignorance.
- In psychosomatic medicine, resentment, condemnation of others, remorse, and hostility are behind maladies ranging from arthritis to cardiac disease.
- These negative emotions cause inflamed and festering wounds in the subconscious mind.
- The only remedy is to cut out and discard hurts through forgiveness.

## Best Practices Mentioned

1. When someone criticizes you and the criticism is valid, immediately take corrective action (e.g., take a public speaking course) and thank the critic for enabling your improvement.
2. When praying for divine guidance, accept what comes with faith, trusting as you trusted your mother when she held you.
3. When dressing and presenting yourself, do so in a way that aligns with a healthy, positive self-image (e.g., dress up, attend to your hair).
4. Take proactive steps to overcome fears and false conditioning (e.g., take dancing lessons from a man, driving lessons, learn to swim, play cards, go on dates).
5. Pray for a divine companion or desired outcome by claiming that Infinite Spirit will attract to you what harmonizes with you.
6. Meet the acid test of forgiveness regularly to ensure no sting or hurt remains in your subconscious mind.
7. Wish the person well and say "Peace be to you" whenever the thought of the offender enters your mind after forgiveness.

## Common Pitfalls Mentioned

1. **Blaming God for your trouble or sickness** — This is silly because God has nothing to do with unhappy conditions; they are caused by negative thinking.
2. **Anger at God** — Believing God is responsible for your misery causes automatic negative reactions from your subconscious mind.
3. **Negative concepts about God** — As long as people entertain negative concepts about God, they will experience automatic negative reactions from their subconscious minds.
4. **Self-condemnation and guilt** — These punish you and prevent you from going forward into healthy, happy, and creative activity.
5. **Resentment, bitterness, and ill will** — These clutter up and impede the free flow of the life-principle and cause physical disease.
6. **Thinking externals cause your experience** — Believing others can mar your happiness makes you the "football of a cruel fate."
7. **Repeating the full forgiveness prayer unnecessarily** — Once you have forgiven, it is unnecessary to repeat the full prayer; simply wish the person well when they come to mind.
8. **Thinking you must like someone to forgive them** — You can love without liking; forgiveness is about releasing them and wishing them well, not about association.
9. **Deceiving yourself about true forgiveness** — If you sizzle at hearing good news about someone who wronged you, the roots of hatred are still there and you are not practicing true forgiveness.
10. **Refusing to forgive yourself** — This is spiritual pride or ignorance and blocks complete healing.
