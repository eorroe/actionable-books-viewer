# Chapter 27 Analysis: The Healing of the Dream
## A Course in Miracles - Section 1: The Picture of Crucifixion

---

## Lesson 1: The Wish to Be Unfairly Treated Is a Compromise

### Key Principles
- The desire to be treated unfairly is an attempt to combine attack and innocence—two wholly incompatible things.
- Crucifixion cannot be experienced alone. If you feel unfairly treated, your brother must suffer the same perceived unfairness.
- Sacrifice is total; if it could occur, it would entail all of God's creation.
- In your release from sacrifice, your brother's release is made manifest as his own.

### Step-by-Step Instructions
1. Notice when you feel you are being deprived, unfairly treated, or in need.
2. Recognize this as an accusation against your brother—you are projecting guilt onto him.
3. Release the wish to suffer, knowing that in your release, he is set free.
4. Do not hold a picture of crucifixion before your brother's eyes; instead, offer him the witness of your innocence.

### Best Practices
- Walk the gentle way and fear no evil or shadows.
- Refuse to place terror symbols on your path.
- Consciously choose not to see your brother as the cause of your suffering.

### Common Pitfalls
- Believing that suffering can be isolated to yourself.
- Using sickness or deprivation as proof of your brother's guilt.
- Thinking that sacrifice can be partial rather than total.

---

## Lesson 2: The Power of Witness

### Key Principles
- The witness is believed because it points beyond itself to what it represents.
- A sick and suffering body is a witness to your brother's guilt—it proves he has sinned and deserves condemnation.
- The Holy Spirit offers a different picture: a body that has never been used for attack and therefore never suffered pain.
- The witness to innocence shows that nothing your brother's madness bid him do was ever done.

### Step-by-Step Instructions
1. Identify what your current physical or emotional state is witnessing to—guilt or innocence?
2. Accept the Holy Spirit's alternative picture: a body without pain, reproach, or accusation.
3. Show this picture to your brother; he will see his forgiveness there.
4. Look beyond forgiveness to the innocence you behold in your brother.

### Best Practices
- Use your body as a sign of life and redemption rather than a sign of guilt.
- Let your healing be your brother's comfort and health.
- Attest to his innocence, not his guilt.

### Common Pitfalls
- Accepting the sick and sorry picture that serves only to punish your brother.
- Believing that sickness proves guilt is real.
- Using your suffering as evidence that your brother has sinned.

---

## Lesson 3: Understanding the Purpose of the World

### Key Principles
- The world's only purpose is to prove guilt real.
- Every worldly thought, act, or feeling has the motivation of proving guilt and death real.
- Sickness is the strongest witness to futility and bolsters all other witnesses to sin.
- The sick find "reason" for their desires in the belief that life is short and passing joys are all they deserve.

### Step-by-Step Instructions
1. Recognize that the world and all its concerns are witnesses to the belief in guilt.
2. See sickness, depression, vanity, and adornment as voices speaking the same message: guilt is real.
3. Undo this purpose by choosing to see the body as neutral, without inherent goal.
4. Remove the goal of sin from the body so Heaven can be remembered.

### Best Practices
- Look past the body's condition to its purpose.
- See the body as neither sick nor well, bad nor good, but as a blank slate awaiting purpose.
- Refuse to judge the body in any way.

### Common Pitfalls
- Believing that the frail are entitled to passing pleasures as payment for their short lives.
- Thinking sickness gives reason for unnatural desires.
- Accepting the body as a symbol of reproach and guilt.

---

## Lesson 4: Your Function Is to Show That Sin Has No Cause

### Key Principles
- Your function is to demonstrate to your brother that sin can have no cause.
- The body you see yourself in is a picture of the proof that your function can never be fulfilled—unless you change its purpose.
- The Holy Spirit's picture removes all signs of accusation and blamefulness from the body without changing the body itself.
- A body pictured without a purpose is seen as neither sick nor well; it has no life, but neither is it dead.

### Step-by-Step Instructions
1. Recognize that your current self-picture (sick, suffering, guilty) is a futile witness against your function.
2. Allow the Holy Spirit to lay a different picture before you: one without pain or reproach.
3. See the body as Pictured without a purpose—neither sick nor well.
4. Let your function and the body's purpose be reconciled as one.

### Best Practices
- Accept that you do not know the body's true purpose.
- Release the illusions of purpose you gave to a thing made to hide your function.
- Let the body stand apart from all experience of love or fear, its purpose open and undetermined.

### Common Pitfalls
- Thinking you must change the body to change its witness.
- Believing that the body has an inherent purpose separate from your function.
- Trying to use the body to prove your function can never be.

## Lesson 5: The Simple Way to Healing

### Key Principles
- The body can become a sign of life, a promise of redemption, and a breath of immortality.
- The simple way to achieve this is to let the body have no purpose from the past.
- The past purpose of the body was to foster guilt; this crippled picture insists on being a lasting sign of what it represents.
- By removing the goal of sin, space is created for a different view and purpose.

### Step-by-Step Instructions
1. Let the body have no purpose from the past—release the belief that its purpose was to foster guilt.
2. Recognize that your crippled picture is a sign that your function can never be fulfilled—and let it go.
3. Remove the goal of sin from the body so a new purpose can be given.
4. Let the body's purpose and your function be reconciled as one.

### Best Practices
- Be willing to see the body as empty of past purpose.
- Allow Heaven to be remembered in the space where sin's goal has been removed.
- Let healing take the place of death by giving the body the purpose of life.

### Common Pitfalls
- Insisting that your crippled picture is a lasting sign of guilt.
- Leaving no space for a different view or purpose.
- Trying to add a new purpose on top of the old one instead of releasing the old.

---

## Lesson 6: Let the Body Represent Life

### Key Principles
- The body can receive the power to represent endless life, forever unattacked.
- Its message can be changed from "Behold me, brother, at your hand I die" to "Behold me, brother, at your hand I live."
- Healing is the proof that illusions are not true.
- Perfect healing takes the place of death when the body's purpose is aligned with the Holy Spirit's.

### Step-by-Step Instructions
1. Choose to give the body the purpose of healing rather than guilt.
2. Let the body send forth the message it receives: health, loveliness, and the truth of life.
3. Use your healed body as a witness to your brother's innocence.
4. Allow the body to proclaim the truth and value of eternal life.

### Best Practices
- Let the body be a breath of immortality to those sick of breathing death.
- Use your body's health to prove that guilt is an illusion.
- Let your brother see that every scar is healed and every tear is wiped away.

### Common Pitfalls
- Continuing to use the body as a sign of death and guilt.
- Believing that healing is for yourself alone and does not affect your brother.
- Thinking that the body cannot represent the eternal.

---

## Chapter Overview

**Chapter 27: The Healing of the Dream** focuses on how the dream of separation and guilt is healed through a change in perception. The body, which appears to be the primary witness to guilt, sickness, and death, can be repurposed by the Holy Spirit to become a witness to life, innocence, and the eternal truth that we cannot be hurt.

**Core Theme:** Healing occurs not by changing the body, but by changing the purpose we assign to it. When we remove the goal of sin from the body, it becomes a sign of life rather than death, and this healing extends to our brother as well.

**Key Transformation:** From "Behold me, brother, at your hand I die" to "Behold me, brother, at your hand I live."
