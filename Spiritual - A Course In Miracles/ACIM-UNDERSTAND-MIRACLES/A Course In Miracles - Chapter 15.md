# Chapter 15 Analysis: "The Holy Instant" — Section 1: The Two Uses of Time

## Overview

This section introduces the central theme of Chapter 15: the "holy instant" as the only real use of time. It contrasts the ego's use of time—which perpetuates guilt, fear, and belief in hell—with the Holy Spirit's use of time, which leads to Heaven, peace, and salvation. The section teaches that time is ultimately transcended in the holy instant, where the past and future dissolve and only the eternal present remains.

---

## Lesson 1: The Two Uses of Time

### Lesson Title
**The Two Uses of Time: Ego vs. Holy Spirit**

### Step-by-Step Instructions
1. Recognize that time has two possible uses: the ego's use and the Holy Spirit's use.
2. Identify when you are using time to support the ego's belief in destruction, guilt, and hell.
3. Choose instead to offer each instant to the Holy Spirit for His use.
4. Practice taking the present instant and regarding it as all there is of time.
5. Extend this instant to your brothers, sharing the holy instant as a gift of freedom.

### Key Principles
- **Time's Purpose**: Time exists to help you learn perfect calm, peace, and freedom from all care.
- **Two Sources**: Both the ego and the Holy Spirit use time, but toward opposite ends.
- **Ego's Goal**: The ego uses time to convince you of inevitability—its end is death, dissolution, and hell.
- **Holy Spirit's Goal**: The Holy Spirit uses time to teach you life, which has no end.
- **The Ego's Paradox**: The ego fears death yet wants you dead; it engenders fear to maintain allegiance while pretending to offer escape.
- **Hell is Made of the Present**: The belief in hell prevents understanding of the present, because you are afraid of it.
- **Instantaneous Salvation**: Learning the holy instant takes no time; being what you are takes no time.

### Best Practices
- When tempted to feel discouraged by the seeming length of the path, ask: "How long is an instant?"
- Do not delay giving the holy instant to the Holy Spirit; He needs no more than the briefest instant.
- Give the instant to your brothers on behalf of their release, because the instant of holiness is shared.
- Use time as a teaching aid to happiness and peace, not as a tool for compounding guilt.

### Common Pitfalls
- **Believing Salvation Takes Long**: The belief that spiritual change requires extended time is a product of the ego and leads to discouragement.
- **Identifying with the Ego's Timeline**: The ego stretches time into an endless future of punishment and hell.
- **Withholding the Instant from Others**: You cannot keep the holy instant for yourself alone; it must be shared.
- **Confusing the Past and Future**: The ego insists the past and future are the same, both being sources of fear and guilt.
- **Forgetting the Present is Free**: The present moment, when freed from the shadow of the past and future, is clean, untarnished, and free of guilt.

## Lesson 2: The Nature of Hell and Heaven in Time

### Lesson Title
**Hell and Heaven: Where They Are Found**

### Step-by-Step Instructions
1. Understand that hell is not a future place; it is what the ego has made of the present.
2. Release the belief that hell is a future destination awaiting punishment.
3. Realize that Heaven is not in the future, but in the holy instant of the present.
4. Accept that fear belongs only to the past and future, which do not exist.
5. Stand in the present instant, clear and separated from the past, with no shadow reaching into the future.

### Key Principles
- **Hell is Not Future**: The ego teaches hell is in the future, but hell is actually the present when distorted by ego.
- **No Fear in the Present**: When each instant stands clear and separated from the past, fear disappears.
- **The Present as Birth**: Each instant is a clean, untarnished birth where the Son of God emerges from the past into the present.
- **The Present Extends Forever**: The true present is eternal and unchanging.
- **Heaven is Now**: Heaven is found in the holy instant, not in some future time.

### Best Practices
- When you feel fear, ask whether it relates to the past or future—both of which are illusions.
- Practice bringing your awareness fully into the present moment, releasing the past and not projecting into the future.
- Recognize that any sense of hell you experience is a choice to view the present through ego eyes.

### Common Pitfalls
- **Projecting Hell into the Future**: The ego uses time to create a future of endless punishment.
- **Believing Punishment Will End in Peace**: If you believe you deserve hell, you cannot believe punishment ends in peace.
- **Identifying with Guilt**: The belief in guilt inevitably leads to the belief in hell.
- **Confusing Eternity with Timelessness**: Eternity is not endless time; it is the absence of time entirely.

## Lesson 3: The Holy Instant as Salvation

### Lesson Title
**The Holy Instant: Your Escape from Guilt**

### Step-by-Step Instructions
1. Take the present instant and regard it as complete in itself.
2. See yourself as bright with freedom in this instant.
3. Allow the holy instant to undo the fear that makes the present seem useless.
4. Remember that remembering God is remembering freedom.
5. Accept that holiness lies not in time, but in eternity.

### Key Principles
- **The Instant is Enough**: The Holy Spirit needs only the tiniest instant to offer you the whole of Heaven.
- **Purity is Unchangeable**: There never was an instant in which God's Son could lose his purity.
- **Time Stands Still in Holiness**: In the holy instant, time is transformed into eternity.
- **Escape from Guilt**: In the crystal cleanness of the release you give to others, you find your own instantaneous escape from guilt.
- **You Must Be Holy to Offer Holiness**: Your ability to give the miracle depends on your willingness to be holy yourself.

### Best Practices
- Practice taking one instant at a time, offering it wholly to the Holy Spirit.
- Do not measure salvation by time; measure it by the instant in which you choose peace.
- Remember that your creations share your immortality; extend the instant to them as well.
- Understand that your changeless state is beyond time, for your purity remains forever beyond attack.

### Common Pitfalls
- **Measuring the Instant by the Ego's Clock**: The ego measures everything in duration; the holy instant is beyond duration.
- **Thinking Holiness is in Time**: Holiness is not achieved over time; it is recognized in the instant.
- **Withholding from Brothers**: You cannot experience the instant alone; it is inherently shared.
- **Confusing Exchange with Loss**: The ego views giving as loss; the holy instant reveals that giving is receiving.

## Lesson 4: Miracles as Instants of Release

### Lesson Title
**Miracles: The Language of the Holy Instant**

### Step-by-Step Instructions
1. Recognize that miracles are instants of release that you offer and will receive.
2. When tempted to attack a brother, remember that his instant of release is yours.
3. Offer the miracle of the holy instant through the Holy Spirit.
4. Leave the giving of the instant to the Holy Spirit; do not try to control how it comes to you.
5. Join with the Holy Spirit in giving, for in giving you receive.

### Key Principles
- **Miracles are Shared**: Miracles attest to your willingness to be released and to offer time to the Holy Spirit.
- **Attack vs. Release**: When you attack, you deny the holy instant to yourself and your brother.
- **The Law of Giving**: As you give the blessed instant, the Holy Spirit offers it to you; you join with Him in giving.
- **Miracles Transcend Time**: Miracles are not bound by time; they are instantaneous shifts in perception.

### Best Practices
- When conflict arises, pause and offer the instant of release rather than reacting.
- Practice offering miracles without concern for the form they take; let the Holy Spirit handle the outcome.
- See every interaction as an opportunity to give or withhold the holy instant.

### Common Pitfalls
- **Trying to Force the Miracle**: The miracle cannot be forced by will alone; it requires willingness and surrender to the Holy Spirit.
- **Withholding During Attack**: In the heat of conflict, it is easy to forget that your brother's release is your own.
- **Confusing Miracle with Magic**: Miracles are changes in perception, not alterations of the ego's world.

## Lesson 5: Time as Friend When Given to the Holy Spirit

### Lesson Title
**Time Transformed: From Enemy to Friend**

### Step-by-Step Instructions
1. Decide to leave time to the Holy Spirit for His use.
2. Do not use time yourself for ego purposes of guilt, fear, and delay.
3. Trust that the Holy Spirit needs very little time to restore God's whole power to you.
4. Recognize that time stands still in holiness and is transformed into forever.
5. Give the eternal instant, that eternity may be remembered for you.

### Key Principles
- **Time is Neutral**: Time itself is not evil; it becomes friend or foe depending on who uses it.
- **The Holy Spirit's Economy**: The Holy Spirit uses very little time to accomplish what the ego cannot accomplish in eternity.
- **Transformation of Time**: When caught in the single instant of eternal sanctity, time is transformed into forever.
- **No Need for More Time**: The Holy Spirit asks no more than the instant; He has no need of more.

### Best Practices
- Whenever you feel rushed or delayed, remember that the holy instant is always available now.
- Use reminders throughout the day to pause and give the present moment to the Holy Spirit.
- Do not project into the future; bring all your attention to this instant.

### Common Pitfalls
- **Using Time for Guilt**: The ego's primary use of time is to compound guilt until it demands vengeance.
- **Thinking You Need More Time**: The belief that you need years of practice is an ego strategy to delay salvation.
- **Looking at the Past with Value**: The ego values the past only because it is no more; do not give it power over the present.
- **Forgetting the Present is All There Is**: The past and future are illusions; only the present is real.

## Practical Application Notes

This section emphasizes that the path to salvation is not a journey through time but a shift in perception that happens instantly. The practice involves:

1. **Moment-to-Moment Awareness**: Constantly returning the mind to the present instant.
2. **Sharing the Instant**: Never keeping the holy instant to yourself; always extending it to others.
3. **Releasing the Past**: Not allowing past shadows to darken the present.
4. **Trusting the Holy Spirit**: Letting go of the need to understand or control how salvation comes.
5. **Immediate Practice**: Beginning now, not in the future, because the holy instant is always available.

The ultimate teaching is that you do not need time to change your mind; you need only the willingness to give the present instant to the Holy Spirit and to your brothers.
