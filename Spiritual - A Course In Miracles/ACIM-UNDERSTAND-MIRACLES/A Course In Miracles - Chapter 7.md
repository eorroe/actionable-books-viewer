# ACIM Chapter 7 Analysis - The Gifts of the Kingdom

## Chapter Overview
**Source:** A Course in Miracles - Chapter 7: The Gifts of the Kingdom  
**Pages Read:** 821-823  
**Section Analyzed:** 1. The Last Step

---

## Lesson 1: Understanding Creative Power

### Key Principles
- God's creative power and our creative power are not in reciprocal relationship
- We communicate fully with God, sharing and creating like Him
- We did not create God, but God created us
- The parallel: parents give birth to children; children give birth to their own children

### Best Practices
- Recognize that you are co-creator with God, not His creator
- Understand that your creative power extends outward from you to your creations, just as God's creative thought proceeds from Him to you
- Accept your power to add to the Kingdom (but not to add to the Creator of the Kingdom)

### Common Pitfalls
- Thinking creation is limited or that the Kingdom cannot increase through its own creative thought
- Forgetting that you are part of God, just as your sons are part of His sons

---

## Lesson 2: To Create Is To Love

### Key Principles
- Creation and love are inseparable
- Love extends outward because it cannot be contained
- Being limitless, love does not stop
- Love creates forever, but not in time
- Eternity is yours because God created you eternal

### Step-by-Step Instructions
1. Recognize that to create is to love
2. Allow love to extend outward without limitation
3. Understand that your creations belong in you, as you belong in God
4. Create only as God creates - in eternity, not in time

### Best Practices
- Embrace the eternal nature of creation
- Avoid confining love within artificial boundaries
- Remember that your sons are part of God's sons

### Common Pitfalls
- Trying to contain love within limits
- Creating in time rather than in eternity
- Forgetting the eternal nature of your existence

---

## Lesson 3: The Ego's Bargaining vs. God's Giving

### Key Principles
- The ego demands reciprocal rights because it is competitive rather than loving
- The ego is always willing to strike a bargain
- To be like another means no bargains are possible
- To gain you must give, not bargain
- To bargain is to limit giving, which is not God's Will
- To will with God is to create like Him

### Step-by-Step Instructions
1. Recognize when you are bargaining instead of giving
2. Shift from competitive giving to unconditional giving
3. Will with God by creating like Him
4. Give without expecting reciprocity

### Best Practices
- Give freely without keeping score
- Avoid bargaining in relationships
- Align your will with God's will
- Understand that God does not limit His gifts

### Common Pitfalls
- Engaging in reciprocal bargains
- Limiting giving due to expectations of return
- Forgetting that God's gifts are unlimited and yours must be like His

---

## Lesson 4: Joy as the Only Thing That Increases Forever

### Key Principles
- Only joy increases forever
- Joy and eternity are inseparable
- God extends outward beyond limits and beyond time
- Co-creators with God extend His kingdom forever and beyond limit
- Eternity is the indelible stamp of creation
- The eternal are in peace and joy forever

### Step-by-Step Instructions
1. Choose joy as your focus
2. Extend joy outward beyond limits
3. Recognize that joy is eternal and cannot be diminished
4. Share joy as God shares it with you

### Best Practices
- Make joy the foundation of your creations
- Understand that joy cannot be contained or limited
- Extend joy to others without restriction

### Common Pitfalls
- Seeking temporary pleasures instead of eternal joy
- Forgetting that joy and eternity are inseparable
- Limiting the extension of joy

---

## Lesson 5: Thinking and Creating Like God

### Key Principles
- To think like God is to share His certainty of what you are
- To create like Him is to share the perfect love He shares with you
- The Holy Spirit leads you to this so your joy may be complete
- The Kingdom of God is whole

### Step-by-Step Instructions
1. Follow the Holy Spirit's guidance
2. Share God's certainty of who you are
3. Create by sharing perfect love
4. Allow your joy to be complete through the wholeness of the Kingdom

### Best Practices
- Accept the Holy Spirit's leadership
- Think with God's certainty
- Create with perfect love
- Recognize the completeness of the Kingdom

### Common Pitfalls
- Doubting your true nature
- Creating without love
- Separating yourself from the Kingdom's wholeness

---

## Lesson 6: The Nature of the Last Step

### Key Principles
- The last step in the reawakening of knowledge is taken by God
- God does not take steps because His accomplishments are not gradual
- God does not teach because His creations are changeless
- God does nothing last because He created first and for always
- "First" as applied to God is not a time concept
- God is the Prime Creator because He created His co-creators
- Time applies neither to God nor to what He created
- The "last step" was true in the beginning, is true now, and will be true forever

### Best Practices
- Trust that the Holy Spirit can translate the useless into the useful
- Trust that the Holy Spirit can translate the meaningless into the meaningful
- Trust that the Holy Spirit can translate the temporary into the timeless
- Accept that what is timeless is always there
- Recognize that God's light was never obscured

### Common Pitfalls
- Trying to explain in words what does not need explanation
- Applying time concepts to God or His creations
- Forgetting that God's light was never hidden or obscured
- Not understanding that what is fully shared cannot be withheld and then revealed
