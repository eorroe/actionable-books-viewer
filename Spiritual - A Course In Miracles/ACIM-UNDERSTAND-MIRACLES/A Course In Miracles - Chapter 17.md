# Chapter 17 Analysis: Forgiveness and the Holy Relationship
## Section 1 - Bringing Fantasy to Truth

---

## Overview

This section reveals that all perceived betrayal and sin are illusions of the dreamer. True forgiveness is not about pardoning real offenses but awakening from the dream of separation. The section teaches that fantasies have no power over reality, and that the belief in an "order of difficulty in miracles" arises from withholding aspects of reality from truth. Healing comes through giving all illusions to truth, not bringing truth to illusions.

---

## Lesson 1: Sin and Betrayal Are Illusions, Not Reality

**Key Principles:**
- The betrayal of the Son of God lies only in illusions
- All "sins" are but his own imagining
- His reality is forever sinless
- He need not be forgiven but awakened

**Step-by-Step Instructions:**
1. Recognize that any sense of betrayal or sin you perceive is an illusion
2. Understand that the "sinner" is dreaming, not acting in reality
3. Release the need to judge or condemn the apparent offense
4. See the other as the sinless Son of God beneath the dream

**Best Practices:**
- When you feel betrayed, remind yourself: "This is a dream. Reality is untouched."
- Do not take dreams seriously, for they have no effect on reality
- Look past the illusion to the underlying sinless reality

**Common Pitfalls:**
- Trying to convince the dreamer the dream isn't real (this is impossible while they are asleep)
- Believing the dream has actually changed reality
- Forgetting that what is done in dreams has not really been done

---

## Lesson 2: Fantasies Cannot Change Reality

**Key Principles:**
- Only in waking is the full release from dreams
- Dreams had no effect upon reality at all and did not change it
- Fantasies change reality only in the mind that would have reality be different
- They cannot change reality in actuality, only in perception

**Step-by-Step Instructions:**
1. Notice when your mind is trying to make fantasies real
2. Recognize that your wish to change reality is what creates the fear
3. Understand that you are acknowledging your power when you use it to distort reality
4. Realize that devoting your creative power to "evil" makes it unreal

**Best Practices:**
- Accept that reality remains unchanged by any illusion
- Do not fear your own creative power - only its misuse in fantasy
- Remember that you cannot be faithful to two masters (truth and fantasy)

**Common Pitfalls:**
- Believing your fantasies have actually altered what is real
- Fearing your own power because you have used it for illusion
- Trying to serve both truth and fantasy simultaneously

---

## Lesson 3: The Order of Difficulty in Miracles Is an Illusion

**Key Principles:**
- Maintaining that there is an order of difficulty in miracles means you withhold some things from truth
- You believe truth cannot deal with certain things only because you keep them from truth
- Your lack of faith in the power that heals all pain comes from wishing to retain aspects of reality for fantasy
- What you reserve for yourself, you take away from the Holy Spirit Who would release you

**Step-by-Step Instructions:**
1. Notice when you believe some problems are too difficult to heal
2. Recognize this as a desire to keep some illusions for yourself
3. Release the special aspect you are withholding
4. Give everything back to the Holy Spirit without reservation

**Best Practices:**
- Accept that all healing is equally possible
- Give all aspects of your experience to truth without exception
- Trust that the Holy Spirit can handle everything you bring to Him

**Common Pitfalls:**
- Believing some illusions are too powerful for truth to dissolve
- Withholding certain thoughts or situations from the healing process
- Establishing an "order of reality" that fragments truth

---

## Lesson 4: Do Not Bring Truth to Fantasy

**Key Principles:**
- Truth has no meaning in illusion
- The frame of reference for truth's meaning must be itself
- Bringing truth to fantasy tries to make illusions real and keep them
- Giving illusions to truth enables truth to teach that illusions are unreal

**Step-by-Step Instructions:**
1. When you encounter an illusion, do not try to apply truth to it directly
2. Instead, give the illusion to the Holy Spirit
3. Let truth be the teacher of what is unreal
4. Reserve not one idea aside from truth

**Best Practices:**
- Let truth be the standard, not fantasy
- Do not try to make illusions real by justifying them with truth
- Surrender all illusions to the Holy Spirit without exception

**Common Pitfalls:**
- Trying to justify or make sense of illusions using truth
- Attempting to use truth to validate fantasy
- Keeping some ideas separate from truth, establishing orders of reality

---

## Lesson 5: Give All to Truth Through the Holy Spirit

**Key Principles:**
- Be willing to give all you have held outside the truth to Him Who knows the truth
- He will accomplish the release; not you
- Be concerned only with your willingness to have this accomplished
- What you give to truth is safe from fantasy

**Step-by-Step Instructions:**
1. Develop willingness to surrender all illusions
2. Do not try to accomplish the release yourself
3. Simply offer your willingness to the Holy Spirit
4. Trust that He will bring all to truth

**Best Practices:**
- Focus on willingness, not results
- Let the Holy Spirit do the work; your role is only to cooperate
- Do not worry about the how or when - just be willing

**Common Pitfalls:**
- Trying to accomplish the release through your own efforts
- Focusing on the content of what you are releasing rather than your willingness
- Forgetting that the Holy Spirit, not you, does the actual work

---

## Lesson 6: Forgiveness Restores Truth When Others Use Fantasy

**Key Principles:**
- When disturbed because another solves problems through fantasy, you refuse to forgive yourself for the same attempt
- You hold both yourself and the other away from truth and salvation
- As you forgive him, you restore to truth what was denied by both of you
- You will see forgiveness where you have given it

**Step-by-Step Instructions:**
1. Notice when you become disturbed by another's behavior
2. Recognize that you are reacting to their fantasy with your own fantasy
3. Forgive yourself for the same attempt to solve problems through fantasy
4. Extend forgiveness to the other person
5. Watch as truth is restored for both of you

**Best Practices:**
- Use others' behavior as a mirror for your own mind
- Never judge another's fantasy without recognizing your own
- See the other's behavior as a call for forgiveness, not condemnation

**Common Pitfalls:**
- Getting angry at others for doing exactly what you do
- Forgetting that their fantasy reflects your own
- Holding onto resentment instead of extending forgiveness

---

## Summary of Key Principles

| Principle | Application |
|-----------|-------------|
| Sin is illusion | See past offenses to the sinless reality beneath |
| Fantasies don't change reality | Do not fear that illusions have altered what is true |
| No order of difficulty | All healing is equally possible through truth |
| Don't bring truth to fantasy | Give illusions to truth, don't justify them |
| Holy Spirit does the work | Focus on willingness, not personal accomplishment |
| Forgiveness restores truth | Forgive others to restore what was denied |

---

## Closing Teaching

> "Be willing, then, to give all you have held outside the truth to him Who knows the truth, and in Whom all is brought to truth. Be not concerned with anything except your willingness to have this be accomplished. He will accomplish it; not you."

The entire path of forgiveness rests on willingness. Your role is not to accomplish the release from fantasy, but to be willing to let it happen. When you are disturbed by another's fantasy, use that as a mirror to forgive the same attempt in yourself. In forgiving both yourself and the other, truth is restored and salvation is seen.
