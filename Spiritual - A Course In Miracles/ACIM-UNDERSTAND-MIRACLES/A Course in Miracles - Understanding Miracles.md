# A Course in Miracles - Understanding Miracles

## Overview

A Course in Miracles is a spiritual curriculum that teaches the path from fear to love through forgiveness, miracle-mindedness, and the undoing of the ego's thought system. It presents a non-dualistic framework in which the world is an illusion projected by a separated mind, and true healing comes through the recognition that God's Son is innocent, whole, and eternally one with his Creator. The Course offers practical lessons, daily practices, and a theological foundation for moving from guilt, attack, and specialness to peace, love, and the holy instant.

## When to Follow Book Teachings

- When experiencing fear, anger, guilt, or perceived conflict with others
- When seeking to deepen spiritual practice beyond intellectual understanding into lived experience
- When the user asks about forgiveness, healing, relationships, or the nature of reality
