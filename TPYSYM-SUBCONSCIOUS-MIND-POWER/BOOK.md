# The Power of Your Subconscious Mind

## Overview

This book teaches that your subconscious mind is an internal creative power within you that responds to your consistent thoughts, beliefs, and mental images. By understanding and applying specific mental techniques—such as autosuggestion, visualization, prayer, and affirmation—you can consciously direct this inner power to produce healing, wealth, harmonious relationships, success, and lasting happiness. Every chapter presents actionable steps grounded in the consistent pattern where your subconscious mind shapes your experiences based on your habitual thoughts.

## When to Follow Book Teachings

- When you need to overcome fear, anxiety, or negative thoughts that limit your progress
- When seeking physical or emotional healing through the power of your own mind
- When building an abundance mindset or improving your financial circumstances
- When pursuing professional advancement, creative achievement, or meaningful life direction
- When desiring to attract a compatible partner or improve your current relationship
- When seeking inner peace, happiness, and freedom from resentment or guilt
- When facing difficult decisions and needing guidance from your inner wisdom
- When wanting to break harmful habits or limiting beliefs
- When you wish to stay youthful in spirit regardless of chronological age
- When striving for harmonious relationships with family, colleagues, and all people

## Lessons From Book

### Lesson 1: Understand How Your Conscious and Subconscious Mind Work Together

#### Examples

##### Example 1: The Ship and Captain Analogy
Your conscious mind is like the captain at the bridge of a ship, directing and issuing orders. Your subconscious mind is like the crew in the engine room, automatically carrying out orders. If the captain gives the right orders, the ship moves smoothly; if the orders are wrong, the ship veers off course.

##### Example 2: The Garden Analogy
Your mind is a garden and you are the gardener. Every thought you think is a seed planted in the soil of your subconscious. As you sow in your subconscious mind, so shall you reap in your body and environment. Thoughts of peace, happiness, right action, good will, and prosperity produce positive results.

#### Step 1: Recognize the Two Levels of Mind
Understand that you have one mind with two distinctive functions: the conscious (rational) mind and your conscious mind. The conscious mind is the watchman at the gate, protecting the subconscious from false impressions. The subconscious is the creative center of emotions, memory, and automatic bodily functions.

#### Step 2: Accept the Law of Subconscious Response
Recognize that any thought your conscious mind holds to be true, your subconscious mind will accept and manifest in your life. This pattern responds to positive and negative thoughts. Your subconscious accepts and responds to the thoughts you consistently hold.

#### Step 3: Guard the Gateway to Your Subconscious
Watch your thoughts carefully. Your job is with your conscious mind — ensure it only receives constructive, harmonious, and peaceful thoughts. Avoid saying "I will fail; I will lose my job; I can't pay the rent." Do not let others do your thinking for you.

#### Step 4: Establish a Major Premise for Life Direction
Formulate a clear written description of the way you want your life to be. State a major premise that reflects this desired state (example: "Infinite Intelligence leads and guides me in all my ways. I am healthy, and harmony operates in my mind and body. Love, peace, and abundance flow into my life."). Repeat slowly, quietly, and lovingly three times per session, knowing it is sinking deep down into your subconscious mind.

#### Best Practices

- ✅ Be open-minded and receptive to new thoughts and ideas
- ✅ Think from the standpoint of eternal truths and principles of life, not from fear, ignorance, and superstition
- ✅ Believe good is happening and is happening now
- ✅ Cultivate positive qualities: friendliness, cooperation, joyousness, and lovability
- ✅ Think independently and make your own decisions
- ❌ Do not accept false beliefs, opinions, superstitions, and fears of mankind
- ❌ Do not let others shape your beliefs and decisions
- ❌ Do not say "I can't" — overcome that fear by substituting: "I can do all things through the power of my subconscious mind"

#### Keep In Mind

- Your subconscious mind responds to suggestion — A suggestion is most effective when you accept and believe it through your own thoughts.
- People may react with acceptance, resistance, or indifference to the same suggestion based on their subconscious conditioning or existing beliefs
- The subconscious mind regulates many vital processes of your body — heartbeat, breathing, digestion, circulation — without your conscious control
- Repeated thoughts from your conscious mind create strong patterns in your subconscious mind, where habits are formed

#### Security & Safety Notes

- Negative suggestions from others have no power over you unless you give them power through your own acceptance.
- Do not allow others' suggestions to control your thinking, feeling, and acting in ways that serve their advantage rather than your own
- Suggestions accepted before age 18 can be released through constructive autosuggestion and reconditioning

#### Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Being full of fears and doubts, saying "I might fail" or "people will laugh at me"
  **Solution:** Decide now to become a person with strong, positive mental patterns — full of confidence and faith — and cease entertaining fear-based thoughts
- **Problem:** Finishing negative statements like "I can't afford that"
  **Solution:** Immediately reframe a negative statement into a positive one. Say instead: "That is mine. It is for sale. I accept it mentally."
- **Problem:** Negative thoughts in your conscious mind can trigger corresponding emotional responses from your subconscious
  **Solution:** Speak authoritatively to your deeper self using the Caruso Technique: "Be still, be quiet, I am in control, you must obey me."
- **Problem:** Trying to change external conditions by working only with conditions and circumstances
  **Solution:** Change the way you use your conscious mind — change your thinking and picturing. Remove the cause in your thinking and the effect disappears.

### Lesson 2: Apply Autosuggestion to Overcome Fear and Negative Conditioning

#### Examples

##### Example 1: The Singer Who Overcame Stage Fright
A young singer used autosuggestion three times daily plus before sleep, repeating "I sing beautifully, I am poised, serene, confident and calm," and transformed from fearful to confident within approximately seven days.

##### Example 2: The 75-Year-Old Woman Who Restored Her Memory
Used induced autosuggestion with specific memory-affirming statements three to five times daily and recovered normal memory within twenty-one days.

##### Example 3: The Man Killed by a Crystal Ball Gazer's Prediction
A healthy man accepted a fortune teller's prediction of death at the next new moon, dwelled on it persistently, and died at the predicted time. His own thought, not the fortune teller's power, caused his death.

#### Step 1: Isolate and Relax for Autosuggestion
Find a quiet, private space where you will not be disturbed. Sit down comfortably in an armchair. Relax your body and close your eyes. Still your mind and body as best you can. Physical relaxation favors mental passivity and renders the mind more receptive to suggestion.

#### Step 2: State Your Counteracting Suggestion Positively
State your desired positive suggestion with feeling. For fear of public speaking: "I speak with poise and confidence. I am relaxed and at ease." For memory: "My memory from today on is improving in every department. I shall always remember whatever I need to know." Repeat slowly, quietly, and with feeling five to seven times during each session.

#### Step 3: Conduct Three Sittings Every Day and One Before Sleep
Conduct three sessions every day at regular intervals (e.g., morning, afternoon, and evening). Add one session immediately prior to sleep. Continue until the desired mental state is established.

#### Step 4: Reject Negative Heterosuggestion Consciously
Notice negative suggestions from others each day. Recognize you do not have to be influenced by destructive suggestions from others. Reject negative suggestions consciously and replace them immediately with constructive autosuggestions for your subconscious mind.

#### Step 5: Recondition Your Mind with New Patterns
Recognize negative messages accepted before age 18. Use constructive autosuggestion to release yourself from repeated negative messages from others. Recondition your mind with new, harmonious, constructive thoughts repeated consistently.

#### Best Practices

- ✅ Use simple, repetitive statements with belief until results manifest
- ✅ Combine repetition with faith and expectancy when conveying ideas to your subconscious
- ✅ Practice constructive autosuggestion regularly to counteract past negative conditioning
- ✅ Notice negative suggestions from others and consciously reject them
- ✅ Be open-minded and receptive — this renders the mind more receptive to suggestion
- ❌ Do not let others do your thinking for you
- ❌ Do not accept suggestions that serve others' advantage rather than your own
- ❌ Do not say "I can't" — substitute with positive declarations

#### Keep In Mind

- Your subconscious mind accepts and responds to the thoughts you consistently hold, while also accessing solutions and resources beyond your conscious awareness.
- Any thought you repeatedly focus on grows stronger in your experience
- The sailor vs. timid passenger example shows the same suggestion produces opposite results based on existing subconscious conditioning
- Unconscious fear thoughts that you invest with strong emotion can manifest as actual failure experiences

#### Security & Safety Notes

- Do not accept negative beliefs, opinions contradicted by evidence, or fears that create negative patterns in your subconscious
- If you have been influenced by repeated negative messages from sources that have not earned your trust, break away from the source of the negative conditioning and revise beliefs that were not serving you.
- Not understanding how your mind works leaves you vulnerable to negative suggestions from others

#### Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Negative autosuggestions from childhood accepted unconsciously
  **Solution:** Use constructive autosuggestion consistently to counteract and recondition past negative conditioning
- **Problem:** Accepting negative suggestions as facts — your subconscious will bring about results accordingly
  **Solution:** Guard your thoughts at the gateway of your conscious mind; accept only thoughts that bless, heal, and inspire
- **Problem:** Allowing others' suggestions to control your thinking
  **Solution:** You have the power to choose how you react to the suggestions and statements of others
- **Problem:** Fear and expectation of negative outcomes manifesting as reality
  **Solution:** Choose life! Choose love! Choose health! Choose happiness! Choose abundance!

### Lesson 3: Use Repetition, Faith, and Expectancy to Plant Ideas in Your Subconscious

#### Examples

##### Example 1: The 75-Year-Old Widow
A 75-year-old widow repeated "I am wanted. I am happily married to a kind, loving and spiritual minded man. I am secure!" three to five times daily for two weeks, resulting in meeting her future husband.

##### Example 2: The Young Girl with a Cough
A young girl with a cough and sore throat declared firmly, "It is passing away now. It is passing away now." It cleared up within an hour.

##### Example 3: Mr. Broke
Out of work, with bills piling up and three children, he repeated "Thank you, Father, for my wealth" for approximately three weeks. He met a former employer who offered him a lucrative position and advanced him $500. According to the account, today he is vice-president of the company.

#### Step 1: Formulate a Clear, Specific Desired Idea
Know specifically what you want to impress upon your subconscious. Be clear and specific about the desired result. The idea of perfect health, wealth, success, or harmonious relationships must be clearly defined in your mind before planting.

#### Step 2: Enter a Relaxed, Receptive State
Relax your body and mind completely. Enter a sleepy, drowsy, or passive state — this reduces mental effort to a minimum and allows the subconscious to receive impressions most effectively. Subconscious receptivity is highest just before sleep and just after waking.

#### Step 3: Repeat Your Desired Idea with Feeling
Repeat your desired idea three times per session with feeling. Combine repetition with faith and expectancy. Convey the idea lovingly and feelingly to your subconscious mind. Repeat slowly, quietly, and with feeling — soft and steady repetition. Each repetition increases the emotional value of the suggestion.

#### Step 4: Release the Idea to Your Deeper Mind
Release the whole thing to your deeper mind, which has the innate problem-solving capacity of your deeper mind. Do not strive, coerce, force, or wrestle mentally — simply decree and release. The power that moves the world is already backing your word.

#### Step 5: Continue Until Inner Conviction Is Reached
Continue the practice until you feel a sustained sense of peace and conviction that the work is done. Do not stop at mere words. When you feel the inner sense of completion, the subconscious has accepted the idea.

#### Best Practices

- ✅ Use simple, straightforward statements — do not over-analyze the process
- ✅ Repeat affirmations with slow, quiet, feeling repetition
- ✅ Use brief, memorable phrases that are easy to remember
- ✅ Thank as if you already have it — Feel genuinely thankful as if the outcome has already occurred
- ✅ Quiet your mind before presenting images to your subconscious
- ❌ Do not repeat affirmations without understanding their meaning
- ❌ Do not strive, coerce, or force — effort blocks the flow
- ❌ Do not keep digging up the planted seed with doubt or fear

#### Keep In Mind

- Your daily repeated thoughts and words build your mental reality over time
- The power of your words is determined by the feeling and belief behind them
- The subconscious never sleeps and never rests — it is always on the job
- The subconscious mind will bring to pass any clear mental picture held daily for several weeks and backed by belief

#### Security & Safety Notes

- Avoid revealing the symbolic nature of the object to a patient after using it, as this could create doubt and cause a setback
- Avoid discussing healings with skeptical or unbelieving people — their criticisms can create thoughts of fear, doubt, and anxiety in your subconscious mind
- Do not name ailments when affirming health — naming your trouble by name inhibits your subconscious mind's natural healing function

#### Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Vague or unfocused prayer that does not form a clear image in the mind
  **Solution:** Prayer must have direction and specificity — formulate a clear mental picture or idea of what you desire
- **Problem:** Mental striving, coercion, and force
  **Solution:** Effort must be reduced to a minimum. The effortless way is always better
- **Problem:** Leaving prayer vague and unfocused
  **Solution:** Form a clear-cut idea in your conscious mind and pass it on to your subconscious to the point of conviction
- **Problem:** Doubting or wavering in faith
  **Solution:** Doubt neutralizes the impression being made on the subconscious. Acceptance must be complete and without doubt

### Lesson 4: Use the Passing-Over and Visualization Techniques for Specific Outcomes

#### Examples

##### Example 1: Property Sale Visualization
Picture the check in your hands, rejoice in it, give thanks for it, and go to sleep feeling the naturalness of the scene. The buyer and seller are brought together through subconscious mental patterns

##### Example 2: Author Finding a Position
The author imagined himself addressing a large audience in an ideal Midwest location, played the role of the actor, dramatized the scene, and felt satisfied. Within days, a telegram arrived offering him the position.

##### Example 3: Lawsuit Settlement (Baudoin Technique)
A woman fighting a will case used the phrase "It is finished in divine order." She repeated it each night in a sleepy state for ten nights. On the eleventh morning, she awoke with a conviction that it was finished. Her attorney called the same day to report the opposing side wished to settle.

#### Step 1: Formulate a Clear Mental Picture
Quiet the mind — silence internal mental activity. Present your desired outcome as a vivid mental image to your subconscious mind. Imagine the completed result in vivid detail, engaging all your senses. Imagine the end desired and feel its reality.

#### Step 2: Enter the Drowsy, Passivity State
Relax mind and body. Enter a sleepy or drowsy state. Become passive and receptive. Allow your conscious request to pass over into your subconscious mind. Use complete simplicity — do not over-intellectualize the process.

#### Step 3: Dramatize the Desired Scene
Dramatize your desired outcome as a full mental scene — act as though it is already happening. Feel the reality of the scene and satisfy yourself that it is already accomplished. Sustain the scene with emotion until a deep sense of peace and fulfillment arises.

#### Step 4: Repeat the Visualization or Phrase
Continue for five to ten minutes, growing in awareness until the image feels real. For the Baudoin Technique, condense your need into a brief, memorable phrase (six words or fewer) and repeat it over and over again slowly, quietly, and with feeling.

#### Step 5: Release and Proceed with Confidence
Release the picture and proceed with confidence that it has been received. This framework teaches that holding a mental picture with belief can influence your experience. and backed by faith. Trust that your subconscious mind will work toward the desired result

#### Best Practices

- ✅ Get into a sleepy, drowsy state before visualizing — this is when the subconscious is most receptive
- ✅ Make mental pictures real and vivid, as if subjectively present
- ✅ Feel the reality of the desired outcome — engage emotion and imagination together
- ✅ Use the Baudoin Technique for complex problems: condense your need to a brief phrase and repeat nightly
- ✅ Repeat until you feel an inner sense of peace and conviction that it is done
- ❌ Do not focus on symptoms or negative appearances — focus entirely on the healing truth or desired outcome
- ❌ Feel the result as happening now, not in the future
- ❌ Do not focus on how the result will happen — focus on the end result and let your subconscious mind determine the path

#### Keep In Mind

- The subconscious mind can access solutions and resources beyond your conscious awareness
- A useful indicator of alignment is a positive feeling about the outcome.
- Within this framework, distance is not viewed as a barrier to mental focus or prayer.
- Mental patterns become experiences — over time, these mental patterns can influence your experience

#### Security & Safety Notes

- When using the Baudoin Technique for legal or serious matters, continue nightly for up to 30 nights or until you feel an inner sense of peace
- Do not analyze or reveal the symbolic nature of visualization techniques afterward, as this could create doubt

#### Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Focusing on symptoms and negative appearances
  **Solution:** Minimize focus on symptoms and focus entirely on the healing truth or desired outcome
- **Problem:** Doubting or wavering in faith after planting a seed
  **Solution:** Once you have planted a desired outcome, do not keep questioning it with doubt, fear, or analytical thinking
- **Problem:** Mental striving and coercion
  **Solution:** Simply decree and release. Avoid coercion, force, or mental wrestling — power is already behind your word
- **Problem:** Confusing the method with the cause of results
  **Solution:** Faith acting upon the subconscious mind can produce the result, not the external method or theory used

### Lesson 5: Apply the "Believe You Have Received" Technique for Healing and Manifestation

#### Examples

##### Example 1: The Minister from Johannesburg
Used the relaxation and affirmation method. According to an account, a minister affirmed: "This healing intelligence within me is now transforming every atom of my being, making me whole and perfect now."

##### Example 2: Madame Bire of France
Regained her sight at Lourdes even though her optic nerves were atrophied and useless. A month later, the seeing mechanism was restored to normal. She was healed by her own subconscious mind responding to her belief.

##### Example 3: Dr. James Esdaille
Performed 400 major operations under mental anesthesia only with a postoperative mortality rate of only 2-3% by suggesting to patients' subconscious minds that no infection would develop.

#### Step 1: Believe and Accept as True That the Desire Is Already Accomplished
Adopt the mindset and feeling as if your desire is already fulfilled. Speak in the past tense and feel it as already done. This is the meaning of "believe you have received, and you shall receive."

#### Step 2: Eliminate All Consideration of Conditions and Circumstances
Minimize fixation on adverse conditions and circumstances. Shut the door to outside distractions and appearances. The small, rational, analytical conscious mind must be shut out when impressing the subconscious.

#### Step 3: Plant the Concept and Leave It Undisturbed
Hold the concept in your mind without repeatedly questioning or analyzing it. Do not keep digging it up with doubt, fear, or analytical thinking. Trust that it will develop into external fruition in its own time.

#### Step 4: Quietly Turn Over the Request to Your Subconscious Mind
Still the mind and relax. Think of your body's natural healing intelligence within you. Quietly release your request to your subconscious mind. Realize that the intelligence of your mind will answer according to your specific needs.

#### Step 5: Refuse to Give Power to Negative Conditions
Avoid giving power to negative conditions. Do not admit for even a second that the healing will not come. This attitude brings about the harmonious union of the conscious and subconscious mind, which releases the healing power.

#### Best Practices

- ✅ Pray with a sense of gratitude and certainty as if you have already received — this impresses the subconscious mind most powerfully
- ✅ Use simple, repetitive prayer — pray aloud for five minutes, two or three times a day
- ✅ Speak to your subconscious mind with authority and conviction — it will conform to your command
- ✅ Give thanks in advance for the healing — thank your subconscious for the healing already taking place
- ✅ Imagine the happy ending or solution to your problem, feel the thrill of accomplishment
- ❌ Do not analyze, question, or consider adverse conditions during the healing process
- ❌ Do not discuss healings with skeptics — their criticisms can undo the benefits
- ❌ Do not name your trouble by name when affirming health

#### Keep In Mind

- Healing flows through your subconscious mind.
- While various approaches exist, the core process described here is confident belief or inner conviction
- Some health conditions are influenced by mental patterns — according to this framework, disease originates in the mind, and therefore healing must also be mental
- Your subconscious mind guides the body's natural processes of converting food into tissue, muscle, bone, and blood.
- Normal conditions can be restored with greater ease than abnormal conditions can be induced — The body's innate healing ability seeks to preserve and protect you

#### Security & Safety Notes

- No practitioner directly heals a patient — they help remove mental blocks that inhibit the body's natural healing ability
- The body's natural healing power does the healing — do not attribute healing to any external object, amulet, relic, or person
- The external object used in rituals has no power in itself.
- Always seek advice from a trained counselor for marital or serious personal problems, not from neighbors or relatives

- **Problem:** Discussing healings with skeptical or unbelieving people
  **Solution:** Consider keeping your healing experiences private, especially around skeptical people. — because their criticisms can create doubt and cause a setback
- **Problem:** Analytical, rational thinking during the healing process
  **Solution:** While faith is emphasized in this framework, it is also important to use reason in practical matters.
- **Problem:** Confusing the method with the cause of healing
  **Solution:** It is belief acting upon the subconscious mind that produces the result, not the external method or theory
- **Problem:** Doubting or disturbing the planted seed
  **Solution:** Frequent questioning or worry may interfere with the process described in this framework.
- **Problem:** Naming ailments when affirming health
  **Solution:** If you are constantly naming your aches and symptoms, you inhibit your subconscious mind's natural healing function — Avoid mentioning the trouble by name.

### Lesson 6: Use Specific Techniques to Build Wealth Consciousness

#### Examples

##### Example 1: The "Wealth - Success" Repetition
Repeating the phrase "Wealth and Success" three times a day for five minutes per session builds an abundance mindset by focusing your mind on your inner creative power.

##### Example 2: Pre-Sleep Prosperity Affirmation
Repeat the statement "By day and by night I am being prospered in all of my interests" before sleep each night.

##### Example 3: Sleep Lullaby Technique
Repeating the single word "Wealth" quietly, easily, and feelingly as you fall asleep programs the subconscious mind for wealth during sleep. This practice is intended to build an abundance mindset over time.

#### Step 1: Repeat "Wealth - Success" Regularly
Repeat the phrase "Wealth and Success" to yourself three times a day for five minutes each session. Focus your mind on your inner creative power. The feeling of abundance will grow within you as you dwell on the idea of wealth.

#### Step 2: Use Conflict-Free Affirmations
Repeat this statement before sleep each night. "By day and by night I am being prospered in all of my interests." This affirmation will not arouse argument because it does not contradict your subconscious mind's impression of financial lack.

#### Step 3: Lull Yourself to Sleep With Wealth
As you go to sleep at night, repeat the word "Wealth" quietly, easily, and with feeling — repeated slowly and steadily for five minutes. Let this be your last thought as you drift off.

#### Step 4: Deposit Prosperity Thoughts Each Morning
Every morning as you awaken, deposit thoughts of prosperity, success, wealth, and peace in your subconscious mind. Dwell upon these concepts. Busy your mind with them throughout the day. These constructive thoughts will bring forth abundance and prosperity.

#### Step 5: Stop Negative Financial Thoughts Immediately
When you are about to say something negative such as "I shall not be able to make that payment," stop the sentence before you get further than "I shall..." Immediately dwell on a constructive statement such as: "By day and by night I am prospered in all my ways."

#### Step 6: Overcome Envy and Rejoice in Others' Prosperity
If you feel envy when seeing a competitor with more money, say to yourself: "Isn't it wonderful! I rejoice in that man's prosperity. I wish for him greater and greater wealth." This neutralizes the negative thoughts in your mind and creates a more positive mindset about wealth.

#### Best Practices

- ✅ Practice effortless living — do the thing you love to do and do it for the joy and thrill of it
- ✅ Maintain a surplus of money rather than living paycheck to paycheck
- ✅ Spend money joyfully, expecting it to circulate back to you
- ✅ Rejoice in the success and wealth of others rather than resenting it
- ✅ Maintain positive thoughts about everyone — eliminate mental blocks by thinking well of others
- ✅ Let seeds grow — when you place a seed (thought) in the ground, do not dig it up
- ❌ Do not believe wealth requires hard labor — striving and slaving hard is not necessary
- ❌ Do not say "I am wealthy" without belief — statements rejected by the conscious mind produce no results
- ❌ Do not write blank checks with negative statements like "There is not enough to go around"

#### Keep In Mind

- Wealth begins as a deep belief within your subconscious mind — you grow into a wealth consciousness by building into your mentality the idea of wealth
- Your subconscious mind responds to whatever you consistently impress upon it, whether the idea of wealth or poverty
- The feeling of wealth produces wealth — keep this in mind at all times
- Envy and condemnation block wealth — criticizing and condemning those who have more wealth than you causes wealth to leave you
- Money is composed of atoms and molecules just like everything else — it has no inherent evil
- What men call death is but a journey to a new city in another dimension of life

#### Security & Safety Notes

- Do not make money a god or worship it exclusively — love of money to the exclusion of everything else causes an unbalanced life
- Do not treat money, possessions, or status as the true source of wealth — bonds, stocks, and money in the bank are only symbols, not the true source of wealth
- Wealth obtained through fraud, dishonesty, or harming others leaves you and manifests as sickness, guilt, and inner disorders

#### Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Saying "I am wealthy" without genuine belief
  **Solution:** Do not make statements your conscious mind rejects. Instead, focus on your inner creative power and a genuine sense of abundance will develop.
- **Problem:** Mental conflict in affirmations
  **Solution:** Use affirmations that align with or gradually shift your current beliefs about financial lack
- **Problem:** Envy and condemnation of the prosperous
  **Solution:** Rejoice in others' prosperity. Envious thoughts place you in a negative position, causing wealth to flow from you instead of to you
- **Problem:** Making money your sole aim
  **Solution:** Claim wealth, happiness, peace, true expression, and love — live a balanced life
- **Problem:** Condemning money
  **Solution:** You lose what you condemn and cannot attract what you criticize. Stop using terms like "filthy lucre" or "I despise money"

- True success means living with ongoing peace, joy, and happiness
- There is no true success without peace of mind

- Whatever you consistently impress upon your subconscious mind shapes your experience
- A clear intention for success includes all the essential elements needed for success

- Hurting others hurts yourself — all problems come from misunderstanding, ignorance, and misuse of the subconscious mind

### Lesson 8: Use Prayer and Mental Techniques to Improve and Heal Relationships

#### Examples

##### Example 1: The Salesman and His Manager
A salesman used prayer and mental imagery before sleep to change his perception of his difficult boss. He imagined his boss congratulating him on fine work, felt the reality of the handshake and smile. Through consistent mental focus, the impression was made on the subconscious mind and expressed automatically in reality.

- If using a symbolic object, Avoid revealing the symbolic nature of the object afterward., as this could create doubt and cause a setback

#### Common Pitfalls

  **Solution:** Keep your healings private to avoid creating doubt in your own subconscious through others' criticisms

  **Solution:** While faith is emphasized in this framework, it is also important to use reason in practical matters

- **Problem:** Doubting or disturbing the planted seed

- **Problem:** Unfocused or vague prayer
  **Solution:** Prayer needs a specific, vividly imagined outcome

### Lesson 14: Use the Subconscious Mind for Harmonious Human Relations

#### Examples

##### Example 1: The Salesman and His Difficult Boss
Changed his perception of his sales manager through prayer and mental imagery. Repeated: "You are responsible for your own thoughts." Imagined his boss congratulating him on fine work. Through consistent mental focus, the impression was expressed automatically in reality.

##### Example 2: The Actress Transforming Audience Hostility
Prior to each performance, focused on love and goodwill. affirmed that peace fills the hearts of all present. During the performance, sent out positive thoughts to the audience. Her goodwill was transmitted to others and felt by them.

##### Example 3: The Office Worker and Critical Colleagues
Used prayer daily in the office: "I think, speak and act lovingly, quietly and peacefully. I now radiate love, peace, tolerance and kindness to all the girls who criticized me and gossiped about me." Criticism and annoyance ceased; relationships transformed.

#### Step 1: Apply the Inner Golden Rule
Think about others in the same manner as you want them to think about you. Feel about others in the same manner as you want them to feel about you. Act toward others in the same manner as you want them to act toward you. Your subconscious mind tends to manifest the patterns you consistently impress upon it.

#### Step 2: Observe Your Reactions and Take Responsibility
Begin to observe yourself throughout the day. Notice how you respond to people, events, and news. Recognize that if the news or others' actions disturb you, it is your negative emotions robbing you of peace and harmony. You are the only person responsible for your own thoughts and reactions.

#### Step 3: Stop Negative Internal Reactions
Realize that being polite on the outside but critical on the inside harms your mental well-being. Negative thoughts can become embedded in your subconscious patterns and create difficulties. Give others freedom to express themselves even when you disagree.

#### Step 4: Use Mental Imagery Before Sleep to Transform Relationships
Before sleep, imagine the specific person behaving in the desired way. Feel the reality of the scene — feel their handshake, hear their tone of voice, see them smile. Create a vivid mental scene and engage fully with it. Practice night after night.

#### Step 5: Practice Empathy Through Imaginative Projection
When tempted to think ill of another, Try to understand the other person's perspective by imagining their thoughts and feelings. Think from the standpoint of that figure's principles. Imagine yourself looking out through the other person's brain and feeling what they feel. If prone to envy or anger, project yourself into the mind of love and think from that standpoint.

#### Step 6: Refuse to Be Manipulated Through Emotional Scenes
Do not permit people to manipulate you through emotional outbursts or threats. Be firm but kind and refuse to yield to their demands. Giving in to manipulation rarely produces positive long-term results. Do that which is right and remain true to your ideal.

#### Best Practices

- ✅ Begin now to observe your reactions to people, conditions, and circumstances
- ✅ Give others freedom to express themselves even when you disagree
- ✅ Disagree without being disagreeable — you have the right to disagree; do so respectfully
- ✅ Practice daily, consistent prayer — establish a regular pattern
- ✅ Use mental imagery before sleep to create desired interactions
- ✅ Repeat prayers out loud slowly, quietly, and feelingly
- ✅ Project empathy — imagine yourself in the other person's position
- ✅ Wish others well — wish health, success, peace of mind, and happiness for others, especially those you find difficult
- ✅ Rejoice in others' success — this attracts good fortune to yourself
- ✅ Anchor your thoughts on peace, harmony, and good will daily
- ❌ Do not be critical and resentful on the inside even if polite on the outside
- ❌ Do not discuss marital or personal problems with neighbors or relatives
- ❌ Do not yield to emotional manipulation — giving in never works

#### Keep In Mind

- The subconscious mind tends to manifest the patterns you consistently impress upon it — it operates according to consistent principles and is not influenced by personal preferences.
- You are the only person responsible for your own thoughts and reactions — the other person is not responsible for the way you think about them.
- Whatever you consistently give out mentally, you receive back
- Love includes understanding, goodwill, and respect for the other person
- The four stages of anger (thinking, deciding to get angry, deciding to act, reacting) all take place in your own mind
- The harmony of the individual contributes to the harmony of the whole — whatever contributes to your peace benefits everyone around you

#### Security & Safety Notes

- Unhappy people often try to bring others down to their level — when you understand this, you remain unaffected

- Always seek advice from a trained counselor for serious relationship problems

  **Solution:** Being polite on the outside but critical on the inside harms your mental well-being — transform your inner thoughts
- **Problem:** Reacting emotionally to news or others' actions
  **Solution:** It does not matter if all other people were wrong and you alone were right — if it disturbs you, it affects you. Change your reaction

  **Solution:** These negative thoughts rob you of vitality, enthusiasm, strength, guidance, and good will — replace with love and goodwill

  **Solution:** Yielding to temper tantrums or emotional manipulation never wins — be firm but kind

  **Solution:** Show appreciation and love daily. Do not assume they will always be there without expressing gratitude
- **Problem:** Nagging or criticism
  **Solution:** Praise and exalt good points. Nagging is usually a craving for love — show love and appreciation instead

### Lesson 15: Use Mental Techniques to Maintain Youthfulness and Overcome Aging

#### Examples

##### Example 1: The 84-Year-Old Surgeon
Operates every morning, visits patients in the afternoon, and writes for medical journals in the evening. This example illustrates that fear of aging may contribute to premature decline — fear of aging is the harmful factor.

##### Example 2: The Author's Father
Learned French at 65, Gaelic at over 60, and taught until passing at 99 with a clear mind at age 99, with improved handwriting and reasoning powers.

##### Example 3: George Bernard Shaw
Active at age 90 with no decline in artistic ability. Demonstrates that the most productive years of life can extend well beyond conventional expectations.

##### Example 4: The Executive Who Reframed Retirement
Viewed forced retirement at 65 as "an exciting new beginning." Went on to travel, lecture, pursue photography, and live a rich, engaged life.

#### Step 1: Reframe Your Understanding of Age
Consider viewing age as a measure of wisdom and experience rather than years alone. Believe this, expect it, and your subconscious mind will bring it to pass. Replace age-limiting language with expansive language. Instead of saying "I am old," say "I am wise and experienced."

#### Step 2: Release the Belief That Years Cause Degenerative Disorders
Understand that it is fear of aging, not aging itself, causes premature decline. Let go of fear-based thinking about aging. Some medical perspectives suggest that years alone do not cause degenerative disorders..

#### Step 3: Cultivate a Vision of Yourself as Vital and Radiant
Picture yourself as happy, healthy, successful, calm, and capable regardless of chronological age. Feel a sense of vitality and renewal flowing through your body. Know and feel that you are inspired, lifted up, rejuvenated, revitalized, and recharged spiritually.

#### Step 4: Continually Generate and Express Qualities That Never Grow Old
Continually practice patience, kindness, truthfulness, humility, good will, and love. By doing so, you can maintain a youthful spirit throughout your life. Spiritual qualities never age or die.

#### Step 5: Keep Your Mind Active and Open to New Learning
Learn something new each day Study new subjects and investigate new ideas. Take up new studies, courses, and interests you were too busy to pursue while making a living. Your mind must stay open to new ideas and learning.

#### Step 6: Welcome Advancing Years Joyfully
View aging as an opportunity for continued learning and growth. Accept your age gracefully. View retirement as a new chapter of opportunity and fulfillment. Shift your focus from making a living to living life.

#### Step 7: Share Your Talents, Experience, and Wisdom
Recognize that you have much to give regardless of age. You can support, guide, and mentor younger people. Give the benefit of your knowledge, experience, and wisdom. Become a producer and not a prisoner of society. Do not hide your light under a bushel.

#### Best Practices

- ✅ Welcome the change of aging joyfully and gladly
- ✅ Accept old age gracefully, recognizing it has its own glory, beauty, and wisdom
- ✅ Continuously cultivate and express spiritual qualities that never age
- ✅ Study new subjects and investigate new ideas throughout life
- ✅ Learn something new each day.
- ✅ Take up new creative pursuits after retirement — photography, travel, lecturing, writing
- ✅ Give attention to living life rather than merely making a living
- ✅ Stabilize, advise, and direct younger generations with your accumulated wisdom
- ✅ Become a producer rather than a consumer/prisoner of society
- ✅ Look ahead constantly, remembering you are gazing into a future of continued growth
- ✅ Trust that life continues and your true nature is spiritual
- ❌ Do not fall into the mental attitude of futility and worthlessness
- ❌ Do not anticipate decline and loss of purpose
- ❌ Avoid retiring mentally and spiritually by staying engaged with new ideas and activities
- ❌ Do not surrender to advancing years and accept limitations imposed by age

#### Keep In Mind

- The subconscious mind operates beyond chronological limitations — it is not limited by age
- Spiritual qualities (patience, kindness, love, good will, joy, happiness, wisdom, understanding) never grow old or die
- Age is not measured in years but in wisdom and spiritual awareness
- Many people find their most productive years extend well beyond conventional retirement age
- Life is self-renewing, enduring, and meaningful for all people
- Your character, quality of mind, faith, and convictions are not subject to decay
- This framework teaches that your self-perception influences your experience of age and ability

#### Security & Safety Notes

- Do not accept stereotypes about aging without questioning them
- While diet and exercise are important, this framework emphasizes that your mental state also plays a role in maintaining youthfulness
- Focusing only on physical appearance rather than inner qualities does not sustain youthfulness

#### Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Falling into a mindset of futility and worthlessness
  **Solution:** Believe that life continues and that you can remain wise, useful, and engaged
- **Problem:** Anticipating senescence and nothingness
  **Solution:** Look forward to growth and development, not decline
- **Problem:** Fear of aging, not aging itself, causes premature decline — release fear-based thinking
  **Solution:** The neurotic fear of time, not time itself, causes premature aging — release fear-based thinking
- **Problem:** Losing interest in life and ceasing to dream
  **Solution:** Learn something new every day. Take up new creative pursuits. Stay enthusiastic about new ideas
- **Problem:** Retiring mentally and spiritually
  **Solution:** Do not allow the mind to stagnate. Be open and receptive to new ideas throughout life
- **Problem:** Focusing only on making a living rather than living life
  **Solution:** Shift focus from making a living to living life — this keeps you engaged and vital
