## Actionable Lessons with Step-by-Step Instructions

### 1. Overcoming Public Speaking Fear
- Sit in an armchair for about five minutes
- Say slowly, quietly, and positively: "I am going to master this fear. I am overcoming it now. I speak with poise and confidence. I am relaxed and at ease."
- Repeat for several nights
- Result: Fear is overcome through the definite law of mind

### 2. Understanding How Thoughts Enter the Subconscious
- Still your mind and relax
- Conscious thoughts sink into the subconscious through osmosis-like process
- Positive seeds grow after their kind, producing poise, serenity, and calm

### 3. Banishing Stage Fright (Detailed Technique)
- Three times a day, isolate yourself in a room
- Sit down comfortably in an armchair
- Relax your body and close your eyes
- Still your mind and body
- Say to yourself: "I sing beautifully, I am poised, serene, confident and calm"
- Repeat slowly, quietly, and with feeling from 5 to 10 times per sitting
- Conduct three sittings per day
- Add one sitting immediately prior to sleep
- Duration: One week for complete transformation

### 4. Overcoming Examination Fear (Amnesia)
- Every night and morning, imagine a parent or friend congratulating you on your wonderful record
- Hold an imaginary letter from them in your hand
- Contemplate the happy result
- Call forth a reciprocal response in yourself
- Let the subconscious power take over and direct your conscious mind
- Result: You imagine the end, thereby willing the means to its realization

### 5. Master Technique for Overcoming Any Specific Fear
- Sit still for five to ten minutes, three to four times a day
- Imagine yourself doing the thing you fear (e.g., swimming, climbing a mountain)
- Make it a subjective, vivid experience
- Feel the chill of the water, the movement of arms and legs
- Make it real and joyous
- Know that what you experience in imagination develops in the subconscious
- Result: You will be compelled to express the image impressed on your deeper mind

### 6. Overcoming Fear of Elevators / Closed Places
- Every night and several times a day, say a blessing over the elevator (or feared place)
- Use the specific affirmation: "The elevator in our building is a wonderful idea. It came out of the universal mind. It is a boon and a blessing to all our employees. It gives wonderful service. It operates in divine order. I ride in it in peace and joy. I remain silent now while the currents of life, love and understanding flow through the patterns of my thought. In my imagination I am now in the elevator, and I step out into my office. The elevator is full of our employees. I talk to them and they are friendly, joyous and free. It is a wonderful experience of freedom, faith and confidence. I give thanks."
- Duration: About ten days of consistent prayer
- Result: Fear dissipates completely

### 7. Overcoming Fear of Water, Heights, or Other Phobias
- Go to the place (pool, mountain) and speak out loud in strong tones: "I am going to master you, I can dominate you"
- Then take practical lessons or action
- Assume a new attitude of mind
- The omnipotent power of the subconscious responds, giving strength, faith, and confidence

### 8. Overcoming Abnormal Fear - Move Mentally to the Opposite
- When fear arises, immediately place attention on the thing desired (the opposite)
- Get absorbed and engrossed in your desire
- Know that the subjective always overturns the objective
- This generates confidence and lifts spirits
- Result: Infinite power of the subconscious moves on your behalf

### 9. Facing Your Fears Directly
- Look at your fears squarely
- Stare them in the face and look them straight in the eye
- They fade and shrink into insignificance

### 10. Overcoming Fear in Emergency Situations (Jungle Example)
- Immediately when fear arises, talk to yourself out loud
- Say: "You can't surrender to your fear. Your fear is a desire for safety and security and a way out."
- Claim: "Infinite Intelligence which guides the planets in their courses is now leading and guiding me"
- Keep saying this out loud for ten minutes or more
- Result: A mood of confidence seizes you and you are guided to safety

### 11. Morning Meditation for Inner Peace
- Sit still for fifteen minutes every morning
- Realize that in the center of yourself is a deep still ocean of peace
- Meditate on this stillness
- Result: Generates tremendous power that overcomes all manner of difficulties and fears

### 12. Refusing to Entertain Fear Suggestions from Others
- When someone suggests fear (e.g., plotting against you), refuse to entertain the suggestion
- Know that no negative thought can manifest unless you emotionalize it and accept it mentally
- Remain calm, peaceful, and centered

### 13. The Formula for Casting Out All Fear
- Use the affirmation: "I sought the Lord, and He heard me, and delivered me from all my fears" (Psalm 34:4)
- Understand "The Lord" as the law/power of your subconscious mind
- Learn the wonders of your subconscious and how it works
- Master the techniques and put them into practice immediately
- Result: You will be free of all fears

## Key Principles and Concepts

1. **The Subconscious is Amenable to Suggestion**: The subconscious mind is controlled by suggestion. Positive or negative, whatever is impressed upon it will grow and manifest.

2. **Osmosis-Like Transfer**: When you still your mind and relax, conscious thoughts sink into the subconscious through a process similar to osmosis, where fluids separated by a porous membrane intermingle.

3. **Fear is a Thought in Your Mind**: Fear is man's greatest enemy, behind failure, sickness, and poor human relations. Most fears have no reality; they are merely "sinister shadows."

4. **Only Two Innate Fears**: Man is born with only two fears: the fear of falling and the fear of noise. All other fears are acquired from parents, relatives, teachers, and early influences.

5. **Normal vs. Abnormal Fear**: Normal fear is protective (e.g., stepping aside from an oncoming car). Abnormal fear occurs when the imagination runs riot, leading to obsession, complexes, and terror.

6. **The Subconscious Takes Fears as Requests**: When you fear something, your subconscious mind takes that fear as a request and proceeds to manifest it in your experience.

7. **Thoughts Enveloped in Fear are Realized**: Thoughts charged with the emotion of fear are realized in the subconscious. Your subconscious responds to the nature of your thought.

8. **The Subconscious is the Storehouse of Memory**: The subconscious has a perfect record of all you have heard and read. It is responsive and reciprocal when approached with relaxation, peace, and confidence.

9. **The Law of the Subconscious**: What you experience in imagination will be developed in your subconscious, and you will be compelled to express the image and likeness impressed on your deeper mind.

10. **Imagination as the Bridge**: You imagine the end, thereby willing the means to the realization of the end. The subjective wisdom takes over and compels you to give an excellent account of yourself.

11. **The Subjective Overturns the Objective**: The subjective (inner) mind always overtakes and overturns the objective (outer) conditions when properly directed.

12. **The Law of Substitution**: Whatever you fear has its solution in the form of your desire. The great law of substitution is the answer to fear.

13. **Thoughts are Creative**: The things you fear do not really exist except as thoughts in your mind. Thoughts are creative, which is why "The thing I feared has come upon me."

14. **The Omnipotent Power Within**: The infinite power of your subconscious mind is always moving on your behalf and cannot fail.

15. **Inner Peace as a Center**: At the center of yourself is a deep still ocean of peace. Meditating on this generates tremendous power.

## Best Practices Mentioned

1. **Sit Still and Relax**: Physical inertia favors passivity and renders the mind more receptive to suggestion.

2. **Speak Slowly, Quietly, and Positively**: Affirmations should be delivered slowly, quietly, and with feeling.

3. **Repeat Consistently**: Conduct sittings three times a day, plus one immediately before sleep. Persevere until the desired result is achieved.

4. **Use Vivid, Subjective Imagination**: Make mental imagery real, vivid, and joyous. Feel the sensations (chill of water, movement of arms, scenery of a mountain).

5. **Bless Instead of Curse**: When you fear something (e.g., an elevator), bless it. Speak of its wonderful qualities and thankfulness.

6. **Morning and Evening Practice**: Practice techniques both morning and night for maximum effect.

7. **Immediate Response to Fear**: Do not let fear linger. Act immediately with positive self-talk or mental redirection.

8. **Use the Opposite Thought**: When fear arises, immediately move your attention to the opposite desire.

9. **Meditate on Inner Peace**: Spend time daily realizing the deep still ocean of peace at your center.

10. **Laugh at Your Fears**: Learn to laugh at your fears - this is described as "the best medicine."

11. **Hold Fears to the Light of Reason**: Examine your fears rationally and they will shrink into insignificance.

12. **Do the Thing You Fear**: Take physical action. Standing before an audience and doing the thing you fear ensures the death of fear.

## Common Pitfalls Mentioned

1. **Permitting Fear Thoughts to Linger**: Allowing fear thoughts to persist causes them to become charged with emotion and realized in the subconscious.

2. **Autosuggestion Through Fear**: Involuntary autosuggestion (a silent fear thought emotionalized and subjectified) is the cause of many failures and breakdowns.

3. **Worrying Before Examinations**: Obsessing with the idea of failure before an exam causes temporary amnesia and self-sabotage.

4. **Living in Negative Mental Movies**: Running a mental movie of failure, bankruptcy, or disaster causes those very things to manifest.

5. **Indulging in Morbid Imagery**: Constantly imagining catastrophe (e.g., airplane crashes, epidemics) attracts what is feared most.

6. **Emotionalizing Negative Thoughts**: Giving concentrated attention and emotional energy to negative suggestions allows them to manifest.

7. **Accepting Fear Suggestions from Others**: Other people's suggestions, statements, or threats have no power unless you accept them mentally.

8. **Stagnating at the Extreme of Fear**: Staying at the extreme of fear causes mental and physical deterioration.

9. **Bemoaning Fate**: Indulging fears and lamenting fate leads to succumbing to the "monster fear" and potentially physical decline.

10. **Vivid Imagination of Disaster**: A vivid imagination that dramatizes feared outcomes (e.g., job loss, accidents) causes neurosis and self-fulfilling failure.

## Techniques and Methods Described

### Technique 1: The Relaxation and Positive Affirmation Method
- Sit quietly in an armchair
- Relax body and still mind
- Repeat positive, specific affirmations slowly, quietly, and with feeling
- Practice multiple times per day with one session before sleep

### Technique 2: The Subjective Imagination Method (Master Technique)
- Sit still 5-10 minutes, 3-4 times daily
- Mentally project yourself into the feared situation
- Experience it vividly with all senses
- Know it is developing in your subconscious
- You will be compelled to act it out physically

### Technique 3: The Mental Rehearsal for Success
- Imagine a loved one congratulating you on success
- Hold imaginary letters or objects
- Feel the joy of the desired outcome
- The subconscious takes over and directs conscious action toward that end

### Technique 4: The Blessing Method
- Speak blessings over the feared object or situation
- Thank it for its service
- Imagine yourself using it peacefully and joyfully
- This reframes the mental association from fear to gratitude

### Technique 5: The Fear-Staring Method
- Look your fear squarely in the face
- Hold it up to the light of reason
- Refuse to look away
- Fear shrinks into insignificance

### Technique 6: The Emergency Self-Talk Method
- When suddenly faced with fear, talk to yourself out loud
- Reframe fear as a desire for safety
- Claim guidance from Infinite Intelligence
- Repeat for 10+ minutes until confidence replaces fear

### Technique 7: The Inner Peace Meditation
- Sit still for 15 minutes each morning
- Realize the deep still ocean of peace at your center
- Generate power through this meditation
- Use this center to overcome difficulties and fears

### Technique 8: The Law of Substitution
- When fear arises, immediately identify the opposite desire
- Place all attention on the desired outcome
- Get absorbed in the desire
- The subjective mind overturns the objective fear

### Technique 9: The Opposite Action Method
- Do the thing you are afraid to do
- Taking physical action while affirming mastery ensures the death of fear

### Technique 10: The Suggestion Rejection Method
- When others suggest fear, refuse to entertain it
- Know that negative thoughts cannot manifest unless you emotionalize and accept them
- Remain centered in peace and positive thought

## Summary Formula: 15 Steps to Freedom From Fear

1. Do the thing you fear. Say "I am going to master this fear."
2. Fear is a negative thought. Supplant it with a constructive thought. Confidence > fear. Faith in God and good.
3. Fear is man's greatest enemy. Love casts out fear. Fall in love with honesty, integrity, justice, goodwill, and success.
4. Counteract fear suggestions with the opposite (e.g., "I sing beautifully; I am poised, serene and calm").
5. Fear causes exam amnesia. Affirm "I have a perfect memory for everything I need to know" or imagine a congratulatory friend. Persevere.
6. If afraid of water, imagine swimming vividly. Feel it mentally. You will be compelled to conquer it physically.
7. If afraid of closed places, mentally ride in the elevator blessing it. Fear dissipates quickly.
8. Born with only two fears (falling and noise). All others are acquired. Get rid of them.
9. Normal fear is good. Abnormal fear is destructive. Constant indulgence causes obsession and terror.
10. Overcome abnormal fear by knowing your subconscious power. Give attention to your desire, not your fear. This is the love that casts out fear.
11. If afraid of failure, dwell on success. If afraid of sickness, dwell on health. If afraid of accidents, dwell on divine protection. If afraid of death, dwell on eternal life.
12. The law of substitution: Whatever you fear has its solution in your desire. Expect the good. Mentally concentrate on the good. Subconscious never fails.
13. Things you fear do not exist except as thoughts. Thoughts are creative. "The thing I feared has come upon me." Think good and good follows.
14. Look at fears, hold them to the light of reason. Laugh at your fears. That is the best medicine.
15. Nothing can disturb you but your own thought. Suggestions of others have no power. Focus on good, and God's power is with your thoughts. There is only one creative power - love and harmony.
