# Analysis: Chapter VIII - How to Get the Results You Want

## Chapter Information
- **Title:** How to Get the Results You Want
- **Chapter Number:** VIII
- **Source:** TPYSYM Subconscious Mind Power

---

## Actionable Lessons with Step-by-Step Instructions

### 1. Overcome Failure Through Faith and Confidence
**Steps:**
1. Get your subconscious to accept your idea or request by feeling its reality now.
2. Trust that the law of your mind will do the rest.
3. Turn over your request with faith and confidence.
4. Allow your subconscious to take over and answer for you.

### 2. Avoid Mental Coercion
**Steps:**
1. Do not try to use mental coercion or force.
2. Instead, use conscious mind acceptance and faith.
3. Understand that your subconscious mind responds to faith, not coercion.

### 3. Achieve Clarity Before Making Requests
**Steps:**
1. Arrive at a definite decision that there is a way out and a solution.
2. Form a clear-cut idea in your conscious mind.
3. Only Infinite Intelligence within your subconscious knows the answer.
4. Come to a clear-cut conclusion in your conscious mind.
5. Make up your mind decisively.

### 4. Practice the "Easy Does It" Approach
**Steps:**
1. Decree health or your desired outcome.
2. Relax completely.
3. Do not be overly concerned with details and means.
4. Focus on knowing the end result, not the process.
5. Get the feel of the happy solution to your problem (health, finances, employment).
6. Feel your new idea subjectively in a finished state, as happening now, not in the future.
7. Let your subconscious be the master mechanic that knows ways and means to heal or resolve any issue.

### 5. Use Imagination Instead of Will Power
**Steps:**
1. Infer no opponent; use no will power.
2. Imagine the end and the freedom state you desire.
3. Persist in maintaining a simple, child-like, miracle-making faith.
4. Picture yourself with the ailment or problem resolved.
5. Imagine the emotional accompaniment of the freedom state you crave.
6. Cut out all red tape from the process; the simple way is the best.

### 6. Practice Disciplined or Scientific Imagination
**Steps:**
1. Recognize your subconscious mind is the builder of your body and controls all vital functions.
2. Believe that whatsoever you ask in prayer with belief, you shall receive.
3. To believe is to accept something as true or to live in the state of being it.
4. Sustain the mood of the fulfilled desire.
5. Experience the joy of the answered prayer.

### 7. Follow the Three Steps to Success in Prayer
**Step 1:** Take a look at the problem.
**Step 2:** Turn to the solution or way out known only to the subconscious mind.
**Step 3:** Rest in a sense of deep conviction that it is done.

**Additional steps from the text:**
- Pass on the idea of health to your subconscious mind to the point of conviction.
- Relax and let the kinetic energy behind the idea take over.
- Say to the condition and circumstance, "This, too, shall pass."
- Get yourself off your hands.

### 8. Avoid Weakening Statements
**Steps:**
1. Do not weaken your prayer by saying "I wish I might be healed" or "I hope so."
2. Your feeling about the work to be done is "the boss."
3. Harmony is yours.
4. Know that health is yours.
5. Become a vehicle for the infinite healing power of the subconscious mind.

### 9. Reconcile the Conflict Between Desire and Imagination
**Steps:**
1. Avoid using mental force, which presupposes opposition.
2. Enter into a drowsy, sleepy state to bring all effort to a minimum.
3. When your mind is concentrated on the means to overcome a problem, it is no longer concerned with the obstacle.
4. Work with your subconscious prior to sleep and just after awakening, when negative thoughts no longer present themselves.
5. Imagine the reality of the fulfilled desire.
6. Feel the thrill of accomplishment.
7. Your subconscious will then bring about the realization of your desire.

### 10. Use Controlled, Directed, and Disciplined Imagination
**Steps:**
1. Use controlled imagination to solve dilemmas and problems.
2. Know that whatever you imagine and feel as true will and must come to pass.
3. Imagine the happy ending vividly prior to sleep.
4. Feel the desired outcome to the best of your ability.
5. Ensure the image in your mind agrees with your heart's desire.
6. Dramatize the desired outcome as vividly as possible.
7. Run the mental picture often enough that it becomes a subjective pattern or "regular train track."
8. During the day, when fear thoughts arise, run the mental movie with gestures, voice, and sound.

## Key Principles and Concepts

### The Law of Your Mind
- Whenever your subconscious mind accepts an idea, it immediately begins to execute it.
- It uses all its mighty resources and mobilizes all mental and spiritual laws of your deeper mind.
- This law works for both good and bad ideas equally.

### The Law of Reversed Effort
- Defined by Coue: "When your desires and imagination are in conflict, your imagination invariably gains the day."
- Mental effort is invariably self-defeated and results in the opposite of what is desired.
- Your subconscious is always controlled by the dominant idea.
- The subconscious will accept the strongest of two contradictory propositions.
- The effortless way is better.

### The Role of the Subconscious Mind
- The subconscious mind is the master mechanic and all-wise one.
- It knows ways and means of healing any organ of your body and resolving your affairs.
- It is the builder of the body and controls all vital functions.

### The Power of Feeling
- Your feeling is the touchstone of all subconscious demonstration.
- The feeling of health produces health; the feeling of wealth produces wealth.
- You are what you imagine yourself to be.

### Harmony Between Conscious and Subconscious
- Prayer is answered when there is harmonious union between your conscious and subconscious on any idea, desire, or mental image.
- The two agreeing may be represented as: you and your desire, your thought and feeling, your idea and emotion, your desire and imagination.

### Best Time for Impregnating the Subconscious
- Prior to sleep and just after awakening are the best times.
- In the sleepy state, negative thoughts and imagery that tend to neutralize your desire no longer present themselves.

### Biblical Reference (Matthew 18:19)
- If two of you agree on earth regarding anything they ask, it shall be done.
- The "two" refers to the harmonious union between your conscious and subconscious.

2. **Feel the Result Subjectively:** Your new idea must be felt in a finished state, as happening now, not in the future.
3. **Use Imagination, Not Will Power:** Avoid will power; use imagination instead.
4. **Maintain Child-like Faith:** Persist in maintaining a simple, child-like, miracle-making faith.
5. **Use Disciplined Imagination:** Practice scientific imagination by vividly picturing the desired outcome.
6. **Focus on End Results:** Get the feel of the happy solution to your problem.
7. **Trust the Subconscious:** Lean heavily upon your subconscious mind.
8. **Avoid Negative Statements:** Refrain from using statements like "Things are getting worse" or "I see no way out."
9. **Sleep in Peace, Wake in Joy:** Imagine the fulfillment of your desire prior to sleep.
10. **Dramatize Your Desires:** Run mental movies with gestures, voice, and sound to make them feel real.
11. **Make Your Mind Up:** Arrive at a definite decision; know there is a solution.
12. **Simplify the Process:** The simple way is the best; cut out all red tape.
13. **Proclaim Health Frequently:** Proclaim that divine right action is taking place in your body.
14. **Use Sleep as a Tool:** Use the state before sleep to avoid conflict between desire and imagination.

## Common Pitfalls Mentioned

1. **Lack of Confidence:** This is one of the principal reasons for failure.
2. **Too Much Effort:** Excessive effort blocks answers to prayers and shows anxiety and fear.
3. **Mental Coercion:** Trying to compel the subconscious mind by exercising will power causes failure.
4. **Negative Self-Talk:** Statements such as "Things are getting worse," "I will never get an answer," "I see no way out," "It's hopeless," "I don't know what to do," and "I'm all mixed up" block the subconscious.
5. **Too Many Directions:** Giving half a dozen different directions in five minutes (like confusing a taxi driver) creates confusion in the subconscious mind.
6. **Using Will Power to Force Remembering:** This causes answers to flee further, as illustrated by exam students who blank out.
7. **Conflict Between Desire and Imagination:** When desires and imagination are in conflict, imagination wins and the desire fails.
8. **Desire Without Conviction:** Saying "I want a healing, but I can't get it" or "I try so hard" undermines the result.
9. **Concentrating on Means Instead of End:** Being overly concerned with the process blocks the outcome.
10. **Visualizing the Future Instead of the Present:** Feeling the result as in the future rather than now prevents demonstration.
11. **Doubting Statements:** "I wish I might be healed" or "I hope so" weakens the prayer.
12. **Overthinking the Problem:** Excessive mental effort creates the opposite of the desired result.

## Techniques and Methods Described

### 1. The Relaxation Technique
- Decree your desired outcome.
- Relax completely.
- Do not focus on details or means.
- Focus on the end result and feel it as if already achieved.

### 2. The Drowsy State Method
- Enter a drowsy, sleepy state prior to sleep.
- In this state, the conscious mind is submerged, minimizing effort.
- Negative thoughts no longer present themselves to neutralize your desire.
- Imagine the fulfillment of your desire over and over again.
- This avoids conflict between conscious and subconscious.

### 3. Disciplined/Scientific Imagination
- Vividly picture the desired outcome.
- Include emotional accompaniment (feel the thrill of accomplishment).
- Make the image subjective and real.
- Repeat the mental picture until it becomes a subjective pattern or "regular train track."
- During the day, replay the mental movie with gestures, voice, and sound.

### 4. The Three-Step Prayer Method
1. Look at the problem.
2. Turn to the solution known only to the subconscious.
3. Rest in deep conviction that it is done.

### 5. The "Get Off Your Hands" Method
- After conveying the idea to your subconscious with conviction, relax.
- Say to the condition: "This, too, shall pass."
- Let the kinetic energy behind the idea take over.

### 6. The Mental Movie Technique
- Dramatize the desired outcome as vividly as possible.
- Include all sensory details (sound of voice, smile, mannerisms).
- Run the movie often enough to establish it as a subjective pattern.
- Replay during the day when negative thoughts arise.

### 7. Using Sleep as a Tool
- Use the time prior to sleep and just after awakening.
- This is when the conscious mind is submerged and negative imagery is minimized.
- Use this time to impregnate the subconscious with desired images and feelings.

### 8. The Decree Method
- Proclaim divine right action frequently.
- Lean heavily upon the subconscious mind.
- Know the health or desired outcome is already yours.
- Pass the idea to your subconscious to the point of conviction.

## Summary of Core Message

The chapter teaches that the key to getting results from the subconscious mind is not through effort, force, or will power, but through:
1. Having faith and confidence.
2. Maintaining clear, positive, and definite ideas.
3. Using imagination rather than will power.
4. Entering relaxed, drowsy states (especially before sleep).
5. Feeling the desired outcome as already real and accomplished.
6. Allowing the subconscious to work effortlessly on the idea once accepted.

The principal reasons for failure are lack of confidence and too much effort. The solution lies in understanding how the subconscious mind functions and working with it through relaxation, imagination, and faith rather than coercion or struggle.
