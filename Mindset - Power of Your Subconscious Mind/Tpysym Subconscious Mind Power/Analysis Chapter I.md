# Analysis: Chapter I - The Treasure House Within You

## Chapter Title and Number

**Chapter I: The Treasure House Within You**

## Actionable Lessons with Step-by-Step Instructions

### 1. Become a Magnetized Person
- Recognize the two types of people: magnetized (full of confidence and faith) vs. demagnetized (full of fears and doubts).
- **Step:** Decide now to become a magnetized person who knows they are born to win and succeed.
- **Step:** Cease entertaining fear-based thoughts like "I might fail" or "people will laugh at me."
- **Step:** Move forward with confidence rather than staying where you are out of fear.

### 2. Contact and Release the Hidden Power of Your Subconscious Mind
- **Step:** Decide now to make your life grander, greater, richer, and nobler than ever before.
- **Step:** Begin to recognize the potentialities of your deeper mind (infinite wisdom, infinite power, infinite supply).
- **Step:** Expect these potentialities to take form in the world without.

### 3. Apply the Law of Belief
- **Step:** Understand that your prayer/thought is answered according to the universal law of action and reaction.
- **Step:** Busy your mind with the concepts of harmony, health, peace, and good will.
- **Step:** Observe that wonders will happen in your life as a result.

### 4. Plant Positive Seeds in Your Subconscious Mind
- **Step:** View your mind as a garden where you are the gardener planting seeds (thoughts).
- **Step:** Begin now to sow thoughts of peace, happiness, right action, good will, and prosperity.
- **Step:** Think quietly and with interest on these positive qualities.
- **Step:** Accept these qualities fully in your conscious reasoning mind.
- **Step:** Continue to plant these wonderful seeds and you will reap a glorious harvest.

### 5. Take Charge of Your Thoughts to Control Conditions
- **Step:** Take charge of your thought processes so you bring forth only desirable conditions.
- **Step:** Ensure thoughts deposited in your subconscious mind are constructive, harmonious, and peaceful.
- **Step:** Apply the powers of your subconscious to any problem or difficulty.

### 6. Change External Conditions by Changing Internal Causes
- **Step:** Recognize that to change external conditions, you must change the cause.
- **Step:** Do not try to change conditions by working only with conditions and circumstances.
- **Step:** Change the way you use your conscious mind (the way you are thinking and picturing).
- **Step:** Remove discord, confusion, lack, and limitation by removing the cause in your thinking.

### 7. Use the Caruso Technique for Controlling Fear and Panic
- **Step:** When your conscious mind is full of fear, worry, and anxiety, and your subconscious floods you with panic, speak authoritatively to your deeper self.
- **Step:** Say with conviction: "Be still, be quiet, I am in control, you must obey me, you are subject to my command, you cannot intrude where you do not belong."
- **Step:** Observe how speaking with authority brings silence, harmony, and peace to your mind.

### 8. Reverse Negative Statements Immediately
- **Step:** Never finish a negative statement.
- **Step:** Reverse it immediately.
- **Step:** Expect wonders to happen in your life as a result.

### 9. Use Repetition, Faith, and Expectancy to Convey Ideas to Your Subconscious
- **Step:** Repeat your desired idea frequently with feeling.
- **Step:** Combine repetition with faith and expectancy.
- **Step:** Convey the idea lovingly and feelingly to your subconscious mind.
- **Step:** Release the whole thing to your deeper mind, which has the "know-how" of accomplishment.

### 10. Give the Right Orders to Your Subconscious Mind
- **Step:** Recognize that you are like a captain navigating a ship; you must give the right orders.
- **Step:** Give the right orders (thoughts and images) to your subconscious mind.
- **Step:** Understand that your subconscious mind controls and governs all your experiences.

### 11. Heal Through Subconscious Suggestion Before Sleep
- **Step:** Lull yourself to sleep every night with the idea of perfect health.
- **Step:** Trust that your subconscious, being your faithful servant, will obey you.

### 12. Affirm Positive Statements Instead of Negative Ones
- **Step:** Avoid using the terms "I can't afford it" or "I can't do this."
- **Step:** Understand that your subconscious mind takes you at your word.
- **Step:** Affirm instead: "I can do all things through the power of my subconscious mind."

### 13. Apply the Philippians 4:8 Filter to Your Thoughts
- **Step:** Think on whatsoever things are true, honest, just, pure, lovely, and of good report.
- **Step:** If there is any virtue or praise, think on these things.

## Key Principles and Concepts

1. **The Master Secret of the Ages:** The marvelous, miracle-working power found in your own subconscious mind.

2. **Infinite Riches Within:** Infinite Intelligence, boundless love, infinite wisdom, infinite power, and infinite supply exist within your subconscious mind, waiting for development and expression.

3. **The Law of Belief:** Your subconscious mind is a principle that works according to the law of belief. A belief is a thought in your mind. It is not the thing believed in, but the belief in your own mind that brings about the result.

4. **Thought Creates Reality:** All experiences, events, conditions, and acts are reactions of your subconscious mind to your thoughts. Every thought is a cause and every condition is an effect.

5. **Conscious vs. Subconscious Mind:** You have one mind with two distinctive functions:
   - **Conscious Mind:** The captain at the bridge, directing and issuing orders.
   - **Subconscious Mind:** The engine room, automatically obeying orders without question.

6. **Subconscious Reactivity:** Your subconscious mind is reactive and responds to the nature of your thoughts. It works 24 hours a day and pours the fruit of your habitual thinking into your lap.

7. **Universal Principles:** The principles governing the subconscious mind are universal and operate with certainty, just like chemical and physical laws (e.g., water seeks its own level, matter expands when heated).

8. **The World Within Creates the World Without:** It is the world within (thoughts, feelings, imagery) that makes the world without. Everything in your world of expression has been created by you in the inner world of your mind, consciously or unconsciously.

9. **Infinite Intelligence at Your Disposal:** The Infinite Intelligence within your subconscious mind can reveal everything you need to know at every moment of time and point of space, provided you are open-minded and receptive.

10. **Miraculous Healing Power:** There is a miraculous healing power in your subconscious that can heal the troubled mind and broken heart, open prison doors of the mind, and free you from material and physical bondage.

## Common Pitfalls Mentioned

1. **Being Demagnetized:** Being full of fears and doubts, saying "I might fail" or "I might lose money" or "people will laugh at me."
2. **Finishing Negative Statements:** Completing thoughts like "I can't afford that car, that trip, that home" and allowing your subconscious to take you at your word.
3. **False Beliefs and Superstitions:** Believing in false beliefs, opinions, superstitions, and fears of mankind instead of eternal verities and truths.
4. **Faulty Conscious Instructions:** Like the captain giving wrong orders, a conscious mind filled with fear, worry, and anxiety issues faulty instructions that cause the subconscious to flood you with panic and despair.
5. **Trying to Change Conditions Directly:** Attempting to change external conditions by working only with conditions and circumstances rather than changing the internal cause (your thinking).
6. **Lack of Knowledge and Application:** Failing to apply principles for definite, specific purposes, resulting in uncertain or absent results.

## Techniques and Methods Described

### 1. The Garden Analogy (Mind as Garden)
- View your mind as a garden and yourself as the gardener.
- Plant thoughts (seeds) in your subconscious mind all day long based on habitual thinking.
- As you sow in your subconscious mind, so shall you reap in your body and environment.

### 2. The Ship/Captain Analogy
- The conscious mind is like the navigator or captain at the bridge of a ship.
- The subconscious mind is like the men in the engine room who obey orders automatically.
- The crew does not talk back to the captain; they simply carry out orders.

### 3. The Caruso Technique (Speaking to the Subconscious)
- When gripped by fear or panic, speak authoritatively and with conviction to your deeper self.
- Use affirmations like: "Be still, be quiet, I am in control, you must obey me, you are subject to my command, you cannot intrude where you do not belong."
- This technique demonstrates that the subconscious is subject to the conscious mind.

### 4. Immediate Reversal of Negative Statements
- Never finish a negative statement.
- Reverse it immediately.
- Example: Instead of saying "I can't afford that bag," say "That bag is mine. It is for sale. I accept it mentally and my subconscious sees to it that I receive it."

### 5. Repetition, Faith, and Expectancy Method
- Repeat your desired idea frequently with feeling.
- Combine repetition with faith and expectancy.
- Release the idea to your deeper mind, trusting it has the "know-how" of accomplishment.
- Example: A 75-year-old widow repeated "I am wanted. I am happily married to a kind, loving and spiritual minded man. I am secure!" many times a day for about two weeks, resulting in meeting her future husband.

### 6. Pre-Sleep Suggestion for Healing
- Lull yourself to sleep every night with the idea of perfect health.
- Trust your subconscious to obey and work on this while you sleep.

### 7. The Philippians 4:8 Filter
- Before accepting or entertaining a thought, filter it through: Is it true? honest? just? pure? lovely? of good report? virtuous? praiseworthy?
- Only think on these things.

### 8. Affirmation Practice
- Use positive affirmations instead of negative statements.
- Example: "I can do all things through the power of my subconscious mind."
- Avoid: "I can't afford it" or "I can't do this."
