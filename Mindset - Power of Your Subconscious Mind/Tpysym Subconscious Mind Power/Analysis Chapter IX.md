## Actionable Lessons and Step-by-Step Instructions

### 1. Build a Wealth Consciousness
**Instructions:**
- Repeat the words "Wealth - Success" to yourself three or four times a day for about five minutes each session.
- Anchor your mind on the substantial power within you.
- Do not say "I am wealthy" — instead, dwell on the real powers within you.
- The feeling of wealth will well up within you as you dwell on the idea of wealth.

**Expected Result:** Conditions and circumstances corresponding to the nature and quality of your thoughts will be manifested in your life.

---

### 2. Avoid Mental Conflict in Affirmations
**Instructions:**
- Make this practical statement frequently, particularly prior to sleep: "By day and by night I am being prospered in all of my interests."
- This affirmation will not arouse argument because it does not contradict your subconscious mind's impression of financial lack.
- For improving sales, repeat: "My sales are improving every day; I am advancing, progressing and getting wealthier every day."

**Expected Result:** This statement engages the cooperation of the conscious and subconscious mind, and results follow.

---

### 3. Sleep and Grow Rich Technique
**Instructions:**
- As you go to sleep at night, repeat the word "Wealth" quietly, easily, and feelingly.
- Do this over and over again, just like a lullaby.
- Lull yourself to sleep with the one word "Wealth."

**Expected Result:** Wealth should flow to you in avalanches of abundance.

---

### 4. Stop Signing Blank Checks (Eliminate Negative Statements)
**Instructions:**
- Stop making statements such as:
  - "There is not enough to go around"
  - "There is a shortage"
  - "I will lose the house because of the mortgage"
- If you are full of fear about the future, recognize that you are writing a blank check and attracting negative conditions.

**Expected Result:** Your subconscious mind will no longer take your fear and negative statements as requests and proceed to bring obstacles, delays, lack, and limitation to your life.

---

### 5. Deposit Prosperity Thoughts Daily
**Instructions:**
- Every morning as you awaken, deposit thoughts of prosperity, success, wealth, and peace in your subconscious mind.
- Dwell upon these concepts.
- Busy your mind with them as often as possible.

**Expected Result:** These constructive thoughts will find their way as deposits in your subconscious mind and bring forth abundance and prosperity (compound interest principle).

---

### 6. Stop Negative Thoughts Immediately
**Instructions:**
- When you are about to say something negative such as "I shall not be able to make that payment," stop the sentence before you get further than "I shall..."
- Immediately dwell on a constructive statement such as: "By day and by night I am prospered in all my ways."

**Expected Result:** This prevents neutralizing the good you have affirmed. Treat thoughts like seeds: once planted, do not dig them up.

---

### 7. Overcome Envy and Rejoice in Others' Prosperity
**Instructions:**
- If you feel envy when seeing a competitor with more money, say to yourself: "Isn't it wonderful! I rejoice in that man's prosperity. I wish for him greater and greater wealth."
- If you are ever annoyed or irritated by the prosperity or great wealth of another, immediately claim that you truly wish for him greater wealth in every possible way.

**Expected Result:** This neutralizes the negative thoughts in your mind and causes an ever-greater measure of wealth to flow to you by the law of your own subconscious mind.

---

### 8. Get on Mental Good Terms with Everyone
**Instructions:**
- If you are worried and critical about someone who is making money (even if you believe dishonestly), cease worrying about him.
- Remember: the block or obstacle to wealth is in your own mind.
- Destroy that mental block by getting on mental good terms with everyone.
- Be careful not to criticize others.

**Expected Result:** This removes the mental block to wealth in your own mind.

---

### 9. Decide to Be Wealthy the Easy Way
**Instructions:**
- Make a conscious decision to be wealthy the easy way, with the infallible aid of your subconscious mind.
- Do not try to accumulate wealth by the sweat of your brow and hard labor (described as "one way to become the richest man in the graveyard").
- Do the thing you love to do and do it for the joy and thrill of it.

**Expected Result:** You align yourself with the effortless way of life, which is described as the best way.

## Key Principles and Concepts

1. **Wealth is a Subconscious Conviction**: Wealth is simply a subconscious conviction on the part of the individual. You grow into a wealth consciousness by building into your mentality the idea of wealth and abundance.

2. **Invisible Means of Support**: The trouble with most people is that they have no invisible means of support. When business falls away or investments are lost, they seem helpless because they do not know how to tap the subconscious mind.

3. **The Subconscious Mind as a Universal Financial Institution**: Your subconscious mind is like a bank. It magnifies whatever you deposit or impress upon it, whether the idea of wealth or of poverty. Choose wealth.

4. **Compound Interest of the Subconscious**: To him that hath the feeling of wealth, more wealth shall be added; to him that hath the feeling of lack, more lack shall be added. Your subconscious multiplies and magnifies whatever you deposit in it.

5. **True Source of Wealth**: Your subconscious mind is never short of ideas. There are within it an infinite number of ideas ready to flow into your conscious mind and appear as cash. Wealth is never truly dependent on bonds, stocks, or money in the bank — these are only symbols.

6. **The Feeling Produces the Reality**: The feeling of wealth produces wealth. Keep this in mind at all times.

7. **Envy and Condemnation Block Wealth**: Envy is the emotion that causes lack of wealth in the lives of many. Criticizing and condemning others who have more wealth than you causes wealth to take wings and fly away.

8. **Words Have Creative Power**: Your words have power to cleanse your mind of wrong ideas and to instill right ideas in their place.

9. **The Dominant Idea is Accepted**: Your subconscious accepts what you really feel to be true, not just idle words or statements. The dominant idea or belief is always accepted by the subconscious mind.

10. **Subconscious Takes Fear as Request**: If you are full of fear about the future, your subconscious mind takes your fear and negative statement as your request and proceeds in its own way to bring obstacles, delays, lack, and limitation to your life.

## Common Pitfalls Mentioned

1. **Believing Wealth Requires Hard Labor**: Do not believe the story that the only way to become wealthy is by the sweat of your brow and hard labor. Striving and slaving hard is not necessary.

2. **Saying "I Am Wealthy" Without Belief**: Many people say "I am wealthy, I am prosperous" for weeks and months with no results because they feel they are lying to themselves. Their statements are rejected by the conscious mind.

3. **Mental Conflict in Affirmations**: Affirmations that arouse argument or contradict your subconscious mind's impression of financial lack will make matters worse because they suggest your lack.

4. **Writing Blank Checks**: Making statements like "There is not enough to go around," "There is a shortage," or "I will lose the house because of the mortgage" attracts negative conditions.

5. **Digging Up the Seed**: Indulging in fear thoughts perhaps ten minutes after affirming good, thereby neutralizing the good you had affirmed.

6. **Condemning the Prosperous**: Criticizing and condemning those who have succeeded in life and raised their heads above the crowd. Calling successful people "ruthless," "crooks," or saying they have a "racket."

7. **Envy and Covetousness**: Being envious and covetous of others' prosperity. Envious thoughts place you in a very negative position, causing wealth to flow from you instead of to you.

8. **Worrying About Others' Dishonest Wealth**: Being worried and critical about someone who appears to be making money dishonestly. This creates a block in your own mind.

9. **Relying on External Symbols for Wealth**: Believing that wealth is truly dependent on bonds, stocks, or money in the bank, rather than understanding these are only symbols of wealth.
