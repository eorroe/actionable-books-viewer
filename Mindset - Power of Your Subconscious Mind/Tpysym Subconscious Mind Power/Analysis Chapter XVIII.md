# Analysis: Chapter XVIII - How Your Subconscious Removes Mental Blocks

## Chapter Information
- **Title**: How Your Subconscious Removes Mental Blocks
- **Number**: XVIII

---

## Actionable Lessons with Step-by-Step Instructions

### 1. Finding Answers When Facing Difficult Situations
- Assume that Infinite Intelligence within your subconscious mind knows all, sees all, has the answer, and is revealing it to you now
- Adopt a new mental attitude that creative intelligence is bringing about a happy solution
- Rest assured that such an attitude will bring order, peace, and meaning to all your undertakings

### 2. Harmonizing Conscious and Subconscious Minds to Break Habits
- Recognize that your destructive habit is automatic but was acquired through conscious choice
- Realize that if you were conditioned negatively, you can be conditioned positively
- Cease thinking of the fact that you are powerless to overcome the habit
- Understand there is no obstacle to healing other than your own thought
- Do not rely on will power alone or great mental effort/coercion

### 3. Building a Mental Picture for Desired Change
- Relax your body and enter a relaxed, drowsy, meditative state
- Fill your mind with a clear picture of the desired end
- Know that your subconscious mind can bring it about in the easiest way
- When your attention wanders, immediately recall the mental picture
- Repeat the mental movie frequently
- Become absorbed in the mental atmosphere
- When temptation arises, switch your imagination from negative reveries to the feeling of having achieved your goal
- Confidently expect to experience the picture you are developing

### 4. Breaking a Jinx or Negative Mental Pattern
- Realize you are one with Infinite Intelligence of your subconscious mind which knows no obstacle, difficulty, or delay
- Live in the joyous expectancy of the best
- Repeat a prayer/affirmation each morning and night to establish a new habit pattern in your subconscious mind

### 5. Three Magic Steps for Overcoming Destructive Habits
- **First Step**: Get still; quiet the wheels of the mind. Enter into a sleepy, drowsy state.
- **Second Step**: Take a brief phrase that can readily be graven on memory and repeat it over and over as a lullaby. Suggested phrase: "Sobriety and peace of mind are mine now, and I give thanks." Prevent the mind from wandering by repeating it aloud or sketching its pronunciation with the lips and tongue. Do this for five minutes or more. You will find a deep emotional response.
- **Third Step**: Just before going to sleep, imagine a friend or loved one in front of you (eyes closed, relaxed, at peace). The loved one is subjectively present and saying, "Congratulations!" See the smile, hear the voice, mentally touch the hand. Make it all real and vivid. The word "congratulations" implies complete freedom. Hear it over and over until you get the subconscious reaction which satisfies.

### 6. Admitting the Problem and Eradicating Hidden Causes
- If you are an alcoholic or drug addict, admit it. Do not dodge the issue.
- Face your problem and look at it directly
- The hidden cause lodged in the subconscious mind has to be eradicated
- Once the cause is removed, healing will follow

### 7. Impressing the Subconscious Mind Through Prayer/Speech
- Repeat affirmations/prayers three or four times a day
- Speak statements out loud, slowly, lovingly, and meaningfully
- The statements will gradually sink down into the subconscious mind like seeds that grow after their kind
- Concentrate on these truths so they go in through the eyes and ears
- The healing vibrations of these words reach the subconscious mind
- Knowing what you are doing and why gives you the necessary faith and confidence

### 8. Choosing Your Thoughts to Protect Inner Peace
- Refuse to let thoughts of hatred, anger, hostility, and ill will rob you of peace, harmony, health, and happiness
- Identify your thoughts immediately with your aim in life
- Your aim is peace, health, inspiration, harmony, and abundance
- Feel a river of peace flowing through you now
- Choose to let your thought bless, inspire, and give you peace

### 9. Developing Intense Desire for Change
- Want what you want with the intensity of wanting air when immersed in water
- Come to a clear-cut decision that there is a way out
- Make the firm choice that this is the course you wish to follow
- Victory and triumph are assured when you really have an intense desire to overcome any block

### 10. Using the Law of Substitution
- Realize that something good can come out of your suffering
- Understand that it is foolish to continue to suffer
- Use your imagination to take you to freedom and peace of mind rather than to destructive habits
- Bear the transition like a mother in the pangs of childbirth, knowing you will bring forth a child of the mind
- Your subconscious will give birth to sobriety

## Key Principles and Concepts

1. **The solution lies within the problem** - The answer is in every question
2. **Infinite Intelligence** - The subconscious mind knows all, sees all, and responds to you as you call upon it with faith and confidence
3. **Habit is the function of your subconscious mind** - There is no greater evidence of its power than the force and sway habit holds in your life
4. **The law of compulsion** - The subconscious mind holds you in bondage or gives you freedom depending on how you use it
5. **Second nature** - Automatic habit action of the subconscious mind in response to thinking and acting
6. **Conscious mind as camera, subconscious as sensitive plate** - Whatever mental picture you behold in your conscious mind, your subconscious mind will bring to pass
7. **The only obstacle is your own thought** - The only obstacle to success and achievement is your own thought or mental image
8. **The law of substitution** - Your imagination can take you to destructive habits or to freedom; you choose which direction
9. **Fifty-one percent rule** - If your desire to give up a bad habit is greater than your desire to continue, you are fifty-one percent healed already
10. **Whatever thought you anchor upon magnifies** - The mind magnifies whatever thought you anchor it upon
11. **Whatever idea you emotionalize is accepted by the subconscious** - Emotionalized ideas are brought to pass by the subconscious
12. **You are the only thinker in your universe** - Statements of others cannot hurt you except through your own thoughts and mental participation
13. **As a man thinketh, so is he** - The real cause of destructive habits is negative and destructive thinking in the subconscious

## Best Practices Mentioned

1. **Relaxation and receptivity** - Enter a relaxed, drowsy, meditative state before working with the subconscious
2. **Focused attention** - When your attention wanders, bring it back to contemplation of your good or goal
3. **Disciplining the mind** - Make it a habit to refocus your attention; this is called disciplining the mind
4. **Repetition** - Repeat mental pictures, prayers, and phrases frequently to establish new habit patterns
5. **Faith and confidence** - Back up your statements with understanding of how your mind works
6. **Perseverance** - Keep on keeping on until the day breaks and the shadows flee away
7. **Morning and night practice** - Establish a routine of practice both in the morning before activities and at night before sleep
8. **Vivid imagination** - Make mental pictures real and vivid, as if subjectively present
9. **Slow, meaningful speech** - Speak affirmations slowly, lovingly, and meaningfully for better subconscious absorption
10. **Daily prayer** - Prayer is identified as a good habit to cultivate

---

## Common Pitfalls Mentioned

1. **Will power alone** - Relying solely on will power to suppress desires often makes matters worse
2. **Powerlessness mindset** - The idea of being powerless operates as a powerful suggestion to the subconscious and aggravates weakness
3. **Mental effort and coercion** - Great mental effort and mental coercion are not needed and can be counterproductive
4. **Refusing to admit the problem** - Many people remain stuck because they refuse to admit they have a problem
5. **Dodging the issue** - Avoiding facing the problem directly prevents healing
6. **Living in fear** - Fear thoughts repeated over and over establish negative patterns and attract what you fear
7. **Building negative beliefs** - Gradually building up beliefs in obstruction and last-minute cancellations creates a vicious circle
8. **Continuing to suffer** - It is foolish to continue suffering once you realize the power of your subconscious
9. **Lack of intensity** - Without intense desire and clear decision, change is harder to achieve
10. **Negative self-talk** - Repeating statements of powerlessness reinforces the negative pattern

## Techniques and Methods Described

### Technique 1: Mental Reconditioning Through Vivid Imagery
- Use a relaxed, drowsy, meditative state
- Create a vivid mental picture of the desired outcome
- Include specific details (e.g., a loved one congratulating you)
- Repeat the mental movie frequently
- Switch imagination from negative to positive scenes when tempted

### Technique 2: The Three Magic Steps
1. Stillness and drowsy relaxation
2. Repetition of a brief, memorable phrase with physical engagement (lips/tongue) for five minutes or more
3. Pre-sleep visualization of a loved one subjectively present offering congratulations

### Technique 3: Morning and Night Prayer/Affirmation
- Establish a new habit pattern by repeating affirmations morning and night
- Use specific, present-tense statements of freedom, peace, and success
- Speak slowly, lovingly, and meaningfully

### Technique 4: Law of Substitution
- When the mind goes to a destructive thought or image, substitute it immediately with a constructive one
- Use imagination to take you to freedom rather than to destructive habits
- Bear the transition discomfort with the perspective of birthing a new reality

### Technique 5: Identifying with Your Aim
- When upset by people, conditions, news, or events, immediately identify your thoughts with your aim in life
- Your aim should be peace, health, inspiration, harmony, and abundance
- This protects your inner peace from external thieves (negative thoughts)

### Technique 6: Camera and Plate Analogy
- View your conscious mind as a camera
- View your subconscious mind as the sensitive plate
- Whatever picture you impress on the conscious mind, the subconscious develops
- Mental pictures are developed in the darkroom of the subconscious mind
