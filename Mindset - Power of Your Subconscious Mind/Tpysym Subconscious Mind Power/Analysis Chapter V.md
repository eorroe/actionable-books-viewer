# Analysis: Chapter V — Mental Healings in Modern Times

**Number:** Chapter V

## Actionable Lessons and Step-by-Step Instructions

### 1. How to Release the Healing Power Within You
- Realize that the healing power is in your subconscious mind.
- Change your mental attitude.
- Give correct directions to your subconscious mind.
- Your subconscious will heal your mind and body of all disease when properly directed.

### 2. Modern Mental Therapeutic Procedure (Prayer Therapy — Basic Method)
- Go into your closet (meaning: still your mind).
- Shut the door to outside distractions and appearances.
- Relax and let go.
- Think of the infinite healing presence within you.
- Quietly and knowingly turn over your request or desire to your subconscious mind.
- Realize that the intelligence of your mind will answer according to your specific needs.

### 3. The Core Healing Technique: "Believe You Have Received"
- Imagine the end desired.
- Feel its reality.
- The infinite life principle will respond to your conscious choice and conscious request.
- This is the meaning of "believe you have received, and you shall receive."

### 4. Prayer Therapy for a Specific Difficulty or Sickness
- Be aware that your problem is caused by negative thoughts charged with fear lodged in your subconscious mind.
- Turn to the healing power within your own subconscious mind.
- Remind yourself of its infinite power, intelligence, and capacity to heal all conditions.
- Dwell on these truths to dissolve fear and correct erroneous beliefs.
- Give thanks for the healing you know will come.
- Keep your mind off the difficulty until you feel guided to pray again.
- While praying, absolutely refuse to give any power to negative conditions.
- Do not admit for even a second that the healing will not come.
- This attitude brings about the harmonious union of the conscious and subconscious mind, which releases the healing power.

### 5. How to Achieve Subjective Faith (The Passivity Method)
- Relax the mind and the body.
- Enter a sleepy, drowsy, or passive state.
- In this drowsy state, your passivity becomes receptive to subjective impression.
- The conscious mind's resistance is reduced to a minimum.
- Allow suggestions of perfect health to be conveyed to your subconscious mind.
- Your subjective faith will be made manifest through this passivity and suggestion.

### 6. How to Perform Absent (Distant) Treatment for a Loved One
- Quiet your mind.
- Become conscious of the qualities of health, well-being, and relaxation.
- Project in your own mind the image of wholeness, harmony, and perfect health for the person.
- Recognize that the healing presence is right where your loved one is.
- Understand that disease has no ultimate reality.
- Align yourself with the infinite principle of love and life.
- Know and decree that harmony, health, and peace are now being expressed in their body.
- Realize that your thoughts of health operate through the one universal subjective mind and set a law of motion that manifests as healing in their body.

### 7. How to Release the Kinetic Action of the Subconscious Mind During Sleep
- Before going to sleep, quietly affirm health and wholeness.
- Give your subconscious mind something good to work on as you drop off into slumber.
- Example affirmation: "Every cell, nerve, tissue, and muscle of my lungs are being made whole, pure, and perfect. My whole body is being restored to health and harmony."
- The kinetic action of the subconscious mind continues throughout your sleep period, working on what you gave it before sleep.

## Key Principles and Concepts

### The One Universal Healing Principle
- There is only one universal healing principle operating through everything — the cat, the dog, the tree, the grass, the wind, the earth.
- This life principle operates through the animal, vegetable, and mineral kingdoms as instinct and the law of growth.
- It is called by many names: Nature, Life, God, Creative Intelligence, Subconscious Power.
- It is operative in all men regardless of creed, color, or race. You do not have to belong to any particular church to use it.

### The Law of Belief
- All religions represent forms of belief, and the law of life is belief.
- Belief is a thought in your mind which causes the power of your subconscious to be distributed into all phases of your life according to your thinking habits.
- The Bible is not talking about belief in some ritual, ceremony, form, institution, man, or formula — it is talking about belief itself.
- "It is done unto you as you believe."
- "If thou canst believe, all things are possible to him that believeth." (Mark 9:23)
- It is not the thing believed in that hurts or harms you, but the belief or thought in your mind which creates the result.
- All your experiences, actions, and events are reflections and reactions to your own thought.

### Prayer Therapy as Combined Conscious and Subconscious Function
- Prayer therapy is the synchronized, harmonious, and intelligent function of the conscious and subconscious levels of mind specifically directed for a definite purpose.
- It is also called mental treatment or scientific prayer.
- You must know what you are doing and why you are doing it.
- You consciously choose an idea, mental picture, or plan you desire to experience.
- You realize your capacity to convey this idea to your subconscious by feeling the reality of the state assumed.
- As you remain faithful in your mental attitude, your prayer will be answered.

### The Role of Healers (Doctors, Surgeons, Practitioners)
- No mental or religious science practitioner, psychologist, psychiatrist, or medical doctor ever heals a patient.
- They remove mental, emotional, or physical blocks which inhibit the flow of the healing life principle.
- The surgeon removes the physical block enabling the healing currents to function normally.
- No physician, surgeon, or mental science practitioner claims that "he healed the patient."
- The one healing power does the healing.

### Faith Healing — True vs. Blind Faith
- Popular "faith healing" is not the faith mentioned in the Bible.
- True faith means knowledge of the interaction of the conscious and subconscious mind.
- A faith healer who heals without scientific understanding relies on the patient's blind belief.
- Any method which causes you to move from fear and worry to faith and expectancy will heal.
- Many people claim their personal theory is correct because it produces results — this cannot be true, as there is only one process of healing (faith).

### Blind Faith Explained
- Blind faith produces results without understanding the powers and forces involved.
- Historical example: Franz Anton Mesmer's magnetic fluid cures in 1776 were actually caused by the active imagination of the patients and powerful suggestion to their subconscious minds.
- Dr. Braid later showed that hypnotic sleep by suggestion could produce the same phenomena.
- All such cures were brought about by active imagination and powerful suggestion to the subconscious — termed blind faith because there was no understanding of how the cures were produced.

### The Meaning of Absent Treatment
- There is no absent treatment as opposed to present treatment because the universal mind is omnipresent.
- In the mind principle, there is no time or space.
- Your treatment is a conscious movement of thought — you do not try to send out thoughts or hold a thought.
- As you become conscious of the qualities of health, well-being, and relaxation, these qualities are resurrected in the experience of your loved one.

### Kinetic Action of the Subconscious Mind
- The kinetic action of the subconscious mind continues throughout your sleep period.
- Therefore, you should give the subconscious mind something good to work on before sleep.
- Naming your aches and symptoms inhibits the kinetic action and inhibits the release of healing power and energy.

## Best Practices

- Develop a definite plan for turning over your requests or desires to your subconscious mind.
- Imagine the end desired and feel its reality; follow it through for definite results.
- Decide what belief is: know that belief is a thought in your mind, and what you think you create.
- It is foolish to believe in sickness or something that will hurt or harm you.
- Believe in perfect health, prosperity, peace, wealth, and divine guidance.
- Great and noble thoughts upon which you habitually dwell become great acts.
- Apply the power of prayer therapy in your life: choose a plan, idea, or mental picture, and mentally and emotionally unite with that idea.
- Faith comes with understanding — if you want the power to heal, you can have it through faith, which means knowledge of the working of your conscious and subconscious mind.
- Learn to pray for your loved ones who may be ill.
- Cease talking about your ailments or giving them a name.
- Become a mental surgeon — your troubles will be cut off like dead branches are pruned from a tree.

## Common Pitfalls

- **Naming ailments:** If you are constantly naming your aches and symptoms, you inhibit the kinetic action of your subconscious mind, which means you inhibit the release of healing power and energy.
- **Giving power to negative conditions:** Refusing to admit that healing will not come — giving any power to negative conditions blocks the healing process.
- **Mentioning trouble by name:** When affirming health, never mention your trouble by name.
- **Blind faith without understanding:** Getting results without understanding the powers and forces involved is blind faith. While it can work, true and consistent results come from understanding the interaction of conscious and subconscious mind.
- **Active opposition from the conscious mind:** Resistance from the conscious mind blocks subjective faith. You must enter a state of passivity and receptivity to allow the subconscious to be controlled by suggestion.
- **Fear and worry:** Fear and worry charge negative thoughts in the subconscious mind and cause disease. Dwelling on them inhibits healing.
- **Focusing on appearances:** Closing the door to appearances is necessary to turn within and contact the healing presence.
