# Chapter IV - Mental Healings In Ancient Times
## Analysis: Actionable Lessons, Principles, and Techniques

---

## List of All Actionable Lessons with Step-by-Step Instructions

### Lesson 1: The Biblical Prayer Technique (Mark 11:24)
- Believe that you have already received what you desire, as though it is already completed and fulfilled.
- Eliminate from your mind all consideration of conditions, circumstances, or anything implying adverse contingencies.
- Plant the concept (seed) in your mind and leave it undisturbed.
- Trust that it will infallibly germinate into external fruition.

### Lesson 2: Developing Faith as a Way of Thinking
- Accept as true what your reason and senses may deny.
- Shut out the rational, analytical, conscious mind.
- Embrace an attitude of complete reliance on the inner power of your subconscious mind.
- Treat your thought, idea, plan, or purpose as already real on its own plane — as real as your hand or your heart.

### Lesson 3: The Seed Analogy for Manifestation
- Plant the idea (seed) in your mind.
- Water and fertilize it with expectancy.
- Do not disturb it — leave it to grow undisturbed.
- Trust that, like seeds in the ground, it will come forth after its kind.

### Lesson 4: Believing in the Reality of Your Ideas Now
- Believe in the reality of your idea, plan, or invention in the present moment.
- As you do, it will become manifest in physical reality.

### Lesson 5: Silent Inner Knowing for Healing Others
- When praying for or healing another, maintain a silent inner knowing of their wholeness, beauty, and perfection.
- This inner knowing and conviction changes negative, destructive patterns in the other person's subconscious mind.
- Wonderful results follow automatically from this internal mental change.

### Lesson 6: Understanding How Your Subconscious Mind Responds
- Recognize that your subconscious mind responds to whatever thought you hold in mind.
- The object of your faith does not need to be objectively real — whether real or false, your subconscious will respond to the thought in your mind.
- Treat faith simply as thought in your mind; that is sufficient to activate your subconscious.

### Lesson 7: The Daily Reminder Practice
- Remind yourself frequently that the healing power is in your own subconscious mind.

## Key Principles and Concepts

1. **The Subconscious Mind Is the Only Healer**: All healing — regardless of method, religion, or practitioner — flows through the subconscious mind. There is no other healing power.

2. **Faith Is the One Process of Healing**: Despite countless different theories and methods, there is only one process of healing, and that is faith — a confident expectancy or inner conviction.

3. **All Disease Originates in the Mind**: Nothing appears on the body unless there is a mental pattern corresponding to it. The cause of disease is mental, and therefore healing must also be mental.

4. **The Subconscious Mind Is Constantly Amenable to Suggestion**: The subconscious mind receives and acts upon whatever is suggested to it, whether from oneself or from another.

5. **The Subconscious Mind Governs All Bodily Functions**: The subconscious mind has complete control over the functions, conditions, and sensations of the body — from healing a cut to regulating temperature and organ function.

6. **Imagination + Faith = Healing**: The union of imagination aroused to an intense degree and confident expectancy is what brings about healing. The physical object or ritual used is irrelevant.

7. **All Healing Methods Share One Universal Power**: Whether through osteopathy, chiropractic, naturopathy, psychiatry, religious prayer, hypnotism, or shrine rituals — all healings operate through the same universal power resident in the subconscious mind.

8. **As a Man Thinketh in His Heart (Subconscious Mind), So Is He**: What is impressed upon the subconscious mind — the heart — becomes the lived reality of the individual.

9. **Faith Is Like a Seed**: Faith grows after its kind. Plant it, nourish it, and it will manifest — just as seeds planted in the ground grow according to their nature.

10. **The Authority of the Spoken Word**: When a healer speaks with authority, their command is an appeal to the subconscious mind of the patient, coupled with the healer's own absolute trust in the subconscious mind's response.

## Best Practices Mentioned

1. **Cultivate Blind Belief / Faith**: Develop a strong, unquestioning belief in the healing power of your subconscious mind. This is the primary tool for all healing.

2. **Arouse the Imagination**: Use rituals, symbols, stories, or ceremonies that fire the imagination to an intense degree. This amplifies the effectiveness of suggestion.

3. **Use the Power of Expectancy**: Water your mental seeds with expectancy. A confident expectancy acts as a powerful suggestion to the subconscious mind.

4. **Pray with the Feeling of Already Having Received**: When praying, adopt the feeling and conviction that your desire is already fulfilled. This impresses the subconscious mind most powerfully.

5. **Use Simple Faith**: Simple, childlike faith is potent. The girl at the Diabutsu shrine in Japan restored her voice through simple faith in Buddha, combined with ritual offerings and fasting.

6. **Speak with Authority When Healing Others**: When working to heal another, speak with inner knowing and authority — your conviction activates the patient's subconscious mind.

7. **Use Rituals to Condition the Mind**: If a ritual (offerings, fasting, touching a relic, etc.) helps kindle faith and expectancy in you or another, use it. The ritual itself is not the cause of healing, but it conditions the mind to belief.

8. **Leave Your Mental Seed Undisturbed**: Once you have planted a desired outcome in your subconscious mind through faith and belief, do not keep digging it up with doubt, fear, or analytical thinking.

---

## Common Pitfalls Mentioned

1. **Discussing Healings with Skeptics**: After receiving a healing, avoid telling others about it — particularly skeptical or unbelieving people — because their criticisms can deposit thoughts of fear, doubt, and anxiety in your subconscious mind, undoing the benefits of the healing.

2. **Analytical, Rational Thinking During the Healing Process**: The conscious mind's tendency to analyze, question, or consider adverse conditions is a barrier to faith. The small, rational, analytical conscious mind must be shut out when impressing the subconscious.

3. **Confusing the Method with the Cause of Healing**: Many groups claim their specific theory, ritual, or method is responsible for the healing. This is a mistake — it is always faith acting upon the subconscious mind that produces the result, not the external form or theory used.

4. **Doubting or Disturbing the Planted Seed**: After planting a desire or healing concept in the subconscious, continually questioning it, worrying about conditions, or entertaining adverse contingencies prevents the seed from germinating and manifesting.

5. **Reliance on the External Object Rather Than Inner Belief**: Attributing healing to an amulet, relic, ring, or statue rather than to one's own imagination and faith. The external object is merely a tool to stimulate belief — it has no power in itself.

## Techniques and Methods Described

### Technique 1: Biblical Prayer / Mental Impress Technique (Mark 11:24)
- **What it does**: Impresses a desired outcome upon the subconscious mind so that it becomes real and manifests in physical reality.
- **Steps**:
  1. Identify the desire you wish to manifest or the healing you seek.
  2. Believe and accept as true that the desire has already been accomplished and fulfilled — speak in the past tense and feel it as already done.
  3. Eliminate all consideration of conditions, circumstances, or adverse possibilities from your mind.
  4. Plant the concept (seed) in your mind and leave it undisturbed.
  5. Trust and expect that it will germinate into external fruition in its own time.
- **Key requirement**: Faith — an inner conviction and expectancy that the thought is already a fact in your mind.

### Technique 2: The Jesus Healing Method (Matthew 9:28-30 / Luke 4:36)
- **What it does**: Effectively heals through appealing directly to the patient's subconscious mind.
- **Steps**:
  1. Establish faith in the patient — ask the patient if they believe you are able to do this (or otherwise awaken their expectancy).
  2. Speak with inner knowing, conviction, and authority — decree the healing inwardly as already true.
  3. Appeal to the cooperation of the patient's subconscious mind through your command.
  4. Instruct the healed person not to discuss the healing with others, to avoid depositing doubt in their subconscious.
- **Key requirement**: The healer's own silent inner knowing and absolute trust in the response of the subconscious mind.

### Technique 3: Hypnotic Suggestion (Demonstrated by Bernheim)
- **What it does**: Demonstrates and utilizes the power of suggestion upon the subconscious mind to induce or remove physical conditions.
- **Steps**:
  1. Place the subject in a hypnotic or highly receptive state.
  2. Give a direct, clear suggestion — whether for healing (restoring speech, movement) or for inducing a physical symptom (producing a blister, causing a sneeze, inducing allergic symptoms).
  3. The subject's subconscious mind acts on the suggestion and produces the corresponding physical result.
- **Key principle demonstrated**: Symptoms of disease can be induced by suggestion, proving the cause of disease is mental — and healing is therefore also mental.

### Technique 4: Ritual and Symbolic Methods (Ancient Temples, Shrines)
- **What it does**: Uses symbolic actions, offerings, and ceremonies to fire the imagination and arouse expectancy, thereby activating the subconscious mind's healing power.
- **Steps** (as practiced historically):
  1. Perform a ritual or procedure that is meaningful and emotionally engaging to the patient (e.g., mixing substances under the crescent moon, making offerings at a shrine, touching a relic).
  2. Tell the patient that the ritual, object, or deity will heal them during sleep or through the ritual itself.
  3. The patient's aroused imagination and expectancy condition their subconscious mind to believe in the healing.
  4. The subconscious mind responds and produces the physical healing.
- **Key principle**: The ritual itself has no inherent power — it is the suggestion and aroused faith that activates the subconscious.

### Technique 5: The Use of a Substitute or Symbolic Object (Case of the Tuberculosis Patient)
- **What it does**: Uses a false or symbolic object to stimulate the patient's imagination and faith to an intense degree, triggering a powerful subconscious healing response.
- **Steps**:
  1. Present the patient with an object believed to have healing power (even if it is not genuinely what it is claimed to be — e.g., a splinter of wood presented as a piece of the true cross).
  2. Tell the patient that touching or using the object will bring healing.
  3. Inflame and fire the patient's imagination to the point of intense expectancy.
  4. Allow the patient to interact with the object in faith (e.g., placing it over the chest, praying silently).
  5. The union of aroused imagination and confident expectancy activates the subconscious mind, producing healing.
- **Warning**: Never reveal the trick or deception to the patient afterward, as this could deposit doubt and cause a relapse.

### Technique 6: Silent Inner Knowing for Healing Others
- **What it does**: Changes negative patterns in another person's subconscious mind through the healer's own silent, inner conviction of the person's wholeness and perfection.
- **Steps**:
  1. Enter a state of silent inner knowing — feel and know inwardly that the person is already whole, beautiful, and perfect.
  2. Hold this conviction firmly in your own mind, free of doubt or consideration of current conditions.
  3. Your inner knowing and conviction acts as a suggestion to the other person's subconscious mind.
  4. The negative, destructive patterns in their subconscious are changed, and healing follows automatically.
