## Actionable Lessons with Step-by-Step Instructions

### 1. Autosuggestion Technique for Banishing Fear
Use this to overcome fear, anxiety, or stage fright:
1. Isolate yourself in a room three times a day.
2. Sit down comfortably in an armchair.
3. Relax your body and close your eyes.
4. Still your mind and body as best you can.
5. State your counteracting suggestion positively and with feeling (e.g., "I sing beautifully, I am poised, serene, confident and calm.").
6. Repeat the statement slowly, quietly, and with feeling from five to ten times at each sitting.
7. Conduct three sittings every day and one immediately prior to sleep.
8. Continue until the desired mental state is established.

### 2. Induced Autosuggestion for Memory Improvement
Use this to restore or enhance memory:
1. Practice induced autosuggestion several times a day.
2. State specifically: "My memory from today on is improving in every department. I shall always remember whatever I need to know at every moment of time and point of space. The impressions received will be clearer and more definite. I shall retain them automatically and with ease. Whatever I wish to recall will immediately present itself in the correct form in my mind. I am improving rapidly every day, and very soon my memory will be better than it has ever been before."
3. Repeat with belief until results manifest.

### 3. Autosuggestion for Overcoming Bad Temper or Irritability
1. Repeat the following statements three or four times a day - morning, noon, and night prior to sleep for about a month:
   "Henceforth, I shall grow more good humored. Joy, happiness and cheerfulness are now becoming my normal states of mind. Every day I am becoming more and more lovable and understanding. I am now becoming the center of cheer and good will to all those about me, infecting them with good humor. This happy, joyous and cheerful mood is now becoming my normal, natural state of mind. I am grateful."

### 4. Counteracting Negative Suggestions (Heterosuggestion)
1. Monitor the negative autosuggestions people direct at you daily.
2. Recognize you do not have to be influenced by destructive heterosuggestion.
3. Reject negative suggestions consciously.
4. Replace them immediately with constructive autosuggestions for your subconscious mind.
5. Remember: suggestions from others have no power except the power you give them through your own thoughts.

### 5. Establishing a Major Premise for Life Direction
1. Formulate an overall picture of the way you want your life to be.
2. State a major premise that reflects this desired state (example: "Infinite Intelligence leads and guides me in all my ways. Perfect health is mine, and the Law of Harmony operates in my mind and body. Beauty, love, peace and abundance are mine. The principle of right action and divine order govern my entire life.").
3. Repeat the statement slowly, quietly, and lovingly several times a day.
4. Know that it is sinking deep down into your subconscious mind.
5. Trust that results must follow.

### 6. Overcoming Frustration, Mental Blocks, and Confusion
When seeking an answer to a problem or feeling blocked:
1. Still the wheels of your mind.
2. Relax and let go.
3. Quietly affirm: "My subconscious knows the answer. It is responding to me now. I give thanks because I know the infinite intelligence of my subconscious knows all things and is revealing the perfect answer to me now. My real conviction is now setting free the majesty and glory of my subconscious mind. I rejoice that it is so."
4. Avoid saying: "I don't think there is any way out; I am all mixed up and confused; why don't I get an answer?" (This neutralizes your prayer).

### 7. Overcoming Negative Mental Programming from the Past
1. Recognize negative suggestions accepted in infancy, childhood, or teens.
2. Use constructive autosuggestion as a means of releasing yourself from mass negative verbal conditioning.
3. Recondition your mind with new, harmonious, constructive thoughts frequently repeated.

## Key Principles and Concepts

1. **Two Levels of Mind**: The conscious (rational) mind and the subconscious (irrational) mind are two spheres of activity within one mind.
2. **Subconscious as Creative Mind**: The subconscious mind is the seat of emotions and the creative mind. It creates according to the nature of your habitual thoughts.
3. **Law of Subconscious Response**: Whatever your conscious mind assumes and believes to be true, your subconscious mind will accept and bring to pass. This law works equally for good and bad ideas.
4. **Subconscious Does Not Reason**: Your subconscious mind does not argue, reason, make comparisons, or prove whether thoughts are true or false. It simply accepts and responds.
5. **Subconscious as Soil Analogy**: The subconscious mind is like soil that accepts any seed - good or bad. Your thoughts are the seeds.
6. **Watchman at the Gate**: Your conscious mind is the watchman at the gate, whose chief function is to protect the subconscious mind from false impressions.
7. **Objective vs. Subjective Mind**: The conscious mind is objective (deals with outward objects via five senses and reasoning). The subconscious mind is subjective (perceives by intuition, independent of the five senses, seat of memory and emotion).
8. **Mind Works Like a Syllogism**: Whatever major premise your conscious mind assumes to be true determines the conclusion your subconscious mind comes to regarding any question or problem.
9. **Habit Formation**: Habitual thinking of the conscious mind establishes deep grooves in the subconscious mind, which is the seat of habit.
10. **Suggestion Power**: The subconscious mind is amenable to suggestion. A suggestion has no power in and of itself except it is accepted mentally by you.
11. **Different Reactions to Same Suggestion**: People react differently to the same suggestion based on their subconscious conditioning or existing beliefs.
12. **Subconscious Life Functions**: Automatic processes like heartbeat, breathing, digestion, and circulation are carried on by the subconscious mind without conscious control.
13. **Subconscious Tends Lifeward**: The inherent tendency of the subconscious is to preserve and take care of you.
14. **Inner Assumptions Rule Life**: Each person's inner fears, beliefs, and opinions rule and govern their lives.

## Best Practices Mentioned

1. **Guard Your Thoughts**: Select thoughts, ideas, and premises which bless, heal, inspire, and fill your soul with joy.
2. **Watch Your Words**: You have to account for every idle word. Never say: "I will fail; I will lose my job; I can't pay the rent."
3. **Choose Your Responses**: You have the power to choose how you will react to the suggestions and statements of others.
4. **Reject "I Can't"**: Never say "I can't." Overcome that fear by substituting: "I can do all things through the power of my subconscious mind."
5. **Choose Life-Affirming Statements**: Choose life! Choose love! Choose health! Choose happiness! Choose abundance!
6. **Think Independently**: Do not let others do your thinking for you. Choose your own thoughts and make your own decisions.
7. **Think from Eternal Truths**: Begin to think from the standpoint of the eternal truths and principles of life, not from fear, ignorance, and superstition.
8. **Believe Good is Happening**: Choose to believe that something good can happen and is happening now.
9. **Cultivate Positive Qualities**: Choose to be friendly, cooperative, joyous, and lovable. This is the best way to develop a wonderful personality.
10. **Use Constructive Autosuggestion**: Practice constructive autosuggestion to counteract negative verbal conditioning from childhood and daily life.
11. **Regularly Check Your Language**: Monitor the negative autosuggestions that people make to you and consciously reject destructive heterosuggestion.
12. **Give the Right Request**: If you want the subconscious to work for you, give it the right request and attain its cooperation.
13. **Formulate a Positive Major Premise**: Establish a major premise in your thinking based on eternal truths and repeat it daily.
14. **Relax for Receptivity**: Physical inertia favors mental passivity and renders the mind more receptive to suggestion.

## Common Pitfalls Mentioned

1. **Negative Autosuggestions from Childhood**: From infancy on, most people receive negative suggestions from parents, friends, relatives, teachers, and associates which are unconsciously accepted and shape behavior patterns.
2. **Fear of Failure**: Involuntary autosuggestions like silent fear thoughts emotionalized and subjectified manifest as actual failure experiences.
3. **Accepting False Suggestions**: The subconscious mind cannot take a joke. If you consciously assume something false as true, your subconscious will accept it and bring about results accordingly.
4. **Heterosuggestion Control**: Allowing others' suggestions to control your thinking, feeling, and acting in ways that serve their advantage rather than your own.
5. **Self-Blocking**: Setting up obstacles, impediments, and delays in your conscious mind denies the wisdom resident in your subconscious and brings lack, limitation, and frustration.
6. **Fear and Expectation of Negative Outcomes**: Dwelling on fear (e.g., fear of illness, fear of death) can manifest the very outcome feared, as illustrated by the man who died after a fortune teller predicted his death.
7. **Superstition and Ignorance**: Not knowing the laws of your mind leaves you vulnerable to destructive suggestions from fortune tellers, crystal gazers, and negative influences.
8. **Mental and Emotional Congestion**: Arguing with your subconscious or saying "I don't think there is any way out" neutralizes your ability to receive answers.
9. **Idle Words**: Saying things like "I can't afford it," "I will fail," or "I'm too old now" impregnates the subconscious with negative thoughts.
10. **Letting Others Do Your Thinking**: Allowing others to shape your beliefs and decisions rather than choosing your own thoughts.

## Techniques and Methods Described

1. **Autosuggestion (Self-Suggestion)**
   - Definition: Suggesting something definite and specific to oneself.
   - Purpose: Banish fear, overcome negative conditions, recondition the mind.
   - Method: Quiet, slow repetition with feeling, several times daily.

2. **Induced Autosuggestion**
   - Definition: Deliberately planting specific positive suggestions in the subconscious mind.
   - Purpose: Restore memory, improve mental faculties, change habits.
   - Method: Repeated affirmation of specific desired outcomes with conviction.

3. **Constructive Autosuggestion (Reconditioning Therapy)**
   - Definition: Using positive autosuggestion to counteract negative verbal conditioning from the past.
   - Purpose: Release yourself from mass negative conditioning, develop good habits.
   - Method: Regular repetition of constructive, harmonious thoughts.

4. **Heterosuggestion**
   - Definition: Suggestions from another person.
   - Context: Can be used to discipline and control others, but also has destructive potential.
   - Awareness: Recognize when you are receiving negative heterosuggestion and reject it consciously.

5. **Major Premise Formulation**
   - Definition: Establishing a fundamental truth or belief in your conscious mind that guides your subconscious conclusions.
   - Purpose: Direct your life in a specific desired direction.
   - Method: Write or state a comprehensive positive premise about your life and repeat it daily.

6. **Physical Relaxation for Mental Receptivity**
   - Principle: Physical inertia favors mental passivity and renders the mind more receptive to suggestion.
   - Application: Relax the body completely before engaging in autosuggestion.

7. **Quiet Affirmation for Problem Solving**
   - Purpose: Receive answers from the subconscious mind.
   - Method: Still the mind, relax, let go, and quietly affirm that the subconscious knows the answer and is revealing it now.

8. **The Syllogism Method**
   - Principle: Your mind works like logical deduction. The major premise determines the conclusion.
   - Application: Establish a true and positive major premise, and your subconscious will automatically draw the right conclusions in all areas of life.

## Notable Examples and Illustrations

1. **The Singer Who Overcame Stage Fright**: A young singer used autosuggestion three times daily plus before sleep, repeating "I sing beautifully, I am poised, serene, confident and calm," and transformed from fearful to confident within a week.

2. **The 75-Year-Old Woman Who Restored Her Memory**: Used induced autosuggestion with specific memory-affirming statements several times daily and recovered normal memory within three weeks.

3. **The Man Who Lost His Arm to Save His Daughter**: A man suggested to his subconscious for two years, "I would give my right arm to see my daughter cured." After a car accident took his right arm, his daughter's arthritis and skin disease vanished. Lesson: The subconscious takes you at your word and cannot take a joke.

4. **The Sailor vs. The Timid Passenger**: Same suggestion of seasickness produced opposite results based on existing subconscious conditioning. The sailor's immunity made him laugh; the timid passenger accepted fear and became seasick.

5. **The Man Killed by a Crystal Ball Gazer's Prediction**: A healthy man accepted a fortune teller's prediction of death at the next new moon, dwelt on it constantly, and died as predicted. His own thought, not the fortune teller's power, caused his death. Had he known the laws of his mind, he could have neutralized the suggestion completely.

## Summary of Core Law

**The Law of Your Mind**: You will get a reaction or response from your subconscious mind according to the nature of the thought or idea you hold in your conscious mind.

You give the command or decree, and your subconscious will faithfully reproduce the idea impressed upon it. The only thing necessary is to get your conscious mind to accept your idea, and the law of your own subconscious mind will bring forth the health, peace, or position you desire.
