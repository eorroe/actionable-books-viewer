# Chapter III: The Miracle-Working Power of Your Subconscious

## Chapter Title and Number
**Chapter III - The Miracle-Working Power of Your Subconscious**

---

## Actionable Lessons with Step-by-Step Instructions

### 1. How to Use Your Subconscious Mind to Achieve Specific Goals
- **Step 1:** Prior to going to sleep, plainly state to your subconscious mind the specific thing you wish to accomplish.
- **Step 2:** Be clear and specific about the desired result.
- **Step 3:** Trust that forces within you will be "released" leading to the desired result.
- **Step 4:** Expect the miracle-working power to respond.

### 2. How to Restore Normal Body Functions When Mentally Disturbed
- **Step 1:** When experiencing worry, anxiety, fear, or depression that interferes with bodily functions, let go and relax.
- **Step 2:** Still the wheels of your thought processes.
- **Step 3:** Speak to your subconscious mind with authority and conviction, telling it to take over in peace, harmony, and divine order.
- **Step 4:** Trust that all functions of your body will become normal again.

### 3. How to Convey the Idea of Perfect Health to Your Subconscious Mind (Relaxation Method)
- **Step 1:** Several times a day, make certain you are completely relaxed mentally and physically.
- **Step 2:** Relax your body by speaking to it: "My heart and lungs are relaxed, my head is relaxed, my whole being is completely relaxed."
- **Step 3:** After about five minutes, when you are in a sleepy, drowsy state, affirm: "The perfection of God is now being expressed through me. The idea of perfect health is now filling my subconscious mind. The image God has of me is a perfect image, and my subconscious mind recreates my body in perfect accordance with the perfect image held in the mind of God."
- **Step 4:** Repeat this affirmation regularly until the healing manifests.

### 4. How to Use Disciplined/Scientific Imagination for Healing
- **Step 1:** Make a vivid mental picture of yourself as already healed and performing the activities you would do if healed.
- **Step 2:** Live the role and actually feel yourself back in the situation (e.g., walking in your office, touching the desk, answering the telephone).
- **Step 3:** Know that you are giving your subconscious mind something definite to work upon.
- **Step 4:** Condition your mind frequently with this mental picture over several weeks.
- **Step 5:** When the subconscious mind accepts the mental imagery, the healing will follow automatically.

### 5. How to Keep Your Conscious Mind Aligned for Success
- **Step 1:** Keep your conscious mind busy with the expectation of the best.
- **Step 2:** Make sure the thoughts you habitually think are based on whatsoever things are lovely, true, just, and of good report.
- **Step 3:** Take care of your conscious mind, knowing in your heart and soul that your subconscious mind is always expressing, reproducing, and manifesting according to your habitual thinking.

### 6. How to Claim the Healing Presence Within
- **Step 1:** Claim that the healing presence in your subconscious is flowing through you as harmony, health, peace, joy, and abundance.
- **Step 2:** Think of it as a living intelligence, a lovely companion on the way.
- **Step 3:** Firmly believe it is continually flowing through you, vivifying, inspiring, and prospering you.
- **Step 4:** Trust that it will respond exactly as you believe.

### 7. How to Use Prayer for Healing (Personal Example)
- **Step 1:** Recognize that your body and all its organs were created by infinite intelligence in your subconscious mind.
- **Step 2:** Acknowledge that your subconscious mind knows how to heal you and fashioned all your organs, tissues, muscles, and bones.
- **Step 3:** Affirm: "This infinite healing presence within me is now transforming every atom of my being, making me whole and perfect now."
- **Step 4:** Give thanks for the healing you know is taking place now.
- **Step 5:** Pray aloud for about five minutes, two or three times a day, repeating the prayer.
- **Step 6:** Continue until the healing manifests (example: about three months for skin malignancy).

## Key Principles and Concepts

1. **Your subconscious mind is always working** - It is active night and day, whether you act upon it or not.

2. **The subconscious mind never sleeps and never rests** - It is always on the job.

3. **What you write on the inside, you will experience on the outside** - Your thoughts become your experiences.

4. **Your subconscious cannot argue** - It acts only from what you write on it. It accepts your conscious mind's verdict as final.

5. **The Law of Action and Reaction** - Your thought is action, and the reaction is the automatic response of your subconscious mind to your thought.

6. **As above, so below; as within, so without** - Whatever is impressed in your subconscious mind is expressed on the screen of space as conditions, experiences, and events.

7. **Motion and emotion must balance** - As in heaven (your own mind), so on earth (in your body and environment).

8. **The impression and expression must be equal** - All frustration is due to unfulfilled desire.

9. **Your subconscious mind is one with infinite intelligence and boundless wisdom** - It is fed by hidden springs and is called the law of life.

10. **Belief is a thought in the subconscious mind** - It means to accept something as true. The thought accepted executes itself automatically.

11. **Whatsoever you feel as true subjectively is expressed as conditions, experiences, and events.**

12. **According to your belief is it done unto you.**

13. **It is done unto you as you believe.**

14. **The Life Principle flows through you according to the nature of your thoughts** - Just as water takes the shape of the pipe it flows through.

15. **Your subconscious mind controls all vital processes and functions of your body** - It started your heartbeat, controls circulation, digestion, assimilation, and elimination.

16. **Your subconscious mind is the builder of your body** - It transmutes food into tissue, muscle, bone, and blood.

17. **The solar plexus is called the brain of your mind** - When your conscious mind accepts a thought completely, it is sent to the solar plexus where it becomes flesh and is made manifest in your experience.

18. **Your vitality, body, financial status, friends, and social status represent a perfect reflection of the idea you have of yourself.**

19. **Negative thoughts generate destructive emotions** - These emotions, being of a negative nature, are frequently expressed as ulcers, heart trouble, tension, and anxieties.

20. **Feed your subconscious mind life-giving thoughts** - You will wipe out all the negative patterns lodged therein. As you continue to do this, all the past will be wiped out and remembered no more.

21. **The creative principle that created can recreate** - What the creative principle created, it can recreate (e.g., restoring dead optic nerves).

## Best Practices Mentioned

1. **Watch your thoughts carefully** - Whatever thoughts, beliefs, opinions, theories, or dogmas you write on your subconscious mind, you shall experience them as objective manifestations of circumstances, conditions, and events.

2. **Impress your subconscious with right ideas and constructive thoughts** - Whatever you impress upon your subconscious mind, the latter will move heaven and earth to bring it to pass.

3. **Use simple, repetitive prayer** - Pray aloud for about five minutes, two or three times a day, repeating the prayer.

4. **Speak to your subconscious mind with authority and conviction** - It will conform to your command.

5. **Use the relaxation method before affirming** - Enter a sleepy, drowsy state before giving suggestions to your subconscious mind.

6. **Use vivid mental imagery** - Create detailed mental pictures of the desired outcome and live the role.

7. **Practice scientific or disciplined imagination** - Use your imagination to impress the idea of health or the desired outcome on your subconscious mind.

8. **Give thanks in advance for the healing** - Thank your subconscious for the healing that is already taking place.

9. **Let go and relax when mentally disturbed** - Still the wheels of your thought processes and let your subconscious take over.

10. **Trust completely in the wisdom of your subconscious** - Entrust yourself entirely to its wonder-working power.

11. **Focus on the happy ending** - Imagine the happy ending or solution to your problem, feel the thrill of accomplishment, and what you imagine and feel will be accepted by your subconscious mind.

## Common Pitfalls Mentioned

1. **Negative thinking** - Thinking negatively, destructively, and viciously generates destructive emotions that manifest as physical ailments (ulcers, heart trouble, tension, anxieties).

2. **Worry, anxiety, fear, and depression** - These interfere with the normal rhythm of the heart, lungs, stomach, and intestines by disrupting the harmonious functioning of the subconscious mind.

3. **Getting angry, fearful, jealous, or vengeful** - These are poisons that enter your subconscious mind and injure yourself.

4. **Dwelling on obstacles, delays, and difficulties** - Your subconscious mind responds accordingly, and you block your own good.

5. **Lack of understanding of the interaction between conscious and subconscious minds** - This causes chaos and misery in the world.

6. **Not using the right ideas and constructive thoughts** - Failing to impress your subconscious with the right ideas prevents miracles from happening.

7. **Conscious interference with subconscious processes** - Your conscious mind can interfere with the normal rhythm of bodily functions through negative thought patterns.

## Techniques and Methods Described

- State your specific request to your subconscious mind prior to sleep.
- Trust that forces within you will be released to bring about the desired result.

### 2. The Relaxation Method

- Enter a sleepy, drowsy state.
- Affirm the desired truth in this relaxed state.

- Pray aloud for about five minutes, two or three times a day.

- Give thanks for the healing already taking place.

- Live the role and feel yourself in that situation.

- Condition your mind frequently with this mental picture over time.

### 5. The Authority Method
- Speak to your subconscious mind with authority and conviction.
- Command it to take over in peace, harmony, and divine order.
- Trust that it will conform to your command.

### 6. The Claiming Method
- Claim that the healing presence in your subconscious is flowing through you as harmony, health, peace, joy, and abundance.
- Think of it as a living intelligence.
- Firmly believe it is continually flowing through you, vivifying, inspiring, and prospering you.

### 7. The Mental Movie Method (Implied)

- Watch this mental movie repeatedly to impress the idea on your subconscious.

### 8. The Book of Life Concept
- Recognize that you are always writing on the "book of life" through your thoughts.
- Whatever you write on the inside (your subconscious), you will experience on the outside (your reality).
