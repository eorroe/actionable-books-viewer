# Analysis: Chapter VII - The Tendency of the Subconscious is Lifeward

## Chapter Title and Number

**Chapter VII: The Tendency of the Subconscious is Lifeward**

---

## Actionable Lessons with Step-by-Step Instructions

### 1. Charge the Subconscious Before Sleep (The Stevenson Method)
- **Step 1**: Before going to sleep, mentally charge your subconscious with a specific task.
- **Step 2**: Ask your subconscious to provide a solution, story, or answer to a specific problem.
- **Step 3**: Trust that your deeper mind will work on the request during sleep.
- **Step 4**: Receive the answer piece by piece upon waking or in subsequent days.

### 2. The Affirmation Practice (Frederick Elias Andrews Method)
- **Step 1**: Create a comprehensive personal affirmation covering desired qualities (e.g., "I am whole, perfect, strong, powerful, loving, harmonious and happy").
- **Step 2**: Repeat the affirmation many times throughout the day.
- **Step 3**: Make it the last utterance on your lips at night before sleep.
- **Step 4**: Make it the first thought in the morning upon waking.
- **Step 5**: When thoughts of fear, anger, jealousy, or envy arise, immediately start the counteracting affirmation in your mind.
- **Step 6**: Persevere consistently; faith and repetition will produce results.

### 3. Drowsy State Visualization Technique (For Healing or Specific Outcomes)
- **Step 1**: Each night before sleep, enter a drowsy, meditative state (the condition akin to sleep).
- **Step 2**: Immobilize and focus your attention on the specific issue or desired outcome.
- **Step 3**: Imagine a trusted authority figure (e.g., a doctor) present before you.
- **Step 4**: Imagine hearing or plainly hearing the authority figure confirming your desired result (e.g., "A miracle has happened!").
- **Step 5**: Repeat the imagined message over and over for approximately five minutes before sleep.
- **Step 6**: Continue this practice consistently (e.g., for three weeks or until the result manifests).

### 4. Thought Substitution Technique
- **Step 1**: Become aware when a negative thought arises.
- **Step 2**: Immediately substitute the negative thought with a positive, good thought.
- **Step 3**: Use the analogy: darkness is overcome with light, cold with heat, negative thought with good thought.
- **Step 4**: Affirm the good; the bad will vanish as a result.

### 5. Eliminate Destructive Thoughts to Increase Vital Force
- **Step 1**: Identify thoughts of fear, worry, anxiety, jealousy, hatred, and other destructive emotions.
- **Step 2**: Actively eliminate these thoughts from your consciousness.
- **Step 3**: By removing these thoughts, you allow the vital forces of your subconscious mind to increase in flow and distribution throughout your system.

### 6. Send Thoughts of Love and Health to Others
- **Step 1**: Direct thoughts of love and health toward others.
- **Step 2**: Maintain this attitude consistently.
- **Step 3**: Understand that this attitude of mind will return to you multiplied many times.

## Key Principles and Concepts

1. **Over ninety percent of mental life is subconscious.** Those who fail to use this power live within very narrow limits.

2. **The subconscious is always lifeward and constructive.** Its impulses and ideas are always directed toward growth, advancement, and preservation.

3. **The subconscious is the builder and maintainer of the body.** It operates twenty-four hours a day, never sleeps, and constantly works to help and preserve you from harm.

4. **The subconscious is in touch with infinite life and boundless wisdom.** Great aspirations, inspirations, and visions for a nobler life spring from the subconscious.

5. **Profoundest convictions come from the subconscious.** These cannot be argued about rationally because they do not originate from the conscious mind.

6. **The subconscious communicates through intuitions, impulses, hunches, intimations, urges, and ideas.** It constantly tells you to rise, transcend, grow, advance, adventure, and move forward.

7. **The cerebrospinal nervous system is the organ of the conscious mind.** It receives conscious perception through the five physical senses and controls voluntary body movement.

8. **The sympathetic nervous system is the organ of the subconscious mind.** Its center is the solar plexus (the "abdominal brain"), and it unconsciously supports vital body functions.

9. **The vagus nerve connects the conscious and subconscious systems.** It forms a physical link between the voluntary and sympathetic systems, making the human being a single entity physically and mentally.

10. **Every thought accepted as true is sent to the solar plexus.** From there, it is made into flesh and brought forth into the world as reality.

11. **The subconscious has its own will and acts continuously.** It works night and day whether or not you consciously act upon it.

12. **The innate principle of harmony.** The subconscious has a life of its own that is always moving toward harmony, health, and peace. This is the divine norm within.

13. **To think correctly is to be in harmony with Infinite Intelligence.** Every thought or action not in harmony results in discord and limitation.

14. **The body is rebuilt every eleven months.** You are physically only eleven months old at any given time.

15. **You are the sum total of your own thoughts.** Your habitual thinking patterns determine your physical reality.

16. **Normal conditions can be restored with greater ease than abnormal conditions can be induced.** The innate life principle seeks to preserve and protect you.

17. **It is normal to be healthy, vital, and strong. It is abnormal to be sick.** Being sick means going against the stream of life and thinking negatively.

18. **The law of life is the law of growth.** Where there is growth and expression, there is life; where there is life, there is harmony; where there is harmony, there is perfect health.

19. **Thoughts not in accord with harmony cause disease.** Fear, false beliefs, and negative patterns registered in the subconscious cause the subconscious to act on those "blueprint specifications."

20. **Faith in the subconscious healing power makes you whole.** The subconscious made your body and contains the perfect pattern for healing it.

21. **What you affirm consciously and feel as true will be made manifest.** This applies to your mind, body, and affairs.

## Best Practices Mentioned

1. **Watch your thoughts carefully.** Every thought accepted as true is sent to your solar plexus and becomes your reality.

2. **Feed your subconscious mind with true premises.** Your job is with your conscious mind; ensure it only receives constructive, lifeward ideas.

3. **Remake yourself by giving a new blueprint to your subconscious mind.** You have the power to redesign your life through conscious thought.

4. **Persevere in affirmation.** Faith and repetition pay off with big dividends.

5. **Use the drowsy, meditative state before sleep.** This is an ideal time to impress the subconscious mind.

6. **Affirm the good to make the bad vanish.** Rather than fighting negative thoughts directly, substitute them with positive affirmations.

7. **Send thoughts of love and health to others.** This creates a multiplier effect that returns to you.

---

## Common Pitfalls Mentioned

1. **Interfering with the subconscious's life-giving patterns through negative thinking.** Fear, worry, anxiety, and destructive thoughts disrupt the body's natural building processes.

2. **The conscious mind interfering based on five-sense evidence.** Relying on outer appearances leads to false belief, fears, and mere opinion, which override the subconscious's wisdom.

3. **Entertaining negative thoughts and imagery.** These are sent to the solar plexus and manifested as physical reality.

4. **Persisting in thoughts not in harmony with the principle of harmony.** Fear, anger, jealousy, and ill will cling to you, harass you, worry you, and finally bring about disease or death if persisted in.

5. **Building defects into your body.** Thoughts of fear, anger, jealousy, and ill will rebuild defects into your physical form every eleven months.

6. **Tearing down nerves and glands.** Thoughts of jealousy, fear, worry, and anxiety destroy the body tissue that controls elimination of waste, leading to mental and physical diseases.

7. **Going against the stream of life by thinking negatively.** This causes discord and limitation of all kinds.

## Techniques and Methods Described

### 1. Pre-Sleep Programming
- Method: Charge the subconscious with a specific task or question before sleep.
- Purpose: To receive answers, stories, solutions, or creative inspiration during sleep.
- Example: Robert Louis Stevenson asked his subconscious to evolve marketable thrillers when his bank account was low.

### 2. Affirmation
- Method: Repeatedly state positive, lifeward declarations about oneself.
- Purpose: To impress the subconscious mind with desired qualities and outcomes, causing them to manifest in body and affairs.
- Key elements: Repetition, faith, perseverance, feeling the truth of the words.

### 3. Drowsy-State Visualization with Imagined Authority
- Method: Enter a drowsy, meditative state before sleep; visualize an authority figure confirming your healing or desired outcome.
- Purpose: To bypass conscious doubt and impress the subconscious with a desired result.
- Key elements: Attention immobilization, imagined sensory experience (hearing), repetition over time.

### 4. Thought Substitution
- Method: When a negative thought arises, immediately replace it with a positive thought.
- Purpose: To prevent the subconscious from accepting and manifesting negative patterns.
- Principle: You cannot overcome darkness with more darkness; you must substitute light.

### 5. Counteracting Affirmation
- Method: When negative emotions arise, immediately begin the counteracting affirmation in the mind.
- Purpose: To prevent negative patterns from registering in the subconscious and to restore harmony.

### 6. Sending Thoughts of Love and Health (Prayer for Others)
- Method: Direct loving, healthy thoughts toward other people.
- Purpose: This creates a positive mental state that returns multiplied to the sender.

## Summary

This chapter teaches that the subconscious mind is inherently lifeward, constructive, and always working to preserve and protect you. The key to harnessing this power lies in conscious thought management: feeding the subconscious only true, harmonious, and positive ideas through techniques such as affirmation, pre-sleep programming, and thought substitution. The chapter emphasizes that you are the architect of your physical reality through your habitual thoughts, and that normal health is the natural state that can be restored by aligning conscious thinking with the innate principle of harmony within the subconscious.
