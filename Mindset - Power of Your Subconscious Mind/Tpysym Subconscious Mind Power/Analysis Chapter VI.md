# Analysis: Chapter VI — Practical Techniques in Mental Healing

## Chapter Title and Number

**Chapter VI: Practical Techniques In Mental Healing**

This chapter presents a systematic framework for working with the subconscious mind through prayer, mental imagery, and specific techniques to produce healing and desired outcomes in life.

---

## List of All Actionable Lessons with Step-by-Step Instructions

### 1. The Passing-Over Technique for Impregnating the Subconscious

**Steps:**
1. Recognize that your deeper mind contains infinite intelligence and infinite power.
2. Calmly think over what you want — form a clear desire or request.
3. Mentally see it coming into fuller fruition from this moment forward.
4. Enter a reverie-like (semi-drowsy, relaxed) state to facilitate the transfer.
5. Allow your conscious request to pass over into your subconscious mind.
6. Use complete simplicity and naivety — do not over-intellectualize the process.

**Example given:** A young girl with a cough and sore throat declared firmly, "It is passing away now. It is passing away now." It cleared up within an hour.

---

### 2. The Mental Blueprint Principle (Building Your Mental Home)

**Steps:**
1. Recognize that your thoughts, ideas, and mental imagery are your blueprint for life.
2. Audit your current mental blueprint — identify patterns of fear, worry, anxiety, or lack.
3. Intentionally replace negative patterns with positive ones: health, joy, harmony, peace, abundance.
4. Build silently by realizing your desired state in the present moment.
5. Dwelling on and claiming positive qualities allows the subconscious mind to accept the new blueprint and bring these things to pass.

**Key insight:** "By their fruits ye shall know them" — your results reveal your mental blueprint.

---

### 3. The Science and Art of True Prayer

**Steps:**
1. Formulate a clear mental picture or idea of what you desire before praying.
2. Reach a point of acceptance in your mind — an unqualified and undisputed state of agreement with the truth of your desire.
3. Approach prayer with a feeling of joy and restfulness, foreseeing the certain accomplishment of your desire.
4. Maintain the complete confidence that your conscious thought will gain a definite response from your subconscious mind.
5. The science behind prayer is the definite response of creative mind to your mental picture. The art is your personal technique and process.

**Scriptural basis:** Matthew 7:7 — "Ask, and it shall be given you; seek, and ye shall find; knock, and it shall be opened unto you."

---

### 4. The Visualization Technique

**Steps:**
1. Quiet the mind — silence internal mental activity.
2. Present your desired outcome as a vivid mental image to your subconscious mind.
3. See the completed result as clearly as if it were already alive and real.
4. Sustain the mental picture steadily while imagining hearing the voices of people affirming the outcome (e.g., "I am healed," "I feel wonderful").
5. Continue for at least 10 minutes or more, growing in awareness until the image feels real.
6. Release the picture and proceed with confidence that it has been received.

**Example given:** Before speaking from a platform, the author visualizes the entire auditorium filled with illuminated, inspired people, hears their affirmations of healing, sustains the image, then releases it.

---

### 5. The Mental Movie Method

**Steps:**
1. Relax completely and immobilize your attention.
2. Enter a quiet, passive, drowsy or sleepy state.
3. Dramatize your desired outcome as a full "mental movie" — act as though it is already happening.
4. Feel the reality of the scene and satisfy yourself that it is already accomplished.
5. Sustain the scene with emotion until a deep sense of peace and fulfillment arises.
6. Release the image and allow the subconscious to work.

**Example (property sale):** Picture the check in your hands, rejoice in it, give thanks for it, and go to sleep feeling the naturalness of the scene. The buyer and seller are brought together through the deeper currents of the mind.

**Example (finding a location):** The author imagined himself addressing a large audience in an ideal Midwest location, played the role of the actor, dramatized the scene, and felt satisfied. Within days, a telegram arrived offering him the position.

---

### 6. The Baudoin Technique

**Steps:**
1. Enter a drowsy, sleepy state — reduce all mental effort to a minimum.
2. Quietly and passively receive the idea you wish to implant.
3. Condense your need or desire into a brief, memorable phrase (six words or fewer).
4. Repeat the phrase over and over again as a lullaby — slowly, quietly, and feelingly.
5. Continue nightly (or morning and night) until you feel an inner sense of peace and conviction that it is done.
6. Then go into normal sleep, carrying the feeling of completion with you.

**Example (lawsuit settlement):** A woman fighting a will case used the phrase "It is finished in divine order." She repeated it each night in a sleepy state for about 10 nights. On the eleventh morning, she awoke with a conviction that it was finished. Her attorney called the same day to report the opposing side wished to settle. A harmonious agreement was reached.

---

### 7. The Sleeping Technique

**Steps:**
1. Assume a comfortable posture and fully relax your body.
2. Enter a sleepy, drowsy state (this is when the subconscious is most receptive — just before sleep and just after awakening).
3. In that sleepy state, quietly and lovingly repeat your desired affirmation over and over, slowly, like a lullaby.
4. Repeat for 5–10 minutes each night and morning.
5. Each repetition increases the emotional value of the suggestion.
6. When the urge for a negative habit arises, repeat the formula out loud to yourself.
7. This induces the subconscious to accept the idea, producing a healing or transformation.

**Example (breaking a habit):** Repeat: "I am completely free from this habit; harmony and peace of mind reign supreme."

---

### 8. The Thank You Technique

**Steps:**
1. Identify your desire or need clearly.
2. Every night and morning, in a relaxed, peaceful manner, say: "Thank you, Father, for [your desire]."
3. Imagine you are addressing the infinite power and intelligence within you.
4. Feel genuine gratitude and joy as though you have already received it.
5. When thoughts of fear, lack, or distress arise, repeat the phrase of thanks as often as necessary.
6. Continue until your mind is lifted to the point of full acceptance — the feeling of thankfulness dominates your awareness.

**Key principle:** The thankful heart is always close to the creative forces of the universe, and blessings flow toward it by the law of reciprocal relationship (cosmic action and reaction).

**Example (Mr. Broke):** Out of work, with bills piling up and three children, he repeated "Thank you, Father, for my wealth" for about three weeks. He met a former employer who offered him a lucrative position and advanced him $500. Today, he is vice-president of the company.

---

### 9. The Affirmative Method

**Steps:**
1. Formulate a clear, specific, positive statement of truth about your desired state.
2. Avoid vain repetition — understand the meaning and truth behind the words.
3. Affirm the universal principle that corresponds to your desire (e.g., health, not sickness; harmony, not discord; abundance, not poverty).
4. State the affirmation with full confidence as truth, regardless of all evidence to the contrary.
5. Repeat the affirmation several times a day.
6. Maintain the attitude of mind until the subconscious reacts and the truth is demonstrated in your experience.

**Key principles:**
- There is a principle of mathematics but none of error; a principle of truth but none of dishonesty; a principle of intelligence but none of ignorance; a principle of harmony but none of discord; a principle of health but none of disease; a principle of abundance but none of poverty.
- Affirming the truth does not create the truth — the truth already exists; your affirmation aligns you with it.

**Example (author's sister):** The author affirmed over his sister in England (6,500 miles away), stating that the healing intelligence of her subconscious mind was transforming every cell, nerve, tissue, muscle, and bone. He affirmed several times a day for two weeks. Her X-ray proved negative.

---

### 10. The Argumentative Method (Dr. Quimby's Method)

**Steps:**
1. Convince both the patient and yourself that sickness or ailment is due to false beliefs, groundless fears, and negative patterns lodged in the subconscious mind.
2. Reason clearly: the disease is a distorted, twisted pattern of thought that has externalized itself in the body.
3. Explain to the patient that the basis of all healing is a change of belief.
4. Point out that the subconscious mind created the body and all its organs — therefore it knows how to heal and is healing now.
5. In the "courtroom of your mind," argue the case: present evidence on behalf of the healing power within that created all organs and holds their perfect pattern.
6. Render a verdict in favor of health — liberate the patient through faith and spiritual understanding.
7. Because there is only one mind, what you feel as true will be resurrected in the experience of the patient.

---

### 11. The Absolute Method

**Steps:**
1. Mentally mention the name of the patient for whom you are praying.
2. Quietly and silently contemplate the qualities and attributes of God/Infinite Intelligence: boundless love, infinite intelligence, all-powerful, boundless wisdom, absolute harmony, indescribable beauty and perfection.
3. Allow your consciousness to rise to a new spiritual wavelength through this contemplation.
4. Feel that the infinite ocean of love and life is now dissolving everything unlike itself in the mind and body of the person for whom you pray.
5. Feel that all power and love are focused on the patient, and whatever is bothering or vexing them is completely neutralized.
6. Hold this state of elevated consciousness until you feel the conviction of completion.

**Analogy:** Like modern sound wave therapy, where targeted sound waves dissolve physical deposits — this method generates spiritual electronic waves of harmony, health, and peace.

---

### 12. The Decree Method

**Steps:**
1. Formulate a clear, authoritative decree for what you wish to bring into being.
2. Speak or think the decree with full confidence and feeling.
3. Know that the power that moves the world is moving on your behalf and backing up your word.
4. Do not strive, coerce, force, or wrestle mentally — simply decree and release.
5. Release the person or situation unto God/Infinite Intelligence, affirming they are in their true place at all times.
6. Declare that your words go forth into infinite mind and that it brings it to pass.

**Scriptural basis:** Job 22:28 — "Thou shalt decree a thing, and it shall be established unto thee: and the light shall shine upon thy ways."

**Example:** A young girl being pursued by an unwanted suitor decreed: "I release [his name] unto God. He is in the true place at all times. I am free and he is free. I now decree that my words go forth into infinite mind and it brings it to pass. It is so." The suitor vanished and never returned.

## Key Principles and Concepts

1. **Methods and techniques are primary:** Just as an engineer uses mathematical principles, proven methods, and a blueprint to build a bridge, so must you use proven mental and spiritual techniques to govern and direct your life.

2. **Prayer is the soul's sincere desire:** Your desire, when formulated clearly and presented to the subconscious mind, IS prayer. It comes from your deepest needs and reveals what you truly want.

3. **The subconscious mind is infinite intelligence and infinite power:** It contains the blueprint for your body and all its organs, and it knows how to heal.

4. **Thought precedes form:** Unless there is first an image in the mind, the power of the subconscious cannot move toward it. Your mental act must be accepted as an image before it can be made productive.

5. **The world operates by law and order:** Nothing happens by chance. There is always a direct, scientific response from the subconscious mind to conscious thinking.

6. **No time or space in the mind principle:** Infinite intelligence is present at every point simultaneously. Distance does not limit prayer or mental healing.

7. **Faith and acceptance are essential:** You must ask believing if you are to receive. The unqualified and undisputed state of agreement (acceptance) in the mind is the foundation for answered prayer.

8. **Mental patterns become experiences:** Every thought, idea, belief, and mental scene held in the "hidden studio of your mind" becomes impressions that are ultimately manifested as facts and experiences in your life.

9. **A mental picture is worth a thousand words:** The subconscious mind will bring to pass any picture held in the mind and backed by faith — this is the foundation of all the techniques described.

10. **Your word is real, silent, and invisible:** Every thought and word is real and contributes to the building of your mental home — your personality, identity, and life story.

11. **There is always a principle and never its opposite:** Principle (health, truth, harmony, abundance, intelligence) always exists; its opposite (disease, error, discord, poverty, ignorance) has no actual principle behind it — it is merely the absence of the true principle.

12. **The decreed word has power:** The power that goes into your word is determined by the feeling and faith behind it. When you know the power that moves the universe is backing your word, confidence and assurance grow.

## Best Practices Mentioned

1. **Be simple and naive:** Use the Passing-Over Technique with complete simplicity — do not over-complicate the process.
2. **Get into a sleepy, drowsy state:** This reduces mental effort to a minimum, submerges the conscious mind, and allows the subconscious to receive impressions most effectively. The highest degree of subconscious outcropping occurs just before sleep and just after waking.
3. **Repeat affirmations as a lullaby:** Slow, quiet, feeling repetition is more effective than rapid or effortful repetition. Let the emotional value grow with each repetition.
4. **Feel the reality of your mental picture:** It is not enough to think the thought — you must feel the reality of the desired outcome. Engage emotion and imagination together.
5. **Thank as if you already have it:** Adopt an attitude of gratitude before the objective result appears. This lifts the mind to the point of acceptance.
6. **Decree without mental striving:** Avoid coercion, force, or mental wrestling. Simply decree and release — power is already behind your word.
7. **Quiet the wheels of the mind:** Before presenting images to the subconscious, still all mental activity and internal chatter.
8. **Repeat until you get the subconscious reaction:** Continue the practice until you feel an inner sense of peace, conviction, or knowing that the work is done — do not stop at mere words.
9. **Use brief, memorable phrases:** Condense your need into a simple phrase that is "easily graven on the memory" for the Baudoin Technique.
10. **Withdraw attention from symptoms:** When using the Affirmative Method, withdraw all thought from the contemplation of symptoms and focus entirely on the healing truth.
11. **Apply techniques to others as well as yourself:** All techniques described can be used for self-healing, personal goals, and prayer treatment for others.
12. **Experiment scientifically:** Test these principles personally and consistently until you prove to yourself that there is always a direct response from the subconscious mind to conscious thinking.

## Common Pitfalls Mentioned

1. **Using vain repetition without understanding:** Repeating affirmations mechanically without understanding their meaning and truth robs them of power.
2. **Mental striving, coercion, and force:** Trying to add power to power through effort, struggle, or mental wrestling blocks the flow. Effort must be reduced to a minimum.
3. **Maintaining a negative blueprint:** Continuing to harbor fear, worry, anxiety, or lack in your mental imagery brings more of the same into your experience.
4. **Focusing on symptoms and negative appearances:** Contemplating the disease or problem reinforces the negative pattern. The focus must shift entirely to the healing truth.
5. **Doubting or wavering in faith:** Doubt neutralizes the impression being made on the subconscious. Acceptance must be unqualified and undisputed.
6. **Thinking the principle creates the truth:** Affirmation does not create the truth — it aligns you with it. Health, harmony, and abundance are already universal principles; your role is to recognize and claim them.
7. **Leaving prayer "up in the air like a balloon":** Unfocused or vague prayer that does not form a clear image in the mind accomplishes nothing. Prayer must have direction and specificity.
8. **Relying on formal, ritualistic prayer alone:** While group worship has its place, personal, applied prayer with a mental image is what transforms daily life.

## Summary

Chapter VI presents eleven distinct yet interrelated techniques for working with the subconscious mind, each with a specific procedure, mechanism, and best application. The foundational principle across all methods is that a clear mental image or thought, when impressed upon the subconscious with faith, feeling, and proper technique, will inevitably be brought to pass. The chapter emphasizes that these are scientific laws — not random hopeful thinking — and that consistent, faithful application produces tangible results in health, relationships, finances, and personal growth.
