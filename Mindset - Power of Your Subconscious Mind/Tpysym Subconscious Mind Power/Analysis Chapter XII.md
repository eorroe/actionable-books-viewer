Chapter XII: Scientists Use the Subconscious Mind

## ACTIONABLE LESSONS WITH STEP-BY-STEP INSTRUCTIONS

## 1. How to Receive Guidance from Your Subconscious Mind
When facing a difficult decision or an unsolved problem:

Step 1: Quiet the mind and still the body.
Step 2: Tell the body to relax; remind it that it has no volition, initiative, or self-conscious intelligence, and must obey you.
Step 3: Mobilize your attention and focus your thought on the solution to your problem.
Step 4: Try to solve the problem with your conscious mind first. Gather all available information through research and consultation.
Step 5: Think about how happy you would be upon receiving the perfect solution.
Step 6: Sense the feeling you would have if the perfect answer were yours right now.
Step 7: Let your mind play with this mood in a relaxed way.
Step 8: Drop off to sleep.
Step 9: If you awaken without the answer, get busy with something else.
Step 10: The answer will likely come into your mind when you are preoccupied with something else.


## 2. How to Use Your Subconscious Mind to Find a Lost Object
Step 1: Prior to sleep, talk to your subconscious mind as you would talk to anyone.
Step 2: Say: "You know all things; you know where [object] is, and you now reveal to me where it is."
Step 3: Trust and believe in the subconscious mind's ability to answer.
Step 4: Follow the inner voice or hunch when it comes, even if it seems strange.


## 3. How to Turn a Request Over to Your Subconscious Mind
Step 1: Prior to sleep, talk to your deeper mind.
Step 2: State the request: "I now turn this request over to the subconscious mind. It knows just where [the answer/solution] is and reveals it to me."
Step 3: Condense your request down to one word (e.g., "Answer").
Step 4: Repeat this word over and over again as a lullaby.
Step 5: Lull yourself to sleep with the word.
Step 6: When you awaken, follow the first overpowering hunch or inner awareness.


## 4. How to Seek Guidance for Major Decisions
Step 1: Sit quietly in your armchair or at your desk.
Step 2: Remember the universal law of action and reaction: your thought is the action; the response from your subconscious mind is the reaction.
Step 3: Contemplate right action. The subconscious mind is reactive and reflexive; it responds by corresponding (law of correspondence).
Step 4: You will automatically experience a reaction or response within yourself that represents the guidance or answer of your subconscious mind.
Step 5: From that point onward, allow the subjective wisdom within you to direct and control your course of action.


## 5. How to Develop and Perfect an Invention Using Your Subconscious
Step 1: When an idea for a new invention comes into your mind, build it up in your imagination.
Step 2: Know that your subconscious mind will reconstruct and reveal to your conscious mind all the parts needed for its manufacture in concrete form.
Step 3: Quietly contemplate every possible improvement.
Step 4: Spend no time correcting defects.
Step 5: Trust the subconscious to provide the perfect product of your mind.


## 6. How to Avoid Delaying Answers from the Subconscious
Step 1: Do not expect to always get the answer overnight; answers may take time.
Step 2: Every night, prior to sleep, turn the problem over to your subconscious mind as if you had never done it before.
Step 3: Do not believe the problem is major or that it will take a long time to solve.
Step 4: Do not postulate the answer in the future.
Step 5: Believe that you have the answer now.
Step 6: Have an abiding faith in the outcome.
Step 7: Become convinced now that there is an answer and a perfect solution.


## 7. How to Use Disciplined Imagination to Access the Subconscious (Archaeological Method)
Step 1: Study the materials before you (ancient ruins, fossils, scrolls, etc.).
Step 2: Engage in keen concentration and disciplined imagination.
Step 3: Awaken the latent powers of your subconscious mind.
Step 4: Through imaginative perception, allow your subconscious mind to aid you in reconstructing ancient scenes or hidden realities.


## KEY PRINCIPLES AND CONCEPTS

1. The subconscious mind has determined the success and achievements of all great scientific workers (Edison, Marconi, Kettering, Poincaré, Einstein, Tesla, Banting, Agassiz, von Blenk-Schmidt).

2. The subconscious mind is the storehouse of memory, containing all experiences since childhood.

3. The subconscious mind is timeless and spaceless.

4. The subconscious mind has no problems, only answers. It knows only the answer, never the problem.

5. The subconscious mind is reactive and reflexive by nature. It responds by corresponding (law of correspondence / law of action and reaction).

6. True thinking is free from fear. Fear and worry are not true thinking.

7. The body is an emotional disk that records your beliefs and impressions. It has no volition, initiative, or self-conscious intelligence, and must obey your commands to relax.

8. Mental pictures, backed by faith and perseverance, will come to pass through the miracle-working power of the subconscious.

9. Guidance comes as a feeling, an inner awareness, an overpowering hunch — a sense of "knowing that you know."

10. The subconscious mind will always answer if you trust it.

11. You are under a "subjective compulsion" to do the right thing when you operate under the subjective wisdom within you.

12. The subconscious mind has a memory of everything that has ever transpired and can reconstruct ancient scenes or hidden realities through disciplined imagination.


## BEST PRACTICES

1. Gather all available information consciously through research and consultation before turning a problem over to the subconscious.

2. Quiet the mind and still the body before seeking guidance or answers.

3. Turn requests over to your subconscious mind prior to sleep, every night, until the answer comes.

4. Trust and believe in the subconscious mind's power without doubt or questioning.

5. Use a single word or condensed statement as a mental lullaby before sleep to focus the subconscious on your request.

6. Sense and feel the joy of the answer already being yours; your subconscious responds to feeling.

7. Contemplate right action rather than anxiously searching for answers.

8. When a hunch or inner awareness arises, follow it immediately without hesitation.

9. Be persistent and patient; answers may not come overnight.

10. Use vivid, detailed visualization (as Tesla and von Blenk-Schmidt did) to impress the subconscious with your desired outcome.

11. If you awaken without an answer, get busy with something else to allow the subconscious to work unimpeded.


COMMON PITFALLS

1. Being fearful and worried — these are not true thinking and block the subconscious.

2. Looking upon a problem as major or believing it will take a long time to solve. This delays the answer.

3. Postulating the answer in the future rather than believing you have it now.

4. Doubting or questioning the powers of the subconscious mind.

5. Expecting overnight answers and becoming discouraged if they do not come immediately.

6. Spending time correcting defects or flaws in a creative vision instead of trusting the subconscious to provide the perfect form.

7. Trying to force the answer rather than letting it come naturally when you are preoccupied.


TECHNIQUES AND METHODS DESCRIBED

1. The One-Word Lullaby Technique
   - Condense your request to a single word.
   - Repeat it over and over as you fall asleep.
   - Example: "Answer" used repeatedly until sleep.

2. The Direct Conversation Technique
   - Talk to your subconscious mind verbally before sleep, as you would talk to another person.
   - State clearly what you need and trust it to provide the answer.

3. The Relaxation and Focus Technique
   - Quiet the mind and still the body.
   - Tell the body to relax and obey.
   - Mobilize attention and focus thought on the solution.
   - Sense the feeling of the answer already being yours.
   - Let the mind play with the desired mood in a relaxed state.
   - Fall asleep in this state.

4. The Vivid Visualization Technique
   - Imagine your desired outcome in vivid detail.
   - Make the mental pictures as real and natural as actual surroundings.
   - Used by Tesla to build inventions and by von Blenk-Schmidt to escape the prison camp.

5. The Right Action Contemplation Technique
   - Sit quietly and contemplate right action rather than anxiously seeking answers.
   - Trust that your subconscious will respond with an inner feeling, awareness, or hunch.
   - Follow this inner compulsion to take correct action.

6. The Dream Incubation Technique
   - Place paper and pencil beside the bed before sleep.
   - Intend to receive a dream revelation of the answer.
   - Upon waking in darkness, record impressions immediately before they fade.
   - Used by Professor Agassiz to reconstruct a fossil fish.

7. The Preoccupation Release Technique
   - If no answer comes upon waking, get busy with something else.
   - The answer often comes spontaneously when the conscious mind is occupied elsewhere.
