# TTBT-LIBERATION-HEARING-BARDO

## Overview

The Tibetan Book of the Dead is a guide for navigating the intermediate state (Bardo) between death and rebirth. It teaches how to recognize the Clear Light and the appearances of Peaceful and Wrathful Deities to achieve liberation without needing to take rebirth. It exists because the moments after death and during the 49-day Bardo offer the highest opportunity for enlightenment, provided one is properly instructed and remembers the teachings at the critical junctures.

## When to Follow Book Teachings

- When a loved one is dying or has recently died
- When preparing for death through meditation and spiritual practice
- When seeking to understand the nature of consciousness after death
- When working with the dying as a lama, officiant, or spiritual friend
- When studying Tibetan Buddhist teachings on the intermediate state
- When seeking methods to close the womb-door and avoid undesirable rebirth
- When practicing the recognition of Clear Light during meditation
- When reciting prayers for the deceased during funeral rites

## Lessons From Book

### Lesson 1: Understanding the Bardo and the Opportunity for Liberation

#### Examples

**Example 1: The Clear Light at the Moment of Death**

At the moment of death, the Clear Light of the Dharma-Kaya dawns for approximately the duration of a meal. Those who have practiced the setting-face-to-face with the Clear Light during life recognize it immediately and attain Buddhahood without needing to traverse the Bardo. The text states: "The first [glimpsing] of the Bardo of the Clear Light of Reality, which is the Infallible Mind of the Dharma-Kaya, is experienced by all sentient beings." (page 6)

**Example 2: The Peaceful Deities Appear on Days 1-7**

If the Clear Light is not recognized, the deceased experiences the Chonyid Bardo where Peaceful Deities appear sequentially from the first through the seventh day. Each day brings a specific Buddha from one of the five realms with a colored light-path. The text explains that these deities are not external beings but thought-forms from the deceased's own intellect: "The deities, too, are not come from somewhere else: they exist from eternity within the faculties of thine own intellect." (page 19)

**Example 3: The Wrathful Deities Appear on Days 8-14**

If the Peaceful Deities are not recognized, the Wrathful Deities appear from the eighth through the fourteenth day. These are the same Peaceful Deities in a changed aspect, appearing as blood-drinking Herukas. Recognition at this stage is equally liberating. The text says: "The least of the least of mantrayanic devotees... as soon as he sees these blood-drinking deities, will recognize them to be his tutelary deities." (page 24)

#### Step 1: Prepare During Life Through Study and Practice

The teachings must be memorized, understood, and practiced while alive. The text emphasizes: "The acquiring of experience while living is important: they who have [then] recognized the true nature of their own being, and thus have had some experience, obtain great power during the Bardo." (page 1222) Study the Bardo Thodol, commit it to memory, and practice the setting-face-to-face instructions regularly.

#### Step 2: Apply the Transference at the Moment of Death

When death is imminent, apply the Transference of the Consciousness-Principle. Turn the dying person onto the right side (Lying Posture of a Lion), press the arteries gently but firmly, and read the instructions distinctly in the ear. The text instructs: "When the breathing is about to cease, it is best if the Transference hath been applied efficiently; if [the application] hath been inefficient, then address the deceased thus: 'O nobly-born, the time hath now come for thee to seek the Path in reality.'" (page 206)

#### Step 3: Read the Thodol Near the Dead Body

If the Transference is not successfully employed, read the Bardo Thodol correctly and distinctly near the dead body. Summon the spirit of the deceased and imagine it listening. The text states: "If the Transference hath not been effectually employed, then this Thodol is to be read, correctly and distinctly, near the dead body." (page 64) Ensure no relatives weep or wail nearby, as this disturbs the deceased.

#### Step 4: Guide Through the Clear Light of the Chikhai Bardo

For about three and one-half days after death, the Clear Light dawns. Read the setting-face-to-face instructions to help the deceased recognize this light as their own mind. Repeat: "O nobly-born, that which is called death being come to thee now, resolve thus: 'O this now is the hour of death...'" (page 253) Continue until a yellowish liquid appears from the bodily apertures.

#### Step 5: Guide Through the Chonyid Bardo - Peaceful Deities

From days 1-7, the Peaceful Deities appear. Call the deceased by name and explain each day's deity. Instruct: "O nobly-born, listen with full attention, without being distracted: There are six states of Bardo..." (page 377) For each day, explain the colored light-path and the Buddha appearing, warning against attachment to the dull lights of the Six Lokas.

#### Step 6: Guide Through the Chonyid Bardo - Wrathful Deities

From days 8-14, the Wrathful Deities appear. These are blood-drinking Herukas in various colors from the brain of the deceased. Instruct: "O nobly-born, listen undistractedly. Not having been able to recognize when the Peaceful Deities shone upon thee in the Bardo above, thou hast come wandering thus far. Now, on the Eighth Day, the blood-drinking Wrathful Deities will come to shine." (page 939) Emphasize that these are thought-forms, not external threats.

#### Step 7: Guide Through the Sidpa Bardo and Closing the Womb-Door

If liberation is not attained in the Chonyid Bardo, the deceased enters the Sidpa Bardo. Here the instructions focus on recognizing the Bardo body, avoiding attachment to worldly visions, and closing the womb-door. The text provides five methods for closing the womb-door. Key teaching: "The first method: Meditate upon the tutelary deity as apparent yet non-existent, like a moon reflection in water." (page 1627)

#### Step 8: Select a Favorable Rebirth or Transfer to a Pure Realm

If entering a womb is unavoidable, use the selection teachings. Avoid births in the Hell-world, Preta-world, Asura-world, Brute-world, and the Eastern and Northern Continents where religion does not prevail. Seek birth in the Southern Continent of Jambu or transfer miraculously to a Pure Realm such as the Western Realm of Amitabha. The text instructs: "Direct thy wish thus: 'Ah! I ought to take birth as a Universal Emperor... and, being born so, be endowed with great merit so as to be able to serve all sentient beings.'" (page 1886)

#### Best Practices

- ✅ Master the teachings through study and memorization while alive
- ✅ Recite the Path of Good Wishes prayers seven times or thrice before reading the Thodol
- ✅ Read distinctly with proper intonation, near the body or at the accustomed seat
- ✅ Press the arteries gently but firmly to prevent the vital-force from returning
- ✅ Maintain an undisturbed, quiet environment; restrain weeping relatives
- ✅ Use the specific name of the deceased when addressing them
- ✅ Repeat instructions many times until recognition occurs
- ✅ Meditate upon the tutelary deity as apparent yet non-existent
- ✅ Cultivate one-pointed resolve when closing the womb-door
- ✅ Choose rebirths that allow service to sentient beings

#### Keep In Mind

- The Bardo body possesses supernormal faculties: unimpeded motion, ability to traverse solid matter, and karmic miraculous powers (page 1323)
- The deceased's intellect is nine times more lucid in the Bardo than in life (page 1761)
- Whatever thought occurs during the Sidpa Bardo wields great power; impious thoughts can cause rebirth in lower realms (page 1549)
- The Peaceful and Wrathful Deities are not external but emanate from within the heart of the deceased (page 699)
- Even the most evil cannot fail to be liberated if they recognize the Clear Light at the moment of death (page 1222)
- The text should be read for forty-nine days after death (page 1766)
- Those who have practiced the Great Perfection and Great Symbol will recognize the Clear Light at death and need not read the Thodol (page 924)

#### Security & Safety Notes

- Do not allow relatives to weep or wail near the dying or dead body; this creates obstacles (page 70)
- Do not perform animal sacrifices on behalf of the deceased; these create negative karma (page 1962)
- Do not judge funeral rites as incorrect even if they seem flawed; impious thoughts about the Sangha harm the deceased (page 1534)
- Avoid entering wombs that appear impure; be free from attraction and repulsion (page 1901)
- Do not be startled by visions of males and females in union; these are natural signs of rebirth (page 1664)
- If the body is not present, occupy the bed or seat to which the deceased was accustomed (page 68)

#### Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Being startled by the Clear Light and failing to recognize it
  **Solution:** Repeat the setting-face-to-face instructions clearly and distinctly before and after death; persist until yellowish liquid appears from the apertures (page 233)

- **Problem:** Attachment to the dull lights of the Six Lokas instead of the Wisdom lights
  **Solution:** Instruct the deceased: "Be not fond of the dull white light of the devas. Be not attached; be not weak. If thou be attached to it, thou wilt wander into the abodes of the devas." (page 455)

- **Problem:** Fearing the Wrathful Deities and thinking them external threats
  **Solution:** Explain that they are one's own thought-forms: "As soon as the setting-face-to-face is heard one recognizeth them to be one's own tutelary deities and one's own thought-forms." (page 1038)

- **Problem:** Being unable to close the womb-door due to strong propensities
  **Solution:** Use the meditation on the Clear Light: "All substances are mine own mind; and this mind is vacuousness, is unborn, and unceasing." (page 1744)

- **Problem:** Misunderstanding the teachings because they were not studied during life
  **Solution:** Commit the words and meanings to memory now; "it should be so that the words and the meanings will not be forgotten even though a hundred executioners were pursuing thee." (page 1230)

- **Problem:** Creating impious thoughts when seeing incorrect funeral rites
  **Solution:** Reflect: "What! Mine own thoughts must be impure! How can it be possible that the words of the Buddha should be incorrect?" (page 1536)

### Lesson 2: The Bardo of the Moments of Death - Chikhai Bardo

#### Examples

**Example 1: The Primary Clear Light**

When the expiration ceases, the vital-force sinks into the nerve-center of Wisdom and the Knower experiences the Clear Light of the natural condition. The duration varies: in those with dhyana practice, it endures a long time; in those of unsound nerves, only as long as snapping a finger; generally about three and one-half to four days. The text states: "The interval between the cessation of the expiration and the cessation of the inspiration is the time during which the vital-force remaineth in the median-nerve." (page 227)

**Example 2: The Secondary Clear Light**

After the primary Clear Light, a secondary Clear Light dawns somewhat more than a meal-time period after expiration ceases. The consciousness-principle hovers, unsure if it is dead. At this time, instructions should be applied for those in the perfected or visualizing stages, or for common folk: "Meditate upon the Great Compassionate Lord." (page 323)

#### Step 1: Recognize Symptoms of Death

Before death, the dying person or their attendant should examine the symptoms as they gradually appear, following the teaching of Self-Liberation by Observing the Characteristics of the Symptoms of Death. When all symptoms are complete, apply the Transference.

#### Step 2: Apply the Transference

If capable, the dying person should diagnose their own symptoms and apply the Transference. If not, the guru, shishya, or brother in the Faith should be present. The text instructs: "When the symptoms of earth sinking into water are come..." (page 249) Enjoin the resolution: "O this now is the hour of death. By taking advantage of this death, I will so act, for the good of all sentient beings, peopling the illimitable expanse of the heavens, as to obtain the Perfect Buddhahood..." (page 254)

#### Step 3: Apply the Setting-Face-to-Face with the Clear Light

After expiration ceases, press the nerve of sleep firmly. A lama or learned person should impress these words: "O nobly-born, thou art now experiencing the Radiance of the Clear Light of Pure Reality. Recognize it." (page 274) Repeat distinctly three or seven times. The text explains: "Thine own consciousness, shining, void, and inseparable from the Great Body of Radiance, hath no birth, nor death, and is the Immutable Light -- Buddha Amitabha." (page 287)

#### Step 4: Address the Secondary Clear Light if Primary Was Missed

If the primary Clear Light was not recognized, apply the setting-face-to-face during the secondary Clear Light. For those in the perfected stage, call them thrice by name and repeat the Clear Light instructions. For those in the visualizing stage, read the introductory descriptions and text of Meditation on the tutelary deity. For common folk, say: "Meditate upon the Great Compassionate Lord." (page 323)

#### Best Practices

- ✅ Read instructions distinctly, clearly, and with proper intonation
- ✅ Turn the dying person onto the right side (Lion's Lying Posture)
- ✅ Press the arteries gently but firmly to guide the vital-force out through the Brahmanic aperture
- ✅ Repeat the setting-face-to-face until yellowish liquid appears from the apertures
- ✅ Use the specific name of the deceased when addressing them
- ✅ Maintain the environment free from weeping and wailing

#### Keep In Mind

- The duration of the primary Clear Light state is about three and one-half to four days (page 238)
- In those who have had even a little practice of dhyana, this state continues for a long time (page 232)
- In those of unsound nerves, it endures only as long as snapping a finger (page 237)
- The thought-body during the secondary Clear Light is of the nature of the shining illusory-body (page 338)
- The consciousness-principle hovers, hearing and seeing relatives but unable to communicate (page 305)

#### Security & Safety Notes

- Do not allow weeping or wailing near the dying person; restrain relatives (page 70)
- If the dying person is disposed to sleep, arrest the sleeping state and press the arteries (page 221)
- The larynx should not be touched; only press the arteries on the right and left side of the throat (page 220)

#### Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** The vital-force returns to the left nerve instead of exiting properly
  **Solution:** Apply the directions before the vital-force rushes into the left nerve; the time is while the inspiration is still present or about the time required for eating a meal (page 201)

- **Problem:** The dying person cannot recognize the Clear Light due to strong karma
  **Solution:** A guru or learned person should vividly impress the setting-face-to-face upon the dying person; even those of evil karma who cannot recognize will be helped by this instruction (page 329)

### Lesson 3: The Chonyid Bardo - Experiencing Reality with Peaceful and Wrathful Deities

#### Examples

**Example 1: The Dawning of Vairochana on the First Day**

On the first day, from the Central Realm, Bhagavan Vairochana appears white, seated on a lion-throne, holding an eight-spoked wheel. The blue light of the Dharma-Dhatu Wisdom shines upon the deceased. Alongside, a dull white light from the devas strikes against them. The text instructs: "Put thy faith in it, believe in it firmly, and pray unto it... That light is the light of the grace of Vairochana." (page 454) If the deceased prays with intense humble faith, they merge into the heart of Vairochana and attain Buddhahood in the Sambhoga-Kaya in the Central Realm of the Densely-Packed.

**Example 2: The Dawning of Vajra-Sattva on the Second Day**

On the second day, from the Eastern Realm, Bhagavan Vajra-Sattva appears blue, holding a five-pronged dorje, seated on an elephant-throne. The white light of the Mirror-like Wisdom shines. Alongside, a dull smoke-coloured light from Hell strikes against them. The text states: "Through the power of anger, thou wilt beget fear and be startled at the dazzling white light and wilt wish to flee from it; thou wilt beget a feeling of fondness for the dull smoke-coloured light from Hell." (page 488)

**Example 3: The Dawning of the Blood-Drinking Herukas on the Eighth Day**

On the eighth day, the Great Glorious Buddha-Heruka appears dark-brown with three heads and six hands, body emitting flames. The text says: "Fear that not. Be not awed. Know it to be the embodiment of thine own intellect. As it is thine own tutelary deity, be not terrified." (page 961) Simultaneously with recognition, liberation is obtained.

#### Step 1: Explain the Six Bardos

Call the deceased by name and explain: "O nobly-born, thou wilt experience three Bardos, the Bardo of the moment of death, the Bardo [during the experiencing] of Reality, and the Bardo while seeking rebirth." (page 380) List all six Bardos: natural state in womb, dream-state, ecstatic equilibrium, moment of death, experiencing of Reality, and inverse process of sangsaric existence.

#### Step 2: Provide the Prayer of the Great Secret

Teach the prayer: "Alas! when the Uncertain Experiencing of Reality is dawning upon me here, with every thought of fear or terror or awe for all apparitional appearances set aside, May I recognize whatever visions appear as the reflections of mine own consciousness; May I know them to be of the nature of apparitions in the Bardo." (page 397) Repeat clearly and remember the significance.

#### Step 3: Explain the Sounds, Lights, and Rays

Instruct: "O nobly-born, when thy body and mind were separating, thou must have experienced a glimpse of the Pure Truth, subtle, sparkling, bright, dazzling, glorious, and radiantly awesome... That is the radiance of thine own true nature. Recognize it." (page 406) The natural sound of Reality will reverberate like a thousand thunders. The body is now the thought-body of propensities, incapable of dying. All sounds, lights, and rays are thought-forms.

#### Step 4: Guide Through Each Day of Peaceful Deities (Days 1-7)

For each day, explain the Buddha appearing, their color, throne, and emblem. Explain the Wisdom light shining from their heart and the dull light from one of the Six Lokas. Instruct not to be daunted by the divine light and not to be fond of the dull light. Provide the specific prayer for each day. Key instruction: "O nobly-born, if thou knowest it to be the radiance of thine own intellect... the Divine Body and Light will merge into thee inseparably, and thou wilt obtain Buddhahood." (page 534)

#### Step 5: Guide Through Each Day of Wrathful Deities (Days 8-14)

For each day, explain the blood-drinking deity of the corresponding order (Heruka, Vajra-Heruka, Ratna-Heruka, Padma-Heruka, Karma-Heruka, the Eight Kerimas, the Eight Htamenmas, and the Four Female Door-Keepers). Emphasize: "Know them to be the embodiment of thine own intellect. As it is thine own tutelary deity, be not terrified." (page 989) The size varies: largest equal to the heavens, intermediate as big as Mt. Meru, smallest equal to eighteen bodies. Be not terrified at their vastness.

#### Step 6: Explain the Importance of Recognition

Emphasize that recognition is the key: "O nobly-born, whatever fearful and terrifying visions thou mayst see, recognize them to be thine own thought-forms." (page 1149) "If one recognize not one's own thought-forms, however learned one may be in the Scriptures... one obtaineth not Buddhahood. If one recognize one's own thought-forms, by one important art and by one word, Buddhahood is obtained." (page 1157)

#### Best Practices

- ✅ Explain the six Bardos before guiding through the visions
- ✅ Use the specific name of the deceased when addressing them
- ✅ Read the instructions distinctly with proper intonation
- ✅ Provide the specific prayer for each day's deity
- ✅ Emphasize that all deities and lights are thought-forms from the deceased's own intellect
- ✅ Warn against attachment to the dull lights of the Six Lokas
- ✅ Repeat the setting-face-to-face many times if necessary

#### Keep In Mind

- The Peaceful Deities issue from the heart of the deceased; they are not external (page 699)
- The Wrathful Deities are the Peaceful Deities in changed aspect (page 873)
- The largest bodies of deities equal the limits of the heavens; intermediate equal Mt. Meru; smallest equal eighteen bodies (page 1142)
- The Knower hovers within places limited by its activities (page 343)
- If the Clear Light is recognized at the moment of death, the Dharmakaya is obtained and the Thodol need not be read (page 65)
- Even those of heavy evil karma can be liberated by hearing this doctrine (page 1222)

#### Security & Safety Notes

- Do not allow weeping or wailing during the reading of the Thodol (page 70)
- If the body is not present, occupy the bed or seat to which the deceased was accustomed (page 68)
- No animal should be slain on account of the deceased (page 1962)
- Relatives should perform virtuous deeds, not mourn (page 1963)

#### Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Fearing the divine lights and fleeing from them
  **Solution:** Instruct: "Be not awed thereby, nor terrified, nor awed. That is the radiance of thine own true nature. Recognize it." (page 409)

- **Problem:** Being attracted to the dull lights of the Six Lokas
  **Solution:** "If thou art frightened by the pure radiances of Wisdom and attracted by the impure lights of the Six Lokas, then thou wilt assume a body in any of the Six Lokas and suffer sangsaric miseries." (page 755)

- **Problem:** Not recognizing the Wrathful Deities due to fear
  **Solution:** "By having meditated on the description of these blood-drinking deities, while in the human world... recognition of them will result, and liberation. In this lieth the art." (page 893)

### Lesson 4: The Sidpa Bardo - The After-Death World and the Process of Rebirth

#### Examples

**Example 1: The Bardo Body and Its Supernormal Faculties**

After recovering from the swoon following the Chonyid Bardo, the deceased acquires a desire-body resembling the former body, endowed with all sense-faculties and power of unimpeded motion. The text states: "This body, [born] of desire, is a thought-form hallucination in the Intermediate State, and it is called desire-body." (page 1298) This body can traverse solid matter, appear where wished, and is visible to beings of like nature.

**Example 2: The Judgement by the Lord of Death**

The deceased encounters the Lord of Death who consults the Mirror of Karma. The Good Genius counts good deeds with white pebbles; the Evil Genius counts evil deeds with black pebbles. The Lord of Death looks in the Mirror where every act is vividly reflected. Lying is of no avail. The text says: "The Lords of Death are thine own hallucinations. Thy desire-body is a body of propensities, and void. Voidness cannot injure voidness." (page 1453)

**Example 3: Closing the Womb-Door**

When visions of parents in union appear, the deceased must not enter the womb. The text instructs: "Regarding the father and mother as thy Guru and the Divine Mother, meditate upon them and bow down; humbly exercise thy faith; offer up mental worship with great fervency; and resolve that thou wilt request [of them] religious guidance. By that resolution alone, the womb ought certainly to be closed." (page 1666)

#### Step 1: Recognize the Signs of the Sidpa Bardo

Teach: "At this time, thou must form, without distraction, one single resolve in thy mind. The forming of one single resolve is very important now. It is like directing the course of a horse by the use of the reins. Whatever thou desirest will come to pass." (page 1652) The signs that one is in the Sidpa Bardo: no reflection in water or mirrors, no shadow cast, inability to remain in one place, grey twilight light.

#### Step 2: Cultivate Unattachment to All Things

Instruct: "O nobly-born, whatever cometh in that manner -- whatever delightful pleasures thou mayst experience -- be not attracted by them; dote not on them." (page 1396) "Feeling miserable will avail thee nothing now." (page 1361) "Even though thou couldst enter thy dead body nine times over... finding no place for thyself to enter into, thou wilt be dissatisfied." (page 1422)

#### Step 3: Avoid Anger and Attachment During the Judgement

During the Judgement scene, do not be frightened or tell lies. The text says: "Even at the time that the pebbles are being counted out, be not frightened, nor terrified; tell no lies; and fear not the Lord of Death." (page 1450) "Thy body being a mental body is incapable of dying even though beheaded and quartered. In reality, thy body is of the nature of voidness; thou needst not be afraid."

#### Step 4: Direct Thoughts One-Pointedly

"Thy present intellect in the Intermediate State having no firm object whereon to depend, being of little weight and continuously in motion, whatever thought occur to thee now -- be it pious or impious -- will wield great power; therefore think not in thy mind of impious things, but recall any devotional exercises." (page 1549) If no devotional exercises are known, show pure affection and humble faith and pray to the Compassionate One.

#### Step 5: Recognize the Lights of the Six Lokas

When the lights of the Six Lokas dawn, the light of the destined birth place shines most prominently. The text instructs: "Whichever light shineth upon thee now, meditate upon it as being the Compassionate One; from whatever place the light cometh, consider that [place] to be [or to exist in] the Compassionate One. This is an exceedingly profound art; it will prevent birth." (page 1586)

#### Step 6: Meditate Upon the Tutelary Deity and Clear Light

"Again meditate upon the tutelary deity; again meditate upon the Clear Light: do this alternately. Afterwards, allow thine own intellect also to melt away gradually, [beginning] from the extremities. Wherever the ether pervadeth, consciousness pervadeth; wherever consciousness pervadeth, the Dharma-Kaya pervadeth. Abide tranquilly in the uncreated state of the Dharma-Kaya. In that state, birth will be obstructed and Perfect Enlightenment gained." (page 1593)

#### Step 7: Close the Womb-Door Using the Five Methods

**First Method:** Meditate upon the tutelary deity as apparent yet non-existent, like a moon reflection in water, then melt it away and rest in the Clear Light. (page 1627)

**Second Method:** When seeing parents in union, regard them as Guru and Divine Mother, offer worship, and resolve to request religious guidance. (page 1666)

**Third Method:** When attraction or repulsion arises, meditate: "Alas! what a being of evil karma am I! That I have wandered in the Sangsara hitherto, hath been owing to attraction and repulsion. If I still go on feeling attraction and repulsion, then I shall wander in endless Sangsara." (page 1713)

**Fourth Method:** Meditate that all phenomena are unreal and false: "All substances are unreal and false. Like dreams and like apparitions are they... The illusory mind itself doth not exist from eternity." (page 1724)

**Fifth Method:** Rest the mind in the uncreated state: "Lo! All substances are mine own mind; and this mind is vacuousness, is unborn, and unceasing." (page 1744)

#### Step 8: Select a Favorable Womb-Door or Transfer to a Pure Realm

If unable to close the womb-door, select a favorable rebirth. Avoid the Eastern Continent of Lupa and Western Continent of Balang-Chod (no religion); avoid Asura, Brute, Preta, and Hell births. Prefer the Southern Continent of Jambu (religion prevails). Or transfer miraculously: "I will so act as to be born in The Happy Western Realm, at the feet of the Buddha Amitabha, miraculously from amidst a lotus blossom." (page 1868)

#### Best Practices

- ✅ Form one single resolve in the mind; this is like directing a horse with reins
- ✅ Meditate upon the Compassionate One whenever fear or attachment arises
- ✅ Use the tutelary deity visualization as a bridge to Clear Light
- ✅ View parents in union as the Divine Guru Father-Mother
- ✅ Direct wishes toward rebirth in a realm where religion prevails
- ✅ Maintain the state of the Great Symbol meditation if possible
- ✅ Emit gift-waves of grace upon the womb being entered

#### Keep In Mind

- The Bardo body possesses supernormal perception but limited; relatives appear as in a dream (page 1354)
- The deceased will experience momentary joys and sorrows like a catapult (page 1500)
- Whatever thought occurs in the Bardo wields great power (page 1549)
- The memory is ninefold more lucid than before (page 1761)
- The intellect is without a prop and immediately goes wherever the mind directeth (page 1760)
- Good or bad karma determines the experiences of pleasure, pain, or indifference (page 1393)
- The deceased cannot rest in one place for long (page 1405)

#### Security & Safety Notes

- Do not enter wombs that appear impure; maintain impartiality (page 1901)
- Do not be attracted by visions of the Deva-world if seeking liberation (page 1299)
- Do not be angry at relatives performing funeral rites incorrectly (page 1506)
- Do not cling to worldly goods left behind (page 1509)
- Avoid birth through egg or womb if seeking liberation (page 1680)

#### Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Becoming attached to visions of relatives and feeling miserable
  **Solution:** "Even though thou feelest attachment for thy relatives and connexions, it will do thee no good. So be not attached. Pray to the Compassionate Lord." (page 1363)

- **Problem:** Being attracted to pleasant visions of the Deva-world
  **Solution:** "At that time -- if thou art to be born as a deva -- visions of the Deva-world will appear to thee... Follow not the visions which appear to thee. Be not attracted; be not weak." (page 1299)

- **Problem:** Fearing the Judgement by the Lord of Death
  **Solution:** "Apart from one's own hallucinations, in reality there are no such things existing outside oneself as Lord of Death, or god, or demon, or the Bull-headed Spirit of Death. Act so as to recognize this." (page 1456)

- **Problem:** Being unable to resist entering an undesirable womb
  **Solution:** "Visualize [the tutelary deity] instantaneously. The gift-waves and the power of its grace will separate thee from the tormenting furies and thou wilt obtain the power to select the womb-door." (page 1835)

- **Problem:** Being unable to distinguish good from bad wombs due to karma distortion
  **Solution:** "Even though a womb may appear good, do not be attracted; if it appear bad, have no repulsion towards it. To be free from repulsion and attraction... is the most profound of arts." (page 1901)
