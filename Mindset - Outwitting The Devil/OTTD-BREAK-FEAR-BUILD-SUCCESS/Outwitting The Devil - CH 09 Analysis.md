# Chapter 9: Education and Religion

## Key Lessons
- Lesson 1: Master Definiteness of Purpose
  - Action: Adopt definiteness of purpose in every area of life and reject drifting
  - Step-by-step:
    1. Identify exactly what you want in each major area of life
    2. Formulate a definite plan for attaining it
    3. Pursue it persistently without deviation
    4. When annoyed by unwanted things, immediately divest yourself of them using the same definiteness
    5. Treat definiteness of purpose as your primary form of prayer
  - Best practices:
    - Make decisions promptly but change them slowly and only with definite reason
    - Recognize that a life of peace, contentment, and happiness naturally divests itself of unwanted things
    - View definiteness as the panacea that enables self-determination
  - Common pitfalls:
    - Drifting / lack of definite aim: Solution - choose a definite major purpose and hold to it
    - Submitting to annoyance by things you do not want: Solution - apply definiteness to remove them immediately
    - Praying with fear and doubt: Solution - pray only with definiteness of purpose and faith in attainment

- Lesson 2: Cultivate True Faith
  - Action: Develop faith as a state of mind free from all negative thought
  - Step-by-step:
    1. Recognize faith as the power of positive thought contacting Infinite Intelligence
    2. Eliminate all forms of negative thought (fear, doubt, envy, etc.)
    3. Back your definiteness of purpose with unshakable belief in its attainment
    4. Understand that faith is not begging but appropriating from the universal storehouse
  - Best practices:
    - Treat faith as definiteness of purpose backed by belief
    - Use faith to put natural laws in motion that transmute desires into physical equivalents
  - Common pitfalls:
    - Praying with fear that prayers will not be answered: Solution - only pray with positive, definite expectation
    - Begging God to assume responsibility: Solution - take responsibility through your own mind and effort

- Lesson 3: Embrace Your Primary Duty to Self
  - Action: Prioritize your own full and happy life before taking on responsibility for others
  - Step-by-step:
    1. Recognize that your first duty is to discover how to live a full and happy life
    2. Fulfill your own desires and needs first
    3. Only after self-fulfillment, assume responsibility for helping others with excess time and energy
  - Best practices:
    - Do not confuse selfishness with self-duty
    - Understand that unearned gifts to others (or yourself) can become curses instead of blessings
  - Common pitfalls:
    - Neglecting self to serve others prematurely: Solution - secure your own foundation first
    - Allowing false duty to parents or others to force you into unwanted relationships: Solution - minds that do not harmonize should never be forced together

- Lesson 4: Understand the True Nature of Sin
  - Action: Eliminate sin from your life by recognizing it as anything that causes unhappiness
  - Step-by-step:
    1. Define sin as any thought or action that causes mental or physical misery
    2. Audit your life for the following specific sins: overeating, over-indulgence in sex, negative thoughts (envy, greed, fear, hatred, intolerance, vanity, self-pity, discouragement), cheating/lying/stealing, remaining in ignorance, accepting unwanted things, drifting without purpose
    3. Systematically eliminate each sin from your habits
    4. Remember that fear and ignorance are the commonest and most destructive sins
  - Best practices:
    - View all sin as leading to the habit of drifting
    - Recognize that physical and spiritual health should produce peace and happiness
  - Common pitfalls:
    - Remaining in ignorance: Solution - seek definite knowledge and organize it into plans
    - Accepting life conditions you do not want: Solution - use your mind to change them

- Lesson 5: Transform Your Life Through Self-Forgetfulness and Giving
  - Action: Lose yourself in the service and welfare of others to achieve abounding happiness
  - Step-by-step:
    1. Aspire upward by turning desire toward your Divine source
    2. Give up selfish clinging to impermanent things
    3. Practice giving of substance, intellect, and love without desire for reward
    4. Forget yourself in the sorrows of others and minister to them
    5. Measure success by how many competitors you pass at the end, not by speed alone
  - Best practices:
    - Aspiration is desire turned upward - transmute destructive desires into divine energy
    - The gift of pure love is always attended with bliss
    - Happiness eludes those who selfishly seek it; it comes to those who serve others
  - Common pitfalls:
    - Giving to get praise or reward: Solution - give purely from the heart without expectation
    - Seeking happiness through gratified desire: Solution - rise above self into the divine state of consciousness

- Lesson 6: Recognize Human Superiority and Freedom
  - Action: Exercise your unique human power of independent thought backed by definiteness of purpose
  - Step-by-step:
    1. Recognize that you have freedom to choose positive or negative thoughts
    2. Understand that you possess rights and privileges not available to Omnipotence or the Devil
    3. Use your brain to receive and express positive thoughts
    4. Apply the laws of nature through definite plans
  - Best practices:
    - Remember that the Devil fears only the power of independent thought backed by definiteness of purpose
    - Know that you can achieve whatever you can conceive and believe
  - Common pitfalls:
    - Regarding yourself as powerless: Solution - awaken to your potential power
    - Using your brain for negative thoughts: Solution - choose positive thought deliberately

- Lesson 7: Reform Education Through Private Innovation
  - Action: Introduce practical, mind-developing education through private schools first to create public demand
  - Step-by-step:
    1. Design curricula that teach children how to use their minds, not just memorize facts
    2. Teach definiteness of purpose beginning in early grades
    3. Organize learning by doing practical labor connected to daily life problems
    4. Use round-table/conference systems instead of rigid classes
    5. Bring in business and professional people as auxiliary instructors
    6. Establish demand through private schools first
    7. Pressure public schools to adopt proven methods
  - Best practices:
    - Let students lead and instructors follow where possible
    - Connect all school work to practical affairs of life
    - Teach the whole child: mind, body, habits, emotions, and character
  - Common pitfalls:
    - Relying on public schools alone to innovate: Solution - start private and create market demand
    - Teaching abstract knowledge without practical application: Solution - reverse the system from memorization to mind-use

- Lesson 8: Live by Universal Spiritual Laws
  - Action: Align your life with natural laws through definite knowledge and organized plans
  - Step-by-step:
    1. Recognize that Infinite Intelligence favors those who adapt to its laws
    2. Know what you want and adapt yourself to natural laws to receive it
    3. Understand that nature's law is: "Know what you want, adapt yourself to my laws, and you shall have it"
    4. Apply the law of compensation: render more and better service than expected
    5. Practice the Golden Rule understanding that what you do to others you do to yourself
  - Best practices:
    - Seek definite knowledge and organize it into definite plans
    - View all true philosophy and science as harmonious with definiteness of purpose
    - See all achievement as beginning with ideas
  - Common pitfalls:
    - Expecting miracles without aligning with natural laws: Solution - adapt yourself to the laws
    - Thinking fine character alone impresses the universe: Solution - combine character with definite action

## Actionable Principles
- Definiteness of purpose is the first requisite for success in all earthly undertakings
- Definiteness of purpose, persistently pursued, is the only sort of prayer upon which one can rely
- All prayers bring results: negative prayer brings negative results, positive prayer brings positive results
- The first duty of every human being is to himself: find how to live a full and happy life
- Happiness is an inward state of perfect satisfaction free from desire, found in self-forgetfulness and service to others
- Sin is anything one does or thinks which causes unhappiness
- Fear and ignorance are the commonest and most destructive of all sins
- Faith is definiteness of purpose backed by belief in the attainment of the object of that purpose
- The power of independent thought backed by definiteness of purpose is what the Devil fears most
- Nature's law is: "Know what you want, adapt yourself to my laws, and you shall have it"
- Time is the greatest asset available to human beings and the cheapest
- Ideas are the beginning of all human achievement
- The only real school is the great University of Life where one learns from experience
- Man can achieve whatever man can conceive and believe
- Your space in the world is measured by the quality and quantity of useful service you render
- Every problem has an appropriate solution, often found in the circumstance creating the problem
- Your only real limitations are those you set up or permit others to establish in your mind
- The quickest way to reform public schools is through private schools creating proven demand
- Classes should be replaced by round-table/conference systems
- Schools should teach practical knowledge directly from business and professional people
- Teach children the true nature and function of sex and how to transmute it into a driving force for achievement
- Teach children that temporary defeat is not failure but contains the seed of an equivalent advantage
- Teach children to encourage their sixth sense and examine ideas from unknown sources carefully
- Teach children that all schoolhouses and textbooks are elementary implements; the real value is in the University of Life
