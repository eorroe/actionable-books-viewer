## Ambiguities Found

### 1. Contradictory Definition of "Fall" in Lesson 11

**Location:** Lesson 11 (Terrain)

**Issue:** The term "fall" is defined differently in two places within the same lesson:

- **Example 2 (line 1012):** "fall (a sudden strategic collapse caused by officers acting on resentment under an ignorant commander)"
- **Step 3 (line 1028):** "fall (valiant officers, ineffective soldiers)"

These are two different definitions of the same term. The Example 2 definition describes "fall" as a collapse caused by officer resentment under an ignorant commander, while Step 3 defines it as valiant officers paired with ineffective soldiers. This creates confusion about which definition is correct.

**Recommendation:** Standardize to a single definition. The Example 2 definition aligns more closely with the traditional Sun Tzu concept of "fall" (覆) as a collapse from internal resentment, while the Step 3 definition appears to be a misstatement or variant that contradicts the earlier, more detailed explanation.
