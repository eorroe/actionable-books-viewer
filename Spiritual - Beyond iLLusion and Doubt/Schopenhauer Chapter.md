# BID 8: Schopenhauer — Actionable Lessons

## Lesson 1: Purify Material Desires Through Krishna Consciousness, Do Not Attempt to Extinguish All Desire
**Step-by-step instructions:**
1. Recognize that desire is the constant companion of the soul and cannot be annihilated, even for a second (Bhagavad-gītā 3.5).
2. Understand that stopping all desire would make you like a stone.
3. Instead of trying to put an end to all desire, try to cleanse the diseased form of desire (*sarvopādhi-vinirmukta*).
4. Engage in Krishna consciousness (*bhakti*) as the cleansing process for material desires.

**Key terms and keywords:** desire, will, Krishna consciousness, bhakti, purification, *sarvopādhi-vinirmukta*, material desires, spirit soul, Bhagavad-gītā 3.5

---

## Lesson 2: Understand the Material World as Temporary, Not False
**Step-by-step instructions:**
1. Recognize that the material world is constantly changing and therefore not ultimate reality.
2. Understand that material life is like a dream—temporary, not false.
3. See that the dreamer (the living entity) is the factual reality, while the dream (material world) is temporary.
4. Awaken to the real, eternal spiritual platform so that flickering material dreams can be extinguished.

**Key terms and keywords:** temporary, dream, material world, spiritual platform, *mithyā*, reality, illusion, dreamer, awakening, bhakti

---

## Lesson 3: Practice Krishna Consciousness to Awaken from the Material Dream
**Step-by-step instructions:**
1. Abandon the dream of material existence and awaken to reality through Krishna consciousness.
2. Render devotional service unto the Supreme Personality of Godhead, engaging all senses in His service.
3. When the spirit soul renders service unto the Supreme, two side effects occur:
   - Freedom from all false material designations
   - Purification of the senses

**Key terms and keywords:** Krishna consciousness, bhakti, devotional service, awakening, reality, false material designations, purified senses, Nārada Pañcarātra

---

## Lesson 4: Accept That Desire Is Eternal and Direct It Toward Krishna
**Step-by-step instructions:**
1. Accept that as a living being, you must always have desires and cannot live without them even for a second.
2. Do not attempt to become desireless (as some Buddhists advocate), because the eternal spirit soul does not die after the destruction of the body.
3. Understand that thinking, feeling, and willing are carried from body to body in the process of transmigration.
4. Direct your eternal will toward serving Krishna rather than attempting to annihilate it.

**Key terms and keywords:** desire, will, eternal, living entity, Bhagavad-gītā 3.5, Buddhists, transmigration, spirit soul, material body, eternal willing

---

## Lesson 5: Understand That Death Is Not Annihilation—The Soul Transmigrates
**Step-by-step instructions:**
1. Recognize that death is not the stoppage of life; the living entity simply gets another body.
2. Understand that the will is eternal and carries the living entity from one body to another.
3. Accept that the body may be that of a demigod, a dog, a human, or whatever, according to the quality of one's will.
4. Know that the eternal will of every living entity is to serve Kṛṣṇa always.

**Key terms and keywords:** death, transmigration, eternal soul, will, body, demigod, eternal will, Krishna, service, Bhagavad-gītā

---

## Lesson 6: Tolerate Pleasure and Pain as Temporary Experiences
**Step-by-step instructions:**
1. Recognize that the nonpermanent appearance of happiness and distress is like the appearance and disappearance of winter and summer seasons (Bhagavad-gītā 2.14).
2. Understand that pains and pleasures arise from sense perception and are temporary.
3. Learn to tolerate them without being disturbed.
4. Continue your business of serving Kṛṣṇa despite temporary pleasures and pains.

**Key terms and keywords:** pleasure, pain, tolerance, Bhagavad-gītā 2.14, temporary, seasons, sense perception, suffering, Krishna consciousness

---

## Lesson 7: Seek Permanent Happiness, Not Momentary Relief from Suffering
**Step-by-step instructions:**
1. Do not mistake momentary suspension of suffering for real happiness.
2. Understand that real happiness means being relieved of suffering permanently, not just for a few moments.
3. Recognize that the material world's pleasures are at best a negative state—a momentary pause in suffering.
4. Seek the real, eternal spiritual platform where suffering is extinguished permanently.

**Key terms and keywords:** happiness, suffering, permanent, temporary, relief, material world, negative state, spiritual platform

---

## Lesson 8: Accept That Suffering Implies a Judge and Seek God's Treatment
**Step-by-step instructions:**
1. If you recognize that there is a crime (sinful life), understand that there is someone who punishes you for that crime—a judge.
2. Accept that God provides hospitalization and treatment for the mad world through the process of Krishna consciousness.
3. Do not speak of sinful life while refusing to accept the judge who gives punishment for sinful life.
4. Seek the treatment (Krishna consciousness) for the disease of material existence.

**Key terms and keywords:** crime, punishment, judge, God, sinful life, Krishna consciousness, treatment, hospital, material existence

---

## Lesson 9: Recognize That Sex Is the Center of Material Life, Not Real Love
**Step-by-step instructions:**
1. Understand that the basic principle of those addicted to the material world is the pleasure from sexual intercourse (*maithuna*).
2. Recognize that the strong desire for sex continues as long as one is in material existence because sex is the center of all material pleasure.
3. Seek superior spiritual pleasure in Krishna consciousness to give up inferior material pleasure (*paraṁ dṛṣṭvā nivartate*).
4. When you get a taste of spiritual pleasure, you can give up sex.

**Key terms and keywords:** sex, material life, lust, *maithuna*, spiritual pleasure, *paraṁ dṛṣṭvā nivartate*, inferior pleasure, superior pleasure, Krishna consciousness

---

## Lesson 10: Distinguish Between Lust and Real Love
**Step-by-step instructions:**
1. Recognize that sex is animalistic lust, not love—it is the mutual satisfaction of material senses.
2. Understand that people mistake lust for love under illusion.
3. Develop real love for Krishna and all living entities, thinking: "People are suffering from a lack of Krishna consciousness. Let me do something for them so they can understand the value of life."
4. Direct love toward Krishna first; then it extends to all living entities.

**Key terms and keywords:** lust, love, Krishna, compassion, illusion, material senses, real love, suffering, Krishna consciousness, value of life

---

## Lesson 11: Understand That Immorality Comes from Avoiding Surrender to Krishna
**Step-by-step instructions:**
1. Recognize that immoral acts result from a sense of egoism.
2. Understand that to be immoral means to avoid surrendering to the will of Kṛṣṇa.
3. Avoid demonic thinking such as: "Why should I surrender to Kṛṣṇa? Kṛṣṇa is a person, and I also am a person."
4. Surrender to the supreme will to attain salvation.

**Key terms and keywords:** immorality, egoism, surrender, supreme will, salvation, demonic, Krishna, false ego

---

## Lesson 12: Crush Egoism and Desire by Directing Love Toward Krishna
**Step-by-step instructions:**
1. Develop love for Krishna.
2. Understand that without love for Krishna, you cannot surrender to Him.
3. Recognize that without surrender to Krishna, false egoism will continue.
4. Understand that the more you love Krishna, the more your surrender becomes perfect.
5. Recognize that love cannot be forced—surrender develops naturally from love.
6. Accept that the living entity is free to love Krishna or reject Him; without freedom, there cannot be love.

**Key terms and keywords:** love, Krishna, surrender, egoism, desire, false ego, perfection, freedom, Krishna consciousness

---

## Lesson 13: Practice Compassionate Love for Those Who Are Suffering
**Step-by-step instructions:**
1. See that everyone within material nature is suffering.
2. Understand that people think they are happy because of material possessions (car, apartment, girlfriend), but this is actually suffering.
3. Take up the renounced order (sannyāsa) or the equivalent compassionate preaching mission out of compassion for others.
4. Preach the message of Krishna consciousness to deliver fallen souls from ignorance.

**Key terms and keywords:** compassion, suffering, sannyasa, preaching, Krishna consciousness, material nature, ignorance, fallen souls, deliverance

---

## Lesson 14: Use Frustration as a Motivation to Seek Real Shelter
**Step-by-step instructions:**
1. If you feel frustration with material life, take it seriously—frustration is hell.
2. Use frustration as an opportunity to understand that you should seek real shelter.
3. Seek shelter in Krishna consciousness.
4. Understand that danger can become a source of pleasure when you know how to save yourself from it.

**Key terms and keywords:** frustration, shelter, Krishna consciousness, danger, pleasure, hell, real shelter

---

## Lesson 15: Understand That the Soul Is Behind the Will, Not the Will Itself
**Step-by-step instructions:**
1. Recognize that behind the will and its satisfaction is an eternal person who is willing—the individual soul.
2. Do not merely try to negate or annihilate the temporary material will.
3. Understand the real will of the real person—the eternal servant of Krishna.
4. When the spirit soul understands his real identification as the eternal servant of Krishna, his will is purified.

**Key terms and keywords:** will, soul, eternal, material will, spiritual will, servant of Krishna, purification, real identification, *jīvera 'svarūpa' haya—kṛṣṇera 'nitya-dāsa'*

---

## Lesson 16: Train Your Will to Serve Krishna's Supreme Will
**Step-by-step instructions:**
1. Understand that willing is eternal—it cannot be permanently suppressed.
2. Recognize that stopping one kind of willing only leads to adopting another kind of willing.
3. In the material world, the living entity directs his will toward sense satisfaction because he has forgotten the spiritual field of willing.
4. Direct the same will toward satisfying the senses of the Supreme—Krishna.
5. This is the eternal willing of the living entity: to be the eternal servant of God.
6. Train your will properly to serve Krishna's supreme will; this determines your destination after death.

**Key terms and keywords:** will, training, Krishna, supreme will, eternal, sense satisfaction, spiritual field, servant of God, death, destination

---

## Lesson 17: Study Bhagavad-gītā with a Bona Fide Spiritual Master
**Step-by-step instructions:**
1. Do not merely speculate on the basis of your own experience.
2. Approach a bona fide guru who has actually seen the truth.
3. Study the Bhagavad-gītā thoroughly under the guidance of a spiritual master.
4. Understand that without a real spiritual master, you cannot see the truth even though everything is in the Bhagavad-gītā.

**Key terms and keywords:** Bhagavad-gītā, spiritual master, guru, knowledge, truth, speculation, bona fide, guidance, study

---

## Lesson 18: Purify the Will Through Daily Practice of Bhakti (Chanting and Hearing)
**Step-by-step instructions:**
1. Engage in *śravaṇaṁ kīrtanaṁ viṣṇoḥ*—chanting and hearing the name, qualities, and pastimes of the Lord.
2. Practice Krishna consciousness daily so that lower consciousness automatically vanishes.
3. Understand that practice leads to continuous higher consciousness, not momentary experiences.
4. Apply the aesthetic sense with reverence and respect in Deity worship to derive benefit.
5. Recognize that by practice, just as a child learns to read and write through practice, you can become educated in Krishna consciousness.

**Key terms and keywords:** purification, will, bhakti, *śravaṇaṁ kīrtanaṁ viṣṇoḥ*, practice, chanting, hearing, daily, Deity worship, higher consciousness, aesthetic

---

## Lesson 19: Satisfy the Supreme Will Through Serving the Spiritual Master
**Step-by-step instructions:**
1. Understand that apart from the individual will, there is the supreme will.
2. Know that you cannot know the supreme will directly.
3. Approach a spiritual master who is the representative of God.
4. Satisfy the spiritual master to satisfy the supreme will (*yasya prasādād bhagavat-prasādaḥ*).
5. By satisfying the spiritual master, you become happy.

**Key terms and keywords:** supreme will, spiritual master, satisfaction, *yasya prasādād bhagavat-prasādaḥ*, representative of God, happiness, service

---

## Lesson 20: Train the Will Properly to Determine Your Destination After Death
**Step-by-step instructions:**
1. Understand that since the soul is eternal, willing is also eternal—although the will may be suppressed for some time.
2. Recognize that temporary suspension of the will (such as in the womb for nine months) is not the end of willing.
3. When you emerge from the womb or from any suspension, you resume the willing process.
4. Train your will properly to serve Krishna's supreme will.
5. If you train your will properly, you can go to Krishna's supreme planet immediately after death.
6. If you fail to train your willing process properly, you have to suffer life after life.

**Key terms and keywords:** will, training, Krishna, supreme will, eternal, death, destination, soul, suffering, supreme planet, Bhagavad-gītā 4.9

---

## Lesson 21: Understand Nirvāṇa as Purification of Will, Not Annihilation
**Step-by-step instructions:**
1. Recognize that Schopenhauer's idea of nirvāṇa as annihilation of the will is self-defeating and impossible.
2. Understand that true nirvāṇa means putting an end to the whimsical material will.
3. Come to the platform of willing in Krishna consciousness.
4. Recognize that the eternal will of the living entity is to serve Krishna, not to be extinguished.

**Key terms and keywords:** nirvāṇa, will, annihilation, whimsical will, Krishna consciousness, eternal will, purification, Schopenhauer

---

## Lesson 22: Recognize That Aesthetic Salvation Is Temporary Without Continuous Practice
**Step-by-step instructions:**
1. Understand that momentary aesthetic experiences (contemplating art, music, poetry) provide only temporary transcendence.
2. Do not settle for momentary "will-less" states.
3. Practice Krishna consciousness daily to remain in higher consciousness continuously.
4. Apply the aesthetic sense with reverence and respect in Deity worship for actual aesthetic salvation.

**Key terms and keywords:** aesthetic salvation, temporary, practice, consciousness, Deity worship, art, music, poetry, continuous, transcendental

---

## Lesson 23: Understand Ethical Salvation as Satisfying the Supreme Will
**Step-by-step instructions:**
1. Recognize that apart from individual will, there is the supreme will.
2. Understand that satisfying the supreme will leads to happiness.
3. Approach a spiritual master to know the supreme will, as it cannot be known directly.
4. Satisfy the spiritual master (representative of God) to satisfy the supreme will.

**Key terms and keywords:** ethical salvation, supreme will, spiritual master, happiness, representative of God, satisfaction, service

---

## Lesson 24: Do Not Merely Suppress the Will—Purify It Through Krishna Consciousness
**Step-by-step instructions:**
1. Understand that suppression of the will is temporary and not the solution.
2. Recognize that after death or any suspension, the will resumes.
3. Purify the will through Krishna consciousness rather than attempting to suppress it.
4. When the will is purified, it is directed toward serving Krishna eternally.

**Key terms and keywords:** suppression, will, purification, Krishna consciousness, temporary, eternal, service, Krishna, solution

---

## Lesson 25: Understand the Constant Cycle of Desire, Satisfaction, and New Desire
**Step-by-step instructions:**
1. Recognize that everyone goes through a constant transition from desire to satisfaction and from satisfaction to a new desire.
2. Understand that this flux characterizes the will's activities in the phenomenal world.
3. See that outside this flux, Schopenhauer thought there is only nirvāṇa (extinction of will), but this is not a fact.
4. Understand the real will of the real person—the eternal servant of Krishna—rather than merely negating the temporary material will.

**Key terms and keywords:** desire, satisfaction, flux, will, phenomenal world, nirvāṇa, material will, real will, eternal servant, Krishna

---

## Lesson 26: Recognize That Life After Death Means the Will Continues
**Step-by-step instructions:**
1. Understand that the soul is eternal and carries desires from one life to the next.
2. Recognize that death is simply a transition, not an end.
3. Train your will in this life to serve Krishna so that your next life is spiritual rather than material.
4. Know that the quality of your will determines the type of body you receive in the next life.

**Key terms and keywords:** life after death, will, eternal, soul, transmigration, body, training, Krishna, quality, next life

---

## Lesson 27: Avoid Speculative Philosophy—Seek a Bona Fide Spiritual Master
**Step-by-step instructions:**
1. Do not merely speculate on the basis of your own experience, as Schopenhauer did.
2. Understand that even though everything is in the Bhagavad-gītā, without a real spiritual master you cannot see it.
3. Approach a bona fide guru who has actually seen the truth.
4. Study Vedic philosophy and religion perfectly, not just portions of impersonalist or Buddhistic philosophies.

**Key terms and keywords:** speculation, spiritual master, guru, Bhagavad-gītā, truth, Vedic philosophy, impersonalist, Buddhistic, bona fide, knowledge
