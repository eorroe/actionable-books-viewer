# How to Clear the Negative Ego

## Overview

This book is a comprehensive guide to understanding and transcending the negative ego. Dr. Joshua David Stone identifies transcending the negative ego as a central spiritual practice for lightworkers and spiritual seekers. It presents a revolutionary system called Ascension Psychology that teaches the two distinct ways of thinking—the negative ego mind and the Christ/Buddha/Spiritual mind—and provides practical tools, step-by-step instructions, and advanced spiritual technologies to clear negative ego patterns, balance the four bodies, integrate the Seven Rays, and achieve Integrated Ascension. The book draws on channeled wisdom from Ascended Masters such as Djwhal Khul, Sanat Kumara, and Sai Baba to offer a complete roadmap for mastering the psychological level of spiritual growth, clearing diseases caused by negative ego thinking, and ultimately realizing God-consciousness on Earth.

## When to Follow Book Teachings

- When you feel trapped in negative emotions, fear-based thinking, or emotional victimization
 - When you are a lightworker or initiate who has achieved advanced spiritual development but still struggles with psychological imbalances, ego corruption, or experiences of emotional disturbance or lack of inner peace
- When you want to understand why your spiritual progress feels blocked despite intense spiritual practice
- When you are dealing with chronic health problems and want to explore their spiritual and psychological causes
- When you are in positions of spiritual leadership or public teaching and want to maintain purity of channel and avoid corruption
- When you want to learn how to deal with attacks, criticism, and judgment from others without losing your spiritual equilibrium
- When you are ready to commit to the vow of God-consciousness and transcend the negative ego once and for all
- When you want to balance your feminine and masculine energies, integrate your four bodies, and master your subconscious mind

## Lessons From Book

### Lesson 1: Understanding the Nature of the Negative Ego

#### Examples

##### Example 1: The Two Philosophies of Life

The negative ego mind perceives life through a limited, distorted lens—a separate, lower-self centered, fear-based perspective—rather than perceiving reality through the Christ/Buddha Consciousness. The Christ/Buddha/Spiritual Mind sees through the lens of pure love, unity, and oneness. According to this teaching, every person on Earth thinks with one of these two minds in every moment.

##### Example 2: The Dream Analogy

Living from the negative ego is like dreaming with your eyes open. When you wake up from a nightmare, you are relieved it was just a dream. The book teaches you to wake up from the negative ego's interpretation of life through the practice of denial and affirmation, and to see things as they truly are through the Christ Mind.

#### Step 1: Make the Vow of God-Consciousness

Make a conscious vow to yourself and God that you will choose only God and the Holy Spirit in every moment from this day forward. This is the foundational step. The author made this vow seven years before writing the book and credits it as the primary reason for his spiritual progress. As stated in the book: "I closed the door on the negative ego and lower self for God, and made a choice I would never consciously choose this path again."

#### Step 2: Practice the Science of Denial and Affirmation

Whenever a negative ego thought tries to enter your mind, deny it entrance and push it out. Deliberately redirect your attention to a positive and/or more spiritual thought. This process occurs repeatedly throughout each day. By denying negative thoughts and keeping your mind focused on spiritual truth, negative thoughts will weaken and fade from lack of attention, and a new positive habit will be formed in your subconscious mind.

#### Step 3: Use the Blessing Method

Bless and give thanks for everything that happens in your life. The negative ego curses and gets angry when its attachments and expectations are not met. The Christ Mind, having only preferences rather than attachments, is happy either way because inner peace is a state of mental and emotional equilibrium that comes from aligning with your true divine identity, rather than being dependent on external circumstances.

#### Best Practices

- ✅ Make the vow of God-consciousness as your first spiritual priority
- ✅ Be vigilant for God and the Kingdom of God at all times; never operate on autopilot without conscious spiritual awareness
- ✅ Practice denial and affirmation repeatedly throughout each day until it becomes your natural response
- ✅ View every moment as a spiritual test to choose God or ego
- ✅ Take responsibility for being the cause of your life experience, not the victim of circumstances.
- ✅ Keep your mind centered in the Christ/Buddha Consciousness at all times
- ❌ Do not pursue the study of this material from the point of view of the Negative Ego; study it from the Christ/Buddha Consciousness
- ❌ Do not balance negative ego thinking with Christ/Buddha thinking; they are not equal opposites
- ❌ Do not claim realization of something you have not truly achieved
- ❌ Do not underestimate the seductive power of the negative ego

#### Keep In Mind

- The author teaches that the negative ego is the root cause of negative emotions, negative thoughts, negative behaviors, physical diseases, psychological diseases, relationship problems, and poverty consciousness
- As Sai Baba expressed, "God equals humanity minus ego"—meaning that ego is the barrier between humanity and God-realization.
- The author teaches that the transcendence of the negative ego is the core foundation of Ascension and all spiritual development.
- The author teaches that psychological health involves maintaining a conscious mental diet of positive and spiritual thoughts.
- You are the director of your personality; God and the Ascended Masters will not do this work for you

- Set the intent to read from within your own Christ/Buddha/Spiritual Consciousness, letting your intuitive faculty discern truth from illusion

- **Problem:** The negative ego tells you that you mastered this material years ago or that you can't admit a mistake because that makes you inferior
  **Solution:** Recognize this as the voice of the negative ego; remain humble and open to continuous learning
