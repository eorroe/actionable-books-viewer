# Analysis of Bhagavad Gita Chapter 8 — Attaining the Supreme

## Lesson 1: Remember Krsna at the Time of Death
**Source:** Text 5

**Lesson:** Whoever quits the body remembering the Supreme Lord alone attains His nature without fail.

**Step-by-step instructions:**
1. Chant the Hare Krsna maha-mantra incessantly: Hare Krsna, Hare Krsna, Krsna Krsna, Hare Hare / Hare Rama, Hare Rama, Rama Rama, Hare Hare.
2. Cultivate tolerance greater than a tree.
3. Cultivate humility greater than grass.
4. Offer all respect to others without demanding respect in return.

**Examples / Best practices:**
- Maharaja Kulasekhara prayed to die immediately while healthy so his mind could enter the lotus feet of the Lord before the throat became choked at death.

**Common pitfalls:**
- Remembrance of Krsna is impossible at death for the impure soul who has not practiced Krsna consciousness in devotional service.

---

## Lesson 2: Shape Your Consciousness Through Daily Actions
**Source:** Text 6

**Lesson:** Whatever state of being one remembers when he quits his body, that state he will attain without fail.

**Step-by-step instructions:**
1. Recognize that cumulative thoughts and actions of this life determine thoughts at the moment of death.
2. If transcendentally absorbed in Krsna's service, the next body will be spiritual rather than physical.

**Examples:**
- Maharaja Bharata thought of a deer at the time of death and was transferred to that form of life.

**Common pitfalls:**
- Allowing material thoughts to dominate, which determines a material next birth.

**Best practices:**
- Chanting Hare Krsna is the best process for successfully changing one's state of being to transcendental life.

---

## Lesson 3: Engage in Prescribed Duties While Fixing the Mind on Krsna
**Source:** Text 7

**Lesson:** One should continue prescribed duties while simultaneously thinking of Krsna, dedicating all activities to Him.

**Step-by-step instructions:**
1. Carry out your prescribed duties (e.g., fighting, work, responsibilities).
2. Dedicate all activities to Krsna.
3. Fix the mind and intelligence on Krsna.
4. Chant Hare Krsna while performing duties.

**Best practices:**
- The Lord does not advise giving up prescribed duties; one can continue them while thinking of Krsna.
- This frees one from material contamination and engages mind and intelligence in Krsna.

---

## Lesson 4: Practice Constant Meditation on the Supreme Through Abhyasa (Practice)
**Source:** Text 8

**Lesson:** He who meditates on the Supreme Personality of Godhead with mind constantly engaged in remembering Him, undeviated from the path, will certainly reach the Supreme.

**Step-by-step instructions:**
1. Engage in abhyasa (practice) of yoga with the mind and intelligence.
2. Constantly think of the Supreme Lord.
3. Do not allow the mind to deviate.

**Best practices:**
- Chant Hare Krsna to engage ear, tongue, and mind simultaneously.
- This mystic meditation is very easy to practice.

**Example:**
- A caterpillar constantly thinks of becoming a butterfly and transforms into a butterfly in the same life. Similarly, constant thought of Krsna ensures a spiritual bodily constitution at life's end.

---

## Lesson 5: Meditate on the Specific Attributes of the Supreme Person
**Source:** Text 9

**Lesson:** One should meditate upon the Supreme Person as the one who knows everything, who is the oldest, who is the controller, who is smaller than the smallest, who is the maintainer of everything, who is beyond all material conception, who is inconceivable, and who is always a person. He is luminous like the sun and transcendental to material nature.

**Step-by-step instructions:**
1. Meditate on the Supreme as kavim — one who knows everything (past, present, future).
2. Meditate on Him as puranam — the oldest, origin of everything.
3. Meditate on Him as the supreme controller and maintainer of the universe.
4. Meditate on Him as aniyamsam — smaller than the smallest, entering the heart of the atomic living entity.
5. Meditate on Him as acintya-rupa — inconceivable form beyond material conception.
6. Meditate on Him as aditya-varna — luminous like the sun.
7. Meditate on Him as tamasah parastat — transcendental to material nature.

**Keep in mind:**
- Avoid useless argument and speculation.
- Accept what is stated in scriptures (Vedas, Gita, Srimad-Bhagavatam) and follow their principles.

---

## Lesson 6: Fix Life Air and Mind at the Time of Death (For Yoga Practitioners)
**Source:** Text 10

**Lesson:** One who, at the time of death, fixes his life air between the eyebrows and in full devotion engages himself in remembering the Supreme Lord, will certainly attain the Supreme Personality of Godhead.

**Step-by-step instructions:**
1. Practice yoga during life so that at death the mind is trained.
2. At the time of death, fix the life air between the eyebrows.
3. Engage in full devotion remembering the Supreme Lord.

**Best practices:**
- Without prior yoga practice, one cannot suddenly remember the Supreme Lord at death because the mind is very disturbed.
- For a pure devotee who does not practice hatha-yoga, always fixing the mind on Krsna in devotional service suffices.

**Common pitfalls:**
- Trying to suddenly remember the Supreme at death without prior practice.

---

## Lesson 7: Practice Pratyahara — Withdrawal of the Senses
**Source:** Text 12

**Lesson:** The yogic situation is detachment from all sensual engagements, achieved by controlling all senses and fixing the mind on the heart with life air at the top of the head.

**Step-by-step instructions:**
1. Control all the doors (senses) of the body.
2. Withdraw the mind from sense objects (pratyahara).
3. Fix the mind in the heart on the Supersoul.
4. Raise the life air to the top of the head.
5. Establish oneself in yoga.

**Best practices:**
- If one always fixes the mind on Krsna in devotional service, it is very easy to remain in undisturbed transcendental trance (samadhi).
- This is the practical alternative to the detailed hatha-yoga process described in the Sixth Chapter.

---

## Lesson 8: Vibrate Om and Remember the Supreme While Quitting the Body
**Source:** Text 13

**Lesson:** After being situated in yoga practice and vibrating the sacred syllable om, if one thinks of the Supreme Personality of Godhead and quits his body, he will certainly reach the spiritual planets.

**Step-by-step instructions:**
1. Become situated in yoga practice.
2. Vibrate the sacred syllable om (the supreme, indestructible absolute).
3. Remember the Supreme Personality of Godhead.
4. Quit the body while thus engaged.

**Best practices:**
- In this age, if one quits his body chanting the Hare Krsna maha-mantra, he will reach the spiritual planets.
- Om and Hare Krsna are not different; the sound Hare Krsna contains om.

---

## Lesson 9: Cultivate Unalloyed, Undeviating Devotional Service (Bhakti-Yoga)
**Source:** Text 14

**Lesson:** For one who remembers Krsna without deviation and is constantly engaged in devotional service, Krsna is easy to attain.

**Step-by-step instructions:**
1. Remember Krsna without deviation at all times.
2. Engage constantly in devotional service (nitya-yuktasya yoginah).
3. Desire nothing but Krsna — no desire for heavenly planets, salvation, or liberation.
4. Surrender fully to Krsna.

**Best practices:**
- Bhakti-yoga is very simple, pure, and easy to perform; begin by chanting Hare Krsna.
- Krsna gives sufficient intelligence to the devotee who fully surrenders so that the devotee can ultimately attain Him.
- A pure devotee can live anywhere and create the atmosphere of Vrndavana by devotional service.
- Pure devotees can serve the Lord in five ways: santa (neutrality), dasya (servant), sakhya (friend), vatsalya (parent), and madhurya (conjugal lover).

**Keep in mind:**
- A pure devotee is called niskama — without desire for self-interest.
- The Lord cannot forget His pure devotee for a moment.

---

## Lesson 10: Attain the Highest Perfection — No Return to the Material World
**Source:** Texts 15–16

**Lesson:** After attaining Krsna, the great souls (yogis in devotion) never return to this temporary, miserable world of birth and death.

**Step-by-step instructions:**
1. Progress in Krsna consciousness to the point of transcendental absorption.
2. Desire only Krsna's association, not elevation to material or even spiritual planets for personal enjoyment.
3. Attain the supreme planet, Krsnaloka (Goloka Vrndavana).

**Keep in mind:**
- Even the highest material planets (Brahmaloka, etc.) are places of misery with repeated birth and death.
- All yogis (karma, jnana, hatha, etc.) must eventually attain devotional perfection in bhakti-yoga to enter Krsna's transcendental abode and never return.
- At universal devastation, devotees engaged in Krsna consciousness are transferred to the eternal spiritual kingdom.

---

## Lesson 11: Understand the Cosmic Time Scale — Brahma's Day and Night
**Source:** Texts 17–19

**Lesson:** The material universe operates in cycles of Brahma's days and nights. During Brahma's day, living entities are manifest and active; during Brahma's night, they are helplessly dissolved.

**Key facts:**
- One day of Brahma = 1,000 cycles of four yugas (Satya: 1,728,000 years; Treta: 1,296,000 years; Dvapara: 864,000 years; Kali: 432,000 years).
- Brahma lives 100 such "years" = 311 trillion and 40 billion earth years.
- At the end of Brahma's life, all jivas remain unmanifest for millions of years.

**Keep in mind:**
- The less intelligent jivas who try to remain within the material world are elevated and degraded in various planetary systems.
- Intelligent beings who take to Krsna consciousness transfer themselves even in this life to Krsna's spiritual planet.

---

## Lesson 12: Recognize the Eternal, Transcendental Nature Beyond Material Manifestation
**Source:** Texts 20–22

**Lesson:** Beyond the manifested and unmanifested material world is another eternal, transcendental nature that is never annihilated.

**Step-by-step instructions:**
1. Understand that the material world is manifest and annihilated during Brahma's days and nights.
2. Recognize Krsna's superior spiritual energy as transcendental and eternal.
3. Understand that Krsna's abode (Goloka Vrndavana) is the supreme destination from which there is no return.

**Best practices:**
- Only by bhakti (unalloyed devotional service) can one enter the Vaikuntha (spiritual) planetary system.
- The Supreme is attainable by ananya-bhakti (undeviating devotion).

**Keep in mind:**
- Krsna's supreme abode is described as cintamani-dhama — a place where all desires are fulfilled, with desire trees, surabhi cows, and the Lord served by goddesses of fortune.
- Vrndavana on earth (90 miles southeast of Delhi) is a replica of the supreme Goloka Vrndavana.

---

## Lesson 13: Choose the Right Moment for Departure (For Non-Devotees)
**Source:** Texts 23–26

**Lesson:** There are two paths of departure from this world — one in light and one in darkness. Passing in light leads to no return; passing in darkness leads to return.

**Auspicious conditions for no return (light path):**
- During the influence of the fiery god (agni).
- In the light (jyotih).
- During the day (ahah).
- During the fortnight of the bright moon (sukla).
- During the six months when the sun passes on the northern side (uttarayana).

**Inauspicious conditions for return (darkness path):**
- During smoke (dhuma).
- During the night (ratri).
- During the moonless fortnight (krsna).
- During the six months when the sun passes on the southern side (daksinayana).

**Keep in mind:**
- Mystics advanced in yoga can arrange the time and place to leave the body.
- Those who are not so perfect leave at nature's will.
- For the pure devotee in Krsna consciousness, there is no fear of returning whether departure is auspicious or inauspicious, by accident or arrangement.

---

## Lesson 14: Never Be Bewildered — Always Be Fixed in Devotion
**Source:** Text 27

**Lesson:** Devotees who know these two paths are never bewildered. Therefore, be always fixed in devotional service.

**Step-by-step instructions:**
1. Know the two paths of departure without being disturbed by them.
2. Leave everything in Krsna's hands.
3. Be firmly established in Krsna consciousness.
4. Chant Hare Krsna.
5. Be unattached in the material world.
6. Dovetail all affairs in Krsna consciousness.

**Best practices:**
- The best way to be absorbed in Krsna consciousness is to be always engaged in His service.
- Being yoga-yukta (firmly situated in yoga) means being constantly engaged in Krsna consciousness in all activities.

---

## Lesson 15: Devotional Service Surpasses All Other Paths
**Source:** Text 28

**Lesson:** A person who accepts the path of devotional service is not bereft of the results derived from studying the Vedas, performing sacrifices, undergoing austerities, or giving charity. At the end, he reaches the supreme abode.

**Step-by-step instructions:**
1. Accept the path of devotional service (bhakti-yoga).
2. Continue performing one's prescribed duties, sacrifices, austerities, and charity as appropriate.
3. Let devotional service encompass and surpass all other paths.

**Best practices:**
- By one stroke of engaging in devotional service, one can surpass all rituals of the different orders of life (brahmacarya, householder, vanaprastha, sannyasa).
- Study the Gita in association with pure devotees, not by scholarship or mental speculation alone.

## Summary of Key Actionable Practices

| Practice | How to Apply |
|---|---|
| Chant Hare Krsna | Incessantly, following Lord Caitanya's example |
| Fix mind on Krsna | At all times, during all activities |
| Remember Krsna at death | Through constant practice of Krsna consciousness |
| Practice yoga | During life so mind is trained for death |
| Meditate on Krsna's attributes | Knower of all, oldest, controller, smallest, maintainer, inconceivable |
| Engage in devotional service | Five modes: neutrality, servant, friend, parent, conjugal lover |
| Surrender fully | Leave everything in Krsna's hands |
| Hear from pure devotees | Study Gita in association, not by speculation alone |
