# Chapter 9 Analysis: The Most Confidential Knowledge

## Lesson 1: Hear the Gita from Devotees, Not Mental Speculators
**Source:** Text 1 Purport

**Step-by-step instructions:**
1. Hear the Gita in the association of devotees
2. Through association with devotees, one is placed in devotional service
3. By serving Krsna, His activities, form, pastimes, and name become clear
4. All misgivings are dispelled once doubts are removed
5. Study of the Gita becomes extremely pleasurable
6. Develop taste and feeling for Krsna consciousness
7. In the advanced stage, fall completely in love with Krsna

**Examples/Best practices:**
- The Gita can only be perfectly understood by devotees (stated at the beginning of the Fourth Chapter)
- Hearing from devotees is called faith

**Keep in mind:**
- This process glorifies life beyond all penances, sacrifices, charities, and speculations
- The ultimate goal is transferral to Krsna's abode in the spiritual sky, Goloka Vrndavana

---

## Lesson 2: Practice the Nine Elements of Devotional Service
**Source:** Text 2 Purport

**Step-by-step instructions:**
1. Engage in hearing about Krsna
2. Chant the glories of the Lord
3. Remember Krsna
4. Serve Krsna
5. Worship Krsna
6. Pray to Krsna
7. Obey Krsna
8. Maintain friendship with Krsna
9. Surrender everything to Krsna

**Keep in mind:**
- By practicing these nine elements, one is elevated to spiritual consciousness (Krsna consciousness)
- When the heart is cleared of material contamination, one can understand the science of Krsna

---

## Lesson 3: Engage in Devotional Service to Understand the Supreme Lord
**Source:** Text 2 Purport

**Step-by-step instructions:**
1. Do not rely solely on karma-yoga, jnana, or astanga-yoga
2. Come to the stage of devotional service
3. Execute the process of devotional service
4. Hear Srimad-Bhagavatam or Bhagavad-gita from realized souls
5. When the heart is cleared of all nonsense, one can understand what God is

**Keep in mind:**
- No one can understand the Supreme Personality of Godhead independently through other yogic processes
- The process of devotional service is the king of all education and confidential knowledge
- It is the purest form of religion and can be executed joyfully without difficulty

---

## Lesson 4: Develop Faith Through Association with Devotees
**Source:** Text 3 Purport

**Step-by-step instructions:**
1. Associate with devotees to create faith
2. Have complete conviction that simply by serving Krsna one can achieve all perfection
3. Understand that engaging in transcendental service of the Supreme Lord automatically satisfies all demigods and living entities

**Three classes of Krsna conscious men:**
- **First class:** Well versed in devotional scriptures, attained firm faith
- **Second class:** Not very advanced in scriptures but automatically have firm faith that Krsna-bhakti is the best course
- **Third class:** Neither perfect knowledge nor good faith, trying to follow by association and simplicity

**Keep in mind:**
- Faith is the most important factor for progress in Krsna consciousness
- Third-class persons may fall down, but second-class and first-class persons do not
- First-class persons surely make progress and achieve the result at the end

---

## Lesson 5: Understand Krsna's Inconceivable Opulence
**Source:** Texts 4-7, Purports

**Step-by-step instructions:**
1. Understand that Krsna pervades the entire universe in His unmanifested form
2. Know that all beings are in Krsna, but Krsna is not in them
3. Recognize that everything rests on Krsna's energy, yet He is aloof
4. Understand that at the end of the millennium, every material manifestation enters into Krsna's nature
5. Know that at the beginning of another millennium, Krsna again creates by His potency

**Examples/Best practices:**
- The example of wind resting in ethereal space: as the mighty wind blowing everywhere rests in space, similarly all beings rest in Krsna
- The example of a king and his government: the king is not personally present in every department, yet everything rests on his power

**Keep in mind:**
- Krsna is the maintainer of all living entities and is everywhere, yet His Self is the very source of creation
- The entire cosmic manifestation is under Krsna's control, manifested again and again by His will

---

## Lesson 6: Do Not Deride Krsna When He Descends in Human Form
**Source:** Text 11, Purport

**Step-by-step instructions:**
1. Do not consider Krsna to be merely a powerful man
2. Understand that Krsna is the original Supreme Personality, the cause of all causes
3. Accept that His body is sac-cid-ananda (eternal, blissful, full of knowledge)
4. Recognize that His appearance as an ordinary human being is one of the features of His transcendental body

**Keep in mind:**
- Fools deride Krsna when He descends in human form because they do not know His transcendental nature and supreme dominion
- Those who deride Krsna are demonic or atheistic
- It is a great offense to consider Krsna to be an ordinary man
- Pure devotees accept Krsna as the Supreme Personality of Godhead and completely surrender unto Him

---

## Lesson 7: Take Shelter of the Divine Nature Through Surrender
**Source:** Text 13, Purport

**Step-by-step instructions:**
1. Surrender unto the Supreme Personality of Godhead, Sri Krsna
2. Immediately become freed from the control of material nature
3. Be put under the guidance of the spiritual nature (daivi prakrti)
4. Attain the stage of great soul (mahatma)

**Keep in mind:**
- As soon as the living entity is freed from the control of material nature, he is promoted to spiritual nature
- The mahatma does not divert his attention to anything outside Krsna
- Such a mahatma develops through association with other mahatmas (pure devotees)
- Pure devotees are attracted only by the two-armed form of Krsna

---

## Lesson 8: Cultivate the Symptoms of a Mahatma (Great Soul)
**Source:** Text 14, Purport

**Step-by-step instructions:**
1. Always chant the glories of the Supreme Lord Krsna
2. Have no other business but glorification of the Lord
3. Praise His holy name, eternal form, transcendental qualities, and uncommon pastimes
4. Develop firm determination to achieve association with the Supreme Lord in any of the five transcendental rasas
5. Engage all activities - mental, bodily, and vocal - in the service of the Supreme Lord
6. Strictly observe rules and regulations (such as fasting on Ekadasi, appearance days of the Lord)

**Keep in mind:**
- A mahatma cannot be manufactured by rubber-stamping an ordinary man
- Devotional service can be performed in a happy mood without difficulty
- The mahatma is always engaged in different activities of devotional service: hearing and chanting about Visnu, not demigods or human beings

---

## Lesson 9: Worship Krsna Directly, Not Demigods
**Source:** Texts 23-25, Purports

**Step-by-step instructions:**
1. Offer all worship to the Supreme Lord Krsna only
2. Understand that demigods are like officers and directors in the government of the Supreme Lord
3. Do not offer worship directly to demigods (this is compared to bribing officers instead of following the laws of the government)
4. Recognize that all sacrifices are meant for satisfying Krsna alone

**Keep in mind:**
- Whatever a man may sacrifice to other gods is really meant for Krsna alone, but offered without true understanding
- Those who worship demigods will take birth among demigods
- Those who worship ghosts and spirits will take birth among such beings
- Those who worship ancestors go to the ancestors
- Those who worship Krsna will live with Him
- One should directly approach Krsna, for that will save time and energy

---

## Lesson 10: Offer Simple, Pure Foods with Love and Devotion
**Source:** Text 26, Purport

**Step-by-step instructions:**
1. Offer Krsna a leaf, a flower, fruit, or water with love and devotion
2. Do not offer meat, fish, or eggs (Krsna does not accept these)
3. Prepare nice, simple vegetable dishes
4. Offer them before the picture or Deity of Lord Krsna
5. Bow down and pray for Krsna to accept the humble offering
6. Eat the remnants of foodstuffs offered to Krsna

**Keep in mind:**
- Krsna has no need of food, but He will accept the offering of one who desires to please Him
- Those who do not make an offering of their food are said to be eating only sin
- Offering food to Krsna purifies the body and creates fine brain tissues which lead to clear thinking
- The offering should be made with an attitude of love

---

## Lesson 11: Offer All Actions as Sacrifice Unto Krsna
**Source:** Text 27, Purport

**Step-by-step instructions:**
1. Whatever you do, do it as an offering unto Krsna
2. Whatever you eat, accept it as remnants offered to Krsna
3. Whatever religious ritualistic ceremonies you perform, do them for Krsna (arcana)
4. Whatever you give away in charity, give it to Krsna (utilize surplus money for Krsna consciousness movement)
5. Whatever austerities you perform, do them as offering unto Krsna
6. Chant the Hare Krsna mantra round your beads twenty-four hours

**Keep in mind:**
- It is the duty of everyone to mold his life in such a way that he will not forget Krsna in any circumstance
- Everyone has to work for maintenance of body and soul together; work for Krsna
- This process is called arcana

---

## Lesson 12: Practice Yukta-Vairagya (Renunciation Through Attachment to Krsna)
**Source:** Text 28, Purport

**Step-by-step instructions:**
1. Perform actions under superior direction (Krsna consciousness)
2. Give the fruits of all actions to Krsna
3. This is called yukta-vairagya (renunciation through proper connection with Krsna)
4. Such activities clear the mirror of the mind
5. Gradually make progress in spiritual realization
6. Become completely surrendered to the Supreme Personality of Godhead
7. At the end, become liberated and go back to Godhead (mam upaisyasi)

**Keep in mind:**
- As long as we are in this material world we have to act; we cannot cease acting
- If actions are performed and fruits are given to Krsna, that is called yukta-vairagya
- Anyone who dedicates his life to the service of the Lord is actually a sannyasi
- Whatever action a pure devotee performs, he performs it as service to the Lord
- A pure devotee may sometimes appear to go against prescribed Vedic duties, but actually he does not

---

## Lesson 13: Understand Krsna's Equal Vision and Special Relationship with Devotees
**Source:** Text 29, Purport

**Step-by-step instructions:**
1. Understand that Krsna is equal to all living entities
2. Know that Krsna envies no one nor is partial to anyone
3. Render transcendental service unto Krsna in devotion
4. Develop a reciprocal relationship with Krsna

**Keep in mind:**
- Krsna claims every living entity as His son and provides everyone with necessities of life
- Krsna is like a cloud pouring rain all over, regardless of where it falls
- For His devotees, Krsna gives specific attention
- Those who are in Krsna consciousness are living transcendentalists, situated in Him
- This is a reciprocal relationship: whoever surrenders unto Krsna, proportionately He takes care of him

---

## Lesson 14: Do Not Deride Devotees for Accidental Falldowns
**Source:** Text 30, Purport

**Step-by-step instructions:**
1. Do not deride a devotee for some accidental falldown from the ideal path
2. Even if a devotee commits the most abominable actions, if he is engaged in devotional service without deviation, consider him saintly
3. Continue chanting the Hare Krsna mantra without stoppage to protect from all accidental falldowns

**Keep in mind:**
- Accidental falldowns do not disqualify a devotee
- Krsna consciousness is so strong that such occasional falldown is at once rectified
- The accidental falldown of a devotee does not make him abominable (like a spot on the moon does not impede moonlight)
- No one should take advantage of this verse and commit nonsense, thinking he is still a devotee
- If one does not improve in character by devotional service, he is not a high devotee
- If one derides a devotee for accidental falldown, he is disobeying the order of the Supreme Lord

---

## Lesson 15: Take Shelter of Krsna to Attain Supreme Destination
**Source:** Text 32, Purport

**Step-by-step instructions:**
1. Take shelter in Krsna
2. Take shelter completely of Krsna
3. Associate with pure devotees for proper guidance

**Keep in mind:**
- In devotional service, there is no distinction between lower or higher classes of people
- Everyone is eligible for the supreme destination
- Even those born in sinful families can be elevated by association with a pure devotee
- Devotional service and guidance of a pure devotee are so strong that all lower classes can attain the highest perfection of life
- One who takes shelter completely of Krsna can become much greater than great jnanis and yogis

---

## Lesson 16: Engage in Loving Service Unto Krsna
**Source:** Text 33, Purport

**Step-by-step instructions:**
1. Understand that this world is temporary and full of miseries
2. Engage in loving service unto Krsna
3. Make life perfect through Krsna consciousness

**Keep in mind:**
- This world is temporary but not false; there is a difference between temporary and false
- The devotional service of the Supreme Lord is the only process by which all problems of all classes of men can be solved
- No one should remain in this temporary world full of miseries
- Everyone should attach himself to the bosom of the Supreme Personality of Godhead for eternal happiness

---

## Lesson 17: Always Think of Krsna, Offer Obeisances, and Worship Him
**Source:** Text 34, Purport

**Step-by-step instructions:**
1. Engage your mind always in thinking of Krsna
2. Offer obeisances unto Krsna
3. Worship Krsna
4. Be completely absorbed in Krsna
5. Learn from a bona fide teacher
6. Cultivate knowledge of Krsna continually
7. Engage your mind in the eternal form, the primal form of Krsna

**Keep in mind:**
- Krsna consciousness is the only means of being delivered from the clutches of this contaminated material world
- Unscrupulous commentators may distort the meaning and divert the mind to that which is not feasible
- Krsna is Absolute Truth; His body, mind, and He Himself are one and absolute
- There is no difference in Krsna between Himself and His body
- Understanding Krsna from the wrong source will prove fruitless
- One should cultivate favorable knowledge of Krsna, not envious thinking like King Kamsa

---

## Summary of Key Principles

1. **Association with devotees** is essential for spiritual progress
2. **The nine elements of devotional service** (hearing, chanting, remembering, serving, worshiping, praying, obeying, maintaining friendship, surrendering) elevate one to Krsna consciousness
3. **Faith** is created by association with devotees and is the most important factor for progress
4. **Krsna is the Supreme Personality of Godhead**, not an ordinary human being
5. **Surrender to Krsna** at once frees one from material nature and puts one under spiritual guidance
6. **Offer all actions, food, and resources** as sacrifice unto Krsna
7. **Practice yukta-vairagya** by giving the fruits of all actions to Krsna
8. **Do not deride devotees** for accidental falldowns
9. **There is no discrimination** in devotional service based on birth, gender, or social class
10. **Krsna consciousness is the only means** of deliverance from material existence and the only process that solves all problems
