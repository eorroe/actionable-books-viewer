# Analysis: Introduction — *The Seven Spiritual Laws of Success* by Deepak Chopra

## 1. Actionable Lessons

- **Incorporate spiritual laws into your consciousness.** Doing so gives you the ability to create unlimited wealth with effortless ease and to experience success in each endeavor you pursue.
- **Define success clearly.** Success is the continued expansion of happiness and the progressive realization of goals aligned with your highest values; it is the ability to fulfill your desires with effortless ease.
- **Adopt a spiritual approach to affluence.** Put yourself in harmony with nature and create with carefulness, joy, and love. Affluence is the abundant flow of all that serves your highest good to you.
- **Treat success as a journey, not a destination.** Material abundance is one component among several that together constitute success; it makes the journey more enjoyable but is not the end goal.
- **Nurture the seeds of divinity inside you.** True fulfillment comes from unfolding the divinity within; you are divinity in disguise, and the divine within seeks to be fully materialized.
- **Experience life as the miraculous expression of divinity — in every moment.** This is the true meaning of success.
- **Understand the nature of law.** Law is the process by which the unmanifest becomes the manifest; the observer becomes the observed; the seer and the scenery are recognized as one reality; the dreamer manifests the dream.
- **Apply universal laws to your desires.** The same laws that nature uses to create a forest, a galaxy, a star, or a human body can bring about the fulfillment of your heartfelt desires.
- **Shift from object-referral to self-referral.** Make your internal reference point your own spirit rather than external objects, situations, circumstances, people, or things.
- **Release fear-based patterns.** Let go of patterns rooted in fear: the need for approval, the need to control things, and the need for external power.
- **Cultivate real power.** When you experience the power of the Self, there is an absence of fear, no compulsion to control, and no struggle for approval or external power.

## 2. Key Concepts and Terms

- **Seven Spiritual Laws of Success** — Underlying principles nature uses to create all things in material existence.
- **Affluence** — The flow of all that serves your highest good to you.
- **Law (spiritual)** — The process by which the unmanifest becomes the manifest.
- **Field of Pure Potentiality** — The unmanifest source of all creation; your own Self.
- **Self-referral** — Having your own spirit as your internal reference point.
- **Object-referral** — Being influenced by objects outside the Self; seeking approval, control, or external power.
- **Pure Consciousness** — Your essential nature; pure potentiality, field of infinite possibilities, infinite creativity, pure joy, pure knowledge, infinite silence, perfect balance, invincibility, simplicity, and bliss.
- **Unmanifest / Manifest** — The invisible/unknown transforming into the visible/known.
- **Observer / Process of Observing / Observed** — Three aspects of reality that are expressions of a single underlying reality (spirit, mind, body).
- **Divinity / Spirit** — The source of all creation.
- **Real Power** — The power of the Self, characterized by absence of fear and absence of struggle for approval or control.

## 3. Best Practices

- Use **self-referral** as your primary mode of being.
- Create with **carefulness, joy, and love**.
- View success as a **progressive journey** rather than a fixed destination.
- Recognize that **material wealth** is only one component of a successful life.
- Cultivate **good health, vitality, enthusiasm, relationships that fulfill you, creative expression, emotional balance, holistic well-being, and inner peace**.
- **Nurture divinity within** regularly.
- **Perceive divinity in every person and situation** — in the eyes of a child, the beauty of a flower, the flight of a bird.

## 4. Common Pitfalls

- **Object-referral** — Basing your identity and behavior on validation from others, situations, or objects.
- **Fear-based living** — Allowing fear to drive your need for approval, control, or external power.
- **Equating success solely with material wealth** — Overlooking good health, relationships that fulfill you, creative expression, emotional balance, holistic well-being, and inner peace.
- **Treating success as a destination** — Missing the journey and the ongoing expansion of happiness.
- **Believing wealth creation requires hard work at the expense of others** — Rejecting the spiritual principle of effortless ease and harmony with nature.

## 6. Keep in Mind

- The **field of pure potentiality is your own Self**; there is no separation between you and this field of pure potentiality.
- **Consciousness in motion** expresses itself as manifest forms in the universe in the eternal dance of life.
- **True success** is the experience of wonder and grace — the unfolding of divinity within and the perception of divinity wherever you go.
- **All manifest creation** is the result of the unmanifest transforming itself into the manifest; all things come from the unmanifest.
- The **physical universe** is the Self curving back within Itself to experience Itself as spirit, mind, and physical matter.
- **The Seven Spiritual Laws** are the same underlying principles nature uses to create all things — a forest, a galaxy, a star, or a human body.
