# Analysis of Chapter 10: The Opulence of the Absolute

## Actionable Lessons

### Lesson 1: Engage Directly in Krishna Consciousness
**State:** Take directly to the process of Krishna consciousness and happily live in this material world.

**Step-by-step instructions:**
1. Always engage in Krishna consciousness
2. Live happily in the material world while being Krishna conscious
3. Allow accidental nondevotional activities to diminish through consistent practice
4. Continue until situated in complete perfection without doubt

**Best practices / Keep-in-mind:**
- The pure devotee has no actual chance to fall down because the Supreme Godhead personally takes care of His pure devotees
- The intelligent person should take this path

---

### Lesson 2: Hear About the Lord in the Association of Devotees
**State:** Always hear about the Lord in the association of devotees.

**Step-by-step instructions:**
1. Seek association with devotees who are really anxious to be in Krishna consciousness
2. Engage in discourses in the society of devotees
3. Continuously hear about the Lord

**Best practices / Keep-in-mind:**
- The more one hears about the Supreme God, the more one becomes fixed in devotional service
- Discourses in the society of devotees can take place only among those really anxious to be in Krishna consciousness
- Others cannot take part in such discourses

---

### Lesson 3: Do Not Try to Understand Krishna by Mental Speculation
**State:** Avoid mental speculation to understand the Supreme Truth.

**Step-by-step instructions:**
1. Do not try to understand Krishna by mental speculation
2. Do not consider the impersonal Brahman as the Supreme
3. Accept that scholarship is not the qualification necessary to understand Krishna

**Best practices / Keep-in-mind:**
- Even demigods and great sages have tried to understand Krishna by mental speculation and failed
- One can actually understand Krishna by studying His words in Bhagavad-gita and Srimad-Bhagavatam
- The impersonal Brahman can be conceived by persons in the inferior energy, but the Personality of Godhead cannot be conceived unless one is in the transcendental position

---

### Lesson 4: Follow the Disciplic Succession (Parampara System)
**State:** Understand Bhagavad-gita through the parampara system of disciplic succession.

**Step-by-step instructions:**
1. Receive knowledge through a bona fide spiritual master
2. Follow the disciplic succession
3. Do not rely on so-called academic education alone to understand Bhagavad-gita

**Best practices / Keep-in-mind:**
- The parampara system was lost, and Krishna reestablished it with Arjuna
- Bhagavad-gita should be understood in the parampara system
- Unless one is in the disciplic succession, he cannot understand Bhagavad-gita
- Those proud of academic education, despite evidence in Vedic literatures, stick to obstinate conviction that Krishna is an ordinary person

---

### Lesson 5: Develop the Qualities Created by Krishna
**State:** Cultivate the qualities created by Krishna in living entities.

**Step-by-step instructions:**
1. Cultivate intelligence - power of analyzing things in proper perspective
2. Cultivate knowledge - understanding what is spirit and what is matter
3. Develop freedom from doubt and delusion (asammoha) - accept nothing blindly, accept with care and caution
4. Practice forgiveness (ksama) - excuse the minor offenses of others
5. Practice truthfulness (satyam) - present facts as they are for the benefit of others; do not misrepresent facts; speak truth even when unpalatable
6. Practice self-control (damah) - do not use senses for unnecessary personal enjoyment
7. Practice calmness (samah) - do not allow the mind to indulge in unnecessary thoughts; do not spend time pondering over earning money
8. Align pleasure and pain with Krishna consciousness - accept what is favorable, reject what is unfavorable
9. Understand that birth and death apply to the body, not the soul
10. Eliminate fear - a person in Krishna consciousness has no fear
11. Accept life and death without attachment or aversion
12. Practice equanimity (samata) - accept without attachment or aversion; accept what is favorable for Krishna consciousness, reject what is unfavorable
13. Practice satisfaction (tusti) - do not be eager to gather more material goods; be satisfied with whatever is obtained by grace of the Supreme Lord
14. Practice austerity (tapah) - rise early, take bath, fast on certain days; but do not fast unnecessarily or against Vedic injunctions; do not fast for political purpose (described as fasting in ignorance)
15. Practice charity (danam) - give fifty percent of earnings to a good cause; give to a person engaged in Krishna consciousness; traditionally given to brahmanas
16. Accept fame and infamy without attachment

**Best practices / Keep-in-mind:**
- One who engages in devotional service of the Supreme Lord develops all good qualities
- Academic knowledge obtained by university education pertains only to matter and is not complete knowledge
- Truthfulness means facts should be presented as they are for the benefit of others
- Self-control means restraining senses from unnecessary use
- The mind should be used to understand the prime necessity of human beings, presented authoritatively
- Fasting done in terms of Vedic injunctions enriches one in spiritual knowledge; anything done in ignorance or passion does not lead to spiritual advancement

---

### Lesson 6: Know Krishna as the Source of All
**State:** Understand that Krishna is the source of all spiritual and material worlds.

**Step-by-step instructions:**
1. Know Krishna as the unborn, the beginningless, the Supreme Lord of all worlds
2. Understand that everything emanates from Krishna
3. Accept that Krishna is the Supreme Personality of Godhead

**Best practices / Keep-in-mind:**
- Anyone who knows Krishna as the unborn and Supreme Lord is freed from all sins
- The word "aja" (unborn) regarding Krishna should not be confused with living entities who are also described as aja
- Krishna is different from everything created; He is the creator of Brahma, Siva, and all demigods
- The intelligent person should know Krishna as the Supreme

---

### Lesson 7: Engage in Unalloyed Devotional Service
**State:** Engage in unalloyed devotional service based on knowledge of Krishna's opulences.

**Step-by-step instructions:**
1. Know in truth the glory and power of Krishna
2. Engage in unalloyed devotional service
3. Accept Krishna with great faith and without any doubt
4. Use one's energy in Krishna consciousness

**Best practices / Keep-in-mind:**
- Unless one is firmly convinced of the different opulences of the Supreme Lord, one cannot engage in devotional service
- When one factually knows the opulences of the Supreme, there is no alternative but to surrender to Him
- All Vedic literature agrees that Krishna is the source of Brahma, Siva and all other demigods
- One should not neglect to understand fully how great Krishna is

---

### Lesson 8: Be a Pure Devotee
**State:** Become a pure devotee whose thoughts dwell in Krishna.

**Step-by-step instructions:**
1. Keep thoughts fully engaged in Krishna
2. Live a life surrendered to the service of Krishna
3. Discuss Krishna with other devotees
4. Glorify the pastimes of the Supreme Lord 24 hours daily
5. Constantly chant Hare Krishna: "Hare Krishna, Hare Krishna, Krishna Krishna, Hare Hare/ Hare Rama, Hare Rama, Rama Rama, Hare Hare"
6. Continuously hear transcendental literature about Krishna

**Best practices / Keep-in-mind:**
- Pure devotees engage fully in transcendental loving service of the Lord
- Their minds cannot be diverted from the lotus feet of Krishna
- Their talks are solely on transcendental subjects
- In the preliminary stage, relish transcendental pleasure from service itself; in mature stage, situated in love of God
- The devotional service seed sown in the heart fructifies with regular hearing and chanting
- Srimad-Bhagavatam is very dear to devotees and should be continually heard

---

### Lesson 9: Let Krishna Give You Understanding
**State:** Allow Krishna to give you the understanding by which you can come to Him.

**Step-by-step instructions:**
1. Be constantly devoted to Krishna
2. Worship Krishna with love
3. Render all kinds of services to Krishna with love and devotion
4. Perform some sort of work for Krishna

**Best practices / Keep-in-mind:**
- Krishna gives real intelligence (buddhi-yoga) to those who are constantly devoted and worship Him with love
- Buddhi-yoga is action in Krishna consciousness, the highest intelligence
- Complete yoga (bhakti-yoga or buddhi-yoga) is the highest perfectional stage of life
- If a devotee is sincere and devoted, Krishna gives him a chance to make progress and ultimately attain Him

---

### Lesson 10: Avoid Unauthorized Commentaries on Bhagavad-gita
**State:** Avoid unauthorized commentaries and interpretations on Bhagavad-gita.

**Step-by-step instructions:**
1. Do not try to understand Krishna as a human being
2. Do not read or follow unauthorized commentaries on Bhagavad-gita
3. Proceed in Krishna consciousness with determination and firmness
4. Do not be bewildered by fools

**Best practices / Keep-in-mind:**
- Only a foolish person thinks Krishna to be a human being
- A Krishna conscious person should not be bewildered by fools

---

### Lesson 11: Give Charity to Krishna Conscious Causes
**State:** Practice charity as part of spiritual advancement.

**Step-by-step instructions:**
1. Give fifty percent of earnings to some good cause
2. Give to a person or cause engaged in Krishna consciousness
3. Traditionally, charity should be given to brahmanas

**Best practices / Keep-in-mind:**
- Charity given to a person engaged in Krishna consciousness is not only good cause, but the best cause
- Because Krishna is good, His cause is also good

---

### Lesson 12: Trust That Krishna Provides for the Pure Devotee
**State:** The pure devotee does not need to worry about necessities of life.

**Step-by-step instructions:**
1. Engage in devotional service
2. Constantly chant Hare Krishna
3. Remove darkness from heart through devotional service

**Best practices / Keep-in-mind:**
- When one removes darkness from his heart, everything is provided automatically by the Supreme Lord
- The pure devotee need not be anxious about necessities of life

---

## Summary

Chapter 10 emphasizes that Krishna is the source of all opulences and everything in the material and spiritual worlds. The primary actionable lesson is to engage in unalloyed devotional service (Krishna consciousness) through hearing, chanting, and association with devotees. One should develop godly qualities, practice austerity and charity, avoid mental speculation and unauthorized commentaries, and understand that Krishna is the Supreme Personality of Godhead who maintains and pervades all of creation.
