# Analysis of Chapter 6: Sankhya-yoga (Bhagavad Gita)

## Lesson 1: True Renunciation Is Action Without Attachment to Fruits
**Text references:** Texts 1-2

**Step-by-step instructions:**
1. Perform your obligatory duties and necessary work
2. Do not be attached to or shelter yourself in the fruits/results of your work
3. Work as an offering or duty, not for personal gratification

**Examples/Best practices:**
- The example of Lord Caitanya's prayer: desire only causeless mercy of devotional service, not wealth, followers, or beautiful women
- The body's limbs work for the satisfaction of the whole body, not for themselves

**Keep in mind:**
- Artificial renunciation (like sannyasis who cease fire sacrifices but desire impersonal Brahman) is still self-interested
- The true criterion of success is satisfaction of Krsna/ the Supreme, not self-satisfaction
- One who acts for the Supreme whole without self-interest is the perfect sannyasi and perfect yogi

---

## Lesson 2: Yoga and Renunciation Are the Same — Both Require Renouncing Sense Gratification
**Text references:** Text 2

**Step-by-step instructions:**
1. Understand your constitutional position as a part and parcel of the Supreme
2. Realize you have no separate independent identity
3. Give up all material sense gratification
4. Prepare to satisfy the Supreme rather than yourself

**Keep in mind:**
- If you are unable to give up selfish nature activities, jnana (knowledge) and yoga are of no avail
- The real aim is to give up all selfish satisfaction and be prepared to satisfy the Supreme

---

## Lesson 3: The Progressive Nature of Yoga Practice
**Text references:** Text 3

**Step-by-step instructions:**
1. As a neophyte beginner: use work (karma) as the means to enter yoga
2. As one attains yoga: cessation of all material activities becomes the means
3. Progress gradually through the stages: jnana-yoga, dhyana-yoga, to bhakti-yoga

**Keep in mind:**
- Yoga is like a ladder from the lowest material condition to perfect self-realization
- Different parts of the ladder have different names, but the complete ladder is yoga

---

## Lesson 4: Attainment of Yoga Means Renunciation of All Material Desires
**Text references:** Text 4

**Step-by-step instructions:**
1. Renounce all material desires
2. Do not act for sense gratification
3. Do not engage in fruitive activities for personal gain

**Examples/Best practices:**
- A Krsna conscious person works for satisfaction of Krsna and is perfectly detached from sense gratification

**Keep in mind:**
- Without such realization, one must mechanically try to escape material desires before being elevated

---

## Lesson 5: Elevate Yourself by Your Own Mind — The Mind Is Both Friend and Enemy
**Text references:** Texts 5-6

**Step-by-step instructions:**
1. Elevate yourself by your own mind — do not degrade yourself
2. Train the mind so it delivers you from nescience
3. Always engage the mind in Krsna consciousness
4. Conquer the mind to make it your friend

**Examples:**
- "For man, mind is the cause of bondage and mind is the cause of liberation. Mind absorbed in sense objects is the cause of bondage, and mind detached from sense objects is the cause of liberation." (Amrta-bindu Upanisad 2)

**Common pitfalls:**
- Being attracted to sense objects leads to entanglement in material existence
- A mind that is not conquered means living with the greatest enemy, serving lust, anger, avarice, illusion

---

## Lesson 6: When the Mind Is Conquered, Supersoul Is Reached and Dualities Are Transcended
**Text references:** Text 7

**Step-by-step instructions:**
1. Conquer your mind
2. Attain tranquillity through mind control
3. Approach the Supersoul (Paramatma) completely
4. Treat happiness/distress, heat/cold, honor/dishonor equally

**Keep in mind:**
- The mind must admit some superior dictation and follow it
- When controlled, the mind automatically follows the dictation of Paramatma

---

## Lesson 7: Established in Self-Realization — Seeing Everything Equally
**Text references:** Text 8

**Step-by-step instructions:**
1. Acquire knowledge and realization of the Supreme Truth
2. Become satisfied by virtue of this knowledge
3. Become spiritually situated and self-controlled
4. See everything — pebbles, stones, or gold — as the same

**Examples/Best practices:**
- Book knowledge without realization is useless; one must associate with a person in pure consciousness
- Realized knowledge makes one steady in convictions; mere academic knowledge leads to delusion

**Keep in mind:**
- A Krsna conscious person has realized knowledge by grace of Krsna
- For the realized soul, mundane scholarship is of no greater value than pebbles or stones

---

## Lesson 8: The Advanced Yogi Regards All Beings With Equal Mind
**Text references:** Text 9

**Step-by-step instructions:**
1. Regard the honest well-wisher, friends, enemies, the envious, relatives, pious, sinners, and the indifferent with equal mind
2. Cultivate equal vision (sama-buddhi) toward all beings

**Keep in mind:**
- This is described as being "still further advanced" beyond the previous stage

---

## Lesson 9: The Daily Practice of the Transcendentalist
**Text references:** Texts 10-14

**Step-by-step instructions:**
1. Constantly concentrate your mind on the Supreme Self
2. Live alone in a secluded place
3. Carefully control your mind
4. Be free from desires and feelings of possessiveness
5. Go to a sanctified place and lay kusa grass, cover with deerskin and soft cloth
6. Use a seat that is neither too high nor too low
7. Sit very firmly on the seat
8. Practice yoga by controlling mind and senses, purifying the heart, and fixing the mind on one point
9. Hold body, neck, and head erect in a straight line
10. Stare steadily at the tip of the nose
11. With an unagitated, subdued mind, devoid of fear
12. Be completely free from sex life (brahmacarya vow)
13. Meditate upon the Lord within the heart
14. Make the Lord the ultimate goal of life

**Examples/Best practices:**
- Sacred places for practice include Prayaga, Mathura, Vrndavana, Hrsikesa, Hardwar
- For this age (Kali-yuga), the best means is chanting the holy name of the Lord: "Hare Krsna, Hare Krsna, Krsna Krsna, Hare Hare / Hare Rama, Hare Rama, Rama Rama, Hare Hare"

**Keep in mind:**
- In Kali-yuga, people are short-lived, slow in spiritual realization, and disturbed by anxieties
- Popular so-called yoga societies in big cities are not suitable for actual yoga practice

**Common pitfalls:**
- One who is not self-controlled and whose mind is not undisturbed cannot practice meditation
- One cannot enjoy sex life daily and attend yoga classes and become a yogi
- Sex life is the chief sense gratification to avoid; complete abstinence is required for correct yoga practice

---

## Lesson 10: Regulate Diet, Sleep, Work, and Recreation
**Text references:** Texts 16-17

**Step-by-step instructions:**
1. Eat moderately — not too much, not too little
2. Sleep moderately — not too much, not too little (ideally no more than six hours)
3. Work in a regulated way
4. Recreate in a regulated way

**Examples/Best practices:**
- The best food is remnants offered to Krsna (prasadam)
- Simple food — grains, vegetables, fruits, milk — is in the mode of goodness
- Animal food is for those in the mode of ignorance
- One who cooks for himself without offering to Krsna eats only sin
- Srila Rupa Gosvami could not sleep more than two hours a day
- Thakura Haridasa would not accept prasadam nor sleep without finishing his daily chanting

**Common pitfalls:**
- Eating more than required leads to dreaming while sleeping and needing more sleep
- Sleeping more than six hours out of twenty-four indicates being in the mode of ignorance
- A person in the mode of ignorance is lazy and prone to sleep a great deal

---

## Lesson 11: Attaining Yoga — Disciplined Mind Free from Material Desires
**Text references:** Text 18

**Step-by-step instructions:**
1. Discipline mental activities through yoga practice
2. Become situated in transcendence
3. Become devoid of all material desires (especially sex, which is the chief)

**Keep in mind:**
- A perfect yogi is so well disciplined that he can no longer be disturbed by any material desire
- This stage can automatically be attained by persons in Krsna consciousness

---

## Lesson 12: The Steady Mind — Like a Lamp in a Windless Place
**Text references:** Text 19

**Step-by-step instructions:**
1. Control the mind
2. Constantly engage in meditation on the transcendent self
3. Be steady and unwavering in your practice

**Keep in mind:**
- A truly Krsna conscious person is as steady as a lamp in a windless place

---

## Lesson 13: The Stage of Perfection — Samadhi (Trance)
**Text references:** Texts 20-23

**Step-by-step instructions:**
1. Practice yoga to restrain mental activities from material mental activity
2. See the self by the pure mind
3. Relish and rejoice in the self
4. Experience boundless transcendental happiness
5. Enjoy through transcendental senses
6. Never depart from the truth
7. Consider no gain greater than this attainment

**Keep in mind:**
- In this state, one is never shaken even in the midst of greatest difficulty
- This is actual freedom from all miseries arising from material contact
- The purpose of yoga is cessation of material existence, entering the spiritual sky (abode of the Lord)
- No one can reach the spiritual sky without proper understanding of Krsna

---

## Lesson 14: Practice Yoga with Firm Determination — Abandon All Material Desires
**Text references:** Text 24

**Step-by-step instructions:**
1. Engage in yoga practice with undeviating determination and faith
2. Abandon without exception all material desires born of false ego
3. Control all the senses on all sides by the mind

**Examples:**
- The sparrow who lost her eggs to the ocean: with determination she dried up the ocean, and Garuda came to help. Similarly, practice yoga with determination and the Lord will help.

**Keep in mind:**
- Practice with perseverance and determination in association of devotees
- The process of bhakti-yoga succeeds with enthusiasm, perseverance, determination, following prescribed duties, and association with devotees

---

## Lesson 15: Gradually Fix the Mind on the Self Alone
**Text references:** Text 25

**Step-by-step instructions:**
1. Gradually, step by step, with full conviction
2. Become situated in trance by means of intelligence
3. Fix the mind on the self alone
4. Think of nothing else

**Keep in mind:**
- This cessation of sense activities is called pratyahara
- In Krsna consciousness, this state is easily attained

---

## Lesson 16: Withdraw the Wandering Mind and Bring It Back Under Control
**Text references:** Text 26

**Step-by-step instructions:**
1. Observe wherever the mind wanders due to its flickering and unsteady nature
2. Withdraw it from there
3. Bring it back under the control of the self

**Keep in mind:**
- One who controls the mind (and senses) is called gosvami or svami
- One who is controlled by the mind is called go-dasa, or servant of the senses
- Serving Krsna with purified senses is Krsna consciousness — this is the highest perfection of yoga

---

## Lesson 17: The Yogi Fixed on the Lord Attains the Highest Happiness and Liberation
**Text references:** Texts 27-28

**Step-by-step instructions:**
1. Fix the mind on the lotus feet of Krsna
2. Practice yoga steadily
3. Become freed from all material contamination
4. Touch the supreme consciousness (brahma-samsparsa)

**Keep in mind:**
- One cannot remain in Brahman quality until the mind is fixed on lotus feet of the Lord
- Self-realization means knowing one's constitutional position as part and parcel of the Supreme

---

## Lesson 18: See the Lord in All Beings and All Beings in the Lord
**Text references:** Texts 29-32

**Step-by-step instructions:**
1. Observe the Lord (Supersoul/Paramatma) situated in all beings
2. See every being in the Lord
3. Know that the Lord is present in everyone's heart as Paramatma
4. See that all living entities are servants of God — whether serving material senses or Supreme Lord directly

**Keep in mind:**
- The Lord is neutral to all beings like a mother to different children
- The individual soul is in one heart; the Supersoul is in all hearts
- A Krsna conscious person sees Krsna in the heart of both believer and nonbeliever
- One who sees Krsna everywhere and everything in Krsna is never lost to the Lord

---

## Lesson 19: See the True Equality of All Beings in Happiness and Distress
**Text references:** Text 32

**Step-by-step instructions:**
1. Compare others to your own self
2. See the true equality of all beings in both their happiness and distress
3. Be the perfect yogi by this equal vision

**Keep in mind:**
- The cause of distress is forgetfulness of relationship with God
- The cause of happiness is knowing Krsna as the supreme enjoyer
- A perfect yogi is the best philanthropist, trying to turn every person toward Krsna consciousness

---

## Lesson 20: The Mind Is Restless but Can Be Controlled by Practice and Detachment
**Text references:** Texts 34-36

**Step-by-step instructions:**
1. Acknowledge the mind is restless, turbulent, obstinate, and very strong
2. Practice yoga with constant practice (abhyasa)
3. Practice with detachment (vairagya)
4. Engage the mind fully in Krsna

**Examples/Best practices:**
- The easiest way to control the mind is chanting "Hare Krsna" — the great mantra for deliverance
- Hearing about Krsna is the expert treatment for the mad mind
- Eating foodstuff offered to Krsna is the appropriate diet

**Keep in mind:**
- For an ordinary person in Kali-yuga, the strict eightfold yoga system is impractical
- Krsna consciousness is the easiest method: engage the mind fully in Krsna so there are no other engagements to agitate the mind

---

## Lesson 21: No Fear of Destruction for the Auspicious Transcendentalist
**Text references:** Texts 37-40

**Step-by-step instructions:**
1. Engage in auspicious activities (Krsna consciousness)
2. Even if you fail to attain perfection, do not fear destruction
3. Continue the practice with faith

**Keep in mind:**
- A transcendentalist engaged in auspicious activities does not meet destruction in this world or the next
- One who does good is never overcome by evil
- Even a little endeavor on the transcendental path offers great hope for deliverance

---

## Lesson 22: The Destinations of the Unsuccessful Yogi
**Text references:** Texts 41-44

**Step-by-step instructions:**
1. If you fall from the path of yoga, you may dwell for many years on the planets of the pious
2. Or take birth in a family of righteous people, rich aristocracy, or transcendentalists with great wisdom
3. Upon such birth, revive the divine consciousness of previous life
4. Try again to make further progress toward complete success

**Keep in mind:**
- Such a birth in a family of yogis or transcendentalists is very rare
- Previous practice of yoga attracts one automatically to yogic principles
- Transcendental endeavors never go in vain — the transcendentalist gets repeated opportunities for complete perfection

---

## Lesson 23: By Rigid Practice Over Many Births, Attain the Supreme Goal
**Text references:** Text 45

**Step-by-step instructions:**
1. Engage in sincere endeavor with rigid practice
2. Let yourself be washed clean of all contaminations
3. Continue over many, many births of practice
4. Ultimately attain the supreme goal (Krsna consciousness)

**Keep in mind:**
- Krsna consciousness is the perfect stage of being freed of all contaminations
- After many births of pious activities, when freed from all contaminations and illusory dualities, one becomes engaged in transcendental loving service

---

## Lesson 24: A Yogi Is Greater Than the Ascetic, Empiricist, and Fruitive Worker
**Text references:** Text 46

**Step-by-step instructions:**
1. Be a yogi — one who links consciousness with the Supreme Absolute Truth
2. Understand that bhakti-yoga (Krsna consciousness) is the ultimate perfection of all yogas

**Keep in mind:**
- Asceticism without self-knowledge is imperfect
- Empiric knowledge without surrender to the Supreme Lord is imperfect
- Fruitive work without Krsna consciousness is a waste of time
- Bhakti-yoga is the culmination: karma-yoga → jnana-yoga → astanga-yoga → bhakti-yoga

---

## Lesson 25: The Highest Yogi Is the Devotee Who Always Abides in the Lord
**Text references:** Text 47

**Step-by-step instructions:**
1. Always abide in the Lord (Krsna) with great faith
2. Render transcendental loving service (bhajate) to the Lord
3. Always think of the Lord within yourself

**Keep in mind:**
- The word bhajate means service with love and faith — especially for the Supreme Personality of Godhead
- Every living entity is intended to serve the Supreme Lord by his own constitution
- Failing to do this, one falls down from constitutional position
- Bhakti-yoga is the highest of all yogas
- All other yogas are but means to come to the point of bhakti-yoga

---

## Summary of Key Principles
- **Mind control** is central: the mind must be trained and conquered
- **Krsna consciousness** is the easiest and highest method in this age
- **Regulation** in eating, sleeping, working, and recreation is essential
- **Seclusion** and solitude support deep practice
- **Determination and perseverance** are required — do not give up
- **Service and devotion** (bhakti) surpass all other yogic paths
- **Previous spiritual efforts** never go to waste; they bear fruit in due course
- **Equality of vision** toward all beings is the mark of advancement
- The **mind is the friend** of the self-realized and the **enemy** of the uncontrolled
