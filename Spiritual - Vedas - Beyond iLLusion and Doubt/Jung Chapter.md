# BID 15: Carl Jung — Actionable Lessons Analysis
*Source: Beyond Illusion & Doubt (Bhaktivedanta Vedabase)*

## Lesson 1: Accept Kṛṣṇa as the Supreme Leader — Reject Material Substitutes

### Step-by-Step Instructions
1. Recognize that it is natural to accept a leader, and if we do not accept God as leader, we will accept some material substitute.
2. Identify material substitutes that people accept as leaders in place of God — sex, bodily comforts, nationalism, etc.
3. Understand that accepting any material leader means accepting the leadership of māyā (illusion).
4. Make the conscious decision to accept Kṛṣṇa as the only true leader.
5. Align your life to follow Kṛṣṇa's guidance instead of material impulses.

### Key Terms and Keywords
- **Leader** — the natural tendency to accept a guiding authority
- **Māyā** — illusion; leadership of material energy instead of God
- **Freud's dogma** — example of substituting sex as leader in place of God
- **Kṛṣṇa as leader** — accepting the Supreme Personality of Godhead as one's guide
- **Jīvera svarūpa haya — kṛṣṇera nitya-dāsa** — the living entity is an eternal servant of Kṛṣṇa

## Lesson 2: Sublimate the Sex Impulse Through Kṛṣṇa Consciousness

### Step-by-Step Instructions
1. Recognize that the sex impulse is natural for everyone in the material world.
2. When lusty desire arises, think of Kṛṣṇa embracing Rādhārāṇī or dancing with the gopīs — hear these pastimes from a bona fide source.
3. Allow the sex impulse to be sublimated and weakened by absorption in Kṛṣṇa's pastimes.
4. Understand that this is the process of Jung's "integration" or "individuation" — channeling subconscious sexual energy into conscious, creative activity directed toward God realization.
5. Develop devotional service as the proper channel for all material desires.

### Key Terms and Keywords
- **Sex impulse** — natural material drive present in conditioned souls
- **Sublimation** — channeling material desires into spiritual activity
- **Individuation/Integration** — Jung's concept of integrating the subconscious
- **Kṛṣṇa's pastimes** — transcendental activities of the Lord that purify the heart
- **Devotional service** — bhakti; the proper destination for all desires
- **Gopīs** — Kṛṣṇa's devotees whose example shows the highest devotion

## Lesson 3: Understand Kṛṣṇa as the Only Puruṣa — The Only Enjoyer

### Step-by-Step Instructions
1. Recognize that Kṛṣṇa is the only puruṣa, the only direct enjoyer — all others are indirect enjoyers.
2. Understand that the living entities are predominated (prakṛti) and Kṛṣṇa is the predominator (puruṣa).
3. On the material platform, when a wife helps her husband enjoy, she also becomes an enjoyer — similarly, when we help Kṛṣṇa enjoy, we become enjoyers.
4. Apply the analogy: the hand puts the rasagullā into the mouth so the mouth can taste it; the hand cannot taste directly. Kṛṣṇa is the only direct enjoyer; others enjoy by satisfying Him.
5. Redirect your desire to enjoy toward serving Kṛṣṇa's enjoyment.

### Key Terms and Keywords
- **Puruṣa** — the predominator, the enjoyer; Kṛṣṇa is the only true puruṣa
- **Prakṛti** — the predominated, the enjoyed; the living entity's original position
- **Indirect enjoyer** — one who enjoys by serving Kṛṣṇa
- **Supreme enjoyer** — Kṛṣṇa as the center and source of all enjoyment
- **Rasagullā analogy** — the hand/mouth example illustrating indirect enjoyment

## Lesson 4: Accept a Bona Fide Guru to Acquire Perfect Knowledge

### Step-by-Step Instructions
1. Acknowledge the Vedic injunction: to acquire perfect knowledge, one must approach a guru (tad vijñānārthaṁ sa gurum evābhigacchet).
2. Understand that the guru must be a factual representative of God — one who has seen and experienced God, not merely theoretically.
3. Approach such a guru through service, surrender, and sincere inquiry.
4. Recognize that without a self-realized guide, spiritual search remains frustrated.
5. Do not try to learn from a guru what you cannot attain on your own — accept the truth from one who knows it.

### Key Terms and Keywords
- **Guru** — bona fide spiritual master; representative of God
- **Paramparā** — disciplic succession; the chain of teachers descending from God
- **Tad vijñānārthaṁ sa gurum evābhigacchet** — Vedic injunction to approach a guru for transcendental knowledge
- **Sākṣād-dharitvena samasta-śāstraiḥ** — the guru is worshiped as God because he is God's representative
- **Service, surrender, inquiry** — the threefold process of approaching a guru
- **Self-realized guide** — one who has actually seen and experienced God

## Lesson 5: Awaken From the Sleeping State of the Soul

### Step-by-Step Instructions
1. Recognize that the soul is presently in a sleeping state, identified with the body.
2. Understand that the soul must be awakened with the instruction: "You are not the body! You are not the body!"
3. Realize that only the human form has sufficiently developed consciousness to be awakened.
4. Understand that lower life forms are more or less in a dream state and cannot be raised to Kṛṣṇa consciousness.
5. Make the effort to awaken to your real identity as the eternal servant of Kṛṣṇa.

### Key Terms and Keywords
- **Sleeping state** — the soul's condition of bodily identification (ahaṁ mameti)
- **Awakening** — realizing one's true spiritual identity
- **Not the body** — the fundamental teaching of Kṛṣṇa consciousness
- **Consciousness development** — the human form affords the chance for spiritual awakening
- **Kṛṣṇa consciousness** — the awakened state of understanding one's relationship with God
- **Dream state** — the condition of lower life forms unable to comprehend spirit

## Lesson 6: Take Kṛṣṇa as Shelter and Leader

### Step-by-Step Instructions
1. Understand that every living entity is constitutionally a servant and is seeking some master or shelter.
2. Recognize that this natural tendency is seen even in animals — a puppy seeks shelter, a child seeks protection.
3. When consciousness is developed in the human form, take Kṛṣṇa as the proper shelter and leader.
4. Follow Bhagavad-gītā's ultimate instruction: sarva-dharmān parityajya mām ekaṁ śaraṇaṁ vraja — "Abandon all varieties of religion and just surrender unto Me."
5. Understand that taking Kṛṣṇa's shelter is the ultimate solution to all of life's problems.

### Key Terms and Keywords
- **Constitutional position** — the living entity's original nature as a servant of God
- **Shelter (śaraṇa)** — taking refuge under Kṛṣṇa's protection
- **Sarva-dharmān parityajya mām ekaṁ śaraṇaṁ vraja** — Bhagavad-gītā 18.66; the ultimate instruction
- **Master** — the natural tendency to seek a sheltering authority
- **Kṛṣṇa as leader** — the only leader who can give perfect protection and guidance

## Lesson 7: Understand Kṛṣṇa as the Supreme Father and Yourself as His Eternal Servant

### Step-by-Step Instructions
1. Recognize that Kṛṣṇa, or God, is the supreme father of all living entities.
2. Understand that all men are sons of God, born of their father — this is an experience common to everyone at all times.
3. Realize that you are an eternal servant of God (kṛṣṇera nitya-dāsa).
4. Understand the family structure: God is the supreme father, material nature (prakṛti) is the mother, and living entities are the sons.
5. Recognize that a mother cannot produce a child without a father — the supreme father must be there.
6. Adopt the spiritual conception: "I am a member of God's family."

### Key Terms and Keywords
- **Supreme father** — Kṛṣṇa as the father of all living entities
- **Eternal servant** — the living entity's original identity (kṛṣṇera nitya-dāsa)
- **God's family** — the spiritual family headed by Kṛṣṇa as supreme father
- **Prakṛti** — material nature as mother
- **Material nature** — the mother of all living entities; supplied by the supreme father

## Lesson 8: Surrender to God Completely — Accept the Favorable, Reject the Unfavorable

### Step-by-Step Instructions
1. Understand that surrender to God means accepting that which is favorable to God and rejecting that which is unfavorable.
2. Recognize that the devotee is convinced that God will give all protection.
3. Remain humble and meek, thinking yourself as one of the members of God's family.
4. Understand that this is real spiritual communism — thinking, "I am a member of God's family."
5. Do not think that you are independent or can act without reference to God's will.
6. Apply this understanding to daily life: align your actions with Kṛṣṇa's will.

### Key Terms and Keywords
- **Surrender** — full submission to God's will; accepting the favorable and rejecting the unfavorable
- **Humble and meek** — the devotee's attitude toward God and others
- **God's family** — the spiritual community under Kṛṣṇa's protection
- **Spiritual communism** — recognizing everyone as a member of God's family
- **God's protection** — the devotee's conviction that Kṛṣṇa will always care for him

## Lesson 9: Understand God's Personality — Transcendental, Not Material

### Step-by-Step Instructions
1. Recognize that God has a personality with transcendental attributes, not material characteristics.
2. Study God's qualities: kindness, beauty, knowledge, wealth, fame, renunciation — all present unlimitedly.
3. Understand that whatever qualities we possess are minute manifestations of God's unlimited qualities.
4. Reference Bhagavad-gītā 2.12: "Never was there a time when I did not exist, nor you, nor all these kings, nor in the future shall any of us cease to be" — both God and the living entity are eternally persons.
5. Distinguish God's unlimited personality from the individual's limited personality.
6. Understand that God's body is sac-cid-ānanda — eternal, full of knowledge, and full of bliss.

### Key Terms and Keywords
- **Transcendental personality** — God's attributes are not material but spiritual
- **Unlimited attributes** — God's six opulences in full: wisdom, strength, wealth, fame, beauty, renunciation
- **Material vs. transcendental** — God's qualities are not material; they are spiritual
- **Sac-cid-ānanda** — eternal, full of knowledge, full of bliss; God's nature
- **Paramātmā** — the Supersoul, the superconsciousness present in everyone's heart
- **Unlimited person** — God as the supreme person, distinct from limited individual souls

## Lesson 10: Receive Knowledge from Proper Authority — Reject Mere Speculation

### Step-by-Step Instructions
1. Understand that philosophers cannot give a clear picture of God because they are speculating with imperfect minds and senses.
2. Accept that God must be known by revelation, not by mental speculation.
3. Receive clear information about God from God Himself or from His bona fide representative.
4. Reference Bhagavad-gītā 7.1: "Now hear, O Arjuna, how by practicing yoga in full consciousness of Me, with mind attached to Me, you can know Me in full, free from doubt."
5. Always keep yourself under God's protection or the protection of His representative to understand God perfectly.
6. Avoid philosophers who "know of God only by hearsay" — accept Vedic authorities who accept Kṛṣṇa as the Supreme Person.

### Key Terms and Keywords
- **Speculation** — mental concoction limited by imperfect senses and mind
- **Revelation** — God making Himself known through proper authority
- **Hearsay** — secondhand or speculative knowledge of God
- **Bhagavad-gītā 7.1** — knowing God perfectly by yoga in full consciousness of Him
- **Vedic authorities** — Vyāsadeva, Nārada, Śukadeva Gosvāmī, etc.
- **Kṛṣṇa as Supreme** — accepted as God by all Vedic authorities

## Lesson 11: Elevate Your Consciousness While on Earth — Prepare for the Next Life

### Step-by-Step Instructions
1. Understand that only on earth can the general level of consciousness be raised — this is man's metaphysical task.
2. Recognize that consciousness survives death and continues into the next life.
3. If your yoga practice is incomplete or you die prematurely, your consciousness accompanies you and begins at the point where you left off in the next life.
4. Reference Bhagavad-gītā 6.43: tatra taṁ buddhi-saṁyogaṁ labhate paurva-dehikam — "On taking rebirth, one revives the consciousness of his previous life."
5. Make regular progress in Kṛṣṇa consciousness now so that your hands will not be empty at the end of life.
6. Understand that extraordinary intelligence in some people indicates consciousness developed in a previous life.

### Key Terms and Keywords
- **Consciousness** — the continuing factor that transmigrates at death
- **Metaphysical task** — elevating consciousness during earthly life
- **Bhagavad-gītā 6.43** — revival of previous consciousness at rebirth
- **Kṛṣṇa consciousness** — the elevated state of God consciousness
- **Empty hands** — arriving at death without spiritual advancement
- **Transmigration** — the soul's journey through different bodies according to consciousness

## Lesson 12: Avoid Sinful Activities to Remain Pure and Approach God

### Step-by-Step Instructions
1. Understand that no one can approach God without being purified of all sinful reactions.
2. Recognize that only one standing on the platform of pure goodness (sattva-guṇa) can understand God and engage in His service.
3. Reference Bhagavad-gītā 10.12: Kṛṣṇa is paraṁ brahma paraṁ dhāma pavitraṁ paramaṁ bhavān — "the Supreme Brahman, the ultimate, the supreme abode and purifier."
4. Avoid the four pillars of sinful life: illicit sex, meat-eating, intoxication, and gambling.
5. Understand that purity is the basic principle of God consciousness and is essential for re-establishing your eternal relationship with God.
6. Recognize that Kṛṣṇa consciousness is based on this morality; one who cannot follow these principles falls down from the spiritual platform.

### Key Terms and Keywords
- **Pure goodness (sattva-guṇa)** — the platform of purity necessary to approach God
- **Four pillars of sinful life** — illicit sex, meat-eating, intoxication, gambling
- **Purifier** — Kṛṣṇa as the supreme purifier (pavitraṁ paramam)
- **Paraṁ brahma** — the Supreme Brahman, Kṛṣṇa
- **Purity** — the essential qualification for approaching the supreme pure
- **Sinful reactions** — karma that binds one to material existence

## Lesson 13: Know Your Real Identity — Servant of Kṛṣṇa, Not the False Ego

### Step-by-Step Instructions
1. Recognize that your real persona is that you are the eternal servant of God.
2. Understand that as long as you identify with material designations — "I am American," "I am Hindu," "I am a professor" — you labor under the delusion of the false ego.
3. Realize that when you understand your real identity as servant of Kṛṣṇa, your persona becomes your salvation and perfection.
4. Reference Caitanya Mahāprabhu: gopī-bhartuḥ pada-kamalayor dāsa-dāsānudāsaḥ — "I am the servant of the servant of the servant of Kṛṣṇa, the maintainer of the gopīs."
5. Practice bhakti (devotional service) to be purified of false identifications.
6. Do not become identical with your material personas — the professor with his textbook, the singer with his voice.

### Key Terms and Keywords
- **False ego** — identifying with the body and its designations rather than the soul
- **Real persona** — understanding oneself as the eternal servant of Kṛṣṇa
- **Purified ego** — the real ego that understands one's relationship with God
- **Dāsa-dāsānudāsa** — servant of the servant of Kṛṣṇa; the real identity
- **Material designations** — bodily and social identities that obscure the soul
- **Bhakti** — devotional service; the means to purify false identification

## Lesson 14: Render Service to Kṛṣṇa — The Path to True Happiness

### Step-by-Step Instructions
1. Understand that the living entity's intrinsic characteristic is to render service — to someone.
2. Recognize that if you render service to Kṛṣṇa, you will actually be happy — material service never satisfies fully.
3. Understand Kṛṣṇa's instruction in Bhagavad-gītā 18.66: "Just give up all other service and render service unto Me, and I will free you from all sinful reactions."
4. Do not be diverted by envy of the rich or by materialistic philosophies — engage in God's service.
5. Recognize that both communists and capitalists say "Render service to me," but Kṛṣṇa says "Render service to Me."
6. Understand that serving Kṛṣṇa brings real peace to the world.

### Key Terms and Keywords
- **Service (sevā)** — the living entity's intrinsic function; rendering to Kṛṣṇa
- **Bhagavad-gītā 18.66** — Kṛṣṇa's instruction to surrender and serve Him exclusively
- **Sinful reactions** — karma accumulated by serving material desires
- **Material service** — serving bodily and mental demands that never satisfy
- **Real peace** — the result of universal Kṛṣṇa consciousness
- **God's service** — the only service that brings complete satisfaction

## Lesson 15: Become a Guru by Distributing Kṛṣṇa's Instructions

### Step-by-Step Instructions
1. Understand that it is not necessary to be a learned scholar to become a guru.
2. Accept Caitanya Mahāprabhu's instruction: "Wherever you are, simply become a guru and deliver all these people who are in ignorance."
3. Learn to explain Bhagavad-gītā as it is — whoever explains the Bhagavad-gītā as it is is a guru.
4. Do not worry about being a "learned scholar" — many so-called scholars are fools anyway.
5. Understand that the spiritual master teaches his disciple how to always remain in the ocean of God consciousness.
6. Recognize that one who works under the directions of the ācārya knows everything about God.

### Key Terms and Keywords
- **Guru** — one who distributes Kṛṣṇa's instructions ( Bhagavad-gītā as it is)
- **Ācārya** — the spiritual master who teaches by example and instruction
- **Caitanya Mahāprabhu's movement** — the injunction to become a guru and deliver the ignorant
- **Bhagavad-gītā as it is** — the scripture that makes one a guru when properly explained
- **Learned scholar** — not required; sincerity and understanding of Kṛṣṇa's instructions are sufficient
- **Deliver the ignorant** — the guru's mission to spread Kṛṣṇa consciousness

## Lesson 18: Combat Materialistic Progress With Inner Transformation

### Step-by-Step Instructions
1. Recognize that a materially favorable environment merely strengthens the dangerous tendency to expect everything to originate from outside.
2. Understand that the metamorphosis which external reality cannot provide is a deep-seated change of the inner man.
3. Realize that everything originates from inside, from the soul — not from external material conditions.
4. Understand that material progress is essentially an expansion of the external energy (māyā, illusion).
5. Minimize necessities to live a saintly life — do not pursue luxurious living.
6. Focus life on spiritual progress and Kṛṣṇa consciousness, not on material advancement.

### Key Terms and Keywords
- **Inner man** — the soul; the real locus of transformation
- **External energy (māyā)** — illusion; material progress as expansion of māyā
- **Material progress** — advancement of external civilization; not the goal of life
- **Deep-seated change** — inner spiritual transformation; the only real change
- **Saintly life** — minimizing material necessities for spiritual advancement
- **Materialistic progress** — the enemy of the individual's spiritual development

## Lesson 22: Understand That Personal Karma Is Continuous — The Soul Carries Its Consciousness

### Step-by-Step Instructions
1. Recognize that karma is always personal — one's actions follow the soul at death.
2. Understand that after death, the soul carries the mind, intelligence, and ego (the subtle body) into another gross body.
3. Reference Bhagavad-gītā 4.9 and 6.43: consciousness continues after death and one revives previous consciousness at rebirth.
4. Do not foolishly think that everything is finished at death — the subtle body continues.
5. Reference Bhagavad-gītā: na hanyate hanyamāne śarīre — the soul and its subtle body are never destroyed when the gross body is destroyed.
6. Use this understanding to elevate your consciousness now, because it will continue into your next life.

### Key Terms and Keywords
- **Personal karma** — one's own actions that follow the soul at death
- **Subtle body** — mind, intelligence, and ego that accompany the soul after death
- **Gross body** — the material body that is destroyed at death
- **Bhagavad-gītā 4.9** — one who knows Kṛṣṇa's transcendental nature does not take birth again
- **Bhagavad-gītā 6.43** — revival of previous consciousness at rebirth
- **Na hanyate hanyamāne śarīre** — the soul is not killed when the body is killed
- **Transmigration** — the soul's journey from one body to another according to consciousness

- **Māyā** — illusion; the attempt to imitate Kṛṣṇa as the enjoyer
- **Jīvātmā** — the individual soul
- **Predominated** — the living entity's real position as served by Kṛṣṇa
- **Shadow personality** — Jung's concept; the repressed feminine aspect in males and vice versa

### Step-by-Step Instructions
1. Understand that you are not the body, not the mind, not the intelligence, not the social designations.
2. Use negation: "I am not this, I am not that" — systematically reject all false identifications.
3. After negating all false identities, understand what remains: the soul's real identity.
4. Reference Caitanya Mahāprabhu: gopī-bhartuḥ pada-kamalayor dāsa-dāsānudāsaḥ — "I am the servant of the servant of the servant of Kṛṣṇa."
5. Use bhakti (devotional service) to purify yourself of all false identifications.
6. Do not remain subject to various false identifications — realize your eternal position as servant of Kṛṣṇa.

### Key Terms and Keywords
- **Negation** — the process of rejecting false identifications to find the real identity
- **False identifications** — identifying with body, mind, social position, nationality, etc.
- **Real identity** — the soul's eternal position as servant of Kṛṣṇa
- **Bhakti** — devotional service; the means to purify false identifications
- **Gopī-bhartuḥ pada-kamalayor dāsa-dāsānudāsaḥ** — Caitanya Mahāprabhu's expression of real identity
- **Soul's complexity** — Jung's observation; the soul is of unimaginable complexity and cannot be approached through mere psychology of instinct

## Lesson 25: Recognize That Death Is Joyful for the Devotee — Horrible for the Nondevotee

### Step-by-Step Instructions
1. Understand that death is a joyful event for the devotee — it is a return home, back to Godhead.
2. Recognize that death is a horrible catastrophe for one who is going to accept a lower form of life.
3. Understand that if one has not developed spiritual consciousness (Kṛṣṇa consciousness), death is very horrible.
4. Realize that after death, one must accept a body according to the dictations of nature — karmaṇā daiva-netreṇa.
5. Understand the three modes of material nature (tamo-guṇa, rajo-guṇa, sattva-guṇa) determine the next body.
6. Use the human form to make progress in Kṛṣṇa consciousness so that death becomes a joyful return to God.

### Key Terms and Keywords
- **Return home, back to Godhead** — the devotee's joyful return to Kṛṣṇa at death
- **Karmaṇā daiva-netreṇa** — by higher order, the soul receives a body according to its karma
- **Tamo-guṇa, rajo-guṇa, sattva-guṇa** — the three modes of ignorance, passion, and goodness
- **Lower forms of life** — bodies awarded to those without Kṛṣṇa consciousness
- **Kṛṣṇa consciousness** — the only insurance for a joyful death and return to Godhead
- **Material body** — the temporary covering that is destroyed at death
