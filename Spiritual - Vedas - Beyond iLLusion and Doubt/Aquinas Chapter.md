# BID 6: Thomas Aquinas — Actionable Lessons & Key Insights
## Beyond Illusion and Doubt — Chapter Analysis

---

## Chapter Overview

**Source:** *Beyond Illusion and Doubt* (BID), Chapter 6: Thomas Aquinas  
**Subject:** Thomas Aquinas (1225?–1274), leading Christian philosopher of the Middle Ages  
**Format:** Q&A dialogue between Śrīla Prabhupāda and a disciple, comparing Aquinas's ideas with Vedic wisdom  
**Core Thesis:** Many of Aquinas's ideas agree with Vedic teachings (Bhagavad-gītā, Brahma-saṁhitā, Padma Purāṇa, Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, Vedānta-sūtra, Śvetāśvatara Upaniṣad), but Aquinas errs in claiming the material universe was created *ex nihilo* (out of nothing) and in his views on the soul being created.

## Actionable Lessons

---

### Lesson 1: Pursue Truth Through Both Reason and Divine Revelation

**Scripture Reference (Bg. 4.34):**
> *tad viddhi praṇipātena paripraśnena sevayā upadekṣyanti tad jñānaṁ jñāninas tattva-darśinaḥ*

**Step-by-step Instructions:**
1. Accept that human reason is imperfect and cannot alone arrive at complete truth.
2. Combine philosophical inquiry (logic, reasoning) with scriptural revelation to approach the Absolute Truth.
3. Recognize that both philosophy and revelation are necessary complementary paths to truth.
4. Do not rely solely on intellectual speculation to understand spiritual reality.

**Key Terms & Keywords:** reason, revelation, truth, logic, philosophy, speculation, imperfect conditioned life, absolute truth, Vedānta-sūtra, disciplic succession

### Lesson 2: Accept a Bona Fide Spiritual Master (Guru) to Learn the Truth

**Scripture Reference (Bg. 4.34, Padma Purāṇa):**
> *sevonmukhe hi jihvādau svayam eva sphuraty adaḥ*

**Step-by-step Instructions:**
1. Approach a bona fide spiritual master (guru) who has seen the Absolute Truth through the disciplic succession.
2. Render submissive, humble service to the spiritual master (*sevayā*).
3. Inquire from him in a submissive mood (*paripraśnena*).
4. Offer obeisances to the spiritual master's lotus feet at least three times a day (*yasya prasādād bhagavat-prasādo*).
5. Understand that without the grace of the spiritual master, one cannot make advancement.
6. Receive the message of truth from one who carries it without personal speculation.

**Key Terms & Keywords:** guru, spiritual master, disciplic succession, submissive service, bona fide, representative of the Absolute Truth, mercy, tattva-darśinaḥ, sevonmukha, Śrī Gurv-aṣṭaka

### Lesson 3: Engage the Senses in Devotional Service to Receive Divine Revelation

**Scripture Reference (Padma Purāṇa):**
> *ataḥ śrī-kṛṣṇa-nāmādi na bhaved grāhyam indriyaiḥ / sevonmukhe hi jihvādau svayam eva sphuraty adaḥ*

**Step-by-step Instructions:**
1. Understand that the material senses cannot directly appreciate the Lord's holy name, form, qualities, and pastimes.
2. Awaken to Kṛṣṇa consciousness by engaging the tongue to chant the Lord's holy name.
3. Taste the remnants of the Lord's food (prasāda).
4. Through this engagement, purify one's consciousness.
5. Gradually, Kṛṣṇa will reveal Himself as He really is — by the mercy of the spiritual master and through devotional service.

**Key Terms & Keywords:** devotional service, bhakti, senses, prasāda, chanting, holy name, Kṛṣṇa consciousness, purification, revelation, sevonmukha, indriyas

### Lesson 4: Honor and Understand Aquinas's Five Arguments (Five Ways) for God's Existence

**Scripture Reference (Brahma-saṁhitā 5.1):**
> *īśvaraḥ paramaḥ kṛṣṇaḥ sac-cid-ānanda-vigrahaḥ / anādir ādir govindaḥ sarva-kāraṇa-kāraṇam*

**Step-by-step Instructions:**
1. Accept the five classical proofs for God's existence as valid and confirmed by Vedic wisdom:
   - **First Way:** God necessarily exists as the First Cause — nothing can cause itself; a chain of causes cannot regress infinitely.
   - **Second Way:** The material world cannot create itself; something external/spiritual is required for initial creation.
   - **Third Way:** Because the world exists, there must be a Creator.
   - **Fourth Way:** Relative perfection in the world necessitates an Absolute Perfection underlying it.
   - **Fifth Way:** The creation has design and purpose; therefore a Designer must have planned it.
2. Understand that everything has a cause, and God is the ultimate cause (*sarva-kāraṇa-kāraṇam*).
3. Use the father-mother analogy: without parents, children cannot be brought into existence — similarly, the universe requires a supreme cause.

**Key Terms & Keywords:** Five Ways, five proofs, God's existence, first cause, efficient cause, absolute perfection, design, purpose, creator, sarva-kāraṇa-kāraṇam, īśvaraḥ, Brahma-saṁhitā, cosmological argument, teleological argument

### Lesson 5: Understand That the Material World Is God's Energy — Not "Nothing"

**Scripture Reference (Bg. 7.4, Śvetāśvatara Upaniṣad 6.8):**
> *bhūmir āpo 'nalo vāyuḥ khaṁ mano buddhir eva ca / ahaṅkāra itīyaṁ me bhinnā prakṛtir aṣṭadhā*  
> *parāsya śaktir vividhaiva śrūyate / svābhāvikī jñāna-bala-kriyā ca*

**Step-by-step Instructions:**
1. Reject the idea that the universe was created "out of nothing" (*ex nihilo*).
2. Understand that the material world is an expansion of the Supreme Lord's separated material energy.
3. Recognize that the material world is real but inferior and temporary — like a reflection or mirage of the spiritual world.
4. Accept that God's energy is eternally existing with Him; God cannot create without energy.
5. Use the analogy of the tape recorder: the sound comes from the person but is not the person themselves — similarly, the material world is God's energy emanating from Him.
6. Understand the distinction between the superior spiritual energy and the inferior material energy (*prakṛti*): earth, water, fire, air, ether, mind, intelligence, false ego.

**Key Terms & Keywords:** material energy, spiritual energy, prakṛti, separated energy, emanation, mirage, reflection, illusion, temporary, inferior energy, superior energy, parāsya śaktiḥ, Bhagavad-gītā 7.4, ex nihilo

### Lesson 6: Assent to God's Grace Through Bhakti (Devotional Service) for Salvation

**Scripture Reference (Padma Purāṇa):**
> *sevonmukhe hi jihvādau svayam eva sphuraty adaḥ*

**Step-by-step Instructions:**
1. Understand that original sin (or disobedience to God) causes the soul to fall into the material world.
2. Recognize that individual free will must assent to God's grace for it to function.
3. Understand that this assent is called *bhakti* — devotional service to the Lord.
4. Engage in eternal devotional service (*nitya-siddha kṛṣṇa-prema*) — this is the soul's original constitutional position.
5. When one engages in devotional service, one is automatically liberated (*mukti* = remaining in one's constitutional position as servant of God).
6. Avoid false material activities based on bodily identification, which constitute illusion (*māyā*).

**Key Terms & Keywords:** bhakti, devotional service, salvation, liberation, mukti, māyā, free will, God's grace, constitutional position, eternal engagement, sin, disobedience, Padma Purāṇa

### Lesson 7: Practice Tapasya (Voluntary Austerity), Beginning with Brahmacarya

**Scripture Reference:**
> *tapasā brahmacaryeṇa*

**Step-by-step Instructions:**
1. Begin the path of austerity (*tapasya*) with *brahmacarya* — voluntary celibacy and control of the sex urge.
2. Progressively control all the senses and the mind.
3. Give everything one possesses to the Lord's service.
4. Follow the path of truth and remain clean in thought, word, and deed.
5. Practice yoga with this foundation of austerity.
6. Advance toward the spiritual kingdom through this disciplined process.
7. Recognize that devotional service alone (*bhakti-yoga*) gives the benefits of all austerities, yoga, karma, and jñāna simultaneously — without needing separate effort.

**Key Terms & Keywords:** tapasya, austerity, brahmacarya, celibacy, sense control, yoga, spiritual advancement, bhakti-yoga, voluntary self-denial, purity

### Lesson 8: Realize That the Soul Is Eternally Existing — Never Created

**Scripture Reference (Bg. 2.20):**
> *na jāyate mriyate vā kadācin / nāyaṁ bhūtvā bhavitā vā na bhūyaḥ / ajo nityaḥ śāśvato 'yaṁ purāṇo / na hanyate hanyamāne śarīre*

**Step-by-step Instructions:**
1. Understand that the individual soul is never created — it is eternally existing alongside God.
2. Recognize that the soul is unborn, eternal, ever-existing, and primeval (*ajaḥ, nityaḥ, śāśvataḥ, purāṇaḥ*).
3. Understand that the soul appears to be born and die only because it accepts a material body — the soul itself does not die when the body dies.
4. Know that the soul has the independence to turn from God — like a spark falling from a great fire, it loses its illumination when separated.
5. Understand that the master (God) and His servants (souls) exist eternally together — not separately created at some point.
6. Know that the soul returns to its original spiritual body upon liberation, without needing to accept another material body.

**Key Terms & Keywords:** soul, ātma, eternal, unborn, never created, nitya, ajaḥ, śāśvataḥ, spiritual body, material body, transmigration, liberation, Bhagavad-gītā 2.20, spark of God

### Lesson 9: Know That the Material Body Is Illusory — A Machine/Clothing for the Real Spiritual Form

**Scripture Reference (Bg. 18.61, 3.27):**
> *prakṛteḥ kriyamāṇāni guṇaiḥ karmāṇi sarvaśaḥ / ahaṅkāra-vimūḍhātmā kartāham iti manyate*

**Step-by-step Instructions:**
1. Understand that matter in itself has no form — the spirit soul has the real form.
2. Recognize that matter is like cloth cut to fit the body — a covering for the actual spiritual form.
3. See the material body as a machine given to the soul by material nature for wandering through the universe.
4. Understand that the material form is temporary — when the body becomes old and useless, matter returns to its original unformed state.
5. Realize that the soul is not the doer of material activities — material nature (guṇas) carries them out, and the soul falsely identifies as the doer due to false ego.
6. Avoid identifying with the material body, knowing the real self is the eternal spiritual being within.

**Key Terms & Keywords:** material body, spiritual body, form, covering, illusion, machine, false ego, ahaṅkāra, prakṛti, guṇas, doer, material nature, Bhagavad-gītā 3.27, Bg. 18.61

### Lesson 10: Beget Children Only If You Can Liberate Them From the Cycle of Birth and Death

**Scripture Reference (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam 5.5.18):**
> *gurur na sa syāt sva-jano na sa syāt / pitā na sa syāj jananī na sā syāt / daivaṁ na tat syān na patiś ca sa syān / na mocayed yaḥ samupeta-mṛtyum*

**Step-by-step Instructions:**
1. Do not beget children unless you are qualified to deliver them from the path of repeated birth and death.
2. If you cannot spiritually educate and liberate your dependents, do not assume roles of spiritual master, father, mother, husband, or worshipable demigod.
3. Ensure that children receive a spiritual education from the moment of their conception and upbringing.
4. Recognize that sex should be exclusively for procreation within a spiritual framework — not for sense gratification.

**Key Terms & Keywords:** children, procreation, spiritual education, liberation, birth and death, parents' duty, Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam 5.5.18, sense gratification, illicit sex, conservation of species

### Lesson 11: Obey Divine Law When Human and Divine Laws Conflict

**Step-by-step Instructions:**
1. When human laws conflict with divine law, obey the laws of God.
2. Know what your real welfare is — it is understanding the Absolute Truth (*athāto brahma-jijñāsā*, Vedānta-sūtra).
3. Do not blindly follow imperfect leaders who do not follow the supreme controller.
4. Take lessons from the Supreme Person (Kṛṣṇa), who knows everything perfectly — past, present, and future.
5. Avoid "the blind following the blind" — do not follow imperfect persons who claim to be knowledgeable.
6. You may obey human authorities only if they themselves obey the laws of God.

**Key Terms & Keywords:** divine law, human law, obedience, conflict of laws, Kṛṣṇa consciousness, supreme controller, blind following, Vedānta-sūtra, brahma-jijñāsā, welfare of life, rājarṣi

### Lesson 12: Recognize That All Perfection Emanates from God

**Scripture Reference (Bg. 10.41):**
> *yad yad vibhūtimat sattvaṁ śrīmad ūrjitam eva vā / tat tad evāvagaccha tvaṁ mama tejo-’ṁśa-sambhavam*

**Step-by-step Instructions:**
1. Understand that whatever is opulent, beautiful, glorious, or powerful in this world is a minute spark of God's splendor (*tejo-'ṁśa-sambhavam*).
2. See the spiritual world as the source of absolute perfection.
3. Recognize that whatever perfection exists in the material world is merely a reflection of that spiritual perfection (*janmādy asya yataḥ*, Vedānta-sūtra 1.1.2).
4. Direct your worship and aspiration toward the source of all perfection — the Supreme Personality of Godhead.
5. Do not be satisfied with the temporary reflections of perfection in the material world; seek the source.

**Key Terms & Keywords:** perfection, beauty, opulence, splendor, tejo-'ṁśa-sambhavam, spiritual world, reflection, absolute perfection, relative perfection, Bhagavad-gītā 10.41, janmādy asya yataḥ

### Lesson 13: Accept Perfect Philosophy Only from a Self-Realized Soul

**Step-by-step Instructions:**
1. Understand that philosophy coming from a person in direct contact with the Supreme Personality of Godhead is perfect and beneficial.
2. Reject speculative philosophy based on imagination — it is defective and cannot help society.
3. Recognize that due to conditioned, material life, every ordinary person is defective in their reasoning.
4. Seek out a spiritual master who carries the Lord's message without speculation.
5. Accept philosophy only from one who is tattva-darśī — one who has seen the Absolute Truth.

**Key Terms & Keywords:** perfect philosophy, speculative philosophy, self-realized soul, tattva-darśī, imagination, conditioned life, defectiveness, spiritual master, Absolute Truth, revelation

### Lesson 14: Present Scripture As It Is — Avoid Personal Interpretation

**Scripture Reference (Bg. 10.8):**
> *ahaṁ sarvasya prabhavo mattaḥ sarvaṁ pravartate*

**Step-by-step Instructions:**
1. Accept that the meaning of scripture is singular and original.
2. Do not add personal interpretation to scripture — interpretation is equivalent to changing the words of God.
3. If one's mind is imperfect, one has no right to alter or interpret God's words.
4. Present scripture *as it is*, without motive or commentary that distorts the original purport.
5. Recognize that once interpretation begins, the authority of scripture is lost — leading to endless sectarian divisions.
6. Accept that God, as creator of all things, is a person — secondary (impersonal) meanings are unnecessary when God Himself speaks.

**Key Terms & Keywords:** scripture, interpretation, as-it-is, revealed knowledge, authority of scripture, personal interpretation, sectarianism, impersonalism, God as person, Bhagavad-gītā 10.8, revealed truth

### Lesson 15: Know That God Is Personal and Can Be Known Face to Face

**Scripture Reference (Bg. 14.27):**
> *brahmaṇo hi pratiṣṭhāham amṛtasyāvyayasya ca / śāśvatasya ca dharmasya sukhasyaikāntikasya ca*

**Step-by-step Instructions:**
1. Understand that God is a person, not an impersonal force — since He created all persons, He must be the supreme person.
2. Realize that God can be seen at different stages of advancement:
   - As impersonal Brahman (for those who cannot conceive of His personal form).
   - As Paramātmā (Supersoul within the heart, realized through yoga meditation).
   - Face to face as the Supreme Personality of Godhead (for the most advanced devotees).
3. Accept that when Kṛṣṇa (or Christ) incarnates, people see God face to face — this confirms God's personal nature.
4. Do not accept the view that God cannot be seen in this life — He can be seen by advanced, purified devotees.

**Key Terms & Keywords:** God as person, personalism, impersonalism, Brahman, Paramātmā, Bhagavān, face-to-face realization, Supersoul, yoga meditation, purification, advancement, Bhagavad-gītā 14.27

### Lesson 16: Understand God's Names Come from His Activities and Attributes

**Step-by-step Instructions:**
1. Accept that God has infinite names based on His activities, qualities, and pastimes.
2. Do not limit God to a single name or title — His names are all derived from His nature.
3. Use names that describe God's attributes (e.g., Kṛṣṇa = "all-attractive"; Govinda = "one who gives pleasure to the cows and senses"; Vāsudeva = "one who lives everywhere").
4. Recognize that God's names are not separate from God — each name has the same potency as God Himself (*nāmnām akāri bahudhā nija-sarva-śaktiḥ*).
5. Understand that God is both personal (Bhagavān) and impersonal (Brahman), but the impersonal feature is secondary — the personal is the primary, complete understanding.

**Key Terms & Keywords:** names of God, attributes, Kṛṣṇa, all-attractive, Bhagavān, Brahman, Paramātmā, personalism, nāmnām akāri bahudhā, Vāsudeva, Govinda, Oṁ tat sat, acintya-bhedābheda

### Lesson 17: Recognize That God Is Always the Supreme Lord, With Eternal Servants

**Scripture Reference (Śvetāśvatara Upaniṣad 6.8, Brahma-saṁhitā 5.1):**

**Step-by-step Instructions:**
1. Understand that God is always existing as the Lord — His position as master is eternal.
2. Know that the living entities are God's eternal servants — they always exist with Him.
3. Reject the material conception that God was alone before creating the spiritual world.
4. Understand that the material world is created, but the spiritual world and God's eternal servants exist everlastingly.
5. Know that those souls who refuse to serve God in the spiritual world are placed into the material world to serve the illusory energy (māyā) — but the constitutional position of the soul as servant is eternal in either realm.
6. Accept that God has no beginning — He is *anādi* (without origin) and His servants are also eternal.

**Key Terms & Keywords:** eternal servants, Lord, spiritual world, material world, creation, anādi, master-servant relationship, māyā, constitutional position, eternal existence, spiritual energy, Śvetāśvatara Upaniṣad 6.8

### Lesson 18: Practice Devotional Service as the One Process That Gives All Spiritual Benefits

**Step-by-step Instructions:**
1. Understand that devotional service (bhakti-yoga) is the highest yoga — it automatically gives the benefits of karma-yoga, jñāna-yoga, and all other yogic processes.
2. Recognize that by one stroke of devotional service, one acquires the benefits of all austerities and spiritual disciplines.
3. Dedicate all activities — working, eating, sleeping, fighting — to the Supreme Lord (Kṛṣṇa).
4. Avoid remaining idle even for a second; always seek some service engagement for the Lord.
5. Chant the Hare Kṛṣṇa mahā-mantra: *Hare Kṛṣṇa, Hare Kṛṣṇa, Kṛṣṇa Kṛṣṇa, Hare Hare / Hare Rāma, Hare Rāma, Rāma Rāma, Hare Hare*.
6. Engage in the four regulative principles: no meat-eating, no illicit sex, no intoxication, no gambling.
7. Practice Kṛṣṇa consciousness 24 hours a day — the spiritual platform is absolute, so one never becomes tired.

**Key Terms & Keywords:** bhakti-yoga, devotional service, mahā-mantra, Hare Kṛṣṇa, all yogas combined, 24-hour engagement, Kṛṣṇa consciousness, regulative principles, spiritual platform, transcendental, ISKCON
