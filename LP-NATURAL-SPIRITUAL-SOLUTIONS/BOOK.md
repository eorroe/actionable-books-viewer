### Lesson 6: Chapter 5 — Nutrition to Conquer Inflammation and Pain

#### Examples

#### Step 3: Choose High-Quality Water

Avoid tap water with fluoride and chemicals. Opt for natural spring water from underground sources. Filtered water is acceptable if you add back minerals with a pinch of Celtic, Atlantic, Himalayan, or Dead Sea salt. Avoid purified and distilled waters that lack minerals.

Good non-vegan sources (in moderation): dairy, free-range eggs, whey protein. For low endorphin levels, supplement with L-phenylalanine (500 mg twice daily, up to three times daily for one month only).

I trust you and know that you have only my highest good in mind." Visualize Archangel Raphael's emerald-green energy dissolving the cords linking addictive foods to your body.

Implement a rotational diet: after eating something, avoid it for several days. Try new foods every week. Allergic responses create inflammation and endorphin cycles that trap you in unhealthy relationships with substances.
