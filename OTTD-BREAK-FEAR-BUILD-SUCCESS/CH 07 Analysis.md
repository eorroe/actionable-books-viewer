# Chapter 7: Seeds of Fear

## Key Lessons
- Lesson 1: Your Own Power of Thought is the Direct Road to Infinite Intelligence
  - Action: Trust and use your own power of thought instead of fearing external forces or relying on intermediaries
  - Step-by-step:
    1. Recognize that universal power is as available to you as to any external force
    2. Stop fearing the Devil or flattering God
    3. Direct your mind toward Infinite Intelligence through your own thought
    4. Use this direct road as your sole source for appropriating universal power
  - Best practices:
    - Treat thought as your primary tool for accessing power
    - Reject external authority as a necessary gateway
    - Build daily habits of independent, constructive thinking
  - Common pitfalls:
    - Fear of external forces blocks access to internal power
    - Relying on intermediaries instead of direct mental connection

- Lesson 2: Your Mind Attracts What It Dwells Upon
  - Action: Consciously control what you dwell on to attract desirable outcomes
  - Step-by-step:
    1. Monitor your dominant thoughts throughout the day
    2. Identify whether you are dwelling on constructive or negative ends
    3. Redirect thoughts away from greed, avarice, lust, envy, and hatred
    4. Feed your mind thoughts aligned with your desired outcomes
  - Best practices:
    - The Devil intercepts people by feeding them thoughts that destroy constructive thinking
    - Negative states of mind are planted through external agencies including those that appear allied with good
    - Your mind cannot simultaneously dwell on conflicting outcomes
  - Common pitfalls:
    - Allowing fear and doubt to dominate thought patterns
    - Accepting negative thoughts from external sources without filtering them
    - Unknowingly letting others plant seeds of fear and uncertainty in your mind

- Lesson 3: Hypnotic Rhythm Operates Automatically and Must Be Consciously Directed
  - Action: Voluntarily apply hypnotic rhythm to definite desired ends before it automatically operates to attain undesired ends
  - Step-by-step:
    1. Know exactly what you want from life
    2. Demand it and refuse to accept substitutes
    3. Back your demand by a willingness to pay life's price
    4. Let the law of hypnotic rhythm take over your desire
    5. Allow natural and logical means to transmute desire into its physical counterpart
  - Best practices:
    - Hypnotic rhythm is something to be studied, understood, and voluntarily applied
    - It works like a magnet, attracting things for which it has magnetic affinity
    - The demand must become a habit so hypnotic rhythm takes over automatically in a positive direction
    - All successful people use hypnotic rhythm consciously or unconsciously
  - Common pitfalls:
    - Letting hypnotic rhythm operate automatically on negative thoughts
    - Accepting substitutes for what you truly want
    - Failing to establish the demand as a firm habit before hypnotic rhythm takes over

- Lesson 4: Establish Your Own Rhythm of Thought Before Environmental Forces Take Control
  - Action: Establish your individual rhythm of thought early and make it stronger than environmental influences
  - Step-by-step:
    1. Recognize that every environment has its own definite, discernible rhythm
    2. Understand that nature forces minds to conform to environmental rhythms
    3. Exercise your privilege to establish your own rhythm of thought before hypnotic rhythm forces environmental influences on you
    4. Make your own thoughts stronger than the influences around you
    5. Choose environments aligned with the rhythm you want to establish
  - Best practices:
    - Man alone has the power to establish his own rhythm of thought
    - This privilege must be exercised before hypnotic rhythm has forced environmental influences
    - Every home, place of business, town, village, street, and community center has its own rhythm
    - All forms of rhythm become permanent with time
  - Common pitfalls:
    - Allowing children to take on the nature of environmental influences without stronger personal thoughts
    - Waiting too long to establish personal thought rhythm
    - Surrounding yourself with negative environments that force conformity

- Lesson 5: Success and Failure Are Habits That Establish Your Thought Rhythm
  - Action: Build habits of success-oriented thinking to establish a rhythm that attracts desirable possessions
  - Step-by-step:
    1. Recognize that habit establishes one's rhythm of thought
    2. Identify whether your current thought rhythm attracts power, success, and opulence or misery, failure, defeat, discouragement, and poverty
    3. Build new thought habits aligned with your desired outcomes
    4. Let hypnotic rhythm take over the new habit
    5. Allow the law of harmonious attraction to translate it into its physical equivalent
  - Best practices:
    - The person who thinks in terms of power, success, and opulence sets up a rhythm that attracts these
    - The person who thinks in terms of misery and failure attracts undesirable influences
    - Success multiplies with less effort over time for those with positive thought rhythms
    - Poverty-stricken people herd into the same communities due to shared thought rhythms
  - Common pitfalls:
    - Drifting into negative thought habits
    - Assuming success is purely external rather than a result of thought rhythm
    - Underestimating the power of habit in establishing permanent thought patterns

- Lesson 6: Hypnotic Rhythm Can Be Resisted and Reversed from Negative to Positive
  - Action: Learn and apply methods to resist unwanted environmental influences and reverse hypnotic rhythm
  - Step-by-step:
    1. Acknowledge that hypnotic rhythm cannot change physical heredity but can modify, change, control, and make permanent environmental influences
    2. Recognize that you are forced by nature to become part of your chosen or imposed environment
    3. Learn the seven principles of psychology for using hypnotic rhythm to serve instead of destroy
    4. Apply these principles to resist influences of unwanted environments
    5. Reverse the application of hypnotic rhythm from negative to positive ends
  - Best practices:
    - There are definite methods and means to resist environmental influences
    - Heredity and environment are powerful but not absolute determinants
    - The seven principles of psychology provide the roadmap for reversal
    - Willingness to pay life's price is required for transformation
  - Common pitfalls:
    - Assuming heredity and environment are unchangeable destinies
    - Accepting a negative environment as permanent without attempting reversal
    - Failing to study and apply the psychological principles that enable change

- Lesson 7: The Devil Uses Alliances of the Opposition to Plant Seeds of Fear
  - Action: Recognize how fear is planted through seemingly positive channels and protect your mind from drifting
  - Step-by-step:
    1. Understand that the cleverest tactic is using allies of the opposition to keep fear burning
    2. Recognize that churches and other institutions can be used to plant seeds of fear and doubt
    3. Identify how fear, uncertainty, and indefiniteness cause the habit of drifting
    4. Understand that drifting crystallizes into permanency through hypnotic rhythm
    5. Stop drifting by establishing definite, positive thought patterns
  - Best practices:
    - The Devil uses the allies of his opposition as decoys to attract attention while manipulating minds
    - As long as people fear something, no matter what, control is maintained over them
    - Fear of the Devil and refusal to trust God leaves people with only one source: their own power of thought
  - Common pitfalls:
    - Believing that religious or moral fear is inherently protective
    - Unknowingly participating in systems that plant seeds of fear
    - Letting uncertainty and indefiniteness cause mental drifting

## Actionable Principles
- Universal power is equally available to all humans through their own power of thought, requiring no external intermediary
- Your mind attracts exactly what it dwells upon; consciously direct your thoughts toward constructive ends
- Hypnotic rhythm operates automatically and must be consciously directed toward definite desired ends, or it will operate to attain undesired ends
- Establish your own rhythm of thought before environmental influences force conformity through hypnotic rhythm
- Habit establishes thought rhythm, and that rhythm attracts the object of your dominating thoughts
- Success and failure are both habits that can be deliberately cultivated through conscious thought patterns
- Hypnotic rhythm acts like a magnet, attracting circumstances with magnetic affinity to your dominant thoughts
- Heredity cannot be changed by hypnotic rhythm, but environmental influences can be modified, controlled, and made permanent
- There are seven principles of psychology that must be understood and applied to use hypnotic rhythm to serve rather than destroy
- Refusing to accept substitutes for your definite demands is essential for transmuting desire into physical reality
