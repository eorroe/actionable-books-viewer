# BID 5: Augustine — Actionable Lessons

## Source
- **Book:** Beyond Illusion & Doubt (BID)
- **Chapter:** BID 5: Augustine
- **Pages:** 45–52 (approx.)
- **Format:** Dialogue between a disciple and Śrīla Prabhupāda

---

## Lesson 1: "Soul-Death" Is Not Actual Death — It Is Forgetfulness of God

**Step-by-step instructions from the text:**
1. Augustine taught that some people face both physical death and "soul-death" (when God abandons the soul).
2. Śrīla Prabhupāda explains that the soul is eternally existing and cannot actually be destroyed.
3. The "death" Augustine refers to is figurative — it is the state of being unconscious to one's real identity as a servant of God.
4. A person who has forgotten God is like a man asleep: still alive but unconscious.
5. Freedom from material existence is required to be spiritually alive; until then one is "spiritually dead" even though still existing in a material body.

**Key terms and keywords:** soul-death, forgetfulness of God, spiritual consciousness, spiritual death, material existence, consciousness, God consciousness

---

## Lesson 2: Spiritual Consciousness Can Always Be Revived

**Step-by-step instructions from the text:**
1. Consciousness is like a sleeping person: one may be "eternally abandoned" for millions of years, but this is only temporary.
2. Spiritual consciousness can be revived at any moment.
3. Two primary means of revival:
   - **Good association** (satsang)
   - **The method of hearing and chanting about Krishna**
4. Constant practice awakens the spiritual consciousness.
5. One can then return to normal, spiritual life.

**Key terms and keywords:** consciousness revival, good association, hearing and chanting, spiritual awakening, Krishna consciousness, satsang

---

## Lesson 3: Devotional Service Begins With Hearing (Śravaṇam)

**Step-by-step instructions from the text:**
1. Hearing (śravaṇam) is especially important in the beginning of spiritual life.
2. One must hear the truth from a self-realized soul.
3. This hearing awakens one to spiritual life and keeps one spiritually alive in devotional service.
4. The process of chanting the Hare Krishna mantra also awakens spiritual consciousness.

**Key terms and keywords:** śravaṇam, hearing, self-realized soul, devotional service, spiritual life, Hare Krishna mantra, awakening

---

## Lesson 4: The Soul Is Eternal and Uncreated

**Step-by-step instructions from the text:**
1. Augustine believed the soul is created by God and attains immortality only after God brings it into being.
2. Śrīla Prabhupāda challenges this: if the soul is created, how can it be immortal or eternal?
3. The soul is eternally part and parcel of God; it is not created at any point.
4. The soul existed before taking on a material body and continues after the body's annihilation.
5. Every living entity has an eternal spiritual body.

**Key terms and keywords:** eternal soul, uncreated soul, part and parcel of God, spiritual body, material body, immortal soul, eternal existence

---

## Lesson 5: The Body Is Both Punishment and Gift

**Step-by-step instructions from the text:**
1. The body can be considered punishment because it is subject to suffering, old age, disease, and death.
2. Yet human life is also a gift because it provides the opportunity to approach and realize God.
3. One should view the body as God's mercy — even when given for correction, it can be used to purify oneself.
4. Devotees consider the body a reward because, through undergoing its difficulties willingly and practicing Krishna consciousness, one can progress toward God realization.

**Key terms and keywords:** body as punishment, body as gift, material body, human life, purification, God realization, mercy

---

## Lesson 6: The Soul Is Imprisoned in the Material World Through Different Bodies

**Step-by-step instructions from the text:**
1. The soul, being part and parcel of God, is imprisoned in different types of bodies in the material world.
2. According to one's pious or impious activities and desires, the soul assumes higher or lower bodies.
3. The desires of the soul determine the type of body received.
4. The individual soul remains the same regardless of the body it inhabits.
5. Spiritual advancement means seeing the same quality of soul in every body — whether that of a dog or a brahmana.

**Key terms and keywords:** soul imprisonment, material bodies, reincarnation, transmigration, higher and lower bodies, spiritual advancement, pious and impious activities

---

## Lesson 7: Suffering and Enjoyment Are Relative to One's Body

**Step-by-step instructions from the text:**
1. Suffering and enjoyment depend on the type of body one possesses.
2. Perception of cold, heat, pleasure, and pain is relative to the body.
3. The body is the source of both suffering and enjoyment.
4. One must learn to tolerate the nonpermanent appearance and disappearance of happiness and distress.
5. This tolerance is similar to tolerating winter and summer seasons — they arise from sense perception and pass away in due course.

**Key terms and keywords:** suffering and enjoyment, sense perception, body-dependent experience, tolerance, material dualities, happiness and distress

---

## Lesson 8: God Is the Actual Enjoyer — We Are His Servants

**Step-by-step instructions from the text:**
1. A man in ignorance thinks he is the enjoyer of this world.
2. When one contacts the Supreme Personality of Godhead, he understands that God is the actual enjoyer and proprietor.
3. We, the individual souls, are servants meant to supply enjoyment to God.
4. God has no material needs, yet He enjoys the company of His servants, who in turn enjoy His company.
5. Peace is attained when this relationship is understood and restored.

**Key terms and keywords:** God as enjoyer, servant of God, God as proprietor, spiritual consciousness, Krishna consciousness, relationship with God, peace

---

## Lesson 9: God Is Omnipresent as Paramātmā (Supersoul)

**Step-by-step instructions from the text:**
1. Augustine wrote that God is the maker of all souls but denied His existence as the localized Supersoul accompanying each individual.
2. Śrīla Prabhupāda refutes this: the Supersoul is present in everyone's heart, directing the wanderings of all living entities.
3. God pervades and supports the entire universe through a single fragment of Himself.
4. The all-pervading nature of God (Paramātmā) is confirmed in both the Brahma-saṁhitā and the Bhagavad-gītā.

**Key terms and keywords:** Paramātmā, Supersoul, omnipresence, God in the heart, overseer, all-pervading, localized presence

---

## Lesson 10: The Soul Is Different From the Mind

**Step-by-step instructions from the text:**
1. Augustine held that the human mind and soul were one and the same ("reasonable soul").
2. The soul is placed in various bodies that have different minds (different ways of thinking, feeling, and willing).
3. The mind differs according to the body (a dog's mind is not equal to that of a human being), but the soul always remains the same.
4. The mistake of equating soul with mind led Augustine to deny animals the same eternal, reasonable souls as humans.

**Key terms and keywords:** soul vs. mind, reasonable soul, eternal soul, spiritual body, material body, consciousness

---

## Lesson 11: All Living Entities Are Spirit Souls — Do Not Differentiate

**Step-by-step instructions from the text:**
1. Augustine justified animal-killing by arguing that animals lack sensation and association with humans, so "Thou shalt not kill" applies only to humans.
2. Krishna consciousness philosophy teaches that all living entities — humans, animals, and plants — are spirit souls.
3. One living entity serves as food for another — this is a natural law — but the basis of selection should not be sensitivity ranking.
4. Devotees are concerned with Krishna-prasādam: food offered to Krishna with love and devotion.
5. Only vegetarian foods (leaves, flowers, fruits, water, milk, grains) are acceptable offerings to Krishna.
6. Meat, fish, and eggs cannot be offered to Krishna because He did not request them.

**Key terms and keywords:** spirit souls, all living entities, vegetarianism, Krishna-prasādam, food offering, animal life, plant life, sensitivity, sanctified food

---

## Lesson 12: Peace Comes From Understanding God's Supremacy and Restoring the Servant-Master Relationship

**Step-by-step instructions from the text:**
1. Peace with God consists in ordered obedience, guided by faith, under God's eternal law.
2. The individual soul exists in māyā (illusion) when the servant-master relationship with God is destroyed.
3. Peace is attained when the individual is situated in spiritual consciousness (Krishna consciousness).
4. Through this consciousness one understands: God is the actual enjoyer, proprietor, and Supreme Being.
5. When we understand God's transcendental qualities and serve Him as His servant, we become happy and attain peace.

**Key terms and keywords:** peace, God consciousness, Krishna consciousness, servant of God, God as enjoyer, God as proprietor, māyā, illusion, spiritual consciousness
