# Chapter Eight: Attaining Perfection

## Chapter Title and Overview

**Chapter Eight: Attaining Perfection**

This concluding chapter discusses the ultimate goal of the science of self-realization—full realization of the Supreme Self, Krsna. It explains the development of self-realization from understanding that the self is not material to realizing the eternal relationship with Krsna. The chapter emphasizes that pure love of God is the highest perfection and that it is achieved through the mercy of Lord Caitanya and the chanting of the holy names.

## Actionable Lessons

### Lesson 1: Understand the Three Stages of Self-Realization

**Step 1:** First, realize that the self is not material—not a physical body—but pure spiritual energy.

**Step 2:** Next, realize that the pure spiritual self is eternally a part of the Supreme Self, Krsna.

**Step 3:** Finally, learn how to live fully in that eternal, blissful, transcendental relationship.

**Best Practices:**
- ✅ Progress systematically through the stages of self-realization.
- ✅ Engage in devotional service to realize your relationship with Krsna.
- ❌ Don't stop at impersonal Brahman realization.

**Keep In Mind:**
- One who has attained this state has attained life's supreme perfection.
- The development of self-realization begins with understanding that the self is not material.

---

### Lesson 2: Cultivate Pure Love of God (Prema)

**Step 1:** Understand that pure love of God is the highest perfection of devotional service.

**Step 2:** Realize that this love is exhibited when a devotee refuses to accept any benediction from the Supreme Lord.

**Step 3:** Follow Prahlada Maharaja's example: "I am Your eternal servant. It is my duty to serve You, so how can I accept any benefit from it?"

**Best Practices:**
- ✅ Serve Krsna without expecting anything in return.
- ✅ Refuse all material and spiritual benedictions.
- ❌ Don't become a "merchant" devotee who serves God for profit.

**Keep In Mind:**
- As long as one desires material benefit or even salvation, he cannot attain pure love of Godhead.
- Rupa Gosvami says: "As long as one desires some material benefit, or even if he wants salvation, he must take those ghostly representations."

---

### Lesson 3: Follow Lord Caitanya's Teachings

**Step 1:** Accept Lord Caitanya Mahaprabhu as the most munificent incarnation.

**Step 2:** Understand that He directly gives love of God without requiring the difficult practices of previous ages.

**Step 3:** Chant the Hare Krishna maha-mantra as He instructed.

**Best Practices:**
- ✅ Chant Hare Krishna as the method for developing love of God.
- ✅ Study the teachings of Lord Caitanya.
- ❌ Don't think that self-realization is difficult in this age; Lord Caitanya made it easy.

**Keep In Mind:**
- Lord Caitanya is Krsna Himself appearing as a devotee to teach us how to surrender.
- He gives the highest love of God freely to everyone.

---

### Lesson 4: Avoid Material Desires in Devotional Service

**Step 1:** Check your heart for material desires.

**Step 2:** Understand that if you have material desires or even a desire for salvation, you cannot attain pure love of God.

**Step 3:** Serve Krsna without any motive.

**Best Practices:**
- ✅ Serve Krsna favorably, as He desires.
- ❌ Don't serve Krsna for material profit, distinction, or even salvation.

**Keep In Mind:**
- Pure devotion is devoid of all desires—it is simply to render loving service for its own sake.
- Krsna is so kind that He fulfills all desires of a devotee, even material ones.

---

### Lesson 5: Understand the Nature of the Transcendental World

**Step 1:** Realize that in the transcendental world, everyone wants to serve; no one wants to take service.

**Step 2:** Understand that there is no exploitation in the spiritual world.

**Step 3:** See that Krsna also wants to serve His devotees.

**Best Practices:**
- ✅ Cultivate the desire to serve Krsna and His devotees.
- ❌ Don't think that the spiritual world is like the material world where everyone wants to exploit.

**Keep In Mind:**
- In the material world, everyone wants to pickpocket each other; in the transcendental world, everyone wants to give service.
- There is no hunger, no necessity of eating, but still everyone offers nice things to eat.

---

### Lesson 6: Understand That Krsna Is the Master of the Senses

**Step 1:** Accept that Krsna is Hrsikesa—the master of the senses.

**Step 2:** Realize that our senses are borrowed from Krsna.

**Step 3:** Use the senses for Krsna's sense gratification, not our own.

**Best Practices:**
- ✅ Engage all senses in the service of Krsna.
- ❌ Don't use the senses for personal sense gratification.

**Keep In Mind:**
- Our senses are original but now covered by material desires.
- The best thing is to use the senses for Krsna's sense satisfaction.

---

### Lesson 7: Progress Through Faith, Association, and Service

**Step 1:** Begin with faith (sraddha)—inquire about Krsna consciousness.

**Step 2:** Associate with devotees who are cultivating this knowledge.

**Step 3:** Understand their feelings; become one of them.

**Step 4:** Remove all misgivings; develop firm faith and taste.

**Step 5:** Achieve God realization and finally love of God.

**Best Practices:**
- ✅ Mix with devotees to develop taste.
- ✅ Allow your taste to change naturally.
- ❌ Don't expect instant results; progress systematically.

**Keep In Mind:**
- First faith, then association, then removal of misgivings, then firm faith, then taste, then God realization, then love of God.

---

### Lesson 8: Understand the Four Types of Liberation

**Step 1:** Learn the four primary types of liberation:
- Salokya: living on the same planet as the Lord
- Sarsti: having the same opulences as the Lord
- Sarupya: having the same form as the Lord
- Samipya: always being associated with the Lord

**Step 2:** Understand that pure devotees do not accept these; they simply want to serve Krsna.

**Step 3:** Follow the example of the gopis, who have the highest pure love.

**Best Practices:**
- ✅ Aspire for pure love of God, not liberation.
- ❌ Don't accept any kind of liberation if offered by the Supreme Lord.

**Keep In Mind:**
- The gopis' love is the highest because they loved Krsna unconditionally, not knowing whether He was God.
- Pure devotees have no ambitions or motives.

---

### Lesson 9: See Krsna Everywhere and in Everything

**Step 1:** Understand that Krsna says, "I am the taste of water" (raso 'ham apsu).

**Step 2:** Think of the taste of water as Krsna; this begins God realization.

**Step 3:** See Krsna in the sunshine and moonshine (prabhasmi sasi-suryayoh).

**Best Practices:**
- ✅ Meditate on Krsna by seeing Him in everyday experiences.
- ✅ Use the Bhagavad-gita as your guide to understanding God.
- ❌ Don't think that you cannot see God; He is present at every moment.

**Keep In Mind:**
- God can be seen at every moment by those who know how to see Him.
- If we want to become God conscious, it is not very difficult.

---

### Lesson 10: Surrender to Krsna and His Devotees

**Step 1:** Understand that the spiritual master is as good as God.

**Step 2:** Surrender to the bona fide spiritual master.

**Step 3:** Through the spiritual master, surrender to Krsna.

**Best Practices:**
- ✅ Offer respects to the guru as you would to God.
- ❌ Don't think the guru is an ordinary man.

**Keep In Mind:**
- By the mercy of the spiritual master, one receives the benediction of Krsna.
- The guru is the most confidential servant of the Lord.

---

## Examples

1. **The Residents of Vrndavana**: They loved Krsna unconditionally, not knowing whether He was God.
2. **Govardhana Hill**: Krsna lifted the hill at age seven to save the inhabitants of Vrndavana from Indra's wrath.
3. **Putana Demon**: Putana came to kill Krsna, but He accepted her as His mother and liberated her.
4. **Rupa Gosvami's Story**: Rupa desired food for his brother, and Krsna sent it through Radharani; Sanatana warned him not to accept service from the Lord.
5. **The Gopis**: They gave up everything upon hearing Krsna's flute.
6. **Prahlada Maharaja**: Refused all benedictions, wanting only to serve Krsna eternally.
