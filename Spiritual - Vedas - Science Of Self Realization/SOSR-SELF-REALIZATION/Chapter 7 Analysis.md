# Chapter Seven: Exploring the Spiritual Frontier - Srila Prabhupada Arrives in America

## Chapter Title and Overview

**Chapter Seven: Exploring the Spiritual Frontier - Srila Prabhupada Arrives in America**

This chapter chronicles Srila Prabhupada's journey to America and his early efforts to establish the Krishna consciousness movement in the West. It includes his poem written aboard the ship Jaladuta, his lectures at the University of Nairobi, the story of Pariksit Maharaja's compassion, and examples of Vaisnava compassion from Prahlada Maharaja and the Six Gosvamis. The chapter demonstrates the universal appeal of spiritual consciousness and the missionary spirit of a true devotee.

## Actionable Lessons

### Lesson 1: Develop Compassion for All Living Entities

**Step 1:** Understand that a Vaisnava (devotee) is an ocean of mercy.

**Step 2:** Feel compassion for those suffering in the material world.

**Step 3:** Take it as your mission to deliver fallen souls.

**Best Practices:**
- ✅ See every living entity as a spirit soul, part and parcel of God.
- ✅ Go door to door to spread Krsna consciousness.
- ❌ Don't be indifferent to others' suffering.

**Keep In Mind:**
- A Vaisnava always feels the miseries of others.
- Pariksit Maharaja was afflicted by the miseries of others.

---

### Lesson 2: Understand the Transmigration of the Soul

**Step 1:** Realize that the soul is atomic in size and transmigrates from one body to another.

**Step 2:** Study the evolutionary process: aquatics → plants → insects → birds → beasts → human beings.

**Step 3:** Understand that the human form of life is the culmination of evolution.

**Step 4:** Realize that from this junction, we can either slide down or elevate ourselves to godly life.

**Best Practices:**
- ✅ Use this rare human life for self-realization.
- ❌ Don't waste human life like cats and dogs.

**Keep In Mind:**
- The soul is smaller than an atom.
- Darwin's theory is a partial explanation of transmigration, but he had no conception of the soul.

---

### Lesson 3: Realize Your Eternal Spiritual Identity

**Step 1:** Understand that you are not the body but the eternal spirit soul.

**Step 2:** Accept that the soul has no birth or death (na jayate mriyate va).

**Step 3:** Realize that you are part and parcel of the Supreme Personality of Godhead.

**Step 4:** Understand that your natural position is to serve God.

**Best Practices:**
- ✅ Chant Hare Krishna to revive your original Krsna consciousness.
- ✅ See all living entities as spirit souls.
- ❌ Don't identify with the material body.

**Keep In Mind:**
- When you understand you are Brahman, you become happy and free from hankering and lamenting.
- The soul is valuable, not the body.

---

### Lesson 4: Go Back to Godhead in This Life

**Step 1:** Understand that the spiritual world is eternal and never annihilated.

**Step 2:** Accept that you can attain spiritual life in this very existence.

**Step 3:** Follow Krsna consciousness to prepare your spiritual body.

**Best Practices:**
- ✅ Try your best in this human form to get a spiritual body in the next life.
- ❌ Don't think that after death you become void.

**Keep In Mind:**
- The spiritual body is already within the gross material body.
- It will develop as soon as you become free from material contamination.

---

### Lesson 5: Understand God as the Supreme Proprietor

**Step 1:** Accept that everything in the universe belongs to God (isavasyam idam sarvam).

**Step 2:** Use only what is necessary for yourself.

**Step 3:** Do not encroach upon others' property.

**Best Practices:**
- ✅ See God as the supreme father and proprietor.
- ✅ Treat all living entities with equal vision.
- ❌ Don't think you are the proprietor of land or resources.

**Keep In Mind:**
- No man is an actual proprietor; the proprietor is God.
- If we realize God as proprietor, all quarrels will be solved.

---

### Lesson 6: Chant the Hare Krishna Mantra to Revive Original Consciousness

**Step 1:** Chant: Hare Krishna, Hare Krishna, Krishna Krishna, Hare Hare / Hare Rama, Hare Rama, Rama Rama, Hare Hare.

**Step 2:** Understand that this transcendental vibration cleanses the heart of all material dirt.

**Step 3:** Chant regularly and associate with devotees.

**Best Practices:**
- ✅ Chant attentively on beads.
- ✅ Chant in the association of pure devotees.
- ❌ Don't chant from the lips of nondevotees; avoid their association.

**Keep In Mind:**
- Krsna consciousness is the original, natural energy of the living entity.
- Chanting is not material; one will never get tired of it.

---

### Lesson 7: Follow the Example of Prahlada Maharaja

**Step 1:** Understand that Prahlada Maharaja was not anxious for his own deliverance.

**Step 2:** Feel anxiety for those who are not Krsna conscious.

**Step 3:** Glorify the Lord's heroic activities to deliver fallen souls.

**Best Practices:**
- ✅ Preach Krsna consciousness to deliver others.
- ✅ Feel compassion for the fallen.
- ❌ Don't be interested only in your own liberation.

**Keep In Mind:**
- A Vaisnava refuses to go to the kingdom of God without taking all fallen souls with him.
- Prahlada Maharaja says: "I have learned the art of always remaining in Krsna consciousness."

---

### Lesson 8: Follow the Example of the Six Gosvamis

**Step 1:** Study the lives of Sri Rupa, Sanatana, Sri Jiva, Gopala, Raghunatha dasa, and Raghunatha Bhatta Gosvamis.

**Step 2:** Engage in chanting, dancing, and writing books on Krsna consciousness.

**Step 3:** Follow their example of renunciation and dedication.

**Best Practices:**
- ✅ Write and distribute Krsna conscious literature.
- ✅ Chant and dance in ecstasy.
- ❌ Don't be attached to material comforts.

**Keep In Mind:**
- The Six Gosvamis slept only 1.5 hours daily and ate practically nothing.
- They were practically released from the bodily necessities of eating, sleeping, mating, and defending.

---

### Lesson 9: Protect Yourself from Illusion

**Step 1:** Understand that maya (illusion) tries especially hard to entrap those who have left her service.

**Step 2:** Remain strong under all conditions of temptation.

**Step 3:** View everything material with a pessimistic eye unless it is utilized to serve Krsna.

**Best Practices:**
- ✅ Associate with pure devotees.
- ✅ Chant regularly to stay strong.
- ❌ Don't think the material world is a place of happiness.

**Keep In Mind:**
- The material world is a miserable, negative place, full of danger at every step.
- Those who understand this are described as "great souls."

---

### Lesson 10: Understand That There Is No Love in This Material World

**Step 1:** Realize that what is called "love" in the material world is actually lust (kama).

**Step 2:** Understand that actual love is prema—love of God only.

**Step 3:** Elevate yourself to the platform of loving God.

**Best Practices:**
- ✅ Direct your loving tendency toward Krsna.
- ❌ Don't think that material relationships can satisfy the soul.

**Keep In Mind:**
- Outside of loving God, there is no possibility of loving; there is only lusty desire.
- So-called love means "you gratify my senses, I'll gratify your senses."

---

## Examples

1. **Srila Prabhupada's Poem on the Jaladuta**: Srila Prabhupada's prayer to Krsna upon arriving in America, expressing his humble submission and desire to deliver the fallen souls.
2. **The Nairobi Lecture**: Srila Prabhupada's address to students and government officials in Kenya, advising them to develop spiritually.
3. **Pariksit Maharaja's Compassion**: The king's inquiry about how to deliver sinful persons from hellish conditions.
4. **Prahlada Maharaja's Mission**: Prahlada's refusal to go to the kingdom of God without delivering all fallen souls.
5. **The Six Gosvamis**: Their dedication to chanting, dancing, writing books, and spreading Krsna consciousness.
6. **The Barking Dog Analogy**: A dog barking at you is sinful on the part of the owner; similarly, offensive speech is sinful.
7. **The Lynne Ludwig Letter**: Srila Prabhupada's response to a complaint about disciples having a negative outlook.
8. **The Desmond O'Grady Dialogue**: Srila Prabhupada explains Krsna consciousness to a Western journalist.
