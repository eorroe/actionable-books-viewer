# Chapter Four: Understanding Krsna and Christ - Krsna, Christos, Christ

## Chapter Title and Overview

**Chapter Four: Understanding Krsna and Christ - Krsna, Christos, Christ**

This chapter presents Srila Prabhupada's conversations with Christian authorities, including a Benedictine monk and a Cardinal, demonstrating that the essence of all religions is to know and love God. It establishes that "Christ" is derived from "Krsna" and that the chanting of God's holy name is the common thread in all genuine religious traditions. The chapter also addresses the commandment "Thou shalt not kill" and its implications for a civilized society.

## Actionable Lessons

### Lesson 1: Understand That All Names of God Refer to the Same Supreme

**Step 1:** Accept that "Krsna," "Christ," and "Krsta" all refer to the same all-attractive Supreme Personality of Godhead.

**Step 2:** Understand that God has unlimited names according to His dealings with devotees.

**Step 3:** Realize that the specific name of God is "Krsna," meaning "all-attractive."

**Best Practices:**
- ✅ Chant God's holy name regardless of your religious designation.
- ✅ Accept that the essence of all religion is to know and love God.
- ❌ Don't argue over which name is superior; all genuine names of God are equally powerful.
- ❌ Don't think that "God" is merely a general term and that God has no specific name.

**Keep In Mind:**
- Christ comes from the Greek Christos, which comes from the Sanskrit Krsta, which is another way of pronouncing Krsna.
- God has many names because He has innumerable dealings with His devotees.

---

### Lesson 2: Chant the Holy Name of God

**Step 1:** Chant the Hare Krishna maha-mantra: Hare Krishna, Hare Krishna, Krishna Krishna, Hare Hare / Hare Rama, Hare Rama, Rama Rama, Hare Hare.

**Step 2:** Understand that chanting directly associates you with God, who is absolute.

**Step 3:** Realize that the holy name is nondifferent from the Lord.

**Best Practices:**
- ✅ Chant the names of God found in your own scripture.
- ✅ Chant with understanding of the meaning: "O energy of God, O God, please engage me in Your service."
- ❌ Don't think that chanting is sectarian or sectarian.
- ❌ Don't neglect chanting because you think other processes are sufficient.

**Keep In Mind:**
- Whether you chant "Christ," "Krsta," or "Krsna," you are addressing the same Supreme Personality of Godhead.
- Chanting is the sublime method for practical perfection of life.

---

### Lesson 3: Follow the Commandments of God

**Step 1:** Accept that religion means obeying the laws of God.

**Step 2:** Follow the first and foremost commandment: "Thou shalt not kill."

**Step 3:** Understand that killing animals violates this commandment.

**Best Practices:**
- ✅ Abstain from meat-eating, egg consumption, and fishing.
- ✅ Follow the four regulative principles.
- ❌ Don't interpret "Thou shalt not kill" to mean only "murder" of humans.
- ❌ Don't think that Christianity sanctions meat-eating.

**Keep In Mind:**
- If Jesus meant only human killing, He would have used the word "murder."
- Christians who eat meat are disobeying the commandments of Lord Jesus Christ.

---

### Lesson 4: Recognize That Animals Have Souls

**Step 1:** Observe the common symptoms between animals and humans: eating, sleeping, mating, defending.

**Step 2:** Accept that where there are so many similarities, there must also be the presence of the soul.

**Step 3:** Understand that the soul exists in all 8,400,000 species of life (sarva-yonisu).

**Best Practices:**
- ✅ Treat all living entities with compassion.
- ✅ Don't kill animals unnecessarily.
- ❌ Don't think that only humans have souls.

**Keep In Mind:**
- If the animal's body is cut, there is blood; if your body is cut, there is blood.
- The soul is present in all species of life.

---

### Lesson 5: Practice Bhakti-Yoga (Loving Service to God)

**Step 1:** Understand that bhakti-yoga means to become free from designations like "Hindu," "Muslim," "Christian."

**Step 2:** Simply serve God without material motives.

**Step 3:** Realize that mukti (liberation) is included in bhakti; without bhakti, there is no mukti.

**Best Practices:**
- ✅ Serve God with love and devotion.
- ✅ Become free from material designations.
- ❌ Don't think that bhakti-yoga is sectarian.

**Keep In Mind:**
- When one engages in full devotional service, he at once transcends the modes of material nature (Bg. 14.26).
- The Krsna consciousness movement is a movement of love of God, not a sectarian religion.

---

### Lesson 6: Recognize Jesus Christ as a Representative of God

**Step 1:** Accept Lord Jesus Christ as the son of God and a representative of God.

**Step 2:** Understand that Jesus taught the same message as Krsna—love of God.

**Step 3:** Realize that Christians should follow the teachings of Jesus Christ, including "Thou shalt not kill."

**Best Practices:**
- ✅ Respect all bona fide spiritual masters, including Lord Jesus Christ.
- ✅ Follow the commandments of your scripture.
- ❌ Don't think that Krsna consciousness competes with Christianity; it is the original message.

**Keep In Mind:**
- Jesus Christ was crucified because people could not appreciate his preaching.
- Christ can take the sufferings for previous sins, but only if devotees stop sinful activities.

---

### Lesson 7: Understand That the Cow Is Our Mother

**Step 1:** Accept that the cow gives us milk and therefore is our mother.

**Step 2:** Realize that killing the cow is the greatest sin.

**Step 3:** Follow the Vedic principle of not killing any animals, especially cows.

**Best Practices:**
- ✅ Protect cows and use only milk products.
- ✅ Don't eat meat, especially beef.
- ❌ Don't kill your mother for food.

**Keep In Mind:**
- Civilization means utilizing cow's blood in the form of milk, not directly killing the animal.
- The innocent cow is simply eating grass and supplying milk.

---

### Lesson 8: Cultivate Humility and Meekness

**Step 1:** Understand that humility means intelligence.

**Step 2:** Follow the principle: "The humble and meek own the kingdom of God."

**Step 3:** Learn how to become humble and meek by offering respectful obeisances to Krishna and the spiritual master.

**Best Practices:**
- ✅ Always respect everyone, even if they are disrespectful to you.
- ✅ Give special respect to God and His pure devotees.
- ❌ Don't think that everyone is God; this prevents humility.

**Keep In Mind:**
- If everyone thinks he is God, why should he be humble?
- The qualities of humbleness and meekness lead very quickly to spiritual realization.

---

## Examples

1. **The Conversation with Father Emmanuel**: Srila Prabhupada explains that "Christ" comes from "Krsta" and ultimately "Krsna."
2. **The Discussion with Cardinal Danielou**: Srila Prabhupada argues that "Thou shalt not kill" means all killing, not just murder.
3. **The Soldier Analogy**: A soldier killing under the commander's order gets a medal; similarly, eating prasadam (food offered to Krishna) is not sinful.
4. **The Sun Analogy**: Krsna is like the sun, which can purify even urine; similarly, Krsna is above all sin.
5. **The Four Classes of Men**: Karmis (material workers), jnanis (philosophers), yogis (mystics), and bhaktas (devotees) each achieve different goals.
