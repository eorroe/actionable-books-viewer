# Chapter Six: Finding Spiritual Solutions to Material Problems - Crime: Why and What to Do?

## Chapter Title and Overview

**Chapter Six: Finding Spiritual Solutions to Material Problems - Crime: Why and What to Do?**

This chapter addresses the pressing social problem of crime and proposes a spiritual solution through the sankirtana movement. It explains that crime stems from a godless, sinful mentality and that mass congregational chanting of the holy names of God is the simplest and most effective method for purifying the hearts of the population. The chapter also discusses the varnasrama social system, the peace formula, and the need for God-centered leadership.

## Actionable Lessons

### Lesson 1: Understand That Crime Stems from a Sinful Heart

**Step 1:** Recognize that the difference between a pious man and a criminal is purity versus dirt in the heart.

**Step 2:** Understand that this dirt is uncontrollable lust and greed.

**Step 3:** Realize that crime is widespread because people are in a diseased condition.

**Best Practices:**
- ✅ Focus on purifying the heart rather than just punishing criminals.
- ✅ Support sankirtana as the method of purification.
- ❌ Don't think that more police and prisons are the ultimate solution.

**Keep In Mind:**
- Crime is a symptom of a deeper spiritual disease.
- The simplest process of purification is congregational chanting of the holy names of God.

---

### Lesson 2: Implement Mass Sankirtana to Prevent Crime

**Step 1:** Gather as many people as possible for congregational chanting.

**Step 2:** Distribute prasadam (sanctified food) along with chanting.

**Step 3:** Understand that this process will dissipate dirty things from everyone's heart.

**Best Practices:**
- ✅ Hold mass sankirtana festivals open to all.
- ✅ Provide prasadam to participants.
- ❌ Don't restrict sankirtana to one class or community.
- ❌ Don't think that small-scale sankirtana is sufficient; scale it up.

**Keep In Mind:**
- Mass sankirtana can change the entire society.
- The government should provide facilities for sankirtana.

**Common Pitfalls:**
- **Problem:** Government authorities are skeptical because so-called Christians don't practice what they preach.
  **Solution:** Show that disciples strictly following religious principles produce effective chanting.

---

### Lesson 3: Educate People in the Science of God

**Step 1:** Understand that religion is not faith but science.

**Step 2:** Accept that God is the supreme controller and that religion means obeying His laws.

**Step 3:** Study the Vedas and Bhagavad-gita to understand God scientifically.

**Best Practices:**
- ✅ Teach the science of God in schools, colleges, and universities.
- ✅ Explain that God is the proprietor of everything (isavasyam idam sarvam).
- ❌ Don't accept blind faith; seek scientific understanding of God.

**Keep In Mind:**
- Religion means the laws of God, just as law means the laws of the state.
- Without understanding God, there is no real religion.

---

### Lesson 4: Accept God as the Proprietor of Everything

**Step 1:** Understand that everything in the universe belongs to God.

**Step 2:** Accept only what is necessary for yourself, set aside as your quota.

**Step 3:** Do not encroach upon others' property.

**Best Practices:**
- ✅ Use God's property in the service of God.
- ✅ Recognize that all living entities are children of God and have the right to use their quota.
- ❌ Don't think "I am the proprietor" of land or resources.

**Keep In Mind:**
- The first crime is thinking that America (or any land) is yours.
- Everything belongs to God; we are all His sons.

---

### Lesson 5: Understand That True Religion Is Universal

**Step 1:** Accept that the essence of all religions is the same: to know God and love Him.

**Step 2:** Don't think that one religion is superior to another.

**Step 3:** Follow the injunctions of your own scripture while accepting the universal goal.

**Best Practices:**
- ✅ Try to understand God and love Him, regardless of your religious designation.
- ❌ Don't fight in the name of religion.
- ❌ Don't think that simply being born in a religious family makes you religious.

**Keep In Mind:**
- A Christian gentleman will not be interested in changing his faith to Hindu, nor will a Hindu gentleman change to Christian.
- Everyone should be interested in understanding the philosophy and science of God.

---

### Lesson 6: Train Children Spiritually from Birth

**Step 1:** Begin spiritual training from childhood (kaumara acaret prajnah).

**Step 2:** Teach children to chant Hare Krishna from the very beginning.

**Step 3:** Provide a spiritual atmosphere in the home.

**Best Practices:**
- ✅ Raise children as Krsna conscious from the start.
- ✅ Use the gurukula system for spiritual education.
- ❌ Don't wait until children are corrupted by material education.

**Keep In Mind:**
- Prahlada Maharaja stressed that spiritual education should begin from childhood.
- If parents are devotees, children will automatically advance.

---

### Lesson 7: Direct Human Energy Toward Spiritual Advancement

**Step 1:** Understand that human energy is more valuable than animal energy.

**Step 2:** Use human energy for spiritual advancement, not for competing with dogs in sense gratification.

**Step 3:** Avoid the "dog's race" of material sense gratification.

**Best Practices:**
- ✅ Employ your energy for self-realization.
- ✅ Read Srimad-Bhagavatam and other spiritual texts.
- ❌ Don't waste human energy in simply competing for material comforts.

**Keep In Mind:**
- The dog is busy for sense gratification; the human being should be busy for self-realization.
- In many, many lives, the soul has been in forms of dogs, demigods, cats, birds, and beasts.

---

### Lesson 8: Surrender to Krsna to Transcend Destiny

**Step 1:** Understand that material destiny cannot be checked.

**Step 2:** Accept that Krsna can change destiny for His devotees.

**Step 3:** Surrender to Krsna and take shelter of Him.

**Best Practices:**
- ✅ Take shelter of Krsna to transcend material destiny.
- ❌ Don't think that destiny cannot be changed; Krsna can change it for His devotees.

**Keep In Mind:**
- If one is condemned by the law court, no one can check it; but if the defendant begs the king's mercy, the king can check it.
- Krsna says, "I shall give you all protection from all reactions of sinful activities" (Bg. 18.66).

---

### Lesson 9: Establish the Varnasrama Social System

**Step 1:** Understand the four social orders: brahmana (intellectuals), ksatriya (administrators), vaisya (productive class), sudra (workers).

**Step 2:** Understand the four spiritual orders: brahmacari (celibate student), grhastha (householder), vanaprastha (retired), sannyasa (renounced).

**Step 3:** Accept that this system is created by the Supreme Lord (catur-varnyam maya srstam).

**Best Practices:**
- ✅ Divide society according to quality, not birth.
- ✅ Train people for their respective roles.
- ❌ Don't create a one-class society; it will lead to chaos.

**Keep In Mind:**
- The brain (brahmanas), arms (ksatriyas), stomach (vaisyas), and legs (sudras) are all required for the body to function.
- Unless there are these four divisions, society will be in chaos.

---

### Lesson 10: Please God as the Basis of All Relief Work

**Step 1:** Understand that without pleasing the Supreme Personality of Godhead, no one can become happy.

**Step 2:** Realize that all attempts at relief work without pleasing God are futile.

**Step 3:** Accept that by pleasing Krsna, we please everyone.

**Best Practices:**
- ✅ Perform sankirtana to please God and bring rain and peace.
- ✅ Understand that God's benediction is the real solution to scarcity.
- ❌ Don't think that collecting funds for relief work alone will solve problems.

**Keep In Mind:**
- A diseased man cannot live simply on the strength of an expert physician.
- Due to sankirtana, rain fell after a two-year drought.

---

### Lesson 11: Establish One Nation Under God

**Step 1:** Understand that everything belongs to God.

**Step 2:** Accept that the government's duty is to see that society works toward knowing God.

**Step 3:** Train leaders in varnasrama-dharma.

**Best Practices:**
- ✅ Establish a government under God's direction.
- ✅ Accept the guidance of brahmanas (Vaisnavas) in governance.
- ❌ Don't accept leaders who do not believe in the supremacy of God.

**Keep In Mind:**
- The real duty of the government is to understand what God wants and direct society accordingly.
- Unless leaders are trained as saintly monarchs, there cannot be good government.

---

### Lesson 12: Accept the Peace Formula

**Step 1:** Chant the holy name of God.

**Step 2:** Distribute prasadam.

**Step 3:** Realize that God is the proprietor of everything.

**Best Practices:**
- ✅ Follow the peace formula: chant Hare Krishna, take prasadam, realize God's proprietorship.
- ❌ Don't try to achieve peace without God consciousness.

**Keep In Mind:**
- The claim of proprietorship by the human race is partly or wholly the cause of all disturbances of peace on earth.
- If you want peace at all, you will have to change your consciousness into Krsna consciousness.

---

### Lesson 13: Reject the Materialistic Origin-of-Life Theory

**Step 1:** Understand that Darwin's theory is only a partial explanation of the transmigration of the soul.

**Step 2:** Accept that the soul is the real living force within the body.

**Step 3:** Realize that the soul transmigrates through 8,400,000 species of life.

**Best Practices:**
- ✅ Study the Vedic explanation of the soul's transmigration.
- ❌ Don't accept the atheistic theory that life comes from matter.

**Keep In Mind:**
- Darwin simply took information from Vedic literature but had no conception of the soul.
- The soul is part and parcel of God and has godly qualities in minute quantity.

## Examples

1. **The Doctor Frog**: A frog in the well cannot understand the vast ocean—similarly, we cannot understand the spiritual world by material speculation.
2. **The Judge and Rubber-Stamping Worker**: The judge sits quietly but gets a high salary because of his qualifications; similarly, spiritual advancement is based on qualification.
3. **The Child in Different Laps**: A child sits on the lap of his father or mother—similarly, the soul accepts different bodies according to karma.
4. **The Condemned Prisoner and the King**: If a condemned prisoner begs the king's mercy, the king can save him; similarly, Krsna can change destiny for His devotees.
5. **The Pig and Halvah**: A pig eats stool and is happy; you cannot make it eat halvah—similarly, pleasure is already stored up according to the body.
6. **Arjuna's Consciousness Change**: Arjuna was a royal family member but was a pure devotee; spiritual life does not depend on material condition.
7. **The Drought in Andhra Pradesh**: Due to sankirtana, rain began to fall after a two-year drought.
8. **The Dog and Four Legs Analogy**: The dog runs on four legs, man on four wheels—this is not advancement.
