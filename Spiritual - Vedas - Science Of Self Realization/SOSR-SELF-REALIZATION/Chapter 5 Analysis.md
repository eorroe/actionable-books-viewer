# Chapter Five: Practicing Yoga in the Modern Age - Superconsciousness

## Chapter Title and Overview

**Chapter Five: Practicing Yoga in the Modern Age - Superconsciousness**

This chapter explains the true yoga system as described in the Bhagavad-gita and distinguishes it from the hatha-yoga commonly practiced in the West. It establishes that the ultimate goal of yoga is to contact the Supersoul within the heart and take dictation from the Lord. The chapter presents Lord Caitanya's sankirtana movement (chanting the holy names of Krsna) as the simplest and most effective yoga process for this age.

## Actionable Lessons

### Lesson 1: Understand the True Goal of Yoga

**Step 1:** Recognize that real yoga means to control the senses and concentrate the mind on the Narayana form of the Supreme Personality of Godhead.

**Step 2:** Understand that the ultimate goal is to contact the Supersoul (Paramatma) within the heart.

**Step 3:** Realize that taking dictation from the Lord is the highest perfectional stage of yoga.

**Step 4:** Accept that meditation on something void or impersonal requires a very long time and is not practical in this age.

**Best Practices:**
- ✅ Meditate on the four-handed form of Narayana (Visnu) within your heart.
- ✅ Control the senses, beginning with the tongue.
- ❌ Don't practice hatha-yoga (gymnastic feats) as the ultimate goal.
- ❌ Don't try to meditate on something void or impersonal.

**Keep In Mind:**
- The breathing exercises and meditational practices of yoga are very difficult in this age.
- Arjuna flatly rejected the stringent rules of yoga five thousand years ago.

---

### Lesson 2: Control the Senses, Beginning with the Tongue

**Step 1:** Control the tongue by eating only Krsna prasadam (vegetarian food offered to Krishna).

**Step 2:** Avoid forbidden foods and intoxicants.

**Step 3:** Engage the tongue in chanting the holy name.

**Best Practices:**
- ✅ Eat only prasadam.
- ✅ Avoid meat, fish, eggs, onions, garlic, and intoxicants.
- ❌ Don't allow the tongue to take all kinds of forbidden food and expect to advance in yoga.

**Keep In Mind:**
- You cannot stop the activities of the senses by artificial means, but if you purify the senses, they automatically become controlled.
- Many unauthorized yogis come to the West and say one can indulge in drinking and still practice meditation.

---

### Lesson 3: Practice Bhakti-Yoga (Krsna Consciousness) as the Highest Yoga

**Step 1:** Understand that bhakti-yoga means purifying the senses.

**Step 2:** Realize that when the senses are purified, they are automatically controlled and positively engaged in transcendental service.

**Step 3:** Think, chant, live, eat, talk, hope, and sustain in Krsna.

**Best Practices:**
- ✅ Chant Hare Krishna.
- ✅ Engage all senses in the service of the Lord.
- ❌ Don't try to stop the senses artificially; purify them.

**Keep In Mind:**
- Krsna consciousness is not manufactured by mental speculation; it is prescribed in the Bhagavad-gita.
- When we think in Krsna, chant in Krsna, live in Krsna, we return to Krsna.

---

### Lesson 4: Understand the Eight Superachievements of Yoga

**Step 1:** Learn the eight material perfections of yoga:
1. Becoming smaller than an atom
2. Becoming bigger than a mountain
3. Becoming lighter than air
4. Becoming heavier than any metal
5. Achieving any material effect (creating a planet)
6. Controlling others like the Lord
7. Freely traveling anywhere in the universe
8. Choosing one's own time and place of death

**Step 2:** Understand that these are only steps forward, not the ultimate goal.

**Step 3:** Aspire for the highest perfection: contact with the Supersoul and receiving dictation from Him.

**Best Practices:**
- ✅ Aim for the highest perfectional stage.
- ❌ Don't be satisfied with material yogic achievements.

---

### Lesson 5: Chant the Hare Krishna Mantra as the Yoga for This Age

**Step 1:** Chant: Hare Krishna, Hare Krishna, Krishna Krishna, Hare Hare / Hare Rama, Hare Rama, Rama Rama, Hare Hare.

**Step 2:** Understand that this is the authorized process for Kali-yuga.

**Step 3:** Follow Lord Caitanya's injunction: "In this age, simply go on chanting the Hare Krishna mantra. There is no other alternative."

**Best Practices:**
- ✅ Chant regularly on beads.
- ✅ Chant in congregation (sankirtana).
- ❌ Don't think that other yoga processes are practical in this age.

**Keep In Mind:**
- Kirtanad eva krsnasya mukta-sangah param vrajet: Simply by chanting the Hare Krishna mantra one can become liberated.
- This process is open to everyone without prequalification.

---

### Lesson 6: Understand the Different Yoga Processes for Different Ages

**Step 1:** In Satya-yuga, the meditation process was possible.

**Step 2:** In Treta-yuga, the process was performance of Vedic rituals.

**Step 3:** In Dvapara-yuga, temple worship was the process.

**Step 4:** In Kali-yuga, hari-kirtana (chanting the holy name) is the only effective process.

**Best Practices:**
- ✅ Accept hari-kirtana as the yoga for this age.
- ❌ Don't try to practice astanga-yoga in this age; it is not possible.

**Keep In Mind:**
- In Satya-yuga, men lived for 100,000 years; meditation was possible then.
- In Kali-yuga, the duration of life is diminished, and people are slow in understanding self-realization.

---

### Lesson 7: Understand That Krsna Consciousness Is the Complete Yoga

**Step 1:** Realize that Krsna consciousness is the highest yoga performance by trained devotional yogis.

**Step 2:** Understand that Krsna is the original Absolute Personality, the Godhead.

**Step 3:** Accept that all Visnu forms are plenary expansions of Krsna.

**Best Practices:**
- ✅ Practice Krsna consciousness as the complete yoga.
- ❌ Don't limit yourself to the preliminary practices of yoga.

**Keep In Mind:**
- Real yoga practice is not hatha-yoga but bhakti-yoga.
- Lord Caitanya practically demonstrated Krsna consciousness yoga simply by chanting.

---

### Lesson 8: Recognize Lord Caitanya as the Golden Avatar

**Step 1:** Accept that Lord Caitanya Mahaprabhu is Krsna Himself appearing as a devotee.

**Step 2:** Understand that His specific mission is to distribute love of Godhead freely.

**Step 3:** Keep a picture of Lord Caitanya with His associates and chant Hare Krishna.

**Best Practices:**
- ✅ Worship Lord Caitanya by chanting Hare Krishna.
- ✅ Keep a picture of Sri Caitanya Mahaprabhu at home.
- ❌ Don't think that anyone can claim to be an incarnation without scriptural symptoms.

**Keep In Mind:**
- Caitanya Mahaprabhu is the most munificent incarnation because He directly gives love of God.
- He predicted that His sankirtana movement would spread to every town and village.

---

### Lesson 9: Understand That Human Life Is Meant for Self-Realization

**Step 1:** Recognize that human life is attained after many, many births in the cycle of transmigration.

**Step 2:** Use this rare opportunity to understand God and go back to Him.

**Step 3:** Don't waste human life like animals, simply eating, sleeping, mating, and defending.

**Best Practices:**
- ✅ Use human life to advance spiritually.
- ❌ Don't become mad after sense gratification.
- ❌ Don't think that skyscraper buildings and modern technology mean advancement.

**Keep In Mind:**
- The human being should learn tapasya (austerity).
- India is the land of tapasya, but now it is becoming the land of technology.

---

### Lesson 10: Prepare for the Next Life by Becoming Krsna Conscious

**Step 1:** Understand that when this body is finished, there is no guarantee what kind of body you will take next.

**Step 2:** Practice Krsna consciousness now to prepare for your next spiritual body.

**Step 3:** Realize that if you fail to complete the yoga process in this life, you may be given birth in a spiritually advanced family.

**Best Practices:**
- ✅ Practice Krsna consciousness seriously in this life.
- ✅ Train your children in Krsna consciousness from the very beginning.
- ❌ Don't think that if you fail, you will get another chance; take this opportunity seriously.

---

## Examples

1. **The Yogic Superachievements**: The eight supernormal powers of yoga are only steps, not the goal.
2. **The Child and the Mother**: A child can immediately respond and dance upon hearing; similarly, by nature God has given us ears to hear the holy name.
3. **The Goat at the Kali Temple**: A goat standing contentedly before being sacrificed doesn't know its fate—similar to humans not knowing death is imminent.
4. **Lord Caitanya and Prakasananda Sarasvati**: Lord Caitanya explains that chanting cleanses the heart (ceto-darpana-marjanam).
5. **The Prediction of Lord Caitanya**: "In all the villages and towns all over the world, everywhere, this sankirtana movement will be preached."
