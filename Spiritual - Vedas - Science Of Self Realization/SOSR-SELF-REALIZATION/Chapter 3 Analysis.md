# Chapter Three: Discovering the Roots - The Immortal Nectar of the Bhagavad-gita

## Chapter Title and Overview

**Chapter Three: Discovering the Roots - The Immortal Nectar of the Bhagavad-gita**

This chapter explores the scriptural foundation of Krishna consciousness, focusing on the Bhagavad-gita as the distilled essence of Vedic wisdom. Through Sankara's meditations on the Gita and Srila Prabhupada's elucidation, the chapter establishes Krsna as the Supreme Personality of Godhead and the Gita as the authoritative source of transcendental knowledge. It also includes a correspondence with Dr. J.F. Staal regarding the authority of chanting as the supreme yoga process for this age.

## Actionable Lessons

### Lesson 1: Accept the Bhagavad-gita As It Is

**Step 1:** Understand that the Bhagavad-gita is spoken directly by the Supreme Personality of Godhead, Sri Krsna.

**Step 2:** Accept the Gita without interpretation; it is absolute and does not require symbolic reading.

**Step 3:** Study the Gita from a bona fide disciplic succession, not from impersonalist or demoniac interpreters.

**Best Practices:**
- ✅ Read the Bhagavad-gita As It Is for perfect knowledge.
- ✅ Accept the Gita as the supreme authority.
- ❌ Don't interpret the Gita according to your own opinion.
- ❌ Don't think the Gita is too antiquated for modern man.

**Keep In Mind:**
- The Gita is identical with Krsna; both are absolute.
- Unfortunate persons say the Gita is too old, but they simply want to find God by speculation.

---

### Lesson 2: Understand That Krsna Is the Supreme Personality of Godhead

**Step 1:** Accept Parasara Muni's definition: Bhagavan is full in six opulences—strength, fame, wealth, knowledge, beauty, and renunciation.

**Step 2:** Study Krsna's pastimes and activities in the Srimad-Bhagavatam.

**Step 3:** Realize that Krsna is the original cause of all causes (sarva-karana-karanam).

**Best Practices:**
- ✅ Worship Krsna as the Supreme Personality of Godhead.
- ❌ Don't accept the impersonalist view that God has no form, no senses, no activities.

**Keep In Mind:**
- Even Sankara, the greatest impersonalist, offers his respects to Krsna.
- Krsna is God from the beginning; He does not become God by meditation.

---

### Lesson 3: Follow the Authoritative Disciplic Succession

**Step 1:** Learn the disciplic succession: Brahma → Narada → Vyasadeva → Madhva → ... → Caitanya Mahaprabhu → present acarya.

**Step 2:** Accept that the guru's message must be identical to the original message of Krsna.

**Step 3:** Avoid so-called gurus who give their own opinions.

**Best Practices:**
- ✅ Follow the previous acaryas without deviation.
- ❌ Don't interpret sabda-brahma (divine sound) by your imperfect sense perception.

**Keep In Mind:**
- One who interprets the divine sound by his imperfect sense perception cannot be a real spiritual guru.
- Srila Vyasadeva did not say "This is my opinion"; he wrote "sri-bhagavan uvaca."

---

### Lesson 4: Understand the Three Aspects of the Absolute Truth

**Step 1:** Realize that the Absolute Truth is realized in three aspects: impersonal Brahman, localized Paramatma, and personal Bhagavan (Krsna).

**Step 2:** Understand that Bhagavan is the ultimate realization.

**Step 3:** Accept that Krsna is Parabrahman—the Supreme Brahman.

**Best Practices:**
- ✅ Aim for the personal realization of Krsna.
- ❌ Don't stop at impersonal Brahman realization.
- ❌ Don't think that Brahman, Paramatma, and Bhagavan are different in the ultimate sense.

**Keep In Mind:**
- Learned transcendentalists call the nondual substance Brahman, Paramatma, or Bhagavan.
- The feature of the Supreme Personality is the ultimate realization of God.

---

### Lesson 5: Chant the Holy Name of the Lord

**Step 1:** Chant the Hare Krishna maha-mantra: Hare Krishna, Hare Krishna, Krishna Krishna, Hare Hare / Hare Rama, Hare Rama, Rama Rama, Hare Hare.

**Step 2:** Understand that chanting is the direct means of contacting the Supreme Absolute Truth.

**Step 3:** Follow the injunction of the Brhan-naradiya Purana: "Simply chant Hare Krishna. There is no other alternative in this age of Kali."

**Best Practices:**
- ✅ Chant regularly on beads.
- ✅ Chant in the association of devotees.
- ❌ Don't think that other processes are as effective in this age.

**Keep In Mind:**
- In Kali-yuga, hari-kirtana is the only alternative for self-realization.
- The chanting process is authorized by Vedic principles and inaugurated by Lord Caitanya.

---

### Lesson 6: Understand the Universal Scope of Krsna Consciousness

**Step 1:** Recognize that Krsna consciousness is not a sectarian Hindu cult.

**Step 2:** Understand that the movement is meant for all humanity.

**Step 3:** Accept that the Vedas and the Bhagavad-gita are for everyone, not just Hindus.

**Best Practices:**
- ✅ Invite people of all faiths to understand Krsna consciousness.
- ❌ Don't identify Krsna consciousness with Hinduism.
- ❌ Don't think that only Indians can understand the Vedas.

**Keep In Mind:**
- The word "Hindu" is not in the Bhagavad-gita or Vedic literature.
- Religion means the laws of God, applicable to everyone.

---

### Lesson 7: Test All Processes by Their Practical Result

**Step 1:** Evaluate spiritual processes by their practical effect.

**Step 2:** Observe that students seriously taking to Krsna consciousness advance further than philosophers, religionists, yogis, or karmis.

**Step 3:** Accept chanting as the most effective process for this age based on practical observation.

**Best Practices:**
- ✅ Test the chanting process by personal experience.
- ✅ See the transformation in others who chant seriously.
- ❌ Don't argue endlessly about scriptural quotations; accept the judgment of authority.

**Keep In Mind:**
- We test everything by its practical result.
- In this age, the constant chanting of the Hare Krishna maha-mantra undoubtedly proves very effective.

---

### Lesson 8: Understand the Importance of Sound Vibration (Sabda-Brahma)

**Step 1:** Accept that the transcendental vibration is the essence of all Vedic rituals and mantras.

**Step 2:** Understand that "By sound vibration one becomes liberated" (anavrttih sabdat).

**Step 3:** Chant the holy name as the complete process of spiritual realization.

**Best Practices:**
- ✅ Chant attentively and hear the sound vibration.
- ❌ Don't think that dry philosophical speculation is as effective as sound vibration.

**Keep In Mind:**
- The eight words Hare Krishna contain all Vedic rituals, mantras, and understanding.
- Sound vibration is the direct means of contacting the Supreme Absolute Truth.
