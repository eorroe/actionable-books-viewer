# Science of Self-Realization: Krsna Consciousness for Modern Life

## Overview

Science of Self-Realization teaches the systematic process of understanding one's true identity as an eternal spiritual soul and establishing a loving relationship with the Supreme Personality of Godhead, Krsna. Through the teachings of His Divine Grace A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada, this book provides a practical, authoritative path for transcending material existence and achieving the highest goal of human life. Drawing from the Bhagavad-gita, Srimad-Bhagavatam, and the teachings of Lord Caitanya, this book offers a comprehensive guide to spiritual realization that is accessible to everyone regardless of background.

## When to Follow Book Teachings

 - When you need to understand who you are as a spiritual being beyond the material body
 - When working to solve the four real problems of life: birth, death, old age, and disease
 - When the user asks about life's ultimate purpose and how to achieve permanent happiness
 - When you need to distinguish genuine spiritual teachers from false ones
 - When seeking practical methods for spiritual progress in the modern age
 - When looking for solutions to social problems such as crime and moral decline
 - When you want to understand the systematic process of God consciousness and how to develop love for Krsna

## Lessons From Book

### Lesson 1: Understand Your True Identity as a Spirit Soul

#### Examples

 - **The Coat and Shirt Analogy**: The body is like a shirt or coat; the soul is the conscious being within. Just as a person changes clothes, the soul remains the same while the body changes from childhood to old age.
 - **The Boy and His Father**: A boy speculating about his father's identity by searching the entire city will never find him; the simple process is to ask his mother. Similarly, transcendental knowledge must be received from authority, not by speculation.

#### Step 1: Recognize That You Are Not Your Body

Observe the changes in your body throughout life (child → boy → adult → old person) while your identity remains the same. This continuity of identity proves the soul is constant while the body changes. Understand that at death, the soul leaves the body and takes another body according to karma and desires, just as a person changes clothes.

#### Step 2: Understand the Process of Transmigration

Learn that the spirit soul is invisible and extremely small in size. At death, the subtle body (mind, intelligence, ego) carries the soul to another gross body. This process is like air carrying a fragrance—invisible to the eye but perceptible by its effect. The soul's next birth is decided by association with the three modes of material nature (goodness, passion, and ignorance) during this life.

#### Step 3: Realize That Misunderstanding the Body as the Self Is Ignorance

The whole modern civilization operates on the misunderstanding that the body is the self (dehatma-buddhi). This bodily identification is the same mentality that animals have. If you think you are your body, you will identify yourself by bodily designations such as nationality, race, gender, and social status: "I am American," "I am Indian," etc. Such identification is ignorance.

#### Best Practices

- ✅ Regularly remind yourself: "I am not this body; I am a spirit soul."
- ✅ Observe how others identify themselves by bodily designations and recognize this as ignorance.
 - ❌ Don't become overly concerned with bodily maintenance at the expense of spiritual growth.
 - ❌ Don't think that material progress (nice house, car, job) equals real progress.

#### Keep In Mind

- The soul is eternal, unborn, and undying (Bhagavad-gita 2.20: "For the soul, there is never birth nor death...").
- The body is temporary, but the soul continues.
- Misunderstanding the body as the self leads to endless material struggles.

#### Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Thinking "I am my body" leads to endless material struggles.
  **Solution:** Chant Hare Krishna to awaken spiritual consciousness and realize your true identity.

### Lesson 2: Recognize the Real Problems of Life

#### Examples

 - **The Train Compartment Analogy**: A first-class train compartment is useless if the train is not going toward your intended destination—similarly, material comfort is useless without spiritual progress.
 - **The Dog and Four Legs Analogy**: The dog runs on four legs, a person traveling by automobile—this is not progress.

#### Step 1: Identify the Four Real Problems

Birth, death, old age, and disease are the real problems of life. Material civilization only addresses eating, sleeping, mating, and defense mechanisms—the same activities animals perform.

#### Step 2: Understand That No Material Advancement Solves These Problems

No amount of material progress can eliminate the problems of birth, death, old age, and disease. A man may have atomic weapons, but he still dies.

#### Step 3: Accept That the Human Form of Life Is Meant to Solve These Problems

The human body is an excellent vehicle for attaining eternal spiritual existence. It is a rare vessel for crossing the ocean of material existence, which is characterized by ignorance of one's true spiritual identity.

#### Best Practices

- ✅ Seek spiritual solutions, not material ones, for life's real problems.
- ✅ Use the human form of life to solve the problems of existence.
- ❌ Don't be misled by polished animalism—having a nice apartment doesn't make you advanced.

#### Keep In Mind

 - A dog sleeps, a human sleeps. The biological function is the same.
 - Humanity cannot conquer outer space with atomic bombs; humans are still subject to death.

### Lesson 3: Cut the Five Chains of Material Attachment

#### Step 1: Identify the Five Strong Chains

The five chains that bind the human being to mundane material consciousness are:
1. Attachment to the material body due to ignorance of spiritual facts
2. Attachment to family members due to bodily relationships
3. Attachment to the land of birth and material possessions
4. Attachment to materialistic science without spiritual knowledge
5. Attachment to religious forms without knowing the Supreme Personality of Godhead

#### Step 2: Use the Weapon of Detachment

Cut these attachments through the practice of detachment. Detach from material activities such as work for money or sense gratification by spiritual activities such as chanting, hearing, and serving, not by material inertia such as laziness or renunciation without spiritual engagement.

#### Step 3: Seek the Spiritual Destination

Seek that spiritual destination from which, having once attained it, the soul never returns to material existence, and there surrender to that Supreme Personality of Godhead from whom everything has begun (Bhagavad-gita 15.3-4).

#### Best Practices

 - ✅ Spiritualize all material activities—offer all one's activities to Krsna.
 - ✅ Use detachment to cut material attachments such as attachment to body, family, and possessions.
 - ❌ Don't try to escape material existence by simply renouncing without spiritual practices such as chanting and serving.

#### Keep In Mind

- These chains are compared to a deeply rooted banyan tree (Bhagavad-gita Chapter 15).
 - Material inertia is negative; spiritual activity is positive engagement with reality.

### Lesson 4: Understand That Service Is Our Constitutional Position

#### Examples

 - **The Tree Analogy**: If we want to nourish a tree, we must water the root. If we pour water on the leaves, branches, and twigs, there is little benefit. If the Supreme Personality of Godhead is served, all other parts of the tree will be nourished.

#### Step 1: Recognize That Everyone Is Rendering Service

Human beings render service to some person, group, or cause—family, country, society, or even a pet. All these examples prove that we are constitutionally meant to serve.

#### Step 2: Realize That Material Service Is Frustrating

On the material platform, people experience frustration because their service is directed toward temporary material objects rather than the Supreme.

#### Step 3: Serve the Supreme Personality of Godhead

When Krsna is served, all other parts are satisfied. Therefore, all welfare activities as well as service to society, family, and nation are realized by serving the Supreme Personality of Godhead.

#### Best Practices

 - ✅ Direct your service to Krsna, who is the root of all existence.
 - ✅ Water the root of the tree, not the leaves.
 - ❌ Don't expect permanent satisfaction from serving material people or causes.

#### Keep In Mind

 - We are parts of God, meant for serving Him.
 - Without serving God, all welfare activities remain incomplete.

### Lesson 5: Practice Pure Devotional Service (Bhakti-Yoga)

#### Examples

 - **Rupa Gosvami's Story**: Rupa desired food for his brother, and Krsna provided it through Radharani. Sanatana warned him not to accept service from the Lord, as pure devotees are those whose only desire is to serve Krsna without any material motivation or desire for personal benefit.

#### Step 1: Understand the Definition of Pure Devotional Service

From the Narada-pancaratra: hrsikena hrsikesa-sevanam bhaktir ucyate—"By one's senses, engage the senses in the service of Krsna, who is the master of the senses." This means that devotees should engage their senses, not just their minds, in Krsna's service.

#### Step 2: Purify the Senses

Our senses are originally meant for serving Krsna but are now covered by material desires. All embodied souls must cure themselves and become free from such desires. When one's senses are no longer inclined toward personal sense gratification such as sex, food, sleep, or material enjoyment—one's status is called pure devotion.

#### Step 3: Serve Krsna Favorably, Without Material Desires

Caitanya Mahaprabhu recommends pure devotional service. There should be no desire to fulfill one's own material desires, no attempt to understand Krsna by experimental philosophy, and no activities motivated by desire for personal gain. The devotee's only desire should be to serve Krsna in devotional service in ways that satisfy Him, as described in the Vedas and taught by the spiritual master.

#### Best Practices

- ✅ Serve Krsna favorably, as He desires.
- ✅ Take shelter of the spiritual master to learn what Krsna wants.
 - ❌ Don't serve Krsna with material motives such as desire for money, fame, or personal advantage.
 - ❌ Don't think that meditation alone is pure devotional service; engage the senses in devotional service to Krsna.

#### Keep In Mind

 - Pure devotion is devoid of desires for personal sense gratification—such as the desire for material comfort, fame, or bodily pleasure.
 - Even demoniac enemies of Krsna get liberation (mukti) if killed by Krsna in His pastimes; therefore advanced devotees should not desire liberation but only service.
  - As long as one desires material benefit such as money, fame, or bodily pleasure—or even spiritual liberation (mukti) for oneself, one cannot attain pure love of Godhead.

#### Security & Safety Notes

 - Having material desires such as desire for sex, food, sleep, or material enjoyment while practicing devotional service will prevent pure love of God from developing.

#### Common Pitfalls

 - **Problem:** Wanting material benefits such as money, health, or personal comfort from devotional service.
   **Solution:** Check your own intentions. Do you, as a practitioner, want Krsna or His spiritual benedictions?

### Lesson 6: Realize God Through Authoritative Sources, Not Speculation

#### Examples

- **The Mother and Father Analogy**: If a boy wants to know who his father is, the simple process is to ask his mother. The mother will say, "This is your father." Speculation by searching the whole city will never yield perfect knowledge.

#### Step 1: Accept That God Is Unlimited

God is unlimited; we are limited. Our knowledge and perception are both very limited, so we cannot understand the unlimited by our own speculation.

#### Step 2: Receive Knowledge from the Perfect Source

Receive knowledge from Krsna, the supreme perfect being. The Vedas are like the manual that comes with a product; it explains how to deal with creation.

#### Step 3: Apply the Knowledge Practically

Krsna consciousness is not mere theory. Krsna consciousness can be practiced in daily life and can be verified through personal spiritual experience.

#### Best Practices

- ✅ Accept knowledge from authority (Krsna, the Vedas, the spiritual master).
- ✅ Study the Bhagavad-gita As It Is for perfect knowledge.
- ❌ Don't waste time on mental speculation about God's existence.

#### Keep In Mind

 - The Vedas accompany the cosmic creation (the material universe) to explain how to navigate material existence.
 - Speculative knowledge of God will lead nowhere.

### Lesson 7: Chant the Hare Krishna Mantra

#### Examples

 - **The Gopis**: As soon as the gopis heard the call of Krsna's flute, they gave up their homes and families and rushed to Him. Their husbands, brothers, and fathers asked why they were leaving their social duties, but the gopis did not care about social disapproval.

#### Step 1: Chant the Holy Name

Chant: Hare Krishna, Hare Krishna, Krishna Krishna, Hare Hare / Hare Rama, Hare Rama, Rama Rama, Hare Hare.

#### Step 2: Understand the Meaning

Hare means "O energy of the Lord (His spiritual potency, Srimati Radharani)," and Krishna means "O Lord Krishna." When chanting Hare Krishna, the chanter says, "O Lord Krsna, O energy of Krsna, kindly engage me in Your service."

#### Step 3: Chant Seriously and Regularly

Chant on beads daily. Chant in the association of devotees. Chanting is the most accessible spiritual process for the present age of Kali-yuga.

#### Best Practices

- ✅ Chant attentively on beads.
- ✅ Chant in the association of devotees.
- ❌ Don't think that simply thinking of Krishna is enough; engage the senses in chanting.

#### Keep In Mind

 - Chanting is the most accessible spiritual process for this age (Kali-yuga).
 - Chanting purifies the consciousness and awakens one's dormant Krishna consciousness (one's original spiritual nature).
 - The chanting of the holy name is directly connecting with the Supreme Absolute Truth.

### Lesson 8: Progress Through the Stages of Spiritual Life

#### Step 1: Begin with Faith (Sraddha)

Be inquisitive: "What is this Krsna consciousness movement (the International Society for Krishna Consciousness, or ISKCON)? Let me study this movement."

#### Step 2: Associate with Devotees

Mix with those who are cultivating Krsna conscious knowledge. Try to understand how they experience spiritual life.

#### Step 3: Become a Practitioner

When you become one of the devotees, your misgivings go away. You become more faithful and get a real taste for Krsna consciousness.

#### Step 4: Develop Firm Faith and Taste

Why aren't these young devotees going to see the cinema? Why don't they eat meat or go to the nightclub? Because their spiritual preferences have changed. They hate all these things now.

#### Step 5: Achieve God Realization and Love of God

Progress to the final stage of love of God, the perfection of religion.

#### Best Practices

- ✅ Mix with devotees to develop spiritual taste.
- ✅ Let spiritual taste change naturally.
- ❌ Don't expect instant perfection; progress systematically.

#### Keep In Mind

 - A first-class religion develops pure love of Krsna, not "I believe, you believe" sentimental ceremonies.
- The devotee progresses through these stages: faith → association → removal of misgivings → firm faith → taste → realizing Krsna as the Supreme Personality of Godhead through devotional service → love of God.

### Lesson 9: Recognize Real Religion

#### Step 1: Understand That Religion Means Knowing and Loving God

Primarily, religion means to know the Supreme Personality of Godhead and to love Him. In the present age, because of a lack of proper spiritual training and revelation, people cannot know God, what to speak of loving Him.

#### Step 2: Avoid Cheating Religions

Some religious people are satisfied simply by attending church (a Christian place of worship) and praying, "O God, give us our daily bread." In the Srimad-Bhagavatam this is called a cheating religion, because the aim is not to know and love God but to gain some personal benefit.

#### Step 3: Follow the Four Classes of Spiritual Seekers

 - Karmis are those who work for material profit.
 - Jnanis are those who try to become immortal by merging into the impersonal Brahman.
 - Yogis are those who want mystic powers (siddhis).
 - Bhaktas are devotees who simply want to serve the Supreme Personality of Godhead out of love.

#### Best Practices

- ✅ Strive for the platform of the bhakta—pure love of God.
- ❌ Don't remain on the platforms of karma, jnana, or yoga if you want pure love.

#### Keep In Mind

 - Real love of God is ahaituky apratihata—unmotivated by material desires and uncheatable by material obstacles—devotional service that cannot be checked by any material cause and is offered without any selfish motive.
 - When one attains the stage of pure love of God, that is the perfection of religion.

### Lesson 10: Understand the Different Types of Liberation

#### Step 1: Learn the Five Types of Liberation

- Salokya: live on the same planet as the Lord
- Sarsti: have the same opulences as the Lord
- Sarupya: have the same form as the Lord
- Samipya: always be associated with the Lord
- Sayujya: merge into the impersonal Brahman

#### Step 2: Realize That Pure Devotees Reject All of Them

Pure devotees do not accept any of the five types of material liberation. Pure devotees desire only to serve Krsna in devotional service.

#### Step 3: Follow Prahlada Maharaja's Example

When Prahlada Maharaja was offered whatever he desired, he said: "My Lord, I am Your eternal servant. It is my eternal duty to serve You, so how can I accept any material benefit from You? Then I would not be Your servant; I would be a merchant."

#### Best Practices

- ✅ Cultivate the desire to serve Krsna, not to attain liberation.
- ❌ Don't aspire for liberation as the goal; pure service is higher.

#### Keep In Mind

 - Those who have material desires or even a desire for liberation (mukti) cannot attain pure love of Godhead.
- A pure devotee refuses benedictions because accepting them would make him a merchant, not a servant.

### Lesson 11: Understand the Pleasure Potency of Krsna

#### Step 1: Realize That Krsna Has a Pleasure Potency

The foundation of Krsna consciousness is Krsna's pleasure potency—His capacity to enjoy and to be enjoyed by His devotees. Srimati Radharani (the chief consort of Krsna), the gopis (cowherd girls of Vrndavana), and Krsna's cowherd boyfriends (Krsna's childhood friends) are all expansions of Krsna's pleasure potency (the aspect of Krsna's energy that produces spiritual enjoyment).

#### Step 2: Understand That We Are Part of That Potency

The individual soul (jiva) is originally a part of this pleasure potency. Due to contact with the material energy (maya), the soul has forgotten its actual position.

#### Step 3: Engage in the Pleasure Potency of the Lord

All relationships with Krsna in devotional service that aim at satisfying Krsna's senses are manifestations of Krsna's pleasure potency.

#### Best Practices

- ✅ Engage in service that satisfies Krsna's senses.
- ❌ Don't try to satisfy your own senses independently.

#### Keep In Mind

- Impersonalists cannot think in these terms because they deny the pleasure potency.
 - The individual soul becomes attracted by Krsna's opulences (wealth, strength, fame, beauty, knowledge, and renunciation) when they are imperfectly perceived by conditioned souls (embodied beings in material existence).

### Lesson 12: See Krsna in Everything

#### Step 1: Understand That Krsna Is Present in Everything

Krsna says, "I am the taste of water" (raso 'ham apsu). Everyone drinks water, and the taste of water is there—if one thinks of this taste as Krsna, one begins realizing Krsna every day.

#### Step 2: Meditate on Krsna's Presence

Krsna says, "I am the light of the sun and the moon" (prabhasmi sasi-suryayoh). If one thinks of the source of the sun and moon, one becomes Krsna conscious.

#### Step 3: Use Krsna as the Code Word

Just as a code word (a word that reminds one of Krsna's qualities) reminds you of all of Krsna's qualities and pastimes, when the mind is attracted to Krsna by hearing a small amount of glorification of His qualities, such attraction is the beginning of pure Krsna consciousness.

#### Best Practices

- ✅ Meditate on Krsna by seeing Him in everyday experiences.
- ✅ Use the Bhagavad-gita as your guide.
- ❌ Don't think that one cannot see God; Krsna is present at every moment.

#### Keep In Mind

- God can be seen at every moment by devotees who know how to see Him.
 - If one wants to become God conscious and realize one's own essence, becoming God conscious is not difficult.

### Lesson 13: Follow the Example of the Six Gosvamis

#### Step 1: Study the Lives of the Six Gosvamis

The Six Gosvamis—Sri Rupa, Sanatana, Sri Jiva, Gopala, Raghunatha dasa, and Raghunatha Bhatta—were Lord Caitanya's principal disciples. Lord Caitanya is an incarnation of Krsna who appeared in 1486 to teach congregational chanting of the holy names.

#### Step 2: Engage in Chanting, Dancing, and Writing

They were always engaged in chanting Hare Krishna and dancing in ecstasy (intense spiritual joy). They taught that when a devotee is absorbed in krsna-kirtana (congregational chanting of the holy names), he merges into the ocean of love of Krsna.

#### Step 3: Follow Their Renunciation

They slept only 1.5 hours daily and ate practically nothing. They were almost completely free from the bodily necessities such as eating, sleeping, mating, and defending.

#### Best Practices

- ✅ Write and distribute Krsna conscious literature.
- ✅ Chant and dance in ecstasy.
- ❌ Don't be attached to material comforts.

#### Keep In Mind

  - The mission of saintly persons (advanced devotees) is to elevate those who are suffering (those who are distressed).
 - A learned person who is a devotee of Krsna is honored wherever they go.

### Lesson 14: Understand That a Pure Devotee Has No Enemy

#### Step 1: Realize That a Pure Devotee Is Nonenvious

The pure devotee (one whose only desire is to serve Krsna) is not envious (jealous or resentful) because he sees every living entity (all souls) as a spirit soul, part of Krsna, and therefore holds no enmity toward anyone.

#### Step 2: Follow the Example of Haridasa Thakura

Haridasa Thakura did not enter the Jagannatha temple (the Deity form of Lord Krsna worshipped at Jagannatha Puri) because he was born in a Muslim family. Haridasa Thakura thought, "Oh, why should I go and disturb the temple authorities? I shall chant here." Lord Caitanya came daily to see him. This demonstrates the power of pure devotion: he does not have to go to Jagannatha; Jagannatha comes to him.

#### Step 3: Worship the Devotee

Krsna says that worship (devotional service) of His devotee is even more effective than worship of Himself. A pure devotee can actually deliver Krsna, for he knows the science of Krsna consciousness perfectly.

#### Best Practices

- ✅ Respect pure devotees as representatives of Krsna.
- ❌ Don't discriminate among living entities based on bodily designations.

#### Keep In Mind

 - On the spiritual platform (the platform of Krsna consciousness), there are no distinctions of high and low, male and female.
 - The pure devotee, seeing all living entities with equal vision, is therefore nonenvious (not jealous or resentful).

### Lesson 15: Understand the Position of the Spiritual Master

#### Step 1: Accept That the Spiritual Master Is as Good as God

The spiritual master (the authorized representative of Krsna) is to be honored as much as the Supreme Personality of Godhead, Krsna, because he is the most confidential servant—one who is entrusted with Krsna's most confidential teachings and service (saksad-dharitvena samasta-sastrair: "The spiritual master is to be honored as much as the Supreme Personality of Godhead because he is the most confidential servant of the Lord. This is confirmed in all revealed scriptures.").

#### Step 2: Understand That the Guru Never Claims to Be God

A genuine spiritual master never claims to be the Supreme Personality of Godhead. The disciple's duty (to serve and respect the spiritual master) is to offer respect (through service and obedience) to the spiritual master as Krsna's representative, but the spiritual master never thinks, "My disciples are offering me the same respect they offer to God; therefore I have become God."

#### Step 3: Serve the Spiritual Master

By the mercy of the spiritual master, the disciple receives Krsna's benediction (yasya prasadad bhagavat-prasadah: "By the mercy of the spiritual master, one receives the benediction of Krsna.").

#### Best Practices

- ✅ The disciple should offer all respect to the spiritual master.
- ✅ The disciple should serve the spiritual master faithfully.
 - ❌ The disciple should not think the spiritual master is an ordinary person.
 - ❌ A spiritual aspirant should not accept a so-called guru who claims to be the Supreme Personality of Godhead.

#### Keep In Mind

- Krsna is always Krsna; the spiritual master is always the spiritual master.
 - A spiritual master in the disciplic succession is addressed as prabhupada, meaning "one who has taken the position of the Lord (as the Supreme Personality of Godhead)."
