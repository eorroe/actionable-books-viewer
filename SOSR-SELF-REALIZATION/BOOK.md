# Science of Self-Realization: Krsna Consciousness for Modern Life

## Overview

Science of Self-Realization teaches the science of understanding one's true identity as an eternal spiritual soul and establishing a loving relationship with the Supreme Personality of Godhead, Krsna. Through the teachings of His Divine Grace A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada, this book provides a practical, authoritative path for transcending material existence and achieving life's supreme perfection. Drawing from the Bhagavad-gita, Srimad-Bhagavatam, and the teachings of Lord Caitanya, it offers a comprehensive guide to spiritual realization that is accessible to everyone regardless of background.

## When to Follow Book Teachings

- When you need to understand who you truly are beyond the material body
- When working to solve the real problems of life: birth, death, old age, and disease
- When the user asks about the meaning of life and how to achieve lasting happiness
- When you need to distinguish genuine spiritual teachers from false ones
- When seeking practical methods for spiritual advancement in the modern age
- When looking for solutions to social problems like crime and moral degradation
- When you want to understand the science of God consciousness and how to love God

## Lessons From Book

### Lesson 1: Understand Your True Identity as a Spirit Soul

#### Examples

- **The Coat and Shirt Analogy**: The body is like a shirt or coat; the soul is the person within. Just as you remain the same person when you change clothes, the soul remains the same while the body changes from childhood to old age.
- **The Boy and His Father**: A boy speculating about his father's identity by searching the whole city will never find him; the simple process is to ask his mother. Similarly, transcendental knowledge must be received from authority, not by speculation.

#### Step 1: Recognize That You Are Not Your Body

Observe the changes in your body throughout life (child → boy → adult → old person) while your identity remains the same. This proves the soul is constant while the body changes. Understand that at death, the soul leaves the body and takes another body according to karma and desires, just as you change clothes.

#### Step 2: Understand the Process of Transmigration

Learn that the spirit soul is invisible and atomic in size. At death, the subtle body (mind, intelligence, ego) carries the soul to another gross body. This process is like air carrying a fragrance—invisible but real. Your next birth is decided by association with material nature modes during this life.

#### Step 3: Realize That Misunderstanding the Body as the Self Is Ignorance

The whole modern civilization operates on the misunderstanding that the body is the self (dehatma-buddhi). This is the mentality of cats and dogs. If you think you are your body, you will identify yourself by bodily designations: "I am American," "I am Indian," etc. This is ignorance.

#### Best Practices

- ✅ Regularly remind yourself: "I am not this body; I am a spirit soul."
- ✅ Observe how others identify themselves by bodily designations and recognize this as ignorance.
- ❌ Don't become overly concerned with bodily maintenance at the expense of spiritual growth.
- ❌ Don't think that material advancement (nice house, car, job) equals real advancement.

#### Keep In Mind

- The soul is eternal, unborn, and undying (Bg. 2.20).
- The body is temporary, but the soul continues.
- Misunderstanding the body as the self leads to endless material struggles.

#### Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Thinking "I am my body" leads to endless material struggles.
  **Solution:** Chant Hare Krishna to awaken spiritual consciousness and realize your true identity.

### Lesson 2: Recognize the Real Problems of Life

#### Examples

- **The Train Compartment Analogy**: A first-class train compartment is useless if the train isn't going to your destination—similarly, material comfort is useless without spiritual progress.
- **The Dog and Four Legs Analogy**: The dog runs on four legs, man on four wheels—this is not advancement.

#### Step 1: Identify the Four Real Problems

Birth, death, old age, and disease are the real problems of life. Material civilization only addresses eating, sleeping, mating, and defense—the same activities animals perform.

#### Step 2: Understand That No Material Advancement Solves These Problems

No amount of material advancement can solve the problems of birth, death, old age, and disease. A man may have atomic weapons, but he still dies.

#### Step 3: Accept That the Human Form of Life Is Meant to Solve These Problems

The human body is an excellent vehicle by which we can reach eternal life. It is a rare and very important boat for crossing over the ocean of nescience which is material existence.

#### Best Practices

- ✅ Seek spiritual solutions, not material ones, for life's real problems.
- ✅ Use the human form of life to solve the problems of existence.
- ❌ Don't be misled by polished animalism—having a nice apartment doesn't make you advanced.

#### Keep In Mind

- A dog sleeps, a man sleeps. The business is the same.
- Man cannot conquer the universe with atomic bombs; he is still subject to death.

### Lesson 3: Cut the Five Chains of Material Attachment

#### Step 1: Identify the Five Strong Chains

The five chains that anchor the boat of human life to mundane consciousness are:
1. Attachment to the material body due to ignorance of spiritual facts
2. Attachment to kinsmen due to bodily relations
3. Attachment to the land of birth and material possessions
4. Attachment to material science without spiritual light
5. Attachment to religious forms without knowing the Personality of Godhead

#### Step 2: Use the Weapon of Detachment

Cut these chains with the weapon of detachment. Detach from material activities by spiritual activities, not by material inertia.

#### Step 3: Seek the Spiritual Destination

Seek that place from which, having once gone, one never returns, and there surrender to that Supreme Personality of Godhead from whom everything has begun (Bg. 15.3-4).

#### Best Practices

- ✅ Spiritualize all material activities—offer everything to Krishna.
- ✅ Use detachment to cut material attachments.
- ❌ Don't try to escape material life by simply renouncing without spiritual engagement.

#### Keep In Mind

- These chains are compared to a deeply rooted banyan tree (Bg. 15).
- Material inertia is negative; spiritual activity is positive activation of real life.

### Lesson 4: Understand That Service Is Our Constitutional Position

#### Examples

- **The Tree Analogy**: If we want to render service to a tree, we must water the root. If we pour water on the leaves, branches, and twigs, there is little benefit. If the Supreme Personality of Godhead is served, all other parts and parcels will be automatically satisfied.

#### Step 1: Recognize That Everyone Is Rendering Service

Everyone is rendering service to someone—family, country, society, or even a pet. All these factors prove that we are constitutionally meant to render service.

#### Step 2: Realize That Material Service Is Frustrating

On the material platform, everyone is frustrated because the service being rendered is not properly directed.

#### Step 3: Serve the Supreme Personality of Godhead

When Krsna is served, all other parts and parcels are automatically satisfied. Consequently, all welfare activities as well as service to society, family, and nation are realized by serving the Supreme Personality of Godhead.

#### Best Practices

- ✅ Direct your service toward Krsna; this is the root.
- ✅ Water the root of the tree, not the leaves.
- ❌ Don't expect lasting satisfaction from serving material people or causes.

#### Keep In Mind

- We are parts and parcels of God, meant for serving Him.
- Without serving God, all welfare activities remain incomplete.

### Lesson 5: Practice Pure Devotional Service (Bhakti-Yoga)

#### Examples

- **Rupa Gosvami's Story**: Rupa desired food for his brother, and Krsna sent it through Radharani. Sanatana warned him not to accept service from the Lord, as pure devotees simply want to render service.

#### Step 1: Understand the Definition of Pure Devotional Service

From the Narada-pancaratra: hrsikena hrsikesa-sevanam bhaktir ucyate—"By one's senses, engage the senses in the service of the master of the senses." Krsna is the Lord of the senses. We should engage our senses, not just our minds, in His service.

#### Step 2: Purify the Senses

Our senses are original but now covered by material desires. We have to cure ourselves and become free from such desires. When one's senses are no longer inclined toward material sense gratification, one's status is called pure devotion.

#### Step 3: Serve Krsna Favorably, Without Material Desires

Caitanya Mahaprabhu recommends pure devotional service. There should be no desire to fulfill one's own material desires, no attempt to understand Krsna by experimental philosophy, and no fruitive activities. The only desire should be to serve Him favorably, as He desires.

#### Best Practices

- ✅ Serve Krsna favorably, as He desires.
- ✅ Take shelter of the spiritual master to learn what Krsna wants.
- ❌ Don't serve Krsna with material motives for profit or distinction.
- ❌ Don't think that meditation alone is pure devotional service; engage the senses in Krsna's service.

#### Keep In Mind

- Pure devotion is devoid of all desires for personal sense gratification.
- Even enemies of Krsna get liberation if killed by Him; therefore devotees should not desire liberation but only service.
- As long as one desires material benefit or even salvation, he cannot attain pure love of Godhead.

#### Security & Safety Notes

- Having material desires while practicing devotional service will prevent pure love of God from developing.

#### Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Wanting material benefits from devotional service.
  **Solution:** Check your motives. Do you want Krishna or His benedictions?

### Lesson 6: Realize God Through Authoritative Sources, Not Speculation

#### Examples

- **The Mother and Father Analogy**: If a boy wants to know who his father is, the simple process is to ask his mother. The mother will say, "This is your father." Speculation by searching the whole city will never yield perfect knowledge.

#### Step 1: Accept That God Is Unlimited

God is unlimited; we are limited. Our knowledge and perception are both very limited, so we cannot understand the unlimited by our own speculation.

#### Step 2: Receive Knowledge from the Perfect Source

Receive knowledge from Krsna, the most perfect being. The Vedas are like the manual that comes with a product; they explain how to deal with creation.

#### Step 3: Apply the Knowledge Practically

Krsna consciousness is not dry theorizing. It is practical and can be tested by personal experience.

#### Best Practices

- ✅ Accept knowledge from authority (Krsna, the Vedas, the spiritual master).
- ✅ Study the Bhagavad-gita As It Is for perfect knowledge.
- ❌ Don't waste time on mental speculation about God's existence.

#### Keep In Mind

- The Vedas come along with the cosmic creation to explain how to deal with it.
- Speculative knowledge of God will lead nowhere.

### Lesson 7: Chant the Hare Krishna Mantra

#### Examples

- **The Gopis**: As soon as the gopis heard the sound of Krsna's flute, they gave up everything and rushed to Him. Their husbands, brothers, and fathers asked why they were leaving their duties, but they did not care.

#### Step 1: Chant the Holy Name

Chant: Hare Krishna, Hare Krishna, Krishna Krishna, Hare Hare / Hare Rama, Hare Rama, Rama Rama, Hare Hare.

#### Step 2: Understand the Meaning

Hare means "O energy of the Lord," and Krishna means "O Lord Krishna." When we chant Hare Krishna, we are saying, "O Lord Krsna, O energy of Krsna, kindly engage me in Your service."

#### Step 3: Chant Seriously and Regularly

Chant on beads daily. Chant in the association of devotees. Chanting is the easiest process for this age.

#### Best Practices

- ✅ Chant attentively on beads.
- ✅ Chant in the association of devotees.
- ❌ Don't think that simply thinking of Krishna is enough; engage the senses in chanting.

#### Keep In Mind

- Chanting is the easiest process for this age (Kali-yuga).
- Chanting purifies the heart and awakens dormant Krishna consciousness.
- The chanting of the holy name is directly contacting the Supreme Absolute Truth.

### Lesson 8: Progress Through the Stages of Spiritual Life

#### Step 1: Begin with Faith (Sraddha)

Be inquisitive: "What is this Krsna consciousness movement? Let me study it."

#### Step 2: Associate with Devotees

Mix with those who are cultivating this knowledge. Try to understand how they are feeling.

#### Step 3: Become a Practitioner

When you become one of them, your misgivings go away. You become more faithful and get a real taste for Krsna consciousness.

#### Step 4: Develop Firm Faith and Taste

Why aren't these boys going to see the cinema? Why don't they eat meat or go to the nightclub? Because their taste has changed. They hate all these things now.

#### Step 5: Achieve God Realization and Love of God

Progress to the final stage of love of God, the perfection of religion.

#### Best Practices

- ✅ Mix with devotees to develop taste.
- ✅ Let your taste change naturally.
- ❌ Don't expect instant perfection; progress systematically.

#### Keep In Mind

- First-class religion develops love of God, not "I believe, you believe" sentimental ceremonies.
- The stages are: faith → association → removal of misgivings → firm faith → taste → God realization → love of God.

### Lesson 9: Recognize Real Religion

#### Step 1: Understand That Religion Means Knowing and Loving God

Primarily, religion means to know God and to love Him. Nowadays, because of a lack of training, nobody knows God, what to speak of loving Him.

#### Step 2: Avoid Cheating Religions

People are satisfied simply going to church and praying, "O God, give us our daily bread." In the Srimad-Bhagavatam this is called a cheating religion, because the aim is not to know and love God but to gain some personal profit.

#### Step 3: Follow the Four Classes of Spiritual Seekers

- Karmis work for material profit.
- Jnanis try to become immortal by merging into God.
- Yogis want mystic powers.
- Bhaktas (devotees) simply want to serve God out of love.

#### Best Practices

- ✅ Strive for the platform of the bhakta—pure love of God.
- ❌ Don't remain on the platforms of karma, jnana, or yoga if you want pure love.

#### Keep In Mind

- Real love of God is ahaituky apratihata—unmotivated and uncheatable.
- When you come to the stage of loving God, that is perfection.

### Lesson 10: Understand the Different Types of Liberation

#### Step 1: Learn the Five Types of Liberation

- Salokya: live on the same planet as the Lord
- Sarsti: have the same opulences as the Lord
- Sarupya: have the same form as the Lord
- Samipya: always be associated with the Lord
- Sayujya: merge into the impersonal Brahman

#### Step 2: Realize That Pure Devotees Reject All of Them

Pure devotees do not accept any kind of liberation. They only want to serve Krsna.

#### Step 3: Follow Prahlada Maharaja's Example

When Prahlada Maharaja was offered whatever he liked, he said: "My Lord, I am Your eternal servant. It is my duty to serve You, so how can I accept any benefit from it? Then I would not be Your servant; I would be a merchant."

#### Best Practices

- ✅ Cultivate the desire to serve Krsna, not to attain liberation.
- ❌ Don't aspire for liberation as the goal; pure service is higher.

#### Keep In Mind

- Those who have material desires or even a desire for salvation cannot attain pure love of Godhead.
- A pure devotee refuses benedictions because accepting them would make him a merchant, not a servant.

### Lesson 11: Understand the Pleasure Potency of Krsna

#### Step 1: Realize That Krsna Has a Pleasure Potency

The whole basis of Krsna consciousness and a relationship with Krsna is the pleasure potency of Krsna Himself. Srimati Radharani, the damsels of Vraja, and Krsna's cowherd boyfriends are all expansions of Krsna's pleasure potency.

#### Step 2: Understand That We Are Part of That Potency

As the individual soul is concerned, it is originally a part and parcel of this pleasure potency. Due to contact with material nature, the soul has forgotten its actual position.

#### Step 3: Engage in the Pleasure Potency of the Lord

All relationships with Krsna that aim at satisfying Krsna's senses are manifestations of Krsna's pleasure potency.

#### Best Practices

- ✅ Engage in service that satisfies Krsna's senses.
- ❌ Don't try to satisfy your own senses independently.

#### Keep In Mind

- Impersonalists cannot think in these terms because they deny the pleasure potency.
- The individual soul becomes attracted by Krsna's opulences when they are imperfectly represented by other conditioned souls.

### Lesson 12: See Krsna in Everything

#### Step 1: Understand That Krsna Is Present in Everything

Krsna says, "I am the taste of water" (raso 'ham apsu). Everyone drinks water, and the taste is there—if we think of this taste as Krsna, we begin realizing God every day.

#### Step 2: Meditate on Krsna's Presence

Krsna says, "I am the light of the sun and the moon" (prabhasmi sasi-suryayoh). If we think of the source of these emanations, we will ultimately become God conscious.

#### Step 3: Use Krsna as the Code Word

Just as a code word reminds you of all information behind it, when our minds are attracted to Krsna by hearing a little glorification of His qualities, that is the beginning of pure Krsna consciousness.

#### Best Practices

- ✅ Meditate on Krsna by seeing Him in everyday experiences.
- ✅ Use the Bhagavad-gita as your guide.
- ❌ Don't think that you cannot see God; He is present at every moment.

#### Keep In Mind

- God can be seen at every moment by those who know how to see Him.
- If we want to become God conscious and realize our own essence, it is not very difficult.

### Lesson 13: Follow the Example of the Six Gosvamis

#### Step 1: Study the Lives of the Six Gosvamis

Sri Rupa, Sanatana, Sri Jiva, Gopala, Raghunatha dasa, and Raghunatha Bhatta Gosvamis were Lord Caitanya's principal disciples.

#### Step 2: Engage in Chanting, Dancing, and Writing

They were always engaged in chanting Hare Krishna and dancing in ecstasy. They taught that when one is merged in krsna-kirtana, he merges into the ocean of love of Krsna.

#### Step 3: Follow Their Renunciation

They slept only 1.5 hours daily and ate practically nothing. They were practically released from the bodily necessities of eating, sleeping, mating, and defending.

#### Best Practices

- ✅ Write and distribute Krsna conscious literature.
- ✅ Chant and dance in ecstasy.
- ❌ Don't be attached to material comforts.

#### Keep In Mind

- The mission of saintly persons is to make suffering humanity happy by elevating everyone to spiritual consciousness.
- A learned man, a devotee of God, is honored wherever he goes.

### Lesson 14: Understand That a Pure Devotee Has No Enemy

#### Step 1: Realize That a Pure Devotee Is Nonenvious

A pure devotee has no enemy because he is not envious. He sees every living entity as a spirit soul.

#### Step 2: Follow the Example of Haridasa Thakura

Haridasa Thakura did not enter the Jagannatha temple because he was born in a Muhammadan family. He thought, "Oh, why should I go and disturb them? I shall chant here." Lord Caitanya came daily to see him. This is the power of a pure devotee: he doesn't have to go to Jagannatha; Jagannatha comes to him.

#### Step 3: Worship the Devotee

Krsna says that worship of His devotee is even more valuable than worship of Himself. The devotee is actually able to deliver Krsna, for he knows the science of Krsna consciousness.

#### Best Practices

- ✅ Respect pure devotees as much as Krsna.
- ❌ Don't discriminate among living entities based on bodily designations.

#### Keep In Mind

- On the spiritual platform, there are no distinctions of high and low, man and woman.
- The pure devotee, seeing everything with an equal mind, is therefore nonenvious.

---

### Lesson 15: Understand the Position of the Spiritual Master

#### Step 1: Accept That the Spiritual Master Is as Good as God

The spiritual master is to be honored as much as the Supreme Personality of Godhead because he is the most confidential servitor of the Lord (saksad-dharitvena samasta-sastrair).

#### Step 2: Understand That the Guru Never Claims to Be God

The guru never says, "I am God." The disciple's duty is to offer respect to the guru just as he offers respect to God, but the guru never thinks, "My disciples are offering me the same respect they offer to God; therefore I have become God."

#### Step 3: Serve the Spiritual Master

By the mercy of the spiritual master, one receives the benediction of Krsna (yasya prasadad bhagavat-prasadah).

#### Best Practices

- ✅ Offer all respect to the spiritual master.
- ✅ Serve the spiritual master faithfully.
- ❌ Don't think the spiritual master is an ordinary man.
- ❌ Don't accept a "guru" who claims to be God.

#### Keep In Mind

- God is always God; guru is always guru.
- The guru is addressed as prabhupada—"he who has taken the position of the Lord."
