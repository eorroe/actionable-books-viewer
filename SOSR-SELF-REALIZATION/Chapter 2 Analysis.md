# Chapter Two: Choosing a Spiritual Master - What Is a Guru?

## Chapter Title and Overview

**Chapter Two: Choosing a Spiritual Master - What Is a Guru?**

This chapter explains the essential role of the spiritual master (guru) in spiritual life. It defines who a genuine guru is, how to distinguish a genuine guru from a fake, and the proper method for approaching and serving a bona fide spiritual master. The chapter emphasizes that the guru comes in the disciplic succession and transmits the same message without change.

## Actionable Lessons

### Lesson 1: Understand the Guru's Role

**Step 1:** Recognize that the guru's business is to bring disciples from darkness to light.

**Step 2:** Understand that the guru is a representative of God and His previous acaryas.

**Step 3:** Accept that the guru must be serious about understanding spiritual life, God, proper action, and one's relationship with God.

**Step 4:** Realize that the guru takes on great responsibility to guide the disciple back home, back to Godhead.

**Best Practices:**
- ✅ Respect the guru as much as God because he is God's most confidential servant.
- ✅ Understand that the guru is not God but the representative of God.
- ❌ Don't think the guru is an ordinary man.
- ❌ Don't accept a guru suddenly out of fanaticism.

**Keep In Mind:**
- The guru is as good as God (saksad-dharitvena samasta-sastraih).
- The guru never says, "I am God."

---

### Lesson 2: Find a Bona Fide Guru

**Step 1:** Study the prospective guru carefully before accepting him.

**Step 2:** Ensure the guru comes in the disciplic succession (sampradaya).

**Step 3:** Verify that the guru's message matches the original teachings of Vyasadeva and Krsna.

**Step 4:** Ensure the guru is fixed in the Absolute Truth (brahma-nistham).

**Best Practices:**
- ✅ Take your time; don't rush into a guru-disciple relationship.
- ✅ The guru should also study you to see if you are fit to become a disciple.
- ❌ Don't keep a guru as a fashion statement.
- ❌ Don't accept a guru who gives his own opinion rather than the teachings of the scriptures.

**Keep In Mind:**
- Unless initiated by a bona fide spiritual master in the disciplic succession, the mantra is without effect (Padma Purana).
- The real guru cannot be two; he speaks the same message as his predecessors.

---

### Lesson 3: Approach the Guru Properly

**Step 1:** Surrender to the guru (pranipatena).

**Step 2:** Inquire submissively (pariprasnena).

**Step 3:** Render service (sevaya).

**Step 4:** Let the self-realized soul impart knowledge to you (Bg. 4.34).

**Best Practices:**
- ✅ Go to the guru with a spirit of surrender, not to challenge him.
- ✅ Serve the guru; this is the process of learning.
- ❌ Don't go to a guru simply to argue or debate.

**Keep In Mind:**
- Without surrender, we cannot learn anything.
- Arjuna's example: "I am Your disciple. Please instruct me."

---

### Lesson 4: Recognize the Genuine Guru

**Step 1:** Identify that the genuine guru is God's representative.

**Step 2:** Verify that he speaks only about God and tries to get people to become God's devotees.

**Step 3:** Confirm that he has no interest in materialistic life.

**Step 4:** Test: he should convince people to love God.

**Best Practices:**
- ✅ Find the guru who is most addicted to Krishna.
- ✅ The genuine guru is not a businessman; he does not charge fees.
- ❌ Don't accept a guru who says, "I am God" or "I will make you God."
- ❌ Don't accept a guru who wants money or fame.

**Keep In Mind:**
- A guru cannot be bad; if someone is bad, he cannot be a guru.
- It doesn't matter how the guru is dressed.

---

### Lesson 5: Understand the Process of Disciplic Succession

**Step 1:** Learn that knowledge descends through the disciplic succession: Krsna → Brahma → Narada → Vyasadeva → Madhva → ... → Caitanya Mahaprabhu → present acarya.

**Step 2:** Understand that the guru transmits the message unchanged, like a telegram messenger.

**Step 3:** Realize that the guru is one because the message is one.

**Best Practices:**
- ✅ Study the Srimad-Bhagavatam, Bhagavad-gita, and other Vedic texts.
- ✅ Follow the previous acaryas.
- ❌ Don't interpret the words of Krsna; accept them as they are.

**Keep In Mind:**
- Those who interpret divine sound by imperfect sense perception cannot be real gurus.
- Srila Vyasadeva did not give his own opinion; he wrote "sri-bhagavan uvaca" (the Supreme Personality of Godhead says).

---

### Lesson 6: Accept the Four Regulative Principles

**Step 1:** Give up illicit sex life (sex outside of marriage).

**Step 2:** Give up all intoxicants, including tea, cigarettes, alcohol, marijuana.

**Step 3:** Give up eating meat, eggs, and fish.

**Step 4:** Give up gambling.

**Best Practices:**
- ✅ Follow these principles strictly for purification.
- ❌ Don't think these principles are too difficult; they are essential for spiritual life.
- ❌ Don't accept a "guru" who says you can continue these activities and still advance spiritually.

**Keep In Mind:**
- Without these restrictions, any yoga meditation or spiritual discipline cannot be genuine.
- These principles are the beginning of spiritual life.

---

### Lesson 7: Understand That Spiritual Life Is Simple

**Step 1:** Realize that Krsna consciousness is not complicated.

**Step 2:** Chant the holy name of the Lord: Hare Krishna, Hare Krishna, Krishna Krishna, Hare Hare / Hare Rama, Hare Rama, Rama Rama, Hare Hare.

**Step 3:** Understand that the name, form, and qualities of the Lord are absolute.

**Best Practices:**
- ✅ Chant the mantra on beads daily.
- ✅ Associate with devotees.
- ❌ Don't think you need to become a great philosopher or scientist to understand God.

**Keep In Mind:**
- Krsna consciousness is a great science, but the process is simple.
- Even a child can participate.

---

### Lesson 8: Understand That We Are Born Rascals and Fools

**Step 1:** Accept that we are born in ignorance and need enlightenment.

**Step 2:** Understand that without knowledge, we are no better than animals.

**Step 3:** Take knowledge from the Vedic scriptures to perfect our lives.

**Best Practices:**
- ✅ Be humble and approach the guru as a fool seeking knowledge.
- ✅ Study the Bhagavad-gita and Srimad-Bhagavatam.
- ❌ Don't think you are already learned and don't need a guru.
- ❌ Don't remain in ignorance, thinking you are advanced.

---

### Lesson 9: Understand That the Human Form of Life Is Rare and Precious

**Step 1:** Recognize that the human form of life is attained after many millions of years in the cycle of transmigration.

**Step 2:** Use this rare opportunity for self-realization, not just for eating, sleeping, mating, and defending.

**Step 3:** Follow the principle: "This human form of life is obtained after many, many births, and although it is not permanent, it can offer the highest benefits. Therefore a sober and intelligent man should immediately try to fulfill his mission and attain the highest profit in life before another death occurs."

**Best Practices:**
- ✅ Use human life to understand God and become Krishna conscious.
- ❌ Don't misuse human life for sense gratification like animals.
- ❌ Don't think that economic development is the goal of life.

**Keep In Mind:**
- The path of spiritual realization is very difficult; it is sharp like a razor's edge.
- Those who attain human life and don't use it for spiritual realization are committing suicide.

---

### Lesson 10: Follow the Principle of Renunciation in Krsna Consciousness

**Step 1:** Live in accordance with Krishna consciousness.

**Step 2:** Be without attachment for sense gratification.

**Step 3:** Accept only what is necessary for the upkeep of the body.

**Step 4:** Use everything in the service of Krishna.

**Best Practices:**
- ✅ Use material things for Krishna's service.
- ❌ Don't renounce things that could be used in Krishna's service under the pretext that they are material.

**Keep In Mind:**
- Complete renunciation means to be without attachment for sense gratification.
- Real renunciation is to engage everything in Krishna's service.

---

## Examples

1. **The Telegram Messenger Analogy**: The guru delivers God's message exactly as it is, without adding, editing, or correcting it.
2. **The Aquatic Animal Analogy**: Taking an aquatic animal out of water and providing all comforts on land won't save it; similarly, spirit and matter are completely contradictory.
3. **The Bird in the Cage Analogy**: We concentrate all energies on maintaining the material cage and neglect the captive soul within.
4. **The Patient Who Wants to Keep His Disease**: People want spiritual life while continuing their sinful activities—a contradiction.
5. **The London Times Interview**: Srila Prabhupada explains how to distinguish genuine gurus from fake ones.
6. **The Dog and Stranger Analogy**: The dog thinks "I am a dog" and the stranger is "other"; similarly, materialistic people think in bodily designations.
7. **The Ass and the Donkey**: The asses see spiritualists as lazy, but they are actually busy for a higher subject matter.
8. **The Judge and Rubber-Stamping Worker**: A judge sits quietly but gets a high salary because of his qualifications; similarly, spiritual advancement is based on qualification, not external show.
