# Chapter 2 Analysis: Since I Was Broke - Be Delusional

## Chapter Summary

Chapter 2 makes the case that delusion—defined as unwavering, irrational self-belief—is not just healthy but essential for starting and sustaining any ambitious dream, especially when reality contradicts your aspirations. Vitale draws from his own experience of being broke, sleeping in a basement studio, and surrounding himself with successful artists to feed his delusion. He argues that seeking outside validation means you're already losing, and that audacious self-confidence is the "insanity" required to succeed.

## Actionable Lessons

1. Believe in yourself before anyone else does—you must be the first person who thinks you will achieve what you set out to do.
2. Stop seeking advice or praise from others; if you need outside validation, you are already losing to yourself.
3. Embrace delusion as a healthy tool to pursue your dream despite its contradiction with known reality and others' rational expectations.
4. Surround yourself with people who have already achieved what you want; their presence feeds your delusion and makes your dream feel tangible.
5. Use audacious self-confidence to put your work out into the world and keep striving to be heard.
6. Believe so deeply in your goals that you are simply waiting for the world to catch up to your already-achieved reality.
7. Don't ask for advice from people who haven't lived your experience—you know your potential better than they do.

## Key Quotes

- "LIVING OFF DELUSION IS HEALTHY."

- "You have to be the first person who thinks that you're going to achieve what you have set out to do."

- "That level of self-belief is the kind of insanity that is required to succeed."
- "Get Delusional."

## Best Practices

- Be the first and loudest believer in your own potential.
- Share your goals boldly to externalize your manifestation and hold yourself accountable.
- Seek environments that expose you to people who have already achieved your desired level of success.
- Replace outside validation with internal conviction and self-approval.
- Reframe setbacks and distance from your goal as signs you are getting closer, not farther away.

## Common Pitfalls

- Seeking advice or praise from others (a clear signal of self-doubt).
- Letting reality or others' rational expectations define your limits.
- Waiting for external validation before fully committing to your vision.
- Feeling discouraged by how far away success seems instead of feeling closer to it.
- Comparing your behind-the-scenes journey to others' highlight reels.
