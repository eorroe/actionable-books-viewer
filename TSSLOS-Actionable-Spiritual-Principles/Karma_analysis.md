# Analysis: The Law of Karma (or Cause and Effect)
## From *The Seven Spiritual Laws of Success* by Deepak Chopra

## 1. Actionable Lessons (Step-by-Step Instructions)

1. **Become consciously aware of your choices in every moment.**
   - Step back and witness the choices you are making as you make them.
   - This act of witnessing moves the process from the unconscious realm into the conscious realm.

2. **Apply the two-question framework before every choice:**
   - First, ask: "What are the consequences of this choice that I'm making?"
    - Second, ask: "Will this choice bring lasting fulfillment and genuine well-being — not merely immediate pleasure — to me and to all beings directly and indirectly affected by it?"
   - If the answer is yes, proceed. If no, do not make that choice.

3. **Cultivate spontaneous right action.**
    - There is one choice out of the vast range of possibilities available in each moment that creates fulfillment for you and others.
   - Making that choice leads to spontaneous right action— the right action at the right moment.

4. **Use your body's sensations as a decision-making mechanism.**
   - At the moment you consciously make a choice, pay attention to your body.
    - Bring your awareness to your body's physical sensations and inwardly ask: "If I make this choice, what happens?" — letting the body's intuitive signals surface as felt sensations rather than verbal answers.
    - If your body signals a felt sense of inner ease and alignment — the body's signal of a choice in harmony with the Law of Karma — that indicates the right choice.
    - If your body signals a felt sense of misalignment or unease — the body's signal of discomfort — the choice is not aligned.

5. **Consult your heart for guidance.**
   - Consciously put your attention in the heart and ask your heart what to do.
   - Wait for the response as a physical sensation.
    - The heart taps into the field of pure potentiality (the field of all possibilities), pure knowledge (direct, wordless knowing accessible through the heart's intelligence), and infinite organizing power (the self-ordering intelligence of consciousness itself).

6. **Apply the Law of Karma to create affluence and good things.**
   - Become consciously aware that your future is generated by the choices you make in every moment.
   - Do this regularly to make full use of the Law of Karma.

7. **Handle past karma through three approaches:**
    - **Pay your karmic debts:** Accept that karmic consequences unfold without exception — the universe's moral order functions with the reliability of a perfect accounting system.
    - **Transmute or transform your karma:** As you work with a karmic situation, ask yourself: "What can I learn from this experience? What pattern or lesson is this experience revealing — what understanding is emerging from this karmic encounter?"
   - **Seek guidance from the heart:** Use the body's sensation mechanism to determine the right response to past karmic situations.

## 3. Best Practices

- **Practice present-moment awareness.** The best way to prepare for the future is to be fully conscious in the present moment.
- **Use the two-question framework consistently.** Before every choice, ask about consequences and their impact on lasting fulfillment and well-being for all affected.
- **Witness your choices without judgment.** Simply observing your decision-making process moves it from unconscious to conscious.
- **Listen to your body's sensations.** Use the body's felt signals of inner ease and alignment (comfort) or misalignment and unease (discomfort) as reliable guides for choices in harmony with the Law of Karma.
- **Consult your heart before acting.** The heart has an intuitive intelligence capable of integrating more context than rational thought alone.
- **Make choices that benefit all.** Seek the one choice out of the vast range of possibilities that creates fulfillment for you and all beings affected by it.
- **Learn from karmic experiences.** When paying karmic debts, ask what lesson the experience offers.
- **Become a conscious choice-maker.** Regularly bring your choices into the level of conscious awareness.

## 4. Common Pitfalls

- **Making choices unconsciously.** Operating on autopilot and not recognizing that reactions to stimuli are still choices.
- **Living as bundles of conditioned reflexes.** Allowing Pavlovian-style conditioning to trigger predictable outcomes without awareness.
- **Ignoring consequences.** Making choices without considering their impact on yourself and all beings affected by them.
- **Choosing actions that bring distress.** Selecting paths that cause suffering for yourself or any beings affected by them.
- **Overlooking the heart's guidance.** Relying solely on rational thought while ignoring the body's sensation signals.
- **Failing to witness choices.** Not stepping back to observe decision-making, thus keeping choices in the unconscious realm.
- **Unconsciously paying karmic debts.** Allowing past karma to influence present life without conscious transformation.
- **Forgetting that reactions are choices.** Not realizing that even automatic responses to any stimulus — including praise, criticism, or neutral events — are still choices.
