# Analysis: The Law of Dharma (or Purpose in Life)
## From *The Seven Spiritual Laws of Success* by Deepak Chopra

---

## 1. Actionable Lessons (Step-by-Step Instructions)

### Understanding the Law

1. **Understand the three components of the Law of Dharma.**
   - First component: Discover your true Self, your spiritual nature beyond the ego.
   - Second component: Discover and express your unique talents.
   - Third component: Serve humanity by asking "How can I help?" instead of "What's in it for me?"

2. **Recognize that you are a spiritual being having a human experience.**
   - You are not a human being having occasional spiritual experiences.
   - You are a spiritual being that has taken manifestation in physical form.
   - Inside you is a god or goddess in embryo that wants to be born.

3. **Discover your unique talent.**
   - You have a talent that is unique in its expression; no one else on the planet has it.
   - There is one thing you can do, and one way of doing it, that is better than anyone else.
   - When expressing your unique talent, you lose track of time and enter timeless awareness.

4. **Combine unique talent with service to humanity.**
   - When you blend your unique talent with service to others, you experience ecstasy and exultation of spirit.
   - This combination creates unlimited wealth and abundance.
   - Asking "How can I help?" instead of "What's in it for me?" shifts you from ego to spirit.

### Daily Practices

5. **Nurture the god/goddess within.**
   - Lovingly nurture the divine essence deep within your soul.
   - Pay attention to the spirit that animates both your body and mind.
   - Awaken yourself to the deep stillness within your heart.
   - Carry the consciousness of timeless, eternal Being in the midst of time-bound experience.

6. **Make a list of your unique talents.**
   - List all your unique talents and abilities.
   - List all the things you love to do while expressing those talents.
   - When expressing your unique talents in service of humanity, you lose track of time and create abundance.

7. **Ask yourself daily service questions.**
   - Ask yourself: "How can I help?"
   - Ask yourself: "How can I help all those that I come into contact with?"
   - Answer these questions and put the answers into practice.
   - Use your unique talents to serve the needs of your fellow human beings.

8. **Test if you are in dharma.**
   - Ask: If money was no concern and you had all the time and money in the world, what would you do?
   - If you would still do what you currently do, you are in dharma.
   - Being in dharma means you have passion for what you do and are expressing your unique talents.

### Advanced Understanding

9. **Understand the natural sequence of the laws.**
   - Law of Pure Potentiality is activated by the Law of Giving.
   - Law of Giving activates the Law of Karma.
   - Good karma leads to the Law of Least Effort.
   - Effortless fulfillment of desires leads to the Law of Intention and Desire.
   - Intention and Desire lead to the Law of Detachment.
   - Detachment and understanding lead to the Law of Dharma.

10. **Observe how cells express the Seven Spiritual Laws.**
    - Every cell in your body expresses these laws perfectly.
    - Cells discover their source (higher self), serve fellow cells, and express unique talents.
    - The internal dialogue of every cell is: "How can I help?"
    - Heart cells help immune cells, immune cells help stomach and lung cells, brain cells help all others.

## 3. Best Practices

- ✅ Discover your true Self through spiritual practice and meditation
- ✅ Make a list of your unique talents and what you love to do
- ✅ Ask yourself daily: "How can I help?" and "How can I serve?"
- ✅ Shift your internal dialogue from "What's in it for me?" to "How can I help?"
- ✅ Test if you are in dharma by asking if you would still do your work if money were no concern
- ✅ Combine your unique talent with service to humanity
- ✅ Nurture the divine essence within you daily
- ✅ Carry the consciousness of timeless Being in your daily activities
- ✅ Recognize that every cell in your body models the Seven Spiritual Laws
- ✅ Trust that matching your talents to needs creates unlimited abundance

## 4. Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Focusing on grades, money, and status instead of purpose
  **Solution:** Shift focus to discovering your unique talents and how you can serve humanity

- **Problem:** Asking "What's in it for me?" instead of "How can I help?"
  **Solution:** Consciously shift your internal dialogue to service-oriented questions

- **Problem:** Not knowing your unique talent
  **Solution:** Make a list of things you love to do and what you do better than anyone else

- **Problem:** Being in the wrong career or path
  **Solution:** Test with the "money no concern" question; if you wouldn't still do it, you're not in dharma

- **Problem:** Separating spiritual practice from daily life
  **Solution:** Carry the consciousness of your spirit into all activities and interactions

- **Problem:** Thinking dharma is only about work/career
  **Solution:** Dharma applies to all areas of life; it is about expressing your unique self in service to all
