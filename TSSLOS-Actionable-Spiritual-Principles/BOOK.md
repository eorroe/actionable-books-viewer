# The Seven Spiritual Laws of Success

## Overview

"The Seven Spiritual Laws of Success" by Deepak Chopra presents seven universal spiritual principles that govern all creation and can be consciously applied to achieve success, fulfillment, and abundance in career, relationships, health, finances, and personal growth. These laws are the same principles nature uses to create a forest, a galaxy, a star, or a human body, and they are accessible to anyone willing to shift from object-referral to self-referral and cultivate pure consciousness. True success is defined not as material wealth alone but as the continued expansion of happiness, the progressive realization of worthy goals, and the unfolding of the divinity within, experienced with effortless ease. By incorporating these spiritual laws into daily consciousness, readers can create unlimited wealth, experience success in every endeavor, and live life as a miraculous expression of their true Self.

## When to Follow Book Teachings

- When seeking a spiritual framework for creating abundance, success, and fulfillment in career, relationships, health, finances, and personal growth
- When feeling caught in cycles of stress, struggle, or fear-based patterns such as the need for approval, control, or external power
- When you want to shift from equating success solely with material wealth to embracing a broader definition of success including health, energy, enthusiasm, relationships, creative freedom, emotional stability, and peace of mind
- When making important life decisions and need guidance rooted in inner wisdom rather than external validation
- When facing adversity, setbacks, or karmic situations and want to transform them into opportunities for growth and service
- When looking to discover your unique purpose (dharma) and express your deepest talents in service to humanity
- When you want to manifest desires without struggle, by aligning with the field of pure potentiality and the law of least effort
- When working with or teaching others about spiritual principles, consciousness, and the nature of creation

## Lessons From Book

### Lesson 1 (The Law of Pure Potentiality)

The Law of Pure Potentiality is the first and foundational spiritual law — it states that your essential nature is pure consciousness, the field of all possibilities, infinite creativity, pure joy, pure knowledge, infinite silence, perfect balance, invincibility, simplicity, and bliss. This field, called the field of pure potentiality, is the unmanifest source of all creation, and it is your own Self — there is no separation between you and this field of energy. By shifting from object-referral (basing your identity on external objects, approval, or control) to self-referral (using your own spirit as your internal reference point), you cultivate real power — a permanent power based on knowledge of the Self that magnetizes people, situations, and circumstances to support your desires, characterized by an absence of fear, no compulsion to control, and no struggle for approval or external power.

#### Examples

##### Example 1: The Self-Referral Mindset in Professional Life

A business leader who operates from self-referral makes decisions based on inner values and intuition rather than chasing titles, praise, or external validation. When feedback or criticism arises, self-referral keeps them centered, immune to fear-based reactions. This leader's decisions naturally attract opportunities and collaborators, because self-power is permanent and magnetizing — it draws the right people and circumstances without forcing outcomes.

##### Example 2: Daily Meditation and Silence as Access to Pure Potentiality

Setting aside time each day for silence and meditation (at least 30 minutes each morning and evening) allows you to withdraw from speech, television, radio, and reading, and experience the field of pure silence and pure awareness. In that field, you discover the field of infinite correlation and organizing power — the source from which all desires can be fulfilled. Even brief periods of non-judgment ("Today I shall judge nothing that occurs") create space between thoughts, allowing you to access the gap, the silent space where pure potentiality resides.

##### Example 3: Relationships as Mirrors of the Self

When you understand that all relationships reflect your relationship with yourself, conflicts with others become opportunities to return to self-referral. If a relationship triggers feelings of insecurity, judgment, or the need for approval, it is signaling a disconnection from your own Self. Only intimacy with the Self brings true healing from guilt, fear, and insecurity — relationships then become mirrors that help you return to your true essence.

#### Step 1: Recognize Your Essential Nature as Pure Consciousness

Understand that your true Self is pure potentiality — the field of all possibilities and infinite creativity. Realize that you are the eternal possibility, the immeasurable potential of all that was, is, and will be. This recognition is the foundation of all spiritual power and abundance.

#### Step 2: Shift from Object-Referral to Self-Referral

Make your internal reference point your own spirit, not external objects, situations, or people. Stop seeking approval from others and anticipating responses. Release the need to control outcomes, other people's behavior, and external circumstances, and the need for external power. When you operate from self-referral, you become immune to criticism and free from fear-based patterns.

#### Step 3: Cultivate Self-Power Instead of Ego-Based Power

Self-power is permanent and based on knowledge of the Self. Ego-based power is temporary and tied to titles, jobs, and money. Cultivate self-power by connecting to your inner Self through meditation and silence twice daily. Self-power magnetizes people, situations, and circumstances to support your desires.

#### Step 4: Practice Silence Daily

Set aside time each day to simply Be. Commit to silence for at least one hour each day, gradually extending to two hours or longer as comfortable. Withdraw from speech, television, radio, and reading during your daily practice periods. Experience extended silence (a full day, two consecutive days, or one full week) at least once per month. In silence, you access the field of pure potentiality.

#### Step 5: Meditate Twice Daily

Meditate at least thirty minutes in the morning and thirty minutes in the evening. Use meditation to experience the field of pure silence and pure awareness. In that field, discover the field of infinite correlation and organizing power — the source of all creation and manifestation.

#### Step 6: Practice Non-Judgment

Begin each day with the statement: "Today, I shall judge nothing that occurs." Remind yourself throughout the day not to judge. If needed, commit to non-judgment for shorter periods (one hour, two hours). Judgment creates turbulence in your internal dialogue and constricts energy flow, blocking access to pure potentiality.

#### Step 7: Spend Time in Nature

Regularly commune with nature to sense the harmonious interaction of all elements. Sit silently and watch a sunset, listen to the ocean, or smell a flower. Connection with nature's intelligence helps you access the field of pure potentiality and remember your own essential nature.

#### Step 8: Experience the Coexistence of Stillness and Movement

Carry stillness within you while engaging in dynamic activity. This balance enables you to unleash creativity in all directions. Wherever your attention goes, your stillness goes with you. This is the practice of accessing your true essence — getting in touch with the innermost being beyond the ego.

#### Best Practices

- ✅ Meditate at least 30 minutes in the morning and 30 minutes in the evening
- ✅ Practice silence daily, gradually extending the duration
- ✅ Begin each day with the non-judgment statement: "Today, I shall judge nothing that occurs"
- ✅ Spend time in nature at least three times per week to connect with nature's intelligence
- ✅ Shift your internal reference point from object-referral to self-referral
- ✅ Carry stillness within you during all activities
- ✅ Recognize that self-power is true power and is permanent
- ✅ Use relationships as mirrors of your relationship with yourself

#### Keep In Mind

- The field of pure potentiality is your own Self; there is no separation between you and this field of energy
- Consciousness in motion expresses itself as the objects of the universe in the eternal dance of life
- True success is the experience of the miraculous — the unfolding of divinity within and the perception of divinity wherever you go
- All of creation is the result of the unmanifest transforming itself into the manifest; everything comes from the unknown
- The physical universe is the Self curving back within Itself to experience Itself as spirit, mind, and physical matter
- The Seven Spiritual Laws are the same principles nature uses to create everything — a forest, a galaxy, a star, or a human body

#### Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Living in object-referral, constantly seeking approval from others
  **Solution:** Shift your internal reference point to your own spirit through self-awareness and meditation

  **Solution:** Practice silence, meditation, and non-judgment to quiet the mind and create space between thoughts

  **Solution:** Cultivate self-power through knowledge of your true Self; recognize ego-based power is temporary and fear-based

  **Solution:** Start with short periods and gradually extend; expect initial turbulence as the mind adjusts

- **Problem:** Forgetting to commune with nature at least three times per week
  **Solution:** Schedule daily nature time and make it a non-negotiable practice to access nature's intelligence

  **Solution:** Begin each day with the non-judgment statement and remind yourself throughout the day to refrain from evaluation

### Lesson 2 (The Law of Giving)

The Law of Giving, also known as the Law of Dynamic Exchange, states that the universe operates through giving and receiving as different aspects of the same energy flow. Your body, mind, and energy are in constant exchange with the universe, and stopping this energy circulation is like stopping blood flow — it causes stagnation. Giving engenders receiving, and receiving engenders giving; what goes out must come back. The intention behind giving is more important than the act itself — give with the intention to create happiness for both giver and receiver, and the return is directly proportional to unconditional giving from the heart. Affluence means "to flow in abundance," and money is a symbol of life energy exchanged through service to the universe; hoarding money stops its circulation back into your life, just as a river must keep flowing to stay alive and vital.

#### Examples

##### Example 1: Creating Joy Through Reciprocal Giving in Relationships

If you want joy, give joy to others. If you want love, give love. If you want attention and appreciation, give attention and appreciation. If you want material affluence, help others become materially affluent. The easiest way to get what you want is to help others get what they want. Every time you meet someone, make a decision to give them something — a compliment, a prayer, a flower, or your full attention.

##### Example 2: Silent Giving and Blessing as Powerful Non-Material Exchange

When you meet someone, silently send them a blessing, wishing them happiness, joy, and laughter. Never visit anyone without bringing them something — even if it is just a flower or a compliment. The most powerful forms of giving are non-material: caring, attention, affection, appreciation, and love. Even the thought of giving or a simple prayer has the power to affect others.

##### Example 3: Understanding Money as Circulating Life Energy

Money, from the root "currere" meaning "to run" or "to flow," is a symbol of life energy exchanged through service to the universe. Like a river, money must keep flowing to stay alive and vital. Hoarding stops circulation and causes stagnation. Generosity — in the form of charitable giving, fair service, or supporting others — ensures energy keeps flowing, and abundance returns to you multiplied.

#### Step 1: Understand the Principle of Dynamic Exchange

Recognize that the universe operates through giving and receiving as different aspects of energy flow. Your body, mind, and energy are in constant exchange with the universe. Stopping energy circulation is like stopping blood flow — it causes stagnation. This principle underlies every aspect of life, from cellular function to human relationships.

#### Step 2: Recognize That Giving Engenders Receiving

Every relationship is one of give and take. Giving engenders receiving, and receiving engenders giving. What goes out must come back; receiving is the same as giving. When you receive a gift graciously, you allow the other person to give, completing the cycle of exchange.

#### Step 3: Give Unconditionally and Joyfully

The intention behind giving is more important than the act itself. Give with the intention to create happiness for both giver and receiver. The return is directly proportional to unconditional giving from the heart. If giving makes you feel you have lost something, the gift is not truly given — shift your intention by refocusing on the joy and benefit the receiver will experience from your gift.

#### Step 4: Practice the Principle of Reciprocity

If you want joy, give joy to others. If you want love, give love. If you want attention and appreciation, give attention and appreciation. If you want material affluence, help others become materially affluent. The easiest way to get what you want is to help others get what they want.

#### Step 5: Make a Decision to Give Wherever You Go

Decide that any time you come into contact with anyone, you will give them something. Gifts do not have to be material; they can be a flower, a compliment, or a prayer. The most powerful forms of giving are non-material: caring, attention, affection, appreciation, and love.

#### Step 6: Practice Silent Giving and Blessing

When you meet someone, silently send them a blessing, wishing them happiness, joy, and laughter. Never visit anyone without bringing them something (even if it is just a flower or compliment). Even the thought of giving or a simple prayer has the power to affect others.

#### Step 7: Understand the Nature of Affluence

Affluence means "to flow in abundance" from the root "affluere" meaning "to flow to." Money is a symbol of life energy exchanged through service to the universe. Currency comes from "currere" meaning "to run" or "to flow." Hoarding money stops its circulation back into your life. Like a river, money must keep flowing to stay alive and vital.

#### Step 8: Recognize That You Are Already Affluent

Your true nature is one of pure potentiality and infinite possibilities. Nature supports every need and desire; you lack nothing. You are naturally affluent because your essential nature is pure potentiality. Affluence is the abundant flow of financial income, health, loving relationships, creative opportunities, inner peace, and personal fulfillment to you — it is your birthright.

- ✅ Give with the intention to create happiness for both giver and receiver
- ✅ Make a decision to give something to everyone you meet
- ✅ Practice silent giving through blessings and prayers
- ✅ Never visit anyone without bringing a gift (even if symbolic)
- ✅ Help others get what they want to get what you want
- ✅ Give non-material gifts (attention, affection, appreciation, love)
- ✅ Keep energy circulating by giving and receiving freely
- ✅ Recognize that you are already affluent by nature

#### Keep In Mind

- Giving and receiving are different aspects of the same energy flow in the universe
- The more you give, the more you will receive
- Anything of value in life only multiplies when it is given
- Money must keep flowing like a river; otherwise it stagnates
- Your true nature is one of affluence and abundance
- Silent giving (blessings, prayers) is extremely powerful
- Even a single flower or compliment can be a meaningful gift
- The universe operates through dynamic exchange at all levels

  **Solution:** Shift your intention to create joy for both giver and receiver; give unconditionally from the heart

### Lesson 3 (The Law of Karma)

The Law of Karma (or Cause and Effect) is the third spiritual law of success — it states that every action generates a force of energy that returns to us in like kind; cause and effect are simultaneous. Karma means both action and the consequence of that action, implying conscious choice-making. You are not a bundle of conditioned reflexes making predictable responses; you are an infinite choice-maker in every moment, with access to an infinity of choices each second. The field of pure potentiality gives you access to infinite possibilities, and the one choice out of infinity that creates happiness for you and others is the right choice — this is spontaneous right action, the right action at the right moment. Your future is generated by the choices you make in every moment, and by becoming consciously aware of these choices, you take control of your karma and your destiny.

#### Examples

##### Example 1: The Two-Question Framework for Conscious Choice-Making

Before every choice, apply the two-question framework: First, ask "What are the consequences of this choice that I'm making?" Second, ask "Will this choice bring happiness to me and to those around me?" If the answer is yes, proceed. If no, do not make that choice. This simple framework moves your decision-making from the unconscious realm into the conscious realm, creating evolutionary karma that generates happiness and success.

##### Example 2: Using the Body's Sensation Mechanism as a Decision-Making Tool

At the moment you consciously make a choice, pay attention to your body. Ask your body: "If I make this choice, what happens?" If your body sends a message of comfort, that is the right choice. If your body sends a message of discomfort, it is not the appropriate choice. The heart taps into the field of pure potentiality, pure knowledge, and infinite organizing power — it has computing ability far more accurate than rational thought.

##### Example 3: Handling Past Karma Through Transmutation

When facing past karmic debts, accept that no debt in the universe ever goes unpaid — there is a perfect accounting system. As you pay your karmic debt, ask yourself: "What can I learn from this experience? Why is this happening and what is the message that the universe is sending me?" This transforms a karmic debt into an opportunity for growth. Use the body's sensation mechanism to determine the right response to past karmic situations.

#### Step 1: Become Consciously Aware of Your Choices in Every Moment

Step back and witness the choices you are making as you make them by pausing for three seconds before acting. This act of witnessing moves the process from the unconscious realm into the conscious realm. The best way to prepare for the future is to be fully conscious in the present moment.

#### Step 2: Apply the Two-Question Framework Before Every Choice

Before making any choice, ask: "What are the consequences of this choice that I'm making?" Then ask: "Will this choice bring happiness to me and to those around me?" If the answer is yes, proceed. If no, do not make that choice. Apply this framework consistently to all decisions, large and small.

#### Step 3: Cultivate Spontaneous Right Action

There is only one choice out of the infinity available each second that creates happiness for you and others. Making that choice leads to spontaneous right action — the right action at the right moment. With consistent practice applying the two-question framework to every decision for at least 21 days, you will begin to make this choice automatically and effortlessly, nourishing yourself and everyone else influenced by that action.

#### Step 4: Use Your Body's Sensations as a Decision-Making Mechanism

At the moment you consciously make a choice, pay attention to your body by noticing your breath, muscle tension, and gut feelings. Ask your body: "If I make this choice, what happens?" If your body sends a message of comfort — such as relaxed breathing, open posture, or a sense of lightness — that is the right choice. If your body sends a message of discomfort — such as tightness, tension, or a sinking feeling — it is not the appropriate choice. The response may be faint, but it is present and detectable with practice.

#### Step 5: Consult Your Heart for Guidance

Consciously put your attention in the heart and ask your heart what to do. Wait for the response as a physical sensation. The heart taps into the field of pure potentiality, pure knowledge, and infinite organizing power. The heart is not mushy or sentimental — it is intuitive, holistic, contextual, and relational with a computing ability more precise than rational thought.

#### Step 6: Apply the Law of Karma to Create Affluence and Good Things

Become consciously aware that your future is generated by the choices you make in every moment. Do this during every decision throughout each day to make full use of the Law of Karma. Consciously aware choice-making generates the flow of all good things — affluence, health, love, and success.

#### Step 7: Handle Past Karma Through Three Approaches

First, pay your karmic debts: accept that no debt in the universe ever goes unpaid; there is a perfect accounting system. Second, transmute or transform your karma: as you pay your karmic debt, ask yourself "What can I learn from this experience? Why is this happening and what is the message that the universe is sending me?" Third, seek guidance from the heart: use the body's sensation mechanism to determine the right response to past karmic situations.

- ✅ Listen to your body's sensations — use relaxed breathing and open posture as signals for right choices, and tension or tightness as signals for wrong choices
- ✅ Consult your heart before acting by placing your hand on your chest and noticing physical sensations
- ✅ Make choices that benefit all parties involved — seek the one choice out of infinity that creates happiness for you and those around you

- The heart is not mushy or sentimental — it is intuitive, holistic, contextual, and relational with a computing ability more precise than rational thought

### Lesson 4 (The Law of Least Effort)

The Law of Least Effort is the fourth spiritual law of success — it states that nature's intelligence functions with effortless ease, carefreeness, harmony, and love. This law is rooted in the Vedic Science principle of "economy of effort" — minimize unnecessary struggle and accomplish more with aligned action. When you act in harmony with nature rather than against it, you align with the principle of least action and no resistance, allowing outcomes to unfold naturally just as grass grows, fish swim, and flowers bloom without forced effort. The three components of aligned action are: (1) being in the present moment, (2) accepting people and circumstances as they are, and (3) acting without attachment to the outcome. Love is the binding energy of nature — when actions are love-motivated, energy multiplies rather than depletes, making success and affluence effortless.

#### Examples

##### Example 1: Aligning Action with Nature's Flow Instead of Forcing Outcomes

A writer who faces creative block stops forcing the work and instead sits in stillness for 10 to 15 minutes, allowing inspiration to emerge naturally. Like grass that grows without effort, creative expression flows when the writer's internal reference point shifts from ego (needing to produce) to spirit (being open to what wants to emerge). Unexpected opportunities and ideas — manifestations of nature's intelligence operating without resistance — become common occurrences rather than rare exceptions.

##### Example 2: Love as the Binding Energy That Multiplies Energy

An entrepreneur motivated by genuine love for their product and customers finds that energy flows effortlessly — people are drawn to their work, opportunities emerge naturally, and the business grows without the typical struggle. Because the action is motivated by love rather than ego-based power, control, or personal gain, energy multiplies rather than depletes. This is the principle of economy of effort: aligned action accomplishes more with less.

##### Example 3: Transmuting Adversity Into Opportunity Aligned With Dharma

When facing a setback — such as losing a job — instead of resisting the situation, ask: "What can I learn from this experience?" and "How can I make this experience useful to my fellow human beings?" The adversity becomes a karmic episode that, when tied to your dharma (purpose), transmutes into a new, positive karmic expression. The universe uses every event as an opportunity for evolutionary growth when met with restful alertness.

#### Step 1: Witness Your Choices in Each Moment

Observe the decisions you make throughout the day without judgment by pausing for three seconds before each action to notice your motivation. Recognize that full consciousness in the present is the best preparation for the future. Simply witnessing your choices moves the process from the unconscious realm into the conscious realm.

#### Step 2: Apply the Two Self-Check Questions Before Every Choice

Before acting, ask: "What are the consequences of this choice that I'm making?" Then ask: "Will this choice bring fulfillment and happiness to me and to those who are affected by this choice?" Pause and use heart guidance before proceeding.

#### Step 3: Consult Your Heart for Guidance

Tune into the feeling of comfort or discomfort that arises with each choice. If the choice feels comfortable, proceed with full commitment and without self-sabotaging hesitation. If the choice feels uncomfortable, pause and visualize the consequences with inner vision before acting. The heart is intuitive, holistic, contextual, and relational — its computing ability is more precise than rational thought.

#### Step 4: Align Your Actions with Nature's Principle of Least Effort

Stop forcing specific outcomes; allow your creative ideas and intentions to unfold naturally through inspired action. Let your authentic skills, talents, and values guide your work, just as grass grows, fish swim, and flowers bloom without forced effort. Act in harmony with your own nature and the nature of the situation rather than against them, embracing the principle of least action and no resistance.

#### Step 5: Ensure Your Actions Are Motivated by Love

Before acting, check whether your primary motivation is the benefit and well-being of others rather than personal gain, ego gratification, or the need to prove yourself. Love multiplies energy rather than depleting it, making success and affluence effortless. Love is the binding energy of nature. When actions are love-motivated, energy accumulates and can be channeled into unlimited wealth and creation.

#### Step 6: Shift Your Internal Reference Point from Ego to Spirit

Release the need for power, control, approval, and personal gain. When your reference point is your spirit, you become immune to criticism and fearless in the face of challenge. Spirit as your internal reference point (self-referral) fosters immunity to criticism, fearlessness, and creative use of energy.

#### Step 7: Free the Energy Consumed by Ego

Recognize how much energy is spent upholding your sense of importance (personal importance). Redirect that freed energy toward creative and evolutionary pursuits. Criticism, approval-seeking, power struggles, and control efforts are the most draining activities you can engage in.

#### Step 8: Practice Meditation to Transcend Karma

Enter the gap (the Self, the Spirit) twice daily through meditation. Each meditation session washes away karmic seeds, just as washing a cloth removes stains. Transcending karma means becoming independent of karmic cycles by accessing the field of pure potentiality at least twice daily.

#### Step 9: Transmute Adversity Into Opportunity

When facing a setback, ask: "What can I learn from this experience?" Then ask: "How can I make this experience useful to my fellow human beings?" Tie the seed of opportunity to your dharma (purpose in life). Every problem in your life is a seed of an opportunity for personal growth, increased wisdom, or service to others.

- ✅ Pause before acting and use the two-question framework with heart guidance
