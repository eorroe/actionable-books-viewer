6. **Pronouns with unclear antecedents** (e.g., "they" in line 215)

8. **Speculative attributions of internal states** (e.g., "unconsciously hold you back," "intimidated by your potential success," "wants to keep you dependent")
9. **Undefined terms and concepts** (e.g., "purpose," "WHY," "quality of decisions," "focused work")
