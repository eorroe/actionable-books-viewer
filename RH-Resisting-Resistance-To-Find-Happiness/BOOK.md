# Resisting Happiness

## Overview

Resisting Happiness is a transformative guide that reveals how resistance—the internal force of laziness, fear, doubt, and procrastination that keeps us from doing what we know is good for us and for others—stands between you and the deep, lasting joy God intends for you. Through personal stories, spiritual insights, and practical action steps, the book teaches readers to recognize resistance in its many forms, name it, and systematically break through it to become the best version of themselves. It emphasizes that happiness is a choice rooted in placing God at the center of life, developing strong habits, serving others, and daily choosing to overcome resistance by consistently choosing positive actions despite fear or doubt.

## When to Follow Book Teachings

- When you feel stuck in patterns of procrastination, laziness, or self-sabotage
- When you know what would make you happy but consistently choose not to do it
- When you are searching for deeper meaning and purpose in daily life
- When you want to build stronger spiritual habits and deepen your genuine connection with God
- When you struggle with fear, doubt, or the negative opinions of critics
- When you want to stop resisting the person God calls you to become

## Lessons From Book

### Lesson 1: Name Resistance — The Internal Force That Blocks Your Joy

#### Examples

##### Example 1: The Morning Alarm

The alarm goes off and you hit snooze. You just lost the first battle of the day. The internal force of laziness, fear, doubt, and procrastination that stands between you and happiness has broken your will before you've even gotten out of bed. This ordinary moment reveals how resistance works—it is simple, subtle, and paralyzing if we are not mindful of it.

##### Example 2: The Unfinished Manuscript

An accomplished author sits down to write but instead checks email or thinks about snacks. Even someone who has written twenty books must slay resistance to get started. This is why more than 95 percent of people who start writing a book never finish it. The internal force of laziness, fear, doubt, and procrastination that stands between you and happiness gets the better of them.

#### Step 1: Track the Internal Force for One Week

Write down every time you encounter resistance for a week. Simply naming resistance causes it to lose much of its power over you. Once you can identify it in the moment, you can act to break through it.

#### Step 2: Identify Your Happiness Destroyers

Make a list of what destroys your happiness: worry, comparison, toxic relationships, self-doubt, debt, gossip, laziness, fear, excuses, negative thinking, ingratitude, and jealousy. You cannot change what you do not acknowledge.

#### Step 3: Name Your Specific Struggle

We are all struggling with something. Identify your something. Name it. Own it. Whether it is procrastination, addiction, obsession, or fear, naming it is the first step to breaking its hold on you.

#### Best Practices

- ✅ Keep a resistance journal to track behavioral patterns
- ✅ Review your list weekly to identify forms of resistance that appear repeatedly
- ✅ Share your struggle with a trusted friend or trained spiritual coach
- ✅ Celebrate small, specific victories when you break through resistance

#### Keep In Mind

- The internal force of laziness, fear, doubt, and procrastination that stands between you and happiness appears in many forms: laziness, procrastination, fear, doubt, instant gratification, self-loathing, indecision, escapism, pride, self-deception, friction, tension, self-sabotage, and other subtle forms
- You may break through a specific instance of resistance, but the force itself returns. It is a daily battle that must be fought anew each morning
- A difficult battle to win is one you do not even realize you are fighting

#### Security & Safety Notes

- Do not let resistance to being vulnerable prevent you from seeking help from a trusted person or professional. Isolation reinforces resistance by removing accountability.
- Be careful not to use resistance itself as an excuse for failing to take positive action. Name it, then act.

#### Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Thinking you have permanently eliminated resistance from your life
  **Solution:** Accept that resistance is a daily battle. Wake up each morning ready to choose to act against it again.
- **Problem:** Naming resistance but then doing nothing about it
  **Solution:** Naming is only the first step. Follow it immediately with action.
- **Problem:** Judging yourself harshly for succumbing to resistance
  **Solution:** Be gentle with yourself. Every moment is a chance to start anew with one small positive choice.

### Lesson 2: Place God at the Center of Your Life

#### Examples

##### Example 1: The God-Size Hole

Nothing on earth can satisfy your desire for happiness. You have a deep longing for God that cannot be filled with material possessions, money, status, power, genuine connections, experiences, or achievements. Only God can fill the hole. When you place anything else at the center of your life, you set yourself up for a persistent sense of dissatisfaction that lasts for weeks.

##### Example 2: The Chocolate Eggs

A six-year-old says, "I like Jesus, but I am more interested in the chocolate eggs and the chocolate bunnies." This reveals how often we are more interested in something other than Jesus, something other than the happiness God wants to freely give you.

#### Step 1: Take a Happiness Inventory

Give yourself a happiness score between one and ten over the past three months. Do not base it on how you feel within the next 24 hours or over the past week. Three months gives a better look at your overall happiness.

#### Step 2: Place God at the Center of Your Next Decision

Choose not what you want or what is most advantageous to you, but what you honestly feel God wants you to do. This simple shift in perspective transforms decision-making.

#### Step 3: Establish Daily Prayer

Ask God for his advice. Take ten minutes sometime within the next 24 hours to sit with him in silence. Present to him the biggest question you are struggling with at this time in your life, and ask him, "God, what do you think I should do?"

#### Best Practices

- ✅ Start each day with a few minutes of prayer and reflection before checking email or phone
- ✅ Make decisions by asking "What would Jesus do?" before "What do I want?"
- ✅ Read Scripture daily to keep God's perspective central
- ✅ Practice the Prayer Process: Gratitude, Awareness, Significant Moments, Peace, Freedom, Others

#### Keep In Mind

- The desire for God is a deep inner sense. It cannot be erased.
- You are created by God and for God.
- God never ceases to draw you toward him through a few minutes of prayer, community, and Scripture.
- You will only find the truth and joy you are seeking in God.
- The whole meaning and purpose of your existence is wrapped up in God. Separated from him, you and your life feel empty or without direction.

#### Security & Safety Notes

- Do not let resistance to praying keep you from it. The internal force of laziness, fear, doubt, and procrastination that stands between you and happiness knows that if it can keep you from praying regularly, it will win additional challenges in the hours and decisions that follow.
- Be honest with God in a few minutes of prayer. He already knows your thoughts and feelings.

#### Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Treating a few minutes of prayer as one more item on a to-do list
  **Solution:** Approach a few minutes of prayer as a genuine conversation, not a routine recitation. Speak with God as you would with a friend.
- **Problem:** Believing you do not have time for a few minutes of prayer
  **Solution:** Start with ten minutes. If you are too busy for ten minutes with God, you are too busy.
- **Problem:** Expecting instant answers from a few minutes of prayer
  **Solution:** Listen for guidance over days, weeks, or months. God often speaks in gentle promptings, not dramatic displays.

### Lesson 3: Understand That We Resist God by Resisting Happiness

#### Examples

##### Example 1: The Insanity of Resistance

The definition of resist is "to withstand, strive against, or oppose." To oppose God is a fool's errand. This stubborn resistance can appear in anyone who would resist God, and yet people can fall into this pattern from time to time. When we resist choosing the joy God offers, we are really resisting God because God is the source of true joy.

##### Example 2: The Coach and the Participant

A coach cannot take credit for a participant's successes or blame for the participant's setbacks. The participant's successes and setbacks are their own. God wants you to be happy even more than you want to be happy yourself, but he will not force you. You must choose to cooperate with positive aspects of your life that God calls you to.

#### Step 1: Recognize When You Want Something for Others More Than They Want It for Themselves

This is a subtle form of resistance. You may be trying to push someone toward a change or choice they are not yet open to. Learn to recognize this pattern and respect others' freedom to choose.

#### Step 2: Reflect on the Paradox

You know the things that will make you happy, but you do not always do them. You know how to unleash happiness in your life, but you resist. Why? Because resistance is easier than the consistent choices required for lasting joy.

#### Step 3: Accept That the Internal Force of Laziness, Fear, Doubt, and Procrastination That Stands Between You and Happiness Is Your Daily Enemy

The internal force of laziness, fear, doubt, and procrastination that stands between you and happiness will not quietly go away and leave you alone. You have to consciously choose to act against it, and you have to slay it anew each morning. This is not pessimism; it is practical clarity that empowers you to fight.

#### Best Practices

- ✅ Start each day acknowledging that resistance will be present
- ✅ Do not be surprised when resistance shows up; be prepared
- ✅ Remember that God wants your happiness more than you do
- ✅ Choose cooperation with God rather than resistance to him

#### Keep In Mind

- We resist many forms of resistance for many reasons, but this pattern often appears: we resist choosing the joy God offers.
- At the same time we have a deep, persistent longing for meaning and joy. This paradox is at the heart of the human condition.
- No one is exempt from facing resistance at some point in life. This applies to people in all roles, statuses, and stages of life.

#### Security & Safety Notes

- Do not use the excuse "I did not know" when you resist what you recognize as good for you and for others. Naming resistance removes this excuse.
- Be honest about your resistance. Denial feeds it.

#### Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Thinking resistance only affects "other people"
  **Solution:** Even experienced writers battle resistance daily. If it affects experienced writers, it likely appears in your life too.
- **Problem:** Giving up after a defeat
  **Solution:** You may break through a specific instance of resistance, but the force itself returns. Get back up and choose to act against it again tomorrow.
- **Problem:** Romanticizing resistance as part of the creative process
  **Solution:** The internal force of laziness, fear, doubt, and procrastination that stands between you and happiness is not the spark of inspiration. The internal force of laziness, fear, doubt, and procrastination that stands between you and happiness is the force that kills the spark before the spark can catch. Do not confuse the two.

### Lesson 4: Build Strong Habits to Break Through the Internal Force of Laziness, Fear, Doubt, and Procrastination That Stands Between You and Happiness

#### Examples

##### Example 1: The Path of Least Resistance

Choosing the easiest option often creates negative routines, thought patterns, and rhythms. Do you procrastinate by watching television? Do you try to spend money hoping it will bring contentment? Do you eat when you are not hungry to manage difficult emotions? These are the negative patterns that resistance reinforces.

##### Example 2: The Morning Routine

A morning walk, a few minutes of prayer, and jumping straight into work are a highly effective combination for significantly increasing your chances of having a meaningful day. When you establish strong positive habits, they easily defeat resistance.

#### Step 1: Audit Your Current Habits

Write down your three helpful habits. Now write down three habits that you want to change. Be completely honest. The internal force of laziness, fear, doubt, and procrastination that stands between you and happiness loves the negative patterns in your life.

#### Step 2: Develop One New Positive Habit

If you do not like what you find when you take an inventory of your life, start developing a positive new habit within the next 24 hours. Good habits will help you break through resistance. Good habits easily defeat it.

#### Step 3: Offer Each Hour to God

Set an alarm to go off each hour. Take a deep breath and lift the coming hour up to God. Ten seconds at the beginning of each hour will change your life. Every activity can be offered to God in a few minutes of prayer.

#### Best Practices

- ✅ Schedule your habits. If you do not schedule them, it probably will not happen.
- ✅ Start small. One new habit at a time.
- ✅ Track your progress daily.
- ✅ Find an accountability partner.

#### Keep In Mind

- Our lives change when our habits change.
- The internal force of laziness, fear, doubt, and procrastination that stands between you and happiness opposes what is good for you, and opposes strong, positive habits in your life.
- Good habits—those that benefit you and others—and bad habits both have an enormous impact on our lives.
- God can be found in ordinary activities. Every hour of productive work, every ordinary daily activity, can be offered to God in a few minutes of prayer.

#### Security & Safety Notes

- Do not let resistance convince you that you are too busy to develop habits that are good for you and that include praying for others. Start with at least ten seconds per hour.
- Ideally, replace one habit at a time with another habit. Replace, do not merely remove.

#### Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Trying to change all your habits at once
  **Solution:** Focus on ideally one habit at a time. Small, incremental, consistent changes beat complete life overhauls.
- **Problem:** Skipping one day and abandoning the habit completely
  **Solution:** One missed day is not a setback. Get back on track the following calendar day.
- **Problem:** Waiting for motivation
  **Solution:** The feeling of motivation follows action, though there are exceptions. Start even if you do not feel motivated.

### Lesson 5: Practice Gratitude and Never Get Discouraged

#### Examples

##### Example 1: The Blessing Count

We have relationships, health, and opportunities that bring meaning, and losing sight of this abundance is easy. When you scan the world on any given day, there is still significant suffering such as illness, loss, or injustice visible in the world. But focusing on your relationships, health, and shelter rather than your perceived shortcomings or unmet desires anchors you to the current hour.

##### Example 2: The Finished Book

Fifty-seven percent of books that are purchased are not read to completion. The internal force of laziness, fear, doubt, and procrastination that stands between you and happiness is the reason. You have beaten resistance by reading this far. Happiness is a choice. The internal force of laziness, fear, doubt, and procrastination almost always blocks your progress.

#### Step 1: Count Your Blessings

Make a list of ten people, experiences, and specific things you appreciate. Do this daily. Practicing gratitude strengthens positive thinking patterns to see what is good for you and for others that is already present in your daily life.

#### Step 2: Remember That Every Moment Is a New Chance

If you ever feel discouraged or defeated, remember that each new moment offers a new chance to choose a positive response: each new moment is a chance to start anew with one small positive choice, a chance to turn your situation around with one intentional action. The internal force of laziness, fear, doubt, and procrastination that stands between you and happiness does not get the final say over your life's direction. You choose the outcome.

#### Step 3: Celebrate Breaking Through Resistance

If and when you break through resistance, celebrate breaking through, and then press on by setting one new goal to live the life God imagined for you before the beginning of time. Do not just endure resistance; grow through it.

#### Best Practices

- ✅ Keep a gratitude journal with ten items, a number chosen to encourage thoughtful reflection on each item without feeling like a burdensome chore
- ✅ Share your gratitude with others; express appreciation openly
- ✅ When you feel unmotivated or disheartened, recall past successes such as completing a difficult task despite resistance
- ✅ Remember that you are not alone in the ongoing effort to overcome resistance by consistently choosing positive actions despite fear or doubt

#### Keep In Mind

- Gratitude anchors you to the current hour, reminding you that what matters most is your relationship with God and others, and what matters least is material possessions.
- The internal force of laziness, fear, doubt, and procrastination that stands between you and happiness does not go away, but if you develop the habit of overcoming it, this awareness becomes a reliable tool for overcoming daily challenges.
- Many great stories have a villain and hero as narrative roles representing internal struggle. The internal force of laziness, fear, doubt, and procrastination that stands between you and happiness is the villain, and you are the hero.

#### Security & Safety Notes

- Do not let gratitude become satisfaction without continued growth. Be grateful, but also continue learning and improving.
- Do not compare your gratitude practice to others. Your journey is your own.

#### Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Only being grateful when your plans succeed and you feel content
  **Solution:** Practice gratitude especially when you face setbacks or disappointment. This is when it is most powerful.
- **Problem:** Letting one moment of failure or disappointment ruin your outlook on life
  **Solution:** Return to the gratitude list you keep in your journal. One bad moment does not define your life.
- **Problem:** Thinking you have few things you are aware of being grateful for
  **Solution:** Start with fundamental blessings such as health (including breath), shelter, food, and the ability to read. Build from there by adding one relationship or experience you appreciate each day.
