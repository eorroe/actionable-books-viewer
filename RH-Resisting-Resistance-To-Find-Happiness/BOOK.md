# Resisting Happiness

## Overview

Resisting Happiness is a transformative guide that reveals how resistance—the internal force that keeps us from doing what we know is good—stands between us and the happiness God intends for us. Through personal stories, spiritual insights, and practical action steps, the book teaches readers to recognize resistance in its many forms, name it, and systematically break through it to become the-best-version-of-themselves. It emphasizes that happiness is a choice rooted in placing God at the center of life, developing strong habits, serving others, and daily choosing to slay the dragon of resistance.

## When to Follow Book Teachings

- When you feel stuck in patterns of procrastination, laziness, or self-sabotage
- When you know what would make you happy but consistently choose not to do it
- When you are searching for deeper meaning and purpose in daily life
- When you want to build stronger spiritual habits and deepen your relationship with God
- When you struggle with fear, doubt, or the opinions of critics
- When you want to stop resisting the person God created you to be

## Lessons From Book

### Lesson 1: Name Resistance to Reduce Its Power

#### Examples

##### Example 1: The Morning Alarm

The alarm goes off and you hit snooze. You just lost the first battle of the day. Resistance has broken your will before you've even gotten out of bed. This ordinary moment reveals how resistance works—it is simple, subtle, and paralyzing if we are not mindful of it.

##### Example 2: The Unfinished Manuscript

An accomplished author sits down to write but instead checks email or thinks about snacks. Even someone who has written twenty books must slay resistance to get started. This is why more than 95 percent of people who start writing a book never finish it. Resistance gets the better of them.

#### Step 1: Track Resistance for One Week

Write down every time you encounter resistance for a week. Simply naming resistance causes it to lose much of its power over us. Once you can identify it in the moment, you can act to break through it.

#### Step 2: Identify Your Happiness Destroyers

Make a list of what destroys your happiness: worry, comparison, bad relationships, self-doubt, debt, gossip, laziness, fear, excuses, negative thinking, ingratitude, and jealousy. You cannot change what you do not acknowledge.

#### Step 3: Name Your Specific Struggle

We are all struggling with something. Identify your something. Name it. Own it. Whether it is procrastination, addiction, obsession, or fear, naming it is the first step to breaking its hold on you.

#### Best Practices

- ✅ Keep a resistance journal to track patterns
- ✅ Review your list weekly to identify recurring forms of resistance
- ✅ Share your struggle with a trusted friend or spiritual coach
- ✅ Celebrate small victories when you break through resistance

#### Keep In Mind

- Resistance wears a thousand masks: laziness, procrastination, fear, doubt, instant gratification, self-loathing, indecision, escapism, pride, self-deception, friction, tension, and self-sabotage
- You never defeat resistance once and for all. It is a daily battle that must be fought anew each morning
- The hardest war to win is one you do not even realize you are fighting

#### Security & Safety Notes

- Do not let resistance to vulnerability prevent you from seeking help. Isolation feeds resistance.
- Be careful not to use the concept of resistance as an excuse for inaction. Name it, then act.

#### Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Thinking you have defeated resistance permanently
  **Solution:** Accept that resistance is a daily battle. Wake up each morning ready to fight it again.
- **Problem:** Naming resistance but then doing nothing about it
  **Solution:** Naming is only the first step. Follow it immediately with action.
- **Problem:** Judging yourself harshly for falling to resistance
  **Solution:** Be gentle with yourself. Every moment is a chance to start anew.

### Lesson 2: Place God at the Center of Your Life

#### Examples

##### Example 1: The God-Size Hole

Nothing on earth can satisfy your desire for happiness. You have a God-size hole that cannot be filled with things, money, status, power, sex, drugs, alcohol, other people, experiences, or accomplishments. Only God can fill the hole. When you place anything else at the center of your life, you set yourself up for gnawing dissatisfaction.

##### Example 2: The Chocolate Eggs

A six-year-old says, "I like Jesus, but I am more interested in the chocolate eggs and the chocolate bunnies." This reveals how often we are more interested in something other than Jesus, something other than the happiness God wants to freely give us.

#### Step 1: Take a Happiness Inventory

Give yourself a happiness score between one and ten over the past three months. Do not base it on how you feel today or over the past week. Three months gives a better look at your overall happiness.

#### Step 2: Place God at the Center of Your Next Decision

Choose not what you want or what is most advantageous to you, but what you honestly feel God wants you to do. This simple shift in perspective transforms decision-making.

#### Step 3: Establish Daily Prayer

Ask God for his advice. Take ten minutes sometime today to sit with him in silence. Present to him the biggest question you are struggling with at this time in your life, and ask him, "God, what do you think I should do?"

#### Best Practices

- ✅ Start each day with prayer and reflection before checking email or phone
- ✅ Make decisions by asking "What would Jesus do?" before "What do I want?"
- ✅ Read Scripture daily to keep God's perspective central
- ✅ Practice the Prayer Process: Gratitude, Awareness, Significant Moments, Peace, Freedom, Others

#### Keep In Mind

- The desire for God is written on your heart. It cannot be erased.
- You are created by God and for God.
- God never ceases to draw you to him.
- You will only find the truth and happiness you are looking for in God.
- The whole meaning and purpose of your existence is wrapped up in God. Separated from him, you and your life lose their meaning.

#### Security & Safety Notes

- Do not let resistance to prayer keep you from it. Resistance knows that if it can keep you from praying, it will win many more battles throughout the day.
- Be honest with God in prayer. He already knows your heart.

#### Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Treating prayer as one more task to check off the list
  **Solution:** Approach prayer as a relationship, not a ritual. Speak with God as you would with a friend.
- **Problem:** Believing you do not have time for prayer
  **Solution:** Start with ten minutes. If you are too busy for ten minutes with God, you are too busy.
- **Problem:** Expecting instant answers from prayer
  **Solution:** Listen for guidance over time. God often speaks in quiet whispers, not dramatic displays.

### Lesson 3: Understand That We Resist God by Resisting Happiness

#### Examples

##### Example 1: The Insanity of Resistance

The definition of resist is "to withstand, strive against, or oppose." To oppose God is a fool's errand. Only the insane and egomaniacal would resist God, and yet we all fall into both categories from time to time. When we resist happiness, we are really resisting God because God is happiness.

##### Example 2: The Coach and the Participant

A coach cannot take credit for a participant's successes or blame for their failures. The participant's successes and failures are their own. God wants you to be happy even more than you want to be happy yourself, but he will not force you. You must choose to cooperate with his dream for you.

#### Step 1: Recognize When You Want Something for Others More Than They Want It for Themselves

This is a subtle form of resistance. You may be trying to push someone toward something they are not ready for. Learn to recognize this pattern and respect others' free will.

#### Step 2: Reflect on the Paradox

You know the things that will make you happy, but you do not always do them. You know how to unleash happiness in your life, but you resist. Why? Because resistance is easier than the discipline required for true happiness.

#### Step 3: Accept That Resistance Is Your Daily Enemy

Resistance will not quietly go away and leave you alone. You have to slay it like a dragon, and you have to slay it anew each day. This is not pessimism; it is realism that empowers you to fight.

#### Best Practices

- ✅ Start each day acknowledging that resistance will be present
- ✅ Do not be surprised when resistance shows up; be prepared
- ✅ Remember that God wants your happiness more than you do
- ✅ Choose cooperation with God rather than resistance to him

#### Keep In Mind

- We resist all sorts of things for all sorts of reasons, but it usually comes down to this: we resist happiness.
- At the same time we have an insatiable desire for happiness. This paradox is at the heart of the human condition.
- Nobody gets to escape the battle with resistance: popes and presidents, kings and janitors, rich and poor, educated and uneducated, young and old.

#### Security & Safety Notes

- Do not use the excuse "I did not know" when you resist what you know is good. Naming resistance removes this excuse.
- Be honest about your resistance. Denial feeds it.

#### Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Thinking resistance only affects "other people"
  **Solution:** The author, an accomplished writer with twenty books, battles resistance daily. If it affects him, it affects you.
- **Problem:** Giving up after a defeat
  **Solution:** You never defeat resistance once and for all. Get back up and fight again tomorrow.
- **Problem:** Romanticizing resistance as part of the creative process
  **Solution:** Resistance is not creativity. Resistance is the enemy of creativity. Do not confuse the two.

### Lesson 4: Build Strong Habits to Break Through Resistance

#### Examples

##### Example 1: The Path of Least Resistance

The path of least resistance effortlessly creates negative routines, rituals, and rhythms. Do you procrastinate by watching television? Do you try to spend your way to happiness? Do you eat when you are not hungry to deal with emotions? These are the negative patterns that resistance loves.

##### Example 2: The Morning Routine

A morning walk, prayer, and jumping straight into work is a bulletproof recipe for exponentially increasing your chances of having a fabulous day. When you establish strong positive habits, they effortlessly defeat resistance.

#### Step 1: Audit Your Current Habits

Write down your three best habits. Now write down three habits that you need to change. Be brutally honest. Resistance loves the negative patterns in your life.

#### Step 2: Develop One New Positive Habit

If you do not like what you find when you take an inventory of your life, start developing a great new habit today. Strong habits will help you break through resistance. Good habits effortlessly defeat it.

#### Step 3: Offer Each Hour to God

Set an alarm to go off each hour. Take a deep breath and lift the coming hour up to God. Ten seconds at the beginning of each hour will change your life. Every activity can be transformed into prayer.

#### Best Practices

- ✅ Schedule your habits. If you do not schedule it, it probably will not happen.
- ✅ Start small. One new habit at a time.
- ✅ Track your progress daily.
- ✅ Find an accountability partner.

#### Keep In Mind

- Our lives change when our habits change.
- Resistance hates good, strong, positive habits in your life.
- Habits—good and bad—have an enormous impact on our lives.
- God loves ordinary things. Every hour of work, every ordinary activity, can be transformed into prayer.

#### Security & Safety Notes

- Do not let resistance convince you that you are too busy to develop good habits. Start with ten seconds per hour.
- Be wary of replacing one bad habit with another. Replace, do not merely remove.

#### Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Trying to change everything at once
  **Solution:** Focus on one habit at a time. Small, consistent changes beat dramatic overhauls.
- **Problem:** Skipping a day and abandoning the habit entirely
  **Solution:** One missed day is not failure. Get back on track the next day.
- **Problem:** Waiting for motivation
  **Solution:** Motivation follows action, not the other way around. Start even if you do not feel like it.

### Lesson 5: Practice Gratitude and Never Get Discouraged

#### Examples

##### Example 1: The Blessing Count

We have so much to be happy about, and it is easy to lose sight of this. When you scan the world on any given day, there is still an overwhelming amount of suffering. But focusing on what you have rather than what you lack anchors you to the present moment.

##### Example 2: The Finished Book

Fifty-seven percent of books that are purchased are not read to completion. Why? Resistance. You have beaten resistance by reading this far. Happiness is a choice. Resistance almost always stands between you and happiness.

#### Step 1: Count Your Blessings

Make a list of ten people, experiences, things, or blessings you are grateful for. Do this daily. Gratitude rewires your brain to see the good that is already present in your life.

#### Step 2: Remember That Every Moment Is a New Chance

If you ever feel discouraged or defeated, remember this: every moment is a chance to start anew, a chance to turn it all around. Resistance does not get the final word. You do.

#### Step 3: Celebrate Breaking Through Resistance

When you do break through resistance, celebrate that, and then press on to live the life God imagined for you before the beginning of time. Do not just survive resistance; thrive in spite of it.

#### Best Practices

- ✅ Keep a gratitude journal with ten items each day
- ✅ Share your gratitude with others; express appreciation openly
- ✅ When discouraged, recall past victories over resistance
- ✅ Remember that you are not alone in the battle

#### Keep In Mind

- Gratitude anchors us to the present moment, reminding us of what matters most and what matters least.
- Resistance does not go away, but once you get into the habit of beating it, that knowledge becomes very powerful.
- All great stories have a villain and a hero. Resistance is the villain, and you are the hero.

#### Security & Safety Notes

- Do not let gratitude become complacency. Be grateful, but also keep growing.
- Do not compare your gratitude practice to others. Your journey is your own.

#### Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Only being grateful when things go well
  **Solution:** Practice gratitude especially when things are difficult. This is when it is most powerful.
- **Problem:** Letting one bad moment ruin your perspective
  **Solution:** Return to your gratitude list. One bad moment does not define your life.
- **Problem:** Thinking you have nothing to be grateful for
  **Solution:** Start with the basics: breath, shelter, food, the ability to read. Build from there.
